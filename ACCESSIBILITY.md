# Accessibility in Peek

This page is about Peek's App Store app, on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

## Works today

The App Store lists two accessibility features for Peek, on iPhone, iPad and
Mac:

- **Differentiate Without Color Alone:** every parcel status is written in
  words, with a symbol, never shown by colour alone.
- **Reduced Motion:** with Reduce Motion on, Peek's own animations, the tilt
  effect and the story that plays by itself on first launch stop. On iPhone
  and iPad, opening a parcel cross-fades instead of zooming.

Peek also does these, which the App Store doesn't list yet:

- **Larger Text:** first launch, adding a parcel, the Share extension and pull
  to refresh grow through the largest text sizes.
- **VoiceOver:** archiving, restoring, muting and copying are announced,
  headings are marked, and full-screen views close with the escape gesture.
- **Voice Control:** controls answer to the words they show.
- **Contrast:** secondary text has stronger contrast.

## Still working on

- **Larger Text:** at the very largest sizes, a few screens still overlap on
  iPhone and iPad.
- **VoiceOver and Voice Control:** they need testing on a device before Peek
  claims them.
- **Dark Interface:** Settings has a Dark appearance, but Peek starts light,
  and the Share extension stays light.
- **Sufficient Contrast:** some text, and the Share extension, still fall
  short.

## Report a problem

Write to [hello@peektracker.com](mailto:hello@peektracker.com). Say which
device and which screen, and the feature you use, such as VoiceOver or Larger
Text.

Bugs also go to
[Peek's issues on GitHub](https://github.com/plhery/peek-delivery-tracker/issues).
Issues are public: leave out real tracking numbers, postcodes and sign-in
codes.
