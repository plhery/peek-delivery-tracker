## Your account and what you add

- Your account is tied to your email address, or to your Google or Apple
  sign-in. Keep access to it: whoever can sign in with it can use the account.
- What you add stays yours. You let Peek store it and send tracking numbers to
  carriers and tracking services, as far as that is needed to follow your
  parcels.
- Settings › Account & data › **Download my data** gives you a copy, and
  **Delete account** erases the account with its parcels.

## What carriers publish

Peek shows what carriers and tracking services publish about a parcel. It is
not a carrier, and it cannot redirect, hold or find a parcel. An update can be
late, missing or wrong: for anything that matters, check with the carrier or
the seller. Carriers' names belong to their owners, and Peek is not affiliated
with them.

## Assistants and personal tokens

- An assistant you connect, such as Claude or ChatGPT, acts on your account
  within what you allowed: it sees your parcels and, if you allowed it, adds
  and archives them. What it does with that access is between you and its
  provider, whose own terms apply.
- You can disconnect an assistant at any time in Settings › Assistants.
- Peek is not made or endorsed by the companies behind these assistants.
