ACS Courier is a Greek courier, part of the Quest Group, with its own company in Cyprus and SmartPoint lockers in both countries. Track a parcel with its 10-digit shipping number on the Track and Trace page of acscourier.net (Greece) or cyp.acscourier.net (Cyprus), switching the page to English, or paste the number in the box above and pick ACS Courier. ACS puts that number in every SMS, Viber message and email it sends you about the parcel.

## ACS tracking number: what it looks like

ACS calls it the shipping number; shops often say voucher.

| Shape (invented) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `0000000000` | An ACS shipping number: 10 digits, no letters, the last a check digit. |
| `RR000000005GR` | Not ACS: a postal number ending `GR` belongs to [ELTA Hellenic Post](carrier:elta). |

It's in ACS's SMS, Viber or email, in the locker message and often in the shop's shipping email. The number on the "Absent" note left after a missed delivery works in ACS's tracker too, and a return gets its own new number.

Ten digits are not unique to ACS. [Geniki Taxydromiki](carrier:geniki-taxydromiki) vouchers look exactly the same, and [DHL Express](carrier:dhl-express), [Relais Colis](carrier:relais-colis) and others use 10 digits too. If the shop's email names another carrier, track it there. More in [tracking number formats](guide:tracking-number-formats).

**Cyprus and orders from [Shein, Temu or AliExpress](guide:tracking-from-china).** With DPD or [Speedy](carrier:speedy), ACS texts you its 10-digit number once the parcel reaches Cyprus. No SMS or Viber message yet means, ACS says, it hasn't arrived and ACS can't tell you more.

## ACS Courier tracking statuses in English

Both ACS sites show English on their `/en/` pages and Greek on `/el/`. The Cypriot site lists every scan; the Greek site shows only five milestones. These are the Cypriot site's English scans, in the order a parcel meets them.

| ACS's status | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `Print ACSConnect/WebTools label` | The sender printed the label. ACS doesn't have the parcel yet. |
| `Pick-up from sender` | ACS has collected it. |
| `Departure to destination` | It's leaving for the branch named after these words. |
| `Arrival-departure from HUB` | Passing through a sorting hub, such as HUB ATHINON. |
| `Arrival to` | At the branch that delivers it. |
| `On delivery` | With a courier, out for delivery. |
| `Not delivered` | The courier couldn't deliver it. |
| `Shipment placed in SmartPoint` | Waiting for you at the SmartPoint locker or point named beside it. |
| `Delivery to consignee` | Delivered to you, the consignee. |
| `Delivery via SmartPoint` | You picked it up from the SmartPoint. |

`SENT NOTIFICATION` and `SENT EMAIL NOTIFICATION` only mean ACS messaged you; the parcel hasn't moved. On the Greek site you see milestones instead: `Pick up`, `At the ACS hub` (followed by the hub's city), `At the ACS point`, then `Out for delivery` or `In ACS Smartpoint Locker`, then `Delivered`.

The "Shipment Status" column sums things up. `In Transit` usually means on its way, but ACS also uses it for damage and other causes: read the latest scan. `Absent` means nobody was home and a note was left. `Attention required` means an unknown recipient or a changed, wrong or incomplete address. `Denial of delivery` means the parcel or its charge was refused. `Pending` is a problem awaiting resolution, `Return in transit to sender` and `Returned to sender` a return. `Shipment not found` means ACS doesn't know the number (yet). For the general meaning of each step, see [tracking status meanings](guide:tracking-statuses).

## ACS delivery, SmartPoint lockers and missed deliveries

**Attempts.** In Greece ACS's basic service makes up to three attempts; in Cyprus, up to two.

**Missed it?** If the shop gave ACS your phone or email, ACS messages you with options: another day, a pickup point (an ACS Smart Point or ACS store) or a corrected address. Changes need a PIN ACS sends you. In Greece, ACS ReDirect, through the link in the SMS, offers one of the next 3 working days, the nearest ACS store or a Smart Point at a partner Shell station. The ACS Mobile App also reschedules.

**Can't be reached.** With missing contact details or an unclear address, ACS keeps the parcel at the destination store for 5 calendar days, then sends it back.

**Lockers in Greece.** Open the ACS Smartpoint Locker with the shipping number and the 6-digit PIN from the SMS or Viber. Lost it? Resend it from the locker screen. The parcel usually waits 3 calendar days, then 3 more working days at the ACS store serving that locker, then goes back to the sender. Lockers take parcels up to 10 kg.

**Lockers in Cyprus.** Open them with the link in the SMS, Viber or email, or with the ACS Mobile App over Bluetooth. A parcel usually waits 2 calendar days, then you collect it from the ACS store named in an SMS.

## ACS parcel late, lost or damaged

**Not arrived.** ACS's FAQ sends recipients to the delivery branch, with the parcel number and a description of the goods and packaging. The shop holds the contract with ACS, so ask it too. See [tracking that isn't updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

**Damaged.** ACS's terms want a claim within 24 hours of delivery, by SMS, email or in writing; its charter lets you accept a parcel "with reservation" up to one day after receiving it. Act within a day.

**Lost or late.** ACS treats a parcel as lost when it can't be located 5 working days after pickup or after its last scan. If ACS is at fault for a delay, compensation starts 3 working days after the agreed day; past 15 working days the parcel counts as lost. Compensation goes to whoever booked and paid, usually the shop, so let the shop claim. ACS answers within 21 days.

## Tracking ACS Courier parcels with Peek

[Peek](/) can't tell an ACS number from its 10 digits alone, so it asks the carriers it fits whether they know it: ACS (when the last digit passes ACS's check), DHL Express, Geniki Taxydromiki, [BOX NOW](carrier:box-now) and [DHL eCommerce Iberia](carrier:dhl-ecommerce-es), naming ACS when ACS knows it, unless DHL Express knows the same number. Then, or if ACS isn't asked, pick "ACS Courier" with "Change", or paste an ACS Track and Trace link with `trackingNumber=` from either site, which names ACS at once (myACS links aren't read).

Peek reads ACS's tracker through its Cypriot English site: each scan in ACS's English words, with the branch or hub and its time. While the parcel moves it can show ACS's expected delivery day, and it names the SmartPoint where the parcel waits. No weight; the recipient, signatory, sender, delivery comments and branch contacts are dropped. Peek checks every 10 minutes by day, every 2 minutes once the parcel is out for delivery, and hourly overnight.

When Speedy names ACS as the carrier delivering in Greece or Cyprus, the parcel can be followed to ACS. Notifications come for every scan, important steps only, or on delivery day only.

## Questions about ACS Courier tracking

### What is ACS Courier?

ACS Courier and Postal Services S.A. is a Greek courier in business since 1981 and part of the Quest Group. It calls itself the leading courier in Greece, with more than 350 service points in Greece, Cyprus, Albania and Bulgaria.

### Is ACS tracking the same as DHL?

No, they are separate companies. Both use 10-digit numbers, so a tracker that guesses from the number may show DHL: pick ACS Courier by hand or use acscourier.net.

### How long does ACS Courier take to deliver?

In Greece, for parcels leaving Attica, ACS gives 1 to 2 business days to the mainland, 1 to 3 to the islands and up to 5 to hard-to-reach areas. Within Cyprus, 1 to 2 working days.

### What is ACS Courier's email address?

For complaints in Greece, info@acscourier.gr; in Cyprus, helpdesk@acscyprus.com. Treat emails from other domains as fake.
