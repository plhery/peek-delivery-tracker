Allegro One is the Polish marketplace Allegro's own delivery network: Allegro One Box parcel lockers, Allegro One Punkt pickup points and Allegro One Kurier home delivery, all part of Allegro Delivery. Track a parcel in Allegro under "Moje przesyłki" (My parcels) or on Allegro's tracking page, or paste the number in the box above. The number is in your purchase details, in the app and in the email Allegro sends once the number is added.

## Where to find your Allegro tracking number

Allegro shows the carrier, the parcel number and the status in:

- **"Moje przesyłki"** on allegro.pl or in the app, from the moment the parcel awaits dispatch until it reaches you.
- **"Moje zakupy"** (My purchases), under the purchase's details.
- **The email** you get once the seller adds the number.

Peek's catalog knows these shapes (characters invented):

| Shape | Whose it is |
| --- | --- |
| `A000000000` | Allegro One: `A0` and 8 letters or digits, 10 characters. |
| `AD00000000` | Allegro Delivery: `AD` and 8 letters or digits, named Allegro One too. |
| `ALE000000000000` | An older Allegro One form: `ALE` and 12 digits. |

No number yet? The seller adds it to the order, unless Allegro receives it automatically from a carrier integrated with it. See [how to find your tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number).

Allegro Delivery also uses ORLEN Paczka, DHL and DPD. Their Allegro Delivery parcels carry Allegro's own number and are tracked at Allegro, with the partner's scans in the same wording. A parcel a seller sends outside Allegro Delivery, on their own contract with a carrier, carries that carrier's number and is tracked there, for example with [ORLEN Paczka](carrier:orlen-paczka) or [DPD Poland](carrier:dpd-pl).

## Allegro One tracking statuses explained

Allegro's tracking service words each scan in Polish or English. These are its English wordings, the ones Peek shows, in the order a parcel meets them:

| Allegro One's status | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `Parcel has been prepared by the sender` | Label created. Allegro One doesn't have the parcel yet. |
| `Parcel has been accepted at the sorting center` | At a sorting centre. |
| `Parcel has been dispatched to its destination point` | On its way to the locker, point or branch serving you. |
| `Parcel has been released for delivery` | Out for delivery, also when it's heading for a One Box. |
| `No space in the parcel locker` | The locker was full: a failed attempt. The courier took the parcel back. |
| `Parcel is awaiting pick-up` | Ready for pickup in the One Box or One Punkt. |
| `Parcel is awaiting pick-up — pick-up deadline is about to expire` | A reminder: your time to pick it up is nearly over. |
| `Time for parcel pick-up has expired` | Not picked up in time: it goes back to the seller. |
| `Parcel has been returned to the sender` | On its way back to the sender. Despite the wording, they don't have it yet. |
| `Parcel has been delivered` | Delivered, or picked up by you (see below). |

`Parcel has been delivered` also follows your own pickup from a One Box: there is no separate "picked up" scan.

Some lines look worse than they are. `Parcel may be delivered with delay` and `Parcel delivery time has been arranged with the recipient` are notices; the parcel keeps moving. `Parcel has been redirected to a different point` follows your own choice of another point. `No parcel` and even `Parcel has been lost` can be followed by later scans that find it again.

## One Box, One Punkt and One Kurier delivery

**Pickup notice.** When the parcel reaches the locker or point, Allegro or its partner tells you by email or SMS, or with a notification in the Allegro app, where and by when to pick it up.

**At a One Box**, enter the pickup code, alone or with your phone number, use the QR code sent by SMS or email, or open the locker with the Allegro app. **At a One Punkt**, give the parcel number and the pickup code.

**How long it waits.** Allegro gives 2 calendar days (48 hours) at a One Box and 2 working days at a One Punkt. At selected lockers and points Allegro may let you extend the time under separate terms; the status then reads `Time for pick-up has been extended`. If you don't pick it up in time, the parcel goes back to the seller.

**Full or closed locker?** Allegro may put the parcel in another locker within 3 km (6 km in rural areas) and tells you where and by when to pick it up.

**One Kurier** delivers to the ordered address in selected cities. Allegro may let you redirect a courier parcel once, if its size allows, to a locker or point that takes redirects within the branch delivering it. You ask for it in the Allegro app or on allegro.pl, from posting until it's handed over for delivery; this adds a working day, and cash-on-delivery parcels can't be redirected. If nobody is in, the courier leaves a notice (`Notice`): collect the parcel within 48 hours where it says, or arrange a paid new delivery.

## Allegro One parcel late, lost or damaged

**Waiting too long.** Check "Moje przesyłki". Not there at all? Ask the seller for the tracking number. See also [tracking not updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

**Seller not helping.** Report a "problem z zakupem" (purchase problem); you can do this from an hour after paying. The seller has 24 hours to answer your first message, not counting Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays, and to bring Allegro in, click "Pomoc Allegro" (Allegro help). For a delay, a lost parcel or an item missing from the parcel, the seller has 7 days to resolve it. If Allegro then can't see that it's resolved, it asks you, and if you say it still isn't, it suggests a claim under Allegro Ochrona Kupujących (buyer protection). On Allegro Lokalnie and with sellers who use Magazyn Allegro, Allegro's warehouse, you still open a "Dyskusja" (Discussion).

**Marked delivered but not received.** Check whether someone in your household, a neighbour or your company's reception picked it up. Then see [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).

**Lost, damaged or late: complaints.** Allegro takes delivery complaints as the operator of Allegro Delivery: electronically, logged in to your Allegro account or by confirming the parcel's details, or in another way postal law allows. You may complain if the parcel reached you, or if it didn't and the seller passed you the right to claim. Hidden loss or damage must be reported within 7 days of accepting the parcel; otherwise you have up to 12 months from posting. Allegro answers within 30 days at most.

## Allegro One contact and customer service

Allegro handles customer service for Allegro Delivery parcels, Allegro One's included.

- **Complaints:** to Allegro, as above.
- **A One Box that won't open:** the technical helpline whose number is on the locker.
- **The seller:** for a late parcel, report a purchase problem, as above.

## Questions about Allegro One tracking

### What is Allegro One Box?

Allegro's own parcel locker, part of Allegro One with One Punkt pickup points and One Kurier couriers. Parcels reach it through Allegro Delivery, carried by Allegro One or by DPD, DHL and ORLEN Paczka.

### How long does a parcel wait in an Allegro One Box?

2 calendar days (48 hours); at selected lockers Allegro may let you extend that. At a One Punkt it's 2 working days.

### How much does Allegro One delivery cost?

The seller sets the delivery price in the offer's price list. With Allegro Smart! under its current terms, delivery to lockers, pickup points or by courier is free on purchases of 49.90 zł or more from one seller.
