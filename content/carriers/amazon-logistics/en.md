Amazon Logistics is Amazon's own delivery network: the Delivery Service Partners and Amazon Flex drivers who bring Amazon orders to your door. You track the parcel in Your Orders on the Amazon site you ordered from, under Track Package.

## Amazon Logistics tracking number

On Amazon.co.uk, click Returns & Orders at the top right. In the Amazon app, tap the person icon at the bottom: Your Orders lists past and upcoming deliveries. In a phone browser, tap the person icon at the top right, then Track & Manage Your Orders. Pick the order and choose Track Package.

Once your parcel is 10 stops away, Amazon sends a push notification if you've opted into delivery alerts. Tap it, or choose Track Package, to follow the parcel on Amazon Map Tracking in real time and see when the driver is getting close.

These are the shapes Peek recognises as Amazon Logistics:

| Shape (invented) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `TBA000000000000` | `TBA` and 12 digits. |
| `TBC000000000000` | `TBC` and 12 digits. |
| `TBM000000000000` | `TBM` and 12 digits. |
| `UK0000000000` | A European country code and 10 digits (`UK`, `GB`, `IE`, `FR`, `DE` and others). |

Two lookalikes. An order number such as `000-0000000-0000000` is not a tracking number. And two other letters followed by 10 digits, such as `US0000000000`, aren't an Amazon Logistics shape. No number at all? See [how to find your tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number).

## Amazon Logistics delivery to Lockers and Counters

In the UK, you can have an Amazon order sent to an Amazon Locker or an Amazon Counter instead of your home.

**Amazon Locker.** Pick a Locker at checkout: search by postcode, address or landmark, or add one in Your Addresses. Amazon then emails a delivery confirmation with instructions. You may need the six-digit code, a barcode or the Amazon Shopping app to open the Locker, so take your phone. To let someone else collect, forward them the email. Not every item can go to a Locker: among other rules, it must weigh less than 4.5 kg and measure less than 42 × 35 × 32 cm, and age-restricted items can't be sent there. You have three calendar days to collect. After that, Amazon returns the parcel and refunds you automatically.

**Amazon Counter.** Counters are staffed pickup points in partner shops. At checkout, choose "Find a collect location nearby" to see Counters and Lockers with their pickup times. Amazon emails you when the parcel arrives. Depending on the shop, you may need a pickup code, ID and/or a signature; the code is also in your order's tracking information. If you don't collect it in time, Amazon refunds you and returns the parcel automatically.

## Amazon Logistics parcel not received or late

**Late or missing.** Once an order has shipped, you can't cancel it, but you can refuse the parcel when it's delivered or return it once it arrives. What to do next: [tracking that isn't updating](guide:tracking-not-updating), [missed deliveries](guide:missed-delivery), [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received) and [returned to sender](guide:returned-to-sender).

**Marketplace sellers.** A seller who ships the order itself handles its customer service; if the listing was eligible for Prime, Amazon says to contact Amazon Customer Service instead. To reach a seller, select View Your Item next to the order in Your Orders, then Contact Seller. Sellers should reply within two business days, and their messages appear in your Message Centre. If you want a refund and the seller hasn't answered within 48 hours, most Marketplace orders are covered by the A-to-z Guarantee, which covers on-time delivery and the item's condition: you claim directly from Amazon. Who ships each kind of order: [tracking an Amazon order](guide:amazon-orders).

## Amazon Logistics contact number and customer service

Amazon Customer Service handles questions about your orders. On Amazon.co.uk:

- **Help pages.** The Customer Service page answers common questions, from tracking an order to replacing a damaged item.
- **Live chat.** On the Customer Service page, choose Something else, then Contact Us (in the app: I need more help). Type "Chat with an associate now".
- **Phone.** In the same chat, type "Request a phone call", enter your number and choose "Call me now": Amazon calls you. Associates take calls every day from 6am to midnight.
- **Social media.** @AmazonHelp answers on Facebook, X and Instagram, 24/7.
- **At a Locker.** The direct customer service number is printed on every Amazon Locker.

> If a message mentions a purchase you didn't make, Amazon says not to reply or click any link: check your orders in your account or the app instead. Amazon won't ask for payment by phone or email, and never asks you to buy a gift card. Don't call numbers sent by text or email, or found in search results. Your Message Centre keeps a record of every contact Amazon has had with you, and Amazon's self-service tool lets you report anything suspicious. ## Amazon Logistics tracking in Peek

[Peek](/) can't track or save an Amazon Logistics parcel: Amazon shows its status, history and photo only in your account. Paste one of these numbers, or a track.amazon link, and Peek first checks Amazon's public Amazon Shipping tracker. If Amazon answers, it's an [Amazon Shipping](carrier:amazon-shipping) parcel, and Peek tracks it: "Public tracking is available with Amazon Shipping. Peek will keep this delivery up to date." If Amazon no longer gives its history, it can stay as a reference.

Otherwise Peek says "Amazon Logistics deliveries are usually tracked in Your Orders on Amazon." and offers "Open my Amazon orders", which opens the order history on the site the number points to: amazon.co.uk for `UK` and `GB`, amazon.ie for `IE`, amazon.com for `TBA`. If the check can't run, tap "Check again". An order number gets "That looks like an Amazon order number".

## Questions about Amazon Logistics

### What is Amazon Logistics?

It's the network that takes Amazon orders on the last stretch, from Amazon's delivery stations to your door. In the UK, Amazon works with Delivery Service Partners, small firms that hire their own drivers and run 20 to 40 vans each, and with independent drivers through Amazon Flex. Some orders go to other couriers instead, such as DPD or Royal Mail.

### Is Amazon Logistics legit?

Yes, it's how Amazon delivers its own orders, and the parcel shows in Your Orders. A text about a delivery or purchase you weren't expecting is another matter: don't click, and check Your Orders in the app or on the site instead.

### How long does Amazon Logistics take to deliver?

Your Orders shows the delivery day, and once the driver is 10 stops away you can follow the parcel on Amazon Map Tracking. In Ireland, Amazon delivers seven days a week from its fulfilment centre at Baldonnell in Dublin and its delivery stations in Rathcoole and Ballycoolin.

### Is Amazon Logistics the same as Amazon?

For you, yes: the drivers work for Amazon's Delivery Service Partners or through Amazon Flex, but you deal with Amazon, in Your Orders and through Amazon Customer Service. A third-party seller who ships the order itself handles its own customer service.
