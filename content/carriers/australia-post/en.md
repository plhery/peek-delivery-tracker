Australia Post is Australia's national postal service. Track a parcel on [auspost.com.au/mypost/track](https://auspost.com.au/mypost/track), in the AusPost app or by pasting the number in the box above, no account needed. The number comes from the sender: the shop's order confirmation, an email from Australia Post or your Post Office receipt.

## Australia Post tracking number: where to find it and what it looks like

Australia Post calls it the "tracking number, or article ID". It's in one of these places:

- **Bought online:** the shop's order confirmation or invoice. If the sender gave Australia Post your email, it emails you the number and progress itself.
- **Posted at a Post Office:** often on your receipt.
- **Prepaid satchel:** on the barcode and the removable sticker.

Australia Post can't find a parcel by name or address, so if you have no number, ask the sender. Parcels matched to your MyPost email or mobile appear in your account and the app on their own.

Australia Post publishes no number format. These are the shapes Peek knows, with invented examples:

| Shape (example) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `ABC123456789000123456` | Article ID, 21 characters: 3 letters or digits, 9 digits, `000`, 6 digits. |
| `12ABC123456789000123456` | The same with 2 digits in front (23 characters). |
| `94001234567890000123456` | 23 digits starting `9400`: still Australia Post. |
| `12ABC1234567` | Consignment number, 12 characters. |
| `ABC1234567` | Consignment number, 10 characters. |
| `ABCZ12345678` | StarTrack consignment number: 3 letters or digits, `Z`, 8 digits. |
| `0012345678901234567890` | Prepaid barcode: 22 digits starting `00` to `03`. |
| `EE000000005AU` | EMS, parcel or tracked-letter number: `C`, `E` or `L`, a letter, 9 digits, `AU`. |
| `RR000000005AU` | Other international postal number ending `AU`, such as registered mail. |

Lookalikes: 2 letters and 10 digits (`AB1234567890`) belong to [Aramex Australia](carrier:aramex-au) or [Aramex New Zealand](carrier:aramex-nz); a 22-digit `9400` number is USPS's. Number not found? It may be wrong or not scanned yet: Australia Post suggests trying again in 24 hours. More in [tracking number formats](guide:tracking-number-formats).

## What Australia Post tracking statuses mean

The tracking page shows a short milestone above the scans.

| Australia Post's status | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `Label created by sender` | The sender has made a label; Australia Post doesn't have the parcel yet. |
| `Pending` | Not scanned into Australia Post's system yet. It should update in 24 to 48 hours. |
| `We've got it` | Australia Post has the parcel. |
| `It's on its way` | Posted, processed and moving through the sorting facilities. |
| `International arrival - awaiting clearance` | From abroad: waiting for Australian customs. |
| `Possible delay` | Weather, an emergency, a sender mistake or customs is slowing it down. It still comes. |
| `It's coming today` | On the delivery vehicle today. |
| `Attempted delivery` | No one was available, or the driver couldn't get in. |
| `Awaiting collection` | Waiting at a Post Office or Parcel Locker; the scan names which. |
| `Delivered` | Delivered to the address. |
| `Delivered - Left in a safe place` | Left in a safe place at the address. |
| `Returning to sender` | Not collected, not known at the address, declined or damaged: going back. |

If `Pending` or `Ready for processing` lasts more than a week, ask the sender whether the parcel was really sent. Interstate parcels go through the big centres at Redbank QLD, Chullora NSW or Sunshine West VIC, so a scan far from both of you is normal. On a parcel from abroad, `It's on its way` means it has arrived in Australia, and the status holds until the parcel is on the delivery vehicle.

Posties usually deliver between 7 a.m. and 5 p.m., Monday to Friday; a parcel that hasn't come by 5 p.m. is likely to be prioritised the next day. With `It's coming today`, some areas also get an SMS with a 2-hour delivery window.

`Contact sender` means the parcel went back because the details were wrong or incomplete, or a collection notice went unanswered for 10 business days. `Returned` means it's back with the sender: they arrange redelivery.

## Missed delivery, Post Office pickup and Parcel Lockers

If no one is home and there's no safe place, the parcel goes to a nearby Post Office, or may go to a Parcel Locker if your mobile number is on it (you get an access code). MyPost users get an email, app or SMS notice; others may still get a "Sorry We Missed You" card.

A Post Office holds the parcel for 10 business days, then returns it. There's no redelivery from a Post Office; moving it to another one costs $7.55, paid when you collect (the transfer form itself still says $7.20), and if you don't know why it went to that Post Office, you can report a delivery issue and ask for the fee to be waived. Bring one photo ID, or two non-photo IDs with one showing your address. Someone else can collect with an authorisation from the app, a form or the card, plus their own ID.

Parcel Lockers are free and, at most locations, open 24/7. Collect within 2 business days (48 hours, not counting weekends and public holidays) with the access code or the app's QR code; after that the parcel moves to a nearby Post Office for 10 business days, with a reminder email 24 hours before.

With a free MyPost account you can redirect a matched parcel to another address until it's on board for delivery (allow an extra business day), and choose Safe Drop up to an hour before.

## Australia Post tracking not updating, late or delivered but not received

No scans for a while doesn't mean it stopped: Australia Post says the parcel is still moving. Most late parcels arrive within 5 business days of the expected date. See [tracking that isn't updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

Australia Post sends receivers to the sender first: confirm the number and address, and the sender raises an enquiry for you. If that fails, use the late or missing item form (it needs a free MyPost account) once 10 business days have passed since the expected date. Parcels from overseas can spend up to 15 days in Australian customs, longer if duties are payable; `Possible delay` can mean customs is holding one, and if customs or quarantine hold it for long, they write to you. A long delay on a parcel from abroad is raised with the post it was sent with, so ask the sender.

Marked delivered but not there? Check safe places, your household and the mailroom, look for a delivery photo in the app or the form, then submit the form; if the photo shows it delivered to you and it's gone, report it to the police. Damaged: take the parcel and packaging to a Post Office that does damage assessments. Most services include up to $100 for loss or damage; Australia Post usually pays the sender for lost domestic parcels and the receiver for damaged ones, with claims due within 6 months of posting. Bought online? Contact the seller first. More in [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).

## Australia Post contact number and help

As listed on auspost.com.au on 9 November 2026:

- Phone: 13 76 78 (13POST) in Australia, +61 3 8340 7239 from overseas, 8am to 6pm Monday to Friday except national public holidays.
- Online: the late or missing item and delivery issue forms, and a chat with a bot or live agent, on [auspost.com.au/help](https://auspost.com.au/help).
- In person: any Post Office can help lodge a complaint.

StarTrack, Australia Post's courier and freight arm, has its own phone line (13 23 45) and enquiry forms; its parcels are tracked through the same network and show in the AusPost app too.

> Australia Post never calls, texts or emails asking for unauthorised personal or financial details, or for a payment you aren't expecting. Fake texts mention a "redelivery fee", verifying your postcode or a driver who found no safe place, and some arrive under the "AusPost" sender name. Forward them to scams@auspost.com.au.

## Tracking Australia Post parcels with Peek

[Peek](/) names Australia Post at once for 21- and 23-character article IDs. Consignment numbers, StarTrack's included, and 22-digit prepaid barcodes don't settle the carrier on their own, so Peek asks the likely ones at once and names the one that knows the number; if it can't settle, pick Australia Post with "Change". It also names Australia Post at once for an EMS, parcel or tracked-letter number ending `AU` with a valid check digit; any other postal number ending `AU` shows as a postal item while Peek asks Australia Post. Links from auspost.com.au with `id=` (`#/search?id=` included) or `/mypost/track/details/` work and name Australia Post whatever the number; other older `#/` links work only for article IDs and numbers ending `AU`, so paste any other number.

If Australia Post refuses Peek's plain request, a browser on Peek's server opens auspost.com.au instead. Tracking services hold little of Australia Post's history, so if the browser is refused too, the history can be thin.

Peek shows the status, each scan with its place on a map, the time of delivery, the destination country and the name of the Post Office or Parcel Locker holding the parcel. Statuses stay in Australia Post's English; delivered ones read "Delivered". Peek drops the recipient, the address and delivery photos. While the parcel is on its way, it shows Australia Post's expected delivery date, or its range of days, until that date has passed, and on the day the delivery time slot when Australia Post gives one; it hides the date after a failed delivery or a problem, and once the parcel is delivered, returned or waiting for collection. When Australia Post gives no date, as for a parcel posted in a street posting box or with a hand-written address, Peek shows none either; Australia Post's own tracking tool can still estimate one from your postcode. It can't redirect a parcel or change a locker.

It checks every 10 minutes, and up to every 2 minutes (every 5 while it needs the browser) once Australia Post scans the parcel out for delivery: on that day, from 6 a.m. local time or, when Australia Post gives a delivery window, from half an hour before it opens, while Australia Post's own tracker answers. Through tracking services it checks every 15 minutes. When another carrier takes over the delivery, Peek follows the pace of the carrier delivering the parcel. After two days without news (12 hours while the parcel still shows "Label created" or "Received by carrier"), Peek checks hourly. It can notify you at every scan, at important steps or on delivery day only. A parcel posted abroad with a postal number bound for Australia can be followed on Australia Post once Australia Post shows progress.

## Questions about Australia Post tracking

### What does pending mean on Australia Post tracking?

The parcel hasn't been scanned into Australia Post's system yet: it may not be posted yet, or was posted in a street posting box. It should update within 24 to 48 hours. An old number with no activity for over 3 months also shows `Pending`.

### How long does Australia Post hold parcels in a Parcel Locker?

2 business days. Then the parcel moves to the nearest Post Office offering collection, which holds it 10 business days before returning it.

### Do all Australia Post parcels have tracking numbers?

No. International Post Registered and International Economy (Air and Sea) have no tracking as standard. For a parcel coming to Australia, tracking depends on the sending country's post or courier.
