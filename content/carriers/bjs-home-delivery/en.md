BJS Home Delivery (*BJS Distribution Limited*, headquartered in Wednesbury, West Midlands) is a family-run UK specialist two-man home delivery carrier serving major British high-street and online furniture, appliance, lighting, mirror and gym-equipment retailers for over a decade. Alongside standard two-man room-of-choice delivery with a three-hour delivery window, BJS operates *BJS Extra* (specialist assembly and installation), packaging removal/recycling and old-furniture removal. You can track live status, delivery slot details and map updates on [BJS's tracking portal](https://track.bjshomedelivery.com/), or paste your consignment reference into the box above.

## BJS Home Delivery tracking numbers: B-reference format

Check your retailer's dispatch email, booking SMS or delivery confirmation for your BJS consignment reference. BJS prints its reference as `B`, followed by 7 digits, a hyphen and 4 digits (12 alphanumeric characters without the dash):

| Format (invented) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `B4829173-6405` | BJS Home Delivery consignment number as printed on emails and links (`B` + 7 digits + `-` + 4 digits). |
| `B48291736405` | Same 12-character reference written without the hyphen. Shares `B` + 11-digit shape with [Colis Privé](carrier:colis-prive). |

If your large UK home delivery was dispatched by another bulky courier — such as [XLCourier](carrier:xlcourier), [XDP](carrier:xdp) or [Parcelforce](carrier:parcelforce) — see [tracking number formats](guide:tracking-number-formats).

## What BJS Home Delivery tracking statuses mean

BJS tracks two-man orders across a step-by-step progress bar from retailer release to crew delivery on UK local calendar days (`Europe/London`):

| BJS Home Delivery says | What it means for you | Peek shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Released`, `Outstanding` | The retailer released the order to BJS; BJS is waiting for stock arrival or preparing to propose a delivery date. | "Label created" |
| `Received` | BJS received the goods into its UK warehouse/hub. | "Received by carrier" |
| `Scheduled`, `Confirmed`, `In Transit` | Delivery date booked/confirmed or goods moving between BJS's warehouse and the local delivery crew. | "In transit" |
| `En Route` | Your two-man crew is on the road for delivery today. | "Out for delivery" |
| `Failed` | Delivery attempt could not be completed; the bookings team will contact you to reschedule. | "Delivery attempted" |
| `On Hold`, `Cancelled` | Order paused pending retailer instructions or cancelled. | "Needs attention" |
| `Delivered`, `Completed` | Delivery completed and signed on the crew's handheld device. | "Delivered" |

## Preparing for your BJS two-man delivery and property access rules

According to [BJS Home Delivery](https://www.bjshomedelivery.com/en-gb/home-delivery), the [Help Centre](https://www.bjshomedelivery.com/en-gb/help) and [BJS Terms & Conditions](https://track.bjshomedelivery.com/terms-conditions):

- **Three-hour delivery slot and crew arrival:** BJS books a delivery date and provides a **3-hour delivery window**. On arrival, the two-man team introduces themselves, shows ID, checks access routes and confirms any retailer-booked extras (room-of-choice placement, assembly, packaging removal or recycling of old items) before collecting a signature on their handheld device.
- **Check item dimensions and clear access:** measure hallways, doorways and staircases beforehand, clear external paths/driveways and remove breakable ornaments, wall pictures and trailing cables along the route.
- **Flooring protection & safety footwear:** crews are forbidden from removing safety footwear, so BJS requires customers to lay adequate protective coverings over carpets or flooring (no claims apply for unprotected floors).
- **Tight access waiver:** if the crew judges an item cannot be manoeuvred safely without risking product or property damage, they will offer to leave it in a safe room instead; proceeding past that point requires signing a damage waiver on the delivery note.

## Missed delivery, damage or complaints with BJS Home Delivery

- **Missed delivery (`Failed`):** BJS's bookings team will contact you to arrange a new delivery date, or you can call `01922 645 650` with your `B...` reference ready.
- **Order cancellations and missing extras:** all order cancellations must go through the retailer. If a paid extra (such as assembly) is missing from the crew's delivery notes, check with the retailer.
- **Damaged items:** inspect all packaging and piece counts on arrival and tell the crew immediately if you spot damage. If damage is discovered after they leave, send photos and your order reference to your retailer right away.
- **Formal complaints:** email `customerservice@bjshomedelivery.com` or call `01922 645 650` with your name, order number, delivery address and details — BJS aims to respond **within 7 working days**.

## Track a BJS Home Delivery order with Peek

[Peek](/) recognises pasted `track.bjshomedelivery.com` links (`parcel-id=`, `OrderReference=` or `order-number=`) and `bjshomedelivery.com/?parcel-id=` links as BJS Home Delivery automatically. When you paste a `B` + 11-digit reference (`B4829173-6405` or `B48291736405`, digits invented), Peek asks BJS Home Delivery first, or [Colis Privé](carrier:colis-prive) first when you track from France, and shows "BJS Home Delivery has this parcel." once BJS confirms it; you can also tap "Change" while adding or "Change carrier" on the parcel screen (searching "BJS Distribution" or "BJS Two-Man Home Delivery" finds it too).

Peek reads BJS's mini-track service directly. It gives one status for the order, with that status's day when there is one: Peek shows the stage ("Label created", "Received by carrier", "In transit", "Out for delivery", "Delivery attempted", "Needs attention", "Delivered") and BJS's status wording. BJS gives no time, so Peek also looks for a dated history from tracking services. The 3-hour window, the van's position, the progress rail, the booking options and the personal details stay on BJS's own tracker: open [track.bjshomedelivery.com](https://track.bjshomedelivery.com/) for your slot or the van map.

While you have the parcel open in Peek, or on its delivery day with notifications on, Peek checks up to every 10 minutes; on the day BJS shows `En Route`, every 2 minutes from 6 a.m. UK time, while BJS's own tracker answers. Otherwise Peek checks hourly. After 12 hours with only "Label created" or "Received by carrier", or two days without news, it checks hourly however you follow it, and every 6 hours after seven days at "Label created". When BJS's tracker doesn't answer and Peek follows the parcel through tracking services alone, it checks hourly, every 3 hours after a quiet day, and up to every 15 minutes on the out-for-delivery day.

When BJS's newest status is four days old and the order is still at "Label created", "Received by carrier" or "In transit", Peek flags it with that status's day, or the day Peek saw it change when BJS gives none, such as "No tracking update since Mon 12 Oct". The parcel's page then says "Parcels often go quiet on long flights, at customs or over a weekend. Peek keeps asking BJS Home Delivery.", with a "Get help from BJS Home Delivery" link to BJS's Help Centre. After three weeks with no new scan, it says "Nothing new for over three weeks. If it still hasn’t arrived, ask the seller. Peek keeps asking BJS Home Delivery."

You can pick alert presets for "Every scan", "Important steps" or "Delivery only". Peek monitors delivery status only and cannot reschedule your BJS slot, view live van GPS coordinates or book assembly extras on your behalf.

## Questions about BJS Home Delivery tracking

### What does a BJS Home Delivery tracking number look like?

BJS consignment numbers start with `B`, followed by 7 digits, a hyphen and 4 digits (for example `B4829173-6405`, digits invented), and also work without the hyphen (`B48291736405`).

### What happens if I miss my BJS Home Delivery?

According to [BJS's Help Centre](https://www.bjshomedelivery.com/en-gb/help), BJS's bookings team will contact you to reschedule, or you can call `01922 645 650` (7 days, 8am–6pm) with your consignment number.

### Will BJS crews remove their shoes or assemble furniture?

Under [BJS Terms & Conditions](https://track.bjshomedelivery.com/terms-conditions), crews are forbidden from removing safety footwear, so customers should protect flooring/carpets beforehand. Assembly, room-of-choice placement and packaging removal are carried out where booked by your retailer.

### How do I contact BJS Home Delivery customer service?

Call `01922 645 650` (7 days a week, 8am–6pm), email `customerservice@bjshomedelivery.com` or visit [bjshomedelivery.com/en-gb/help](https://www.bjshomedelivery.com/en-gb/help).
