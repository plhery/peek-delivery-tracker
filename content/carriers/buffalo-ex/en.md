**BUFFALO** (*BUFFALO International Logistics*, often called *Buffaloex* or *Buffalo Express*) is a China-to-Africa cross-border e-commerce carrier operating since April 2017 ([buffaloex.com](https://www.buffaloex.com/)), headquartered in Shanghai with its African branch in Johannesburg. Paste your 17-character `BUFZA...YQ` number into the box above or look it up on [buffaloex.com/tracks.html](https://www.buffaloex.com/tracks.html) to follow China warehousing, air freight, South African customs VAT/duty scans and local delivery. BUFFALO runs 35,000 square metres of integrated warehouses (including a 6,500 m² bonded warehouse) and 300 vehicles, integrates with South African customs and banking systems, and reports holding the top market share in China-to-South Africa e-commerce small parcels.

## BUFFALO tracking numbers (`BUFZA...YQ` format)

Cross-border e-commerce shipments moving from China to South Africa through BUFFALO carry a 17-character barcode starting with `BUFZA` and ending in `YQ`:

| Format (invented) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `BUFZA6482917305YQ` | Standard 17-character BUFFALO China-to-South Africa parcel number (`BUFZA` + 10 digits + `YQ`). |
| `BUFZA4SK73918BUYQ` | Alternative 17-character `BUFZA...BU...YQ` cross-border parcel code. |

BUFFALO's contact page sends order numbers that start with `BUFEX-` to ZA-BUFFALO. If you have lost your tracking number, BUFFALO's FAQ says to ask your supplier for it or to log in to your BUFFALO account. See [tracking number formats](guide:tracking-number-formats).

## What BUFFALO tracking statuses mean

According to [BUFFALO Support](https://www.buffaloex.com/support.html) and BUFFALO's tracking feed, shipments progress through China warehousing, export flight, South African customs tax payment, Johannesburg/regional depots and local delivery:

| BUFFALO says | What it means for you | Peek shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Order Confirmed` | Order created while the parcel is still with the supplier on its way to BUFFALO's China warehouse. | "Label created" |
| `Enter Warehouse` | The parcel reached BUFFALO's warehouse in China. | "Received by carrier" |
| `Out of Warehouse`, `Air Shipping`, `International Shipping` | Departed BUFFALO's China warehouse and dispatched to South Africa by air or sea freight. | "In transit" |
| `Clearance`, `At Destination`, `Arrival at South Africa customs`, `Customs VAT and duty can be paid now, please pay in time`, `Tax Paid`, `Clearance finished` | Export clearance in China while the parcel waits for its flight, or South African import customs; import VAT and duty are due before release. | "At customs" |
| `Inbound`, `AIR DC Received the parcel`, `BUFFALO Airport Warehouse received`, `Transit the parcel from AIRDC to JHBDC`, `JHB DC Received the parcel`, `Dispatching`, `WAYBILL IMPORTED`, `Transit to thirdparty Express`, `RETURNS` | Cleared customs and moving through BUFFALO's Airport (`AIR DC`) and Johannesburg (`JHB DC`) depots, handed to a third-party South African courier or returned to the depot between delivery rounds. | "In transit" |
| `Driver Pick the Order`, `SCAN VEHICLE DEPARTURE` | Loaded with a BUFFALO or local courier driver for delivery today. | "Out for delivery" |
| `Returned to BUFFALO warehouse[...]` | Taken back by the BUFFALO depot in the brackets after the local courier's failed delivery; follow the SMS or email BUFFALO sends, or contact customer service. | "Needs attention" |
| `Client Received` | Delivered to your address or collected at a counter. | "Delivered" |

## South African customs VAT/duty, delivery hours and address changes

According to [BUFFALO Support](https://www.buffaloex.com/support.html) and [BUFFALO About Us](https://www.buffaloex.com/about.html):

- **Import VAT and duty:** when tracking shows `Customs VAT and duty can be paid now, please pay in time`, import VAT and duty are due before South African customs releases the parcel. BUFFALO's VAT & duty videos cover paying by online or bank transfer and uploading proof of payment, and its inquiry form takes questions about tax payments and about ID or importer's codes. See [parcels held at customs](guide:customs).
- **Delivery hours & transit speed:** BUFFALO delivers across South Africa **Monday to Saturday, 08:00 to 17:00** (no deliveries on Sundays or public holidays). Once tracking shows the parcel in transit, BUFFALO expects delivery within **1–5 days**, depending on the recipient's address.
- **Address, phone or customs-value changes:** per BUFFALO's FAQ, declared value, quantity and HS code **cannot** be changed once the parcel enters the domestic transit warehouse, and the delivery address or contact phone number **cannot** be modified after the parcel has arrived in South Africa.

## BUFFALO parcel delayed, returned to depot or missing

- **Tracking not updating:** BUFFALO's FAQ says the parcel is in transit and hasn't reached the next checkpoint yet; customer service can give details. See [why tracking stops updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).
- **Evening `RETURNS` scan:** in a local courier's scans relayed through tracking services, `RETURNS` has come the evening after `SCAN VEHICLE DEPARTURE`: the parcel was back at the depot after a round and left again the next morning. `Returned to BUFFALO warehouse[...]` follows a failed delivery and needs follow-up.
- **Marked `Client Received` but missing:** BUFFALO advises checking with family members, neighbours and estate/building security first, then contacting South Africa customer service (`support@buffaloex.com`). See [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received), [lost parcel](guide:lost-parcel), [damaged parcel](guide:damaged-parcel) and [returned to sender](guide:returned-to-sender).

Peek has no direct BUFFALO lookup and follows BUFFALO parcels through tracking services. It reads their scans with BUFFALO's own wording rules, so an evening `RETURNS` stays "In transit", `SCAN VEHICLE DEPARTURE` shows "Out for delivery" and `Returned to BUFFALO warehouse[...]` shows "Needs attention", and it shows each scan with its stage ("Label created", "Received by carrier", "In transit", "At customs", "Out for delivery", "Needs attention", "Delivered").

While Peek follows the parcel through tracking services, it checks hourly, every 3 hours once a day passes without a new scan, and up to every 15 minutes on the day the parcel is out for delivery, while you have it open or notifications are on.

When the newest scan is four days old and the parcel is still at "Label created", "Received by carrier" or "In transit", Peek flags it with that scan's day, such as "No tracking update since Mon 12 Oct". The parcel's page then says "Parcels often go quiet on long flights, at customs or over a weekend. Peek keeps asking BUFFALO.", with a "Get help from BUFFALO" link to BUFFALO's contact page. After three weeks with no new scan, it says "Nothing new for over three weeks. If it still hasn’t arrived, ask the seller. Peek keeps asking BUFFALO."

You can pick alert presets for "Every scan", "Important steps" or "Delivery only". Peek monitors tracking updates only and cannot pay SARS customs duties, submit importer codes or contact BUFFALO for you.

## Questions about BUFFALO tracking

### What does a BUFFALO tracking number look like?

BUFFALO China-to-South Africa parcels carry a 17-character code starting with `BUFZA` and ending in `YQ` — either `BUFZA` + 10 digits + `YQ` (such as `BUFZA6482917305YQ`, invented) or the `BUFZA...BU...YQ` format (`BUFZA4SK73918BUYQ`, invented). Order numbers starting with `BUFEX-` are handled by ZA-BUFFALO.

### Why is my BUFFALO parcel stuck at South African customs?

When tracking shows `Customs VAT and duty can be paid now, please pay in time`, import VAT and duty must be paid before South African customs releases the parcel. BUFFALO's support page has videos explaining online/bank transfer payment and uploading proof of payment, and its inquiry form handles tax and ID or importer's code queries.

### When does BUFFALO deliver in South Africa and how long does it take?

According to [buffaloex.com/support.html](https://www.buffaloex.com/support.html), BUFFALO delivers Monday to Saturday from **08:00 to 17:00** (no Sunday or public-holiday deliveries) and expects delivery within **1–5 days** once tracking shows the parcel in transit, depending on your address.

### Can I change my delivery address or phone number on a BUFFALO parcel?

According to BUFFALO's FAQ, the delivery address and contact phone number cannot be changed once the parcel has arrived in South Africa, and declared value, quantity or HS code cannot be edited once the parcel enters the domestic transit warehouse.

Email `support@buffaloex.com`, call `+27-10-510-9307`, message WhatsApp `+27-67-758-6614` or submit an enquiry on [buffaloex.com/contact.html](https://www.buffaloex.com/contact.html) with your `BUFZA...YQ` number (China hotline `+86-400-921-9066`, `service@bufflogistics.com`).
