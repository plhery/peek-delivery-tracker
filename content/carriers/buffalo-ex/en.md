**BUFFALO** (*BUFFALO International Logistics*, often known as *Buffaloex* or *Buffalo Express*) is a leading China-to-Africa cross-border e-commerce logistics carrier operating since April 2017 ([buffaloex.com](https://www.buffaloex.com/)). Headquartered in Shanghai with a major South African hub in Johannesburg — encompassing 35,000 square metres of warehousing (including a 6,500 m² bonded facility) and a fleet of 300 self-owned vehicles directly integrated with South African customs (SARS) and local banking systems — BUFFALO carries a large share of SHEIN, Temu and other China-to-South Africa online orders. You can check a shipment on [buffaloex.com/tracks.html](https://www.buffaloex.com/tracks.html) or paste your `BUFZA` tracking number into the box above.

## BUFFALO tracking numbers (`BUFZA...YQ` format)

Cross-border e-commerce shipments moving from China to South Africa through BUFFALO carry a 17-character barcode starting with `BUFZA` and ending in `YQ`:

| Format (invented) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `BUFZA6482917305YQ` | Standard 17-character BUFFALO China-to-South Africa parcel number (`BUFZA` + 10 digits + `YQ`). |
| `BUFZA4SK73918BUYQ` | Alternative 17-character `BUFZA...BU...YQ` cross-border parcel code. |

Note that BUFFALO's contact page mentions separate `BUFEX-` domestic South Africa references handled by ZA-BUFFALO, while international China-to-South Africa parcels use the `BUFZA...YQ` format shown on your marketplace order page. See [tracking number formats](guide:tracking-number-formats).

## What BUFFALO tracking statuses mean

According to [BUFFALO Support](https://www.buffaloex.com/support.html) and BUFFALO's tracking feed, shipments progress through China warehousing, export flight, South African customs tax payment, Johannesburg/regional depots and local delivery:

| BUFFALO says | What it means for you | Peek shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Order Confirmed` | Order created while the parcel is still with the supplier on its way to BUFFALO's China warehouse. | "Label created" |
| `Enter Warehouse` | Physical parcel received at BUFFALO's consolidation warehouse in China (Guangzhou). | "Received by carrier" |
| `Out of Warehouse`, `Air Shipping`, `International Shipping` | Departed BUFFALO's China warehouse and dispatched to South Africa by air or sea freight. | "In transit" |
| `Clearance`, `At Destination`, `Arrival at South Africa customs`, `Customs VAT and duty can be paid now, please pay in time`, `Tax Paid`, `Clearance finished` | Export screening in China or South African import customs — watch for an SMS or email payment link when VAT and customs duties are due. | "At customs" |
| `Inbound`, `AIR DC Received the parcel`, `BUFFALO Airport Warehouse received`, `Transit the parcel from AIRDC to JHBDC`, `JHB DC Received the parcel`, `Dispatching`, `WAYBILL IMPORTED`, `Transit to thirdparty Express`, `RETURNS` | Cleared customs and moving through BUFFALO's Airport (`AIR DC`) and Johannesburg (`JHB DC`) depots, handed to a third-party South African courier or returned to the depot between delivery rounds. | "In transit" |
| `Driver Pick the Order`, `SCAN VEHICLE DEPARTURE` | Loaded with a BUFFALO or local courier driver for delivery today. | "Out for delivery" |
| `Returned to BUFFALO warehouse[...]` | Returned back to a BUFFALO depot after an unsuccessful delivery attempt; check with support to arrange redelivery. | "Needs attention" |
| `Client Received` | Delivered to your address or collected at a counter. | "Delivered" |

## South African customs VAT/duty, delivery hours and address changes

According to [BUFFALO Support](https://www.buffaloex.com/support.html) and [BUFFALO About Us](https://www.buffaloex.com/about.html):

- **SARS customs VAT & duty payment:** once tracking displays `Customs VAT and duty can be paid now, please pay in time`, South African customs will not release the parcel until import VAT and duties are paid through BUFFALO's official payment notice (and if your ID number or importer's code is requested, submit it promptly). See [parcels held at customs](guide:customs).
- **Delivery hours & transit speed:** BUFFALO delivers across South Africa **Monday to Saturday, 08:00 to 17:00** (no deliveries on Sundays or public holidays). Once a parcel enters domestic transit in South Africa (`Inbound` / in transit), BUFFALO estimates **1–5 days** depending on location.
- **Address, phone or customs-value changes:** per BUFFALO's FAQ, declared value, quantity and HS code **cannot** be edited once a parcel enters the China transit warehouse, and the delivery address or contact phone number **cannot** be modified after the parcel has arrived in South Africa.

## BUFFALO parcel delayed, returned to depot or missing

- **Quiet gap after `Air Shipping` or `Tax Paid`:** tracking commonly pauses for a few days while flights land at OR Tambo (`AIR DC`) and SARS releases consolidated batches. See [why tracking stops updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).
- **Evening `RETURNS` scan:** on local courier feeds relayed through tracking services, a `RETURNS` scan the evening after `SCAN VEHICLE DEPARTURE` typically means the parcel returned to the depot at the end of a route and goes back out the next working day — whereas `Returned to BUFFALO warehouse[...]` after a failed delivery needs follow-up.
- **Marked `Client Received` but missing:** BUFFALO advises checking with family members, neighbours and estate/building security first, then contacting South Africa customer service (`support@buffaloex.com`) and your online marketplace. See [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received), [lost parcel](guide:lost-parcel), [damaged parcel](guide:damaged-parcel) and [returned to sender](guide:returned-to-sender).

## Track a BUFFALO parcel with Peek

[Peek](/) detects BUFFALO automatically as the sole candidate when you enter a 17-character `BUFZA...YQ` code (`BUFZA6482917305YQ` or `BUFZA4SK73918BUYQ`, invented), and also recognises pasted `buffaloex.com/tracks.html?order=`, `buffaloex.com/track.html?order=` and `buffaloex.com/express/record?order=` tracking links directly. If you ever need to select BUFFALO manually, tap "Change" while adding or "Change carrier" on the parcel screen (searching "Buffaloex", "Buffalo Express" or "BUFFALO International" finds it too).

Peek tracks BUFFALO parcels through its supported tracking services (applying BUFFALO-specific scan classification rules so depot evening `RETURNS` scans stay `"In transit"`, `SCAN VEHICLE DEPARTURE` shows `"Out for delivery"` and `Returned to BUFFALO warehouse[...]` flags `"Needs attention"`), displaying the mapped stage ("Label created", "Received by carrier", "In transit", "At customs", "Out for delivery", "Needs attention", "Delivered") and scan history as reported.

Because BUFFALO runs through Peek's tracking services rather than a direct carrier API, Peek checks hourly, slows to every 3 hours after a day without change, and checks up to every 15 minutes on delivery day. After 4 days in transit with no new scan, Peek flags the pause (for example "No tracking update since Mon, Oct 12"), shows "Parcels often go quiet on long flights, at customs or over a weekend. Peek keeps asking BUFFALO." and displays "Get help from BUFFALO", which opens BUFFALO's contact page. After more than three weeks without an update, Peek shows "Nothing new for over three weeks. If it still hasn’t arrived, ask the seller. Peek keeps asking BUFFALO."

You can pick alert presets for "Every scan", "Important steps" or "Delivery only". Peek monitors tracking updates only and cannot pay SARS customs duties, submit importer codes or contact BUFFALO for you.

## Questions about BUFFALO tracking

### What does a BUFFALO tracking number look like?

China-to-South Africa BUFFALO tracking numbers are 17 characters long, starting with `BUFZA` and ending with `YQ` — for example `BUFZA6482917305YQ` or `BUFZA4SK73918BUYQ` (invented).

### When does BUFFALO deliver in South Africa?

According to [buffaloex.com/support.html](https://www.buffaloex.com/support.html), BUFFALO delivers Monday through Saturday from **08:00 to 17:00** (excluding Sundays and public holidays), taking **1–5 days** once in domestic transit.

### Why is my BUFFALO parcel paused at South African customs?

If tracking shows `Customs VAT and duty can be paid now, please pay in time`, import VAT and customs duties must be settled (and any requested ID/importer code provided) before SARS releases the parcel to BUFFALO's Airport DC.

### How do I contact BUFFALO South Africa customer service?

Email `support@buffaloex.com` with your `BUFZA...YQ` order number or submit an enquiry through [buffaloex.com/contact.html](https://www.buffaloex.com/contact.html) (China hotline `+86-400-921-9066`).
