Bulgarian Posts (*Български пощи ЕАД*) is Bulgaria's national universal postal operator, handling domestic letters, e-packages and parcels as well as international Universal Postal Union (UPU) mail and EMS / Bulpost courier deliveries. Track an item on [Bulgarian Posts' tracking page](https://www.bgpost.bg/en/postal-services/track-package), or paste the tracking number into the box above.

## Bulgarian Posts tracking numbers: PS and S10 formats

Look for the 13-character barcode on your posting receipt, inside your online shop's dispatch email or on the paper delivery notice (*служебно известие*). On [bgpost.bg/en/postal-services/track-package](https://www.bgpost.bg/en/postal-services/track-package), Bulgarian Posts lists two tracked formats:

| Format (invented) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `PS48291736405` | Domestic item or e-package: `PS` followed by 11 letters and digits (13 characters total). |
| `RR482917367BG` | Registered international letter/small packet (`R`, `C`, `E`, `L` or `V` + second letter + 9 digits + `BG`). |
| `CP482917367BG` | International postal parcel originating in Bulgaria (`CP` + 9 digits + `BG`). |

Inbound parcels posted abroad keep the sending post's UPU S10 code (ending in that country's two-letter code, such as `DE`, `CN` or `US`). Bulgarian Posts notes that outbound international items are tracked on its site until leaving Bulgaria and then on the destination post's website, while inbound items appear on Bulgarian Posts once they arrive in Bulgaria. For other courier barcodes in Bulgaria, check [Sameday](carrier:sameday), [BOX NOW](carrier:box-now) or [tracking number formats](guide:tracking-number-formats).

## What Bulgarian Posts tracking statuses mean

Bulgarian Posts' tracking feed shows English UPU IPS scan names on international legs — tagged `(Otb)` outward or `(Inb)` inward — alongside Bulgarian wording on domestic scans (even when viewing the English page).

| Bulgarian Posts says | What it means for you | Peek shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Receive item from customer (Otb)`, `Получаване пратка от клиент (А10)`, initial `Приемане` | Accepted at a post office from the sender. | "Received by carrier" |
| `Insert item into bag (Otb) and Send item abroad`, `Send item abroad (EDI-received)`, `Receive item at office of exchange (Otb)` / `(Inb)` | Bagged for export, dispatched abroad or received at Bulgaria's international office of exchange. | "In transit" |
| `Receive item at sorting centre (Inb)`, `Insert item into domestic bag`, `Send item to domestic location (Inb)`, `Receive item at location (Inb)`, later `Приемане` | Moving through Bulgarian sorting centres toward your local post office. | "In transit" |
| `Send item to customs (Inb)`, `Record item customs information (Inb)` | Presented to Bulgarian customs or waiting for customs documents/assessment (often accompanied by a detail such as awaiting presentation to a customs commissioner). | "At customs" |
| `Return item from customs (Inb)` | Released by customs back into Bulgarian Posts' network (not a return to the sender). | "In transit" |
| `Handover item to delivery agent` | Out with a postal carrier for doorstep delivery today. | "Out for delivery" |
| `Unsuccessful item delivery attempt (Inb)`, `Първо посещение` | First delivery visit failed; a written notice was left and the item goes to your post office. | "Delivery attempted" |
| `Receive item at collection point for pick-up (Inb)`, `За получаване в ПС`, `Второ посещение` | Waiting for collection at your local post office (`Второ посещение` marks the second written notice sent after 10 calendar days). | "Ready for pickup" |
| `Deliver item (Inb)`, `Доставена`, `Доставена пратка` | Handed over and signed for. | "Delivered" |

Note that `Създаване` (Bulgarian Posts creating its domestic record right after an inbound office-of-exchange scan) is an administrative entry rather than physical movement, so Peek keeps the stage of the preceding scan. Likewise, `Второ посещение` is not a failed doorstep attempt — it records the second pickup notice while your parcel continues waiting at the post office.

## Post-office pickup, holding periods and storage fees

Under Bulgarian Posts' [General Terms and Conditions (ОУ 2023)](https://www.bgpost.bg/cms/static/%D0%9E%D0%A3_2023.pdf) (points 43, 45 and 46, linked from [General Terms](https://www.bgpost.bg/en/about_us/conditions)):

- **Registered items and parcels:** handed over personally to the recipient or an authorised person against signature and photo ID. Postal parcels (*пощенски колети*) are normally delivered at the receiving post office after Bulgarian Posts delivers a written notice (*писмено служебно известие*), unless home delivery was paid for separately.
- **Standard holding time — 20 calendar days:** both registered items left after a missed visit and parcels held at the post office wait for **20 calendar days** from arrival at the delivery post office before returning to the sender.
- **Second notice at 10 days:** if you have not collected within **10 calendar days**, Bulgarian Posts sends a second written notice (`Второ посещение`).
- **Storage fee after 9 days:** for postal parcels not collected within **9 calendar days** of notification, Bulgarian Posts charges a storage fee (*магазинаж*) under its current tariff.
- **Customs-cleared items — 45 calendar days:** under [Bulgarian Posts' customs rules](https://www.bgpost.bg/en/customs), **all items that have gone through customs clearance are collected only at the designated post office** (never delivered to your door) and are held for **45 calendar days** before being returned to the sender.

## Non-EU parcels, customs fees and the €3 duty / €2 EU fee

Shipments sent inside the EU clear no customs. For online purchases and gifts arriving in Bulgaria from outside the EU, [bgpost.bg/en/customs](https://www.bgpost.bg/en/customs) and the [European Commission](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/customs/eu-customs-reform_en) set out the charges currently in force:

- **EU fixed duty and handling fee:** a flat **€3 customs duty per article** applies to online B2C consignments worth up to €150 (in force since 1 July 2026), and a **€2 Union Handling Fee (UHF) per individual article** applies to online purchases from outside the EU regardless of value (in force since 1 November 2026; included in the customs value used for VAT; exempt for private C2C gifts). Both the €3 duty (on B2C items up to €150) and the €2 UHF are **non-refundable** even if you return defective goods.
- **Bulgarian Posts customs representation fees (VAT included, effective 2 November 2026):**
  - B2C up to €150 with IOSS (H7 declaration): **€2.70** (plus customs duty if the shop did not collect it at checkout).
  - B2C up to €150 without IOSS (Special Arrangement, H7): **€3.80** (plus VAT and customs duty).
  - B2C €150 to €1,000 (H6): **€12.00** (plus VAT and duty).
  - B2C over €1,000 (H1): **€20.00** for the first item type plus **€5.00** for each additional type.
  - Private C2C gifts: **€2.70** up to €45 (no VAT/duty), **€3.80** from €45 to €1,000, or **€20.00** (+ €5.00 per extra type, EORI required) over €1,000.
- **Required documents:** Declaration of Consent, Declaration of Value, plus (for purchases) order/invoice matching the tracking number and proof of payment (bank/card/PayPal statement).

See [parcels held at customs](guide:customs) for general EU customs advice.

## Missing, delayed or damaged parcels and consumer rights

When you bought from an online shop, the merchant is responsible for delivery and refunds. Under Bulgaria's Consumer Protection Commission ([KZP online shopping rules](https://kzp.bg/bg/potrebiteli/good-to-know/online-shopping)), online orders must be delivered within 30 days unless otherwise agreed, and consumers have a 14-day right of withdrawal from receipt.

**Filing a claim with Bulgarian Posts.** Under [bgpost.bg/en/complaints](https://www.bgpost.bg/en/complaints) and General Terms points 56–59, the **sender** submits claims before delivery — the recipient may submit a claim before receipt only with the sender's written consent. Claims must be filed within **6 months** of posting (at any post office or online through the RQMS claims portal) with a copy of the posting receipt. Bulgarian Posts must answer within **1 month** for domestic items and **3 months** for international items, paying approved compensation within **1 month** of notification.

**Regulatory escalation.** Unresolved postal-service disputes can be escalated to Bulgaria's Communications Regulation Commission ([CRC / КРС complaints](https://crc.bg/bg/rubriki/271/jalbi)), which responds within 30 days (up to 2 months if additional evidence is needed). For troubleshooting delays or missing scans, see [why tracking stops updating](guide:tracking-not-updating), [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received), [damaged parcel](guide:damaged-parcel), [lost parcel](guide:lost-parcel) and [returned to sender](guide:returned-to-sender).

- **EMS / Bulpost courier:** `*6666` or `0700 19 666` (working days 07:00–19:00).
- **Customs service (1900 Sofia BESC):** `customerservice@bgpost.bg` (Mon–Fri 08:00–16:00); EMS items starting with `E`: `emscustomerservice@bgpost.bg` (Mon–Fri 08:30–16:00); Varna customs PO `psm.varna@bgpost.bg` / `052 604 143`.
- **Claims hotlines (working days 09:00–17:30):** domestic `+359 2 949 3326`, international inbound `+359 2 949 3121`, international outbound `+359 2 949 3343`, RQMS portal help `02 949 31 90`.
- **Headquarters:** 1, Akademik Stefan Mladenov Str., Bl. 31, 1700 Sofia, Bulgaria.

## Track a Bulgarian Posts parcel with Peek

[Peek](/) identifies 13-character `PS` codes (`PS48291736405`, digits invented) as Bulgarian Posts directly with high confidence, and recognises pasted `bgpost.bg/postal-services/track-package?itemid=` (including `/en/`) and legacy `IPSWeb_item_events.asp?itemid=` links. For international UPU S10 codes ending in `BG` (such as `RR482917367BG` or `CP482917367BG`), Peek follows Bulgarian Posts automatically as the preferred origin post, and you can also select Bulgarian Posts for inbound S10 codes from abroad by tapping "Change" while adding or "Change carrier" on the parcel screen (searching "Български пощи", "Bulgaria Post" or "BG Post" finds it too). Note that if a code belongs to Bulpost (`EMS/Bulpost`), Bulgarian Posts redirects it to Bulpost's separate page.

Peek queries Bulgarian Posts' public tracking API directly and displays the stage ("Received by carrier", "In transit", "At customs", "Out for delivery", "Delivery attempted", "Ready for pickup", "Delivered"), English IPS lines (plus Bulgarian domestic lines and customs status notes), office/country locations (dropping postcodes) and delivery timestamps for Bulgarian offices. Scans at Bulgarian offices are dated on Sofia time, and a foreign post's scans on their place's time zone (Germany's for Frankfurt, for example); Peek shows them on your device's clock. Bulgarian Posts' tracker does not provide an estimated delivery date, weight, service name, sender/recipient name or proof-of-delivery photo.

While you have the parcel open in Peek, or on its delivery day with notifications on, Peek checks up to every 10 minutes; on the day Bulgarian Posts shows `Handover item to delivery agent`, every 2 minutes from 6 a.m. Bulgarian time, while Bulgarian Posts' own tracker answers. Otherwise Peek checks hourly. After 12 hours with only "Label created" or "Received by carrier", or two days without news, it checks hourly however you follow it, and every 6 hours after seven days at "Label created". When Bulgarian Posts' tracker doesn't answer and Peek follows the parcel through tracking services alone, it checks hourly, every 3 hours after a quiet day, and up to every 15 minutes on the out-for-delivery day.

When the newest scan is four days old and the parcel is still at "Label created", "Received by carrier" or "In transit", Peek flags it with that scan's day, such as "No tracking update since Mon 12 Oct". The parcel's page then says "Parcels often go quiet on long flights, at customs or over a weekend. Peek keeps asking Bulgarian Posts.", with a "Get help from Bulgarian Posts" link to Bulgarian Posts' contacts page. After three weeks with no new scan, it says "Nothing new for over three weeks. If it still hasn’t arrived, ask the seller. Peek keeps asking Bulgarian Posts."

You can choose notification presets for "Every scan", "Important steps" or "Delivery only". Peek monitors tracking only and cannot submit customs declarations or file claims with Bulgarian Posts for you.

## Questions about Bulgarian Posts tracking

### How long does Bulgarian Posts hold a parcel at the post office?

Standard parcels and registered items wait **20 calendar days** from arrival at your delivery post office (with a second notice sent after 10 days and a storage fee applying to parcels after 9 days). Items that have gone through customs clearance are held for **45 calendar days** at the designated post office.

### What does `Второ посещение` mean in Bulgarian Posts tracking?

It means Bulgarian Posts issued the second written pickup notice after 10 calendar days without collection. Your parcel is still waiting at the post office — Peek keeps it in "Ready for pickup".

### What does `Return item from customs (Inb)` mean?

It means Bulgarian customs cleared the item and handed it back into Bulgarian Posts' network so it can travel to your post office. It does not mean your parcel is being returned to the sender.

### How much does Bulgarian Posts charge for customs clearance?

As of 2 November 2026, Bulgarian Posts' representation fee (VAT included) is €2.70 for IOSS purchases up to €150 (or C2C gifts up to €45), €3.80 for non-IOSS purchases up to €150 (or gifts €45–€1,000), €12.00 for purchases €150–€1,000 and €20.00 (+ €5.00 per extra item type) over €1,000, in addition to applicable EU customs duty (€3 up to €150), the €2 EU handling fee and VAT.
