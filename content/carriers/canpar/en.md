Canpar Express (owned by TFI International) is a private Canadian parcel courier with over 800 SMARTSpot pickup points across Canada. Track a parcel on [canpar.com](https://www.canpar.com/en/tracking/track.htm) with its 22-character barcode or `W` delivery notice number, or paste it in the box above. You'll find the barcode in the shop's shipping email, on your order page or on the shipping label.

## Canpar tracking number: format and where to find it

Each Canpar parcel gets its own barcode number, in the shop's shipping confirmation, on the order page and on the label. Still nothing? See [where else to find a tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number).

Parcels today carry one letter (C, D, K, L, S, U, X or Z) followed by 21 digits, 22 characters in all. Canpar's FAQ also describes shorter ones, 8 to 20 digits, which its tracker still finds with the destination postal code.

| Shape (invented) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `D482917364058291736405` | The parcel's barcode number: the one to track. |
| `W48291736` | A delivery notice number (W + 8 or 9 digits), printed on the card a driver leaves. |
| `P482917364` | A Pick Up Tag (P + 9 digits); return tags are R + 8 digits. |

Canpar's own tracking tool also takes these, and a sender's reference with the destination postal code. A barcode can be searched online for about a year; for ones up to three years old, email customerservice@canpar.com.

No results? Canpar says this usually means the shop has made the label but hasn't handed the parcel over yet. If nothing shows within 24 hours of getting the number, contact the shop.

A full 22-character Canpar number fits no other carrier. Peek tracks Canpar's other numbers too once you pick Canpar (see below); for a sender's reference, use Canpar's own tracker.

## What Canpar's tracking statuses mean

Canpar writes its statuses in English on canpar.com/en (French on canpar.com/fr). These are the ones a parcel usually meets, in order.

| Canpar's status | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `Picked Up and On the Move!` | Canpar has collected it from the sender. |
| `Sorting It Out-Stay Tuned!` | It's being sorted at a Canpar terminal. |
| `Departing sort facility` | It has left a terminal. |
| `Package Has Arrived at Our Facility` | It has reached a terminal, often the one near you. |
| `Out for Delivery-Almost There!` | It's on the driver's vehicle for delivery today. |
| `Consignee Not Home` | The driver came and nobody was home. |
| `Can't Find the Address-Checking Again!` | Not delivered: the address couldn't be found or was incomplete. |
| `Ready for Pickup at Your Local SMARTSpot` | It's waiting at a SMARTSpot pickup point. |
| `Your Package was Delivered with no Signature Required` | Delivered and left without a signature. |
| `Our Package was Delivered` | Delivered. |
| `Returned to Sender` | It's going back to the shop. |

The FAQ still speaks of `With Courier`, the older name for out for delivery. `Our Package was Delivered` is how Canpar words the delivered scan; the summary line on top simply says `Delivered`. `Label created` with nothing after it means the shop has sent Canpar the details but Canpar doesn't have the parcel yet; `No tracking results available for this Barcode` means Canpar holds no scans yet. Peek has nothing to show before the first scan.

Long gaps are normal: between terminals there are no scans until arrival. `Delayed at Facility`, `Handed Off to a Local Delivery Partner` and `Package was sorted to the incorrect terminal` come with Canpar's longer note: "Your shipment is on hold, please connect with a live agent for further updates". `Weather's Slowing Us Down, But We're on It!` is a weather delay, and `Change of Address` means the address is being updated and the parcel forwarded.

## Canpar delivery, SMARTSpots and missed deliveries

Canpar delivers Monday to Friday. Its FAQ gives two answers on hours: homes as late as 7pm and businesses 9am to 5pm, but also 9am to 6pm once a parcel is out for delivery. Drivers don't call ahead. Your estimated delivery date is on the tracking results.

**Missed delivery.** A home address gets one attempt; the driver can't come back the same day, so the parcel goes to a SMARTSpot. Business addresses get up to two attempts, and the notice gives the day of the next one. Type the W number from the card into Canpar's tracking to see where the parcel is held.

**SMARTSpots** are partner shops across Canada (more than 800, says Canpar). A parcel is ready after 12pm on the next business day and kept for at most 5 business days. Bring ID. Someone else can pick it up if their ID shows the delivery address on the label; otherwise they need a completed Authorization Notice.

**Changing the delivery.** Before the driver's attempt, most parcels can be sent to a SMARTSpot: track the parcel on canpar.com, then choose Redirect to SMARTSpot under Delivery Options. A fee applies under Canpar's terms. For another address, or another attempt, call customer service. The FAQ has you pay for an extra attempt by Visa or MasterCard over the phone; the 2026 service guide puts it at $10.00 per attempt plus fuel surcharge, billed to the recipient, though one clause of Canpar's terms bills the original payer instead.

**Leaving it at the door.** Leave a signed note to the outside of your front door saying where to leave it. This doesn't work for parcels needing a signature in person, and Canpar never leaves parcels unattended in apartment or condo buildings. Canpar doesn't deliver to PO boxes, RR addresses or Range Roads.

## Canpar tracking not updating, lost or damaged parcels

No scans for a few days in the middle of the journey is usually the terminal-to-terminal gap. If it lasts, the shop is the one to chase: it holds the contract with Canpar, and Canpar's claim form asks for the shipper's account number and Canpar emails its decision to the shipping account holder. See [tracking that isn't updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

Canpar's own deadlines:

- **Damaged:** report it within 60 days of delivery and keep the item and all packaging until the investigation ends; customer service arranges any inspection.
- **Lost:** file within 180 days of the shipping date, after a trace with customer service. A claim needs the trace number (T + 7 digits) Canpar gives you.
- Canpar's trace and claims team replies within 2 to 5 business days, and investigations can take up to 30 days.

If tracking says delivered and you have nothing, check the proof of delivery link Canpar adds to the tracking results, then see [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).

## Canpar phone number and customer service

Canpar's channels, from its Contact Us page:

- **Phone** 1-800-387-9335, Monday to Friday, 8am to 8pm EST (Quebec: 8am to 5pm EST).
- **Live chat** from the chat icon at the bottom of canpar.com pages, Monday to Friday, 10am to 7pm EST.
- **Email** claims@canpar.com for claim documents (tracking number in the subject line).

Have the barcode or W number ready.

## Tracking Canpar parcels with Peek

[Peek](/) names Canpar as soon as you paste a full 22-character number, or a canpar.com link containing it. For a shorter barcode, a delivery notice (W) number or a pickup or return tag, pick Canpar with "Change": Peek tracks those too, and asks for the destination postal code when Canpar needs it to find a shorter barcode. It doesn't track a sender's reference: look that up on Canpar's own tracker.

It shows Canpar's status in Canpar's English words and the expected delivery day. A SMARTSpot parcel shows as ready for pickup, without the shop's name or address. It drops the proof of delivery, recipient, addresses and references. A handoff to a local partner shows as in transit; the partner isn't followed.

Canpar's scan times carry no time zone: Peek reads each on the clock of the scan's place and lists Canpar's own history, shown on a map.

While you have the parcel open in Peek, or on its delivery day with notifications on, Peek checks up to every 10 minutes, and up to every 2 minutes from 6 a.m. local time on the day Canpar scans the parcel `Out for Delivery-Almost There!`, while Canpar's own tracker answers (through tracking services, every 15 minutes). After two days without news (12 hours while the parcel still shows "Label created" or "Received by carrier"), Peek checks hourly, and every 6 hours once the parcel has shown "Label created" for seven days.

When the newest scan is four days old and the parcel is still at "Label created", "Received by carrier" or "In transit", Peek flags it with that scan's day, such as "No tracking update since Mon 12 Oct". The parcel's page then says "Parcels often go quiet on long flights, at customs or over a weekend. Peek keeps asking Canpar.", with a "Get help from Canpar" link to Canpar's Contact Us page. After three weeks with no new scan, it says "Nothing new for over three weeks. If it still hasn’t arrived, ask the seller. Peek keeps asking Canpar."

Peek can notify you of "Every scan", "Important steps" or "Delivery only".

## Questions about Canpar tracking

### What does a Canpar tracking number or W delivery notice number look like?

Current Canpar barcodes are one letter (C, D, K, L, S, U, X or Z) followed by 21 digits (22 characters total), while older barcodes run 8 to 20 digits (tracked with your postal code). A missed-delivery card carries a `W` number (`W` plus 8 or 9 digits) that shows which SMARTSpot holds your parcel.

### How long does Canpar hold a parcel at a SMARTSpot?

After a missed home delivery, your parcel is ready at the designated SMARTSpot after 12pm the next business day and held for up to 5 business days. Bring ID showing the delivery address, or give someone else a completed Authorization Notice.

### Does Canpar deliver on weekends, and how late do drivers deliver?

Canpar normally delivers Monday to Friday, with weekend deliveries possible during peak season. Its FAQ cites home deliveries until 7pm (businesses 9am to 5pm), and 9am to 6pm once out for delivery, without narrow time slots.

### Is Canpar the same as Canada Post?

No. Canpar Express is a private courier owned by TFI International with its own terminals and SMARTSpots, separate from [Canada Post](carrier:canada-post) and its tracking system.

### What is Canpar's phone number and claim deadline?

Call 1-800-387-9335 (Monday to Friday, 8am to 8pm EST; Quebec 8am to 5pm EST) or use live chat on canpar.com (10am to 7pm EST). Report damaged parcels within 60 days of delivery and lost parcels within 180 days of shipping after opening a trace.
