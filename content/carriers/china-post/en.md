China Post is China's state-owned national post, and EMS is its express brand. If your order from AliExpress, Temu, Shein or another shop came with a tracking number ending in `CN`, China Post issued it, and your own country's post delivers the parcel under that same number. Track it on China Post's site, ems.com.cn, or in the box above. The number is in the shop's shipping details for your order.

## China Post tracking number: what it looks like

A China Post number has two letters, nine digits and `CN`: the international postal format set by the Universal Postal Union (UPU). The first letter says what kind of item it is. With invented digits:

| Number | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `LT000000005CN` | L: a tracked small packet (letter post). |
| `RR000000005CN` | R: registered letter post. |
| `CP000000005CN` | C: a parcel. |
| `EA000000005CN` | E: EMS, China Post's express service. |
| `UA000000005CN` | U: a small packet with goods "without customer-oriented tracking", in the UPU's words. Don't count on scans in your country. |
| `ZS12345678901` | ZS, ZC or ZA and 11 digits: also answered by China Post's tracking. La Poste's numbers have the same length, but none is known to start this way. |
| `LP12345678901234` | Not China Post: a [Cainiao](carrier:cainiao) (AliExpress) logistics reference. |
| `YT1234567890123456` | Not China Post but [YunExpress](carrier:yunexpress). |

The last of the nine digits is a check digit, so a mistyped digit almost always makes the number invalid. The UPU allows only one such number per item, which is why the same number works on your own post's site once the parcel reaches your country.

Before China Post's tracking page shows anything, it can make you pass a picture check. For some items China Post then asks for the last four digits of a contact phone number on the shipment.

## China Post tracking statuses explained

China Post writes its scans in Chinese. Place names go in brackets, here shown as `【…】`: a Chinese sorting office or, abroad, the country's name in Chinese, such as `【英国】` for the UK or `【美国】` for the US. In the order a parcel meets them:

| China Post says | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `已揽收` | Accepted: China Post has the parcel. |
| `邮件到达【…】` | It reached a China Post office or sorting centre. |
| `邮件离开【…】，正在发往下一站` | It left that office for the next stop. |
| `送交出口海关` | Presented to Chinese export customs. |
| `出口海关/留存待验` | Held by export customs for inspection. |
| `出口海关/放行` | Export customs released it. |
| `【…】已出口直封` | Closed into a dispatch for export at one of China's international exchange offices. |
| `已交承运商运输` | Handed to a transport carrier. |
| `航空公司接收`, `航空公司启运` | An airline took it, then departed with it. |
| `飞机进港` | The flight landed. |
| `到达寄达地处理中心` | It reached the processing centre in your country. |
| `离开【…】处理中心` | It left that centre. |
| `已到达【…】投递局` | It reached your delivery office. |
| `【…】安排投递`, `邮件正在派送中` | Out for delivery. |
| `已试投/投递失败` | Your post tried to deliver it and failed. |
| `【…】已妥投`, `已签收` | Delivered. |
| `已退回` | Returned to the sender. |

The last scans come from your country's post, relayed by China Post, sometimes only after the delivery. Your post's own tracking, with the same number, has the last leg first-hand.

## Who delivers China Post parcels in your country

China Post flies the parcel out; in your country the national post takes over, through customs to your door:

- **United States**: [USPS](carrier:usps).
- **United Kingdom**: [Royal Mail](carrier:royal-mail). It takes the parcel through UK customs and tells you if VAT or duty is due.
- **Ireland**: [An Post](carrier:an-post).
- **Canada**: [Canada Post](carrier:canada-post). Look the number up in its Track tool; if nothing appears, Canada Post asks you to have the sender investigate with their country's post.
- **Australia**: [Australia Post](carrier:australia-post). It says tracking for items from overseas depends on the service used in the sending country, and that customs can take up to 15 days.

Delivery attempts, notice cards and how long a parcel waits at the post office follow your post's rules; see its page and [missed a delivery](guide:missed-delivery). A parcel left [uncollected](guide:uncollected-parcel) goes back to the sender. Duties, taxes and handling fees on parcels from outside your country are in [parcels held at customs](guide:customs).

## China Post tracking not updating, lost or returned

**No scans yet.** China Post shows nothing until it has scanned the parcel; before that, the shop has only made a label. Ask the shop when it handed the parcel over.

**Quiet after it left China.** Between the flight and your post's first scan, days can pass without news. Check your own post's tracking with the same number. A number starting with U may never get scans in your country. More in [why tracking stops updating](guide:tracking-not-updating) and [tracking a parcel from China](guide:tracking-from-china).

**Delivered but not there.** Your post made the delivery, so start with its tracking and proof of delivery, then the shop. See [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).

**Lost or damaged.** China Post says that "for formal inquiry of all international items, the sender shall go to local post office". USPS, Canada Post and Australia Post also send recipients to the sender, who asks the post in the sending country. So tell the shop or marketplace and ask for a refund or a new parcel; see [lost parcels](guide:lost-parcel).

**Returned.** `已退回` means the parcel is going back to the sender. Ask the shop what it will do; see [returned to sender](guide:returned-to-sender).

## China Post contact number and customer service

As read on 22 October 2026, China Post's English site lists these lines:

- **Logistics and EMS**: 8610 11183.
- **Postal Business**: 8610 11185.

China Post doesn't say which languages they speak. ems.com.cn also has an online customer service link (在线客服), in Chinese. A formal inquiry has to come from the sender, so the shop is quicker. For delivery, redelivery or customs, contact your own post.

## Track a China Post parcel with Peek

[Peek](/) recognises two letters, nine digits and `CN` as China Post straight away when the check digit is right. With a wrong one, Peek doesn't take it for China Post, and if you pick China Post it says China Post "doesn't issue numbers like this one": check the number in the shop's email. A ZS, ZC or ZA number with 11 digits is recognised as China Post straight away too. Paste any other number all the same: Peek follows more than 3,500 carriers. If it settles on the wrong carrier, pick China Post with "Change".

Peek reads China Post's own tracking. When China Post asks for phone digits, Peek tries the public preview on ems.com.cn instead; Peek never asks you for them. If the preview refuses too and no tracking service has the parcel, Peek says more information is needed: look the number up on China Post's site with the four digits. China Post gives visitors only its three newest scans, so Peek adds the acceptance date when China Post gives one, and takes a fuller history from tracking services when one has it.

It shows the status, the history with the office of each scan, and the destination country. Scans appear in English where Peek knows China Post's wording, otherwise in Chinese. China Post times its own scans on China's clock, even the arrival in your country; the later scans it copies from your national post usually keep that post's local time. Peek shows each scan in your device's time zone. China Post gives no expected delivery date, weight or pickup point, and Peek drops the service name, contacts, sender and recipient details, couriers' names and phone numbers, and map coordinates.

For a number ending in `CN`, once China Post names your country, Peek also asks your national post with the same number (USPS, Royal Mail, An Post, Canada Post or Australia Post) and switches to it once it shows fresh scans, keeping China Post's history. Peek can notify you of "Every scan", "Important steps" or "Delivery only". It can't contact China Post, your post or the shop for you.

## Questions about China Post tracking

### Where is my China Post parcel?

The latest scan tells you. A Chinese office in brackets means it's still in China. From `到达寄达地处理中心` on, it's in your country, and your own post's tracking with the same number shows where.

### Why is my China Post tracking not updating?

Most often the parcel is in the air or waiting for customs, between China Post's last scan and your post's first. Check your national post's tracking with the same number. A number starting with U may have no tracking in your country at all.

### How long does China Post take to deliver?

It depends on the service the shop paid for and where you are: Canada Post says timelines vary with the sending country and the type of service. Go by the delivery estimate the shop or marketplace gives for your order.

### What does 已妥投 mean on China Post tracking?

Delivered. `【英国】已妥投`, for example, means delivered in the UK. If you don't have the parcel, start with your own post: see [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).

### Who delivers China Post parcels in the US or UK?

Your national post: USPS in the US, Royal Mail in the UK. They deliver it under the China Post number, since the UPU allows one number per item.
