Colis Privé is a French parcel carrier delivering online orders in France, Belgium and Luxembourg to homes, pickup points and (in France) outdoor lockers. Track your 12-character parcel number with the delivery postcode on [colisprive.fr](https://colisprive.fr/en/) (France), [colisprive.be](https://colisprive.be/en/) (Belgium and Luxembourg) or in Peek above; below are English meanings for its French statuses, 8-day pickup rules, claims and contacts. On 16 October 2026 Colis Privé announced that it and [Paack](carrier:paack) are uniting under the Paack brand from January 2027, with tracking and deliveries running as normal until then.

## Colis Privé tracking number: where to find it

Colis Privé's help lists four places: the shop's order confirmation email, Colis Privé's notifications (sent only if the shop switched them on), your account on the shop's website and the delivery notice. The reference the shop gives can [differ from the tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number). Its official emails come only from livraison@notification.colisprive.com.

The number is 12 letters and digits:

| Shape | Invented example |
| --- | --- |
| Two letters, ten digits | `KL4829173648` |
| One letter, eleven digits | `K48291736482` |
| Twelve digits | `847291736482` |

The tracking also asks for the delivery postcode; a number it doesn't recognise sends you back to the empty form. Twelve digits alone also fit [FedEx](carrier:fedex), Mondial Relay, [BRT](carrier:brt) and others, `CC` plus ten digits points to Relais Colis and `FR` plus ten digits to Amazon Logistics. Check which carrier your email names, or see [tracking number formats](guide:tracking-number-formats). Colis Privé Store orders have an order number (the order reference) that usually starts with HS or 2R, and they're tracked only on colisprive-store.com with that reference.

## Colis Privé tracking statuses: what the French words mean

Peek reads Colis Privé's tracking in French, one dated sentence per step; its English help pages use labels such as `in transit` instead ([what those mean](guide:tracking-statuses)). The lines Peek recognises, in the order a parcel meets them:

| Colis Privé's words | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `arrivé chez le partenaire d'expédition` | A shipping partner has it and takes it to Colis Privé. |
| `en transit vers` | It's on its way to Colis Privé. |
| `pris en charge` | Colis Privé has your parcel. |
| `arrivé sur notre agence` | It's at the local depot. |
| `rendez-vous est confirmé` | Delivery day booked; the date ends the line. |
| `en cours de distribution par le livreur` | It's out with the driver. |
| `nous avons tenté de livrer` | The driver couldn't deliver it. |
| `vous attend au relais` | It's waiting at a pickup point. |
| `a été livré` | Delivered, in your letterbox or by hand. |
| `incident` | A problem Colis Privé is checking. |
| `retourné à l'expéditeur` | It has been returned to the shop. |

Colis Privé's help also names `Colis attendu par Colis Privé` (`Package awaiting Colis Privé` in the English help), a line Peek doesn't sort into a step. It means Colis Privé hasn't received the parcel from the sender yet: the shop still has it or, the Belgian site adds, it's in international transport or customs. Ask the shop. `pris en charge` sounds like "taken care of" but only means Colis Privé has it. `va être prochainement déposé` announces a pickup-point drop on the date given, and `subi un retard` means a delay.

## Colis Privé delivery hours, missed deliveries and parcel shops

**France.** Colis Privé delivers between 8am and 7pm; its pages don't name the days. The parcel goes in your letterbox if it fits, into your hands if it needs a signature or to a neighbour or caretaker. Otherwise the driver leaves a notice. If the shop allows it, click "Modify my delivery" ("Convenir d'une date de présentation") in the tracking and choose a day at home between noon and 9pm, someone else at an address with the same postcode or a pickup point. If not, the parcel waits at the nearest pickup point. You have 10 working days to reschedule and 8 days to pick it up, then it goes back to the shop.

Colis Privé says it has over 5,500 pickup points in mainland France ([finder](https://client.colisprive-store.com/relais)). Its outdoor lockers open with a code sent by email and text.

**Belgium and Luxembourg.** Delivery runs Monday to Saturday, noon to 10pm. If you're out, the parcel may go to a neighbour named on the notice. A second delivery happens only if you book it in the tracking on colisprive.be: a later day, a pickup point or an address with the same postcode. Book soon: its pages give 8 or 10 days before the parcel goes back. Its 800 pickup points ([finder](https://colisprive.be/trouver-un-relais/)) keep parcels for 8 calendar days.

## Colis Privé package not delivered: late, lost or damaged

Colis Privé handles delivery claims; a refund or replacement depends on the shop's conditions.

**France.** Check colisprive.fr's banner for general delays first. If the parcel is late, damaged or not delivered, a "File a claim" ("Déposer une réclamation") button appears on its tracking page. Its pages differ on the reply time: the claims page says 2 to 7 working days, by email or phone, while the delay and delivered-but-not-received pages say 48 working hours. No button means a claim is already open, perhaps by the shop, or the time limit has passed. Marked delivered but not there? Colis Privé says that means the driver confirmed it: check the letterbox, the caretaker and your neighbours, then claim. See [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).

**Belgium and Luxembourg.** Once Colis Privé has the parcel, delivery generally takes 48 hours. More than 4 days late? Claim on colisprive.be; replies take at least 4 working days. Claim within one month of delivery; after that the button disappears. Once a parcel is back with the sender, only the shop can act. In Belgium, if a complaint was handled badly, the Postal Sector Mediation Service (ombudsmanposte.be) is free.

Nothing moving for days? See [why tracking stops updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

## Colis Privé customer service number and contact

As listed on Colis Privé's pages:

- **France:** 0 806 000 250 (free service plus the price of a call), Monday to Friday 9am to 7pm and Saturday 9am to 1pm French time, per the French page. There's no email address; claims go through the tracking page.
- **Belgium and Luxembourg:** no incoming phone line. colisprive.be gives two email addresses: suividecolis@colisprive.com on its English contact page, opvolgenvanpakketten@colisprive.com on its Dutch pages. Send your name, postcode and parcel number; email claims can take 4 to 7 days.

> Colis Privé says it never asks you to pay to track a parcel, to give bank details by email or text or to call a premium-rate number. In France, you can forward a suspicious text to 33700.

## Tracking a Colis Privé parcel with Peek

[Peek](/) needs the 12-character number and the delivery postcode. Type the number and choose Colis Privé as the carrier: a field appears for the postcode, five digits in France, four in Belgium, or L and four digits in Luxembourg. Or type the number followed by its postcode: `KL4829173648 75001` (France), `KL4829173648 B1000` (Belgium) or `KL4829173648 L4829` (Luxembourg), all invented. Keep the B or L, and Peek finds Colis Privé by itself. Links from colisprive.com or colisprive.fr can be pasted too.

While you have the parcel open in Peek, or on its delivery day with notifications on, Peek checks up to every 10 minutes. Colis Privé's scans carry a date but no time, so the faster checks of a parcel out for delivery, up to every 2 minutes, run only on the day of the round, from 6 a.m. French time, while Colis Privé's own tracker answers. Otherwise, and after two days without news (12 hours while the parcel still shows "Label created" or "Received by carrier"), Peek checks hourly, and every 6 hours after seven days at "Label created" with nothing new. Peek can notify you of every scan, important steps only or delivery day only. It shows the status, the history in French and the expected delivery and, only to you, the recipient's name (under "Recipient"). Peek can't file a claim or contact Colis Privé for you.

## Questions about Colis Privé tracking

### What is Colis Privé, and is it becoming Paack?

Colis Privé is a French parcel carrier founded in 1993 that has also delivered in Belgium and Luxembourg since 2021. On 16 October 2026 it announced it is joining [Paack](carrier:paack) under the Paack brand from January 2027, with current tracking and delivery continuing as before until then.

### How long does Colis Privé take to deliver, and does it deliver on Saturday?

Colis Privé advertises 24 to 48 hours across France, Belgium and Luxembourg. In Belgium and Luxembourg it delivers Monday to Saturday (noon to 10pm); its French pages list 8am to 7pm without naming days.

### How long does Colis Privé hold a parcel at a pickup point?

8 days in France (over 5,500 pickup points) and 8 calendar days in Belgium and Luxembourg (800 pickup points), after which the parcel returns to the sender. In France you have 10 working days to reschedule a missed home delivery.

### How do I claim for a late, damaged or missing Colis Privé parcel?

Use the "File a claim" button on your parcel's tracking page on colisprive.fr or colisprive.be (in Belgium and Luxembourg, within one month of delivery). For refunds or replacements, contact the online shop that sent the parcel.

### What is the Colis Privé customer service phone number or email?

In France, call 0 806 000 250 (Mon–Fri 9am–7pm, Sat 9am–1pm French time; no email). Belgium and Luxembourg have no incoming phone line: email suividecolis@colisprive.com (or opvolgenvanpakketten@colisprive.com) with your name, postcode and parcel number.
