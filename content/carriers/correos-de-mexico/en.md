Correos de México (*Servicio Postal Mexicano* / *SEPOMEX*) is Mexico's national postal service, handling domestic registered mail (*Registrados*), *Mexpost* express courier parcels and international UPU postal deliveries both outbound from Mexico and inbound from postal operators worldwide. You can check a shipment on [Correos de México's tracking page](https://www.correosdemexico.gob.mx/SSLServicios/SeguimientoEnvio/Seguimiento.aspx) (entering both the tracking number and the posting year, *Ejercicio*), or paste your code into the box above.

## Correos de México tracking numbers: UPU S10 formats

Check your Mexican post-office counter receipt, Mexpost waybill or international seller's dispatch email. Correos de México tracks 13-character UPU S10 codes — both outbound codes ending in `MX` and incoming foreign postal S10 items bound for Mexico (such as `...US`, `...CN`, `...ES` or `...JP`):

| Format (invented) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `RR482917367MX` | Registered mail (*Correo Registrado*) sent from Mexico (UPU S10 ending in `MX`). |
| `MP482917367MX` | *Mexpost* domestic or express postal parcel (UPU S10 ending in `MX`). |
| `EE482917367MX` | International EMS express mail from Mexico (UPU S10 ending in `MX`). |

**Year selector (*Ejercicio*) on SEPOMEX.** Unlike most trackers, [Correos de México's Seguimiento portal](https://www.correosdemexico.gob.mx/SSLServicios/SeguimientoEnvio/Seguimiento.aspx) files scans by calendar year (`2026`, `2025`, `2024`, etc.). If a late-December parcel continues travelling into January, you may need to check both years on the website to see the full journey. For private Mexican couriers such as [Estafeta](carrier:estafeta), [J&T Express Mexico](carrier:j-and-t-mx) or [Paquetexpress](carrier:paquetexpress), see [tracking number formats](guide:tracking-number-formats).

## What Correos de México tracking statuses mean

Correos de México displays Spanish event descriptions alongside the Mexican post office or foreign postal exchange office and timestamp:

| Correos de México says | What it means for you | Peek shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Depósito en Oficina de Correos` / `Deposito del Cliente en ...` | Accepted at a Mexican post office counter or posted abroad by the foreign postal operator. | "Received by carrier" |
| `En tránsito hacia destino`, `Recepción en Oficina de Correos`, `En zona de intercambio int. hacia el país destino, previo Aduana`, `Pieza depositada en valija y enviada a oficina del País destino` | Moving between Mexican distribution offices or bagged and dispatched internationally. | "In transit" |
| `Arribo a Zona Intercambio México, en espera de ingreso a la Aduana`, `Inicio de la revisión aduanal en país destino` | Arrived at the international exchange office waiting for or undergoing customs inspection. | "At customs" |
| `Revisión aduanal concluida` | Customs inspection finished; item released back into postal transit. | "In transit" |
| `Con mensajero para entrega` | Out with the local postal carrier for delivery today. | "Out for delivery" |
| `Puesta en ventanilla` | Held at the named local post-office counter for recipient collection (bring official photo ID and your tracking number). | "Ready for pickup" |
| `Pieza devuelta ...` (e.g. `por domicilio destino incorrecto`) | Returned toward the sender with the reason stated. | "Returned to sender" |
| `Entrega` / `Entrega Internacional` | Delivered in Mexico or confirmed delivered abroad by the destination post. | "Delivered" |

## Postal pickup (*Puesta en ventanilla*), customs and claims

According to [Servicio Postal Mexicano on gob.mx](https://www.gob.mx/correosdemexico) and the official [Reclamo de un Envío (Shipment Claim) page](https://www.gob.mx/correosdemexico/es/acciones-y-programas/reclamo-de-un-envio):

- **Counter pickup (*Puesta en ventanilla*):** when tracking shows `Puesta en ventanilla`, your item is waiting at the post office named in the *Origen* column. Bring official photo ID and the 13-character code to collect it before it is returned to the sender.
- **Inbound international customs:** incoming postal parcels entering Mexico pass through Mexican customs inspection (`Inicio de la revisión aduanal...`). Wait times vary by season and customs assessment; see [parcels held at customs](guide:customs).
- **Filing a formal claim (*Reclamo de un Envío*):** formal postal inquiries and compensation claims apply **only to Registered (*Registrados*) and *Mexpost* services** (not ordinary untracked mail). You can file a claim when a shipment exceeds its delivery timeframe or arrives in poor condition — either in person at any Correos de México post office or online. SEPOMEX's maximum response timeframe for Registered and Mexpost claims is **30 working days** (*30 días hábiles*).

## Correos de México parcel stuck, quiet or returned

- **Long pauses in customs or international transit:** international postal parcels entering or leaving Mexico commonly pause for days or weeks at `Arribo a Zona Intercambio México` or between `Pieza depositada en valija` and destination arrival. See [why tracking stops updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).
- **Returned items (`Pieza devuelta`):** once SEPOMEX marks an item `Pieza devuelta` (for example incorrect address or unclaimed at counter), even subsequent `Puesta en ventanilla` and `Entrega` scans refer to returning the parcel back to the original sender.
- For online purchases, contact the seller alongside filing an inquiry. See [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received), [damaged parcel](guide:damaged-parcel), [lost parcel](guide:lost-parcel) and [returned to sender](guide:returned-to-sender).

## Correos de México customer service and contacts

Official channels published on [gob.mx/correosdemexico](https://www.gob.mx/correosdemexico), [Seguimiento.aspx](https://www.correosdemexico.gob.mx/SSLServicios/SeguimientoEnvio/Seguimiento.aspx) and [Reclamo de un Envío](https://www.gob.mx/correosdemexico/es/acciones-y-programas/reclamo-de-un-envio):

- **Official inquiry email:** `contacto@correosdemexico.gob.mx`.
- **Claims & inquiries:** [Reclamo de un Envío](https://www.gob.mx/correosdemexico/es/acciones-y-programas/reclamo-de-un-envio) or in person at your local Mexican post office.
- **Consumer disputes in Mexico:** for unresolved online retail or service disputes, consumers can contact [PROFECO](https://www.gob.mx/profeco).

> Beware of fake SMS or WhatsApp messages pretending to be Correos de México / SEPOMEX demanding an online card payment to "correct your postcode" or "release a parcel". Always check your S10 code directly on [correosdemexico.gob.mx](https://www.correosdemexico.gob.mx/SSLServicios/SeguimientoEnvio/Seguimiento.aspx) or write to `contacto@correosdemexico.gob.mx`. See [delivery scam texts](guide:delivery-scam-texts).

## Track a Correos de México parcel with Peek

[Peek](/) recognises pasted `correosdemexico.gob.mx/SSLServicios/SeguimientoEnvio/Seguimiento.aspx?guia=` links as Correos de México directly. When you paste a 13-character UPU S10 code ending in `MX` (`RR482917367MX`, `MP482917367MX` or `EE482917367MX`, invented — or an incoming foreign S10 code bound for Mexico), Peek classifies it as international post first; tap "Change" while adding or "Change carrier" on the parcel screen and select Correos de México (searching "SEPOMEX", "Mexpost" or "Mexico Post" finds it too).

Peek queries Correos de México directly — stepping back automatically across available posting years so cross-year histories stay complete — and shows the mapped stage ("Received by carrier", "In transit", "At customs", "Out for delivery", "Ready for pickup", "Returned to sender", "Delivered"), Spanish scan wording, post-office or exchange-office location, pickup post office when `Puesta en ventanilla` appears, and delivery timestamp where available. Even after an item is marked `Pieza devuelta`, Peek keeps the later counter-wait and sender-delivery scans in "Returned to sender" so they are not misread as delivered to you. Scans at offices on Mexican Central Time (`America/Mexico_City`, UTC-6 year-round) convert to your device's time zone, while other zones and foreign posts keep local wall clocks. Peek discards signatory names, query timestamps and page notices; SEPOMEX's tracker does not provide an estimated delivery date, weight, service name or proof-of-delivery photo.

Peek checks up to every 10 minutes while you have the parcel open or on delivery day with alerts on, and up to every 2 minutes from 6:00 a.m. Mexico City time on the day a `Con mensajero para entrega` ("Out for delivery") scan arrives. Otherwise it checks hourly, stays hourly while open after 12 hours at "Label created" or "Received by carrier" or 2 days without a new scan, and drops to every 6 hours after 7 days still at "Label created". When tracking services fill in as a fallback, they check hourly, every 3 hours after a day without change, and up to every 15 minutes on delivery day. After 4 days in transit with no new scan, Peek flags the pause (for example "No tracking update since Mon, Oct 12"), shows "Parcels often go quiet on long flights, at customs or over a weekend. Peek keeps asking Correos de México." and displays "Get help from Correos de México", which opens SEPOMEX's shipment-claim page. After more than three weeks without an update, Peek shows "Nothing new for over three weeks. If it still hasn’t arrived, ask the seller. Peek keeps asking Correos de México."

You can pick alert presets for "Every scan", "Important steps" or "Delivery only". Peek monitors tracking updates only and cannot clear customs or file a 30-day *Reclamo de un Envío* with SEPOMEX on your behalf.

## Questions about Correos de México tracking

### What tracking numbers work with Correos de México?

Correos de México tracks 13-character UPU S10 postal codes: 2 letters + 9 digits + 2 country letters (for example `RR482917367MX` or `MP482917367MX`, invented), including incoming foreign S10 mail entering Mexico.

### What does `Puesta en ventanilla` mean?

It means your parcel is being held at the local post-office counter named in the scan's *Origen* column for in-person collection. Bring official photo ID and your tracking number.

### Why didn't Correos de México find my tracking number?

On [Seguimiento.aspx](https://www.correosdemexico.gob.mx/SSLServicios/SeguimientoEnvio/Seguimiento.aspx), make sure you select the right posting year (*Ejercicio*) — especially for items posted late in the previous year — and note that ordinary untracked mail is not scannable.

### How do I file a claim with Correos de México?

For Registered (*Registrados*) and *Mexpost* items that are overdue or damaged, file a *Reclamo de un Envío* at a post office or via [gob.mx/correosdemexico/es/acciones-y-programas/reclamo-de-un-envio](https://www.gob.mx/correosdemexico/es/acciones-y-programas/reclamo-de-un-envio) (30-working-day response window) or email `contacto@correosdemexico.gob.mx`.
