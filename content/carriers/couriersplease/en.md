CouriersPlease (part of FMH Group) delivers parcels across metropolitan and regional Australia through local Franchise Partners. Track your parcel by entering its `CP` consignment number on [CouriersPlease's tracking page](https://www.couriersplease.com.au/tools-track) or pasting it in the box above. The consignment number appears in the shop's dispatch email or CouriersPlease's emails from noreply@couriersplease.com.au.

## Couriers Please tracking number: what it looks like

CouriersPlease calls it the consignment number, also known as the tracking number. Its Help Centre says it is usually up to 15 characters, letters and/or numbers, and generally starts with `CP`. The redelivery page allows up to 20 characters. Not every CouriersPlease number starts with `CP`.

| Example (invented) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `CPABC4829173640` | A typical CouriersPlease consignment number: CP, letters, then digits. |
| `CPA48291736405` | Also a CouriersPlease shape, a shorter one. |
| `CP482917367AU` | Not CouriersPlease: two letters, nine digits and AU is a postal item number, the kind [Australia Post](carrier:australia-post) uses. |

**Where to find it.** Look in the shop's order confirmation email or app. CouriersPlease's own emails always contain it, but are sent only if the sender shared your email address. They come from noreply@couriersplease.com.au, and its texts from the sender name `Courierspls`. A missed-delivery card has a redelivery card number, which the redelivery page also accepts. CouriersPlease can't look a parcel up by name or address. [How to find your tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number) lists more places to look.

**Number not valid or no tracking yet?** Check for typos and confirm with the shop that it went with CouriersPlease. The shipment may be booked before its details reach CouriersPlease's tracking, so CouriersPlease suggests trying again after 1 business day.

## CouriersPlease tracking statuses explained

These are the Help Centre's wordings, in the order a parcel usually meets them:

| CouriersPlease status | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `Shipment information received` | The sender created the shipment. CouriersPlease may not have the parcel yet. |
| `Your parcel has been picked up` | CouriersPlease collected it from the sender. |
| `Arrived at delivery centre` | Moving through CouriersPlease's network; nothing to do. |
| `In transit to regional delivery centre` | Handed to a regional delivery partner. Updates may be slower. |
| `Onboard for delivery` | With a driver or Franchise Partner for delivery. |
| `Delivery attempted – Unsafe to leave – Sent to a collection point` | Nobody could take it and there was no safe place, so it's going to a nearby collection point. |
| `Processing for collection at a nearby collection point` | At the collection point, but not ready to collect yet. |
| `Pickup at Collection Point – Ready for collection` | Go and collect it, with ID and your collection details. |
| `Delivered with Authority to Leave (ATL)` | Left at your address without a signature. |
| `Delivered` | Delivered. Can't find it? Check the proof of delivery. |
| `Collection period expired – Return to sender in progress` | Not collected in time, so it's going back to the sender. |

`Shipment information received` confuses people most: paperwork exists, but the parcel may still be with the shop. CouriersPlease also uses `Manifested` for the same moment, before the parcel is scanned into its network. If it stays there, ask the sender to confirm it was dispatched.

`In transit to regional delivery centre` doesn't necessarily mean the parcel has stopped moving, CouriersPlease says. Its tracking page also shows a Delivery ETA with a badge: `On track`, or `Overdue` once that date has passed without delivery.

`Parcel requires further processing` isn't in the Help Centre's list. It can appear days after the parcel arrived at a facility, and Peek shows it as still in transit.

## Delivery, collection points and redelivery

**Delivery days and times.** CouriersPlease's guide gives 1 to 3 business days from metro to metro, a few extra days across the country, longer for regional or remote areas, and an extra week at peak times like Christmas. All are estimates. You can't pick a delivery time: it depends on the Franchise Partner's route that day.

**When you're not home.** The driver looks for a safe place. If there isn't one, or a signature is needed, the parcel goes to a nearby collection point. Parcels over 10 kg or 100 cm don't go to a collection point: you're asked to arrange a redelivery instead. In regional areas, CouriersPlease says parcels are always left in a safe place.

**Collection points.** CouriersPlease works with HUBBED, which has more than 3,000 collection points across Australia. Find one with the [collection point locator](https://www.couriersplease.com.au/resources/find-a-collection-point). The transfer can take up to 24 hours to confirm, so wait for `Ready for collection`. You need a collection PIN, current photo ID and proof of address. Parcels are generally held for 7 days. The terms of carriage count them from the PIN, then allow 5 more days at the nearest depot before the parcel goes back to the sender. If no PIN arrived, request it from [HUBBED](https://hubbed.com/getpin/).

**Redelivery.** Book it on the [redelivery page](https://www.couriersplease.com.au/book/redelivery) with the consignment or redelivery card number. The link is valid for 6 business days from the attempt, and redelivery usually follows within 1 to 2 business days. You get one redelivery; if that fails, the depot generally holds the parcel up to 6 business days before it may go back. The Help Centre says a parcel already sent to a collection point can't be redelivered to your address and must be collected there, though CouriersPlease's receiving guide still offers a redelivery within 6 days; ask Support before you book one.

**Delivery Choices.** If the sender gave your email and mobile, CouriersPlease's notification has a link to ask for a signature, collect nearby, leave it with a neighbour, change the address or choose another day a few days later. Outside that link, only the sender can redirect a parcel to a new address.

## CouriersPlease parcel overdue, missing or damaged

**No recent updates.** Regional and interstate deliveries, and handovers to a delivery partner, can leave several days between scans. CouriersPlease says most delayed parcels arrive within 24 to 48 business hours of the original estimate. Past the estimate with no recent update, contact Support. More in [tracking not updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

**Delivered but not received.** Check the proof of delivery (a photo for Authority to Leave, which can take up to an hour to appear), safe places, people at the address and any mailroom. Then contact Support; CouriersPlease asks for disputes within 14 business days of the last delivery scan. See also [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).

**Damaged.** As the receiver, contact the sender: the sender or account holder lodges the claim, within 14 business days of delivery. Parcel Protect covers eligible domestic shipments up to AUD 1,000, but not the first AUD 100, and not parcels left under Authority to Leave.

## Tracking CouriersPlease parcels with Peek

[Peek](/) names CouriersPlease straight away for a number of the `CP` shape above. A link from couriersplease.com.au/tools-track (with `no=` or `trackingCode=`) works too. No account, postcode or phone number needed. A CouriersPlease number that doesn't start with `CP` isn't recognised as CouriersPlease: paste the tracking link, or pick CouriersPlease with "Change" as you add it, or "Change carrier" on a parcel already added. Peek then takes any 8 to 30 letters and digits with at least one digit, which covers item labels, prepaid coupons and the SSCCs some senders print.

Peek reads CouriersPlease's own tracking and shows its scans in CouriersPlease's wording, each with its time and state, and the Delivery ETA once CouriersPlease has scanned the parcel, until it's delivered or returned. It shows no collection point, proof of delivery, recipient's name or driver's name for CouriersPlease.

While you have the parcel open in Peek, or on its delivery day with notifications on, Peek checks up to every 10 minutes; on the day CouriersPlease scans the parcel `Onboard for delivery`, every 2 minutes from 6 a.m. local time, in the state of CouriersPlease's newest scan, while CouriersPlease's own tracker answers. Otherwise Peek checks hourly. After 12 hours with only "Label created" or "Received by carrier", or two days without news, it checks hourly however you follow it, and every 6 hours after seven days at "Label created". When CouriersPlease's tracker doesn't answer and Peek follows the parcel through tracking services alone, it checks hourly, every 3 hours after a quiet day, and up to every 15 minutes on the out-for-delivery day.

When the newest scan is four days old and the parcel is still at "Label created", "Received by carrier" or "In transit", Peek flags it with that scan's day, such as "No tracking update since Mon 12 Oct". The parcel's page then says "Parcels often go quiet on long flights, at customs or over a weekend. Peek keeps asking CouriersPlease.", with a "Get help from CouriersPlease" link to CouriersPlease's Help Centre. After three weeks with no new scan, it says "Nothing new for over three weeks. If it still hasn’t arrived, ask the seller. Peek keeps asking CouriersPlease."

Peek can notify you of "Every scan", "Important steps" or "Delivery only". For redelivery, a PIN or a claim, go to CouriersPlease or the shop.

## Questions about Couriers Please tracking

### How long does CouriersPlease take to deliver in Australia?

CouriersPlease estimates 1 to 3 business days between metro areas, a few extra days interstate, longer to regional or remote areas, and up to an extra week at peak times such as Christmas.

### Why is my CouriersPlease tracking stuck on Manifested or not updating?

`Manifested` or `Shipment information received` means paperwork exists before CouriersPlease scans the parcel; regional or interstate handovers can also leave multi-day scan gaps. Most delayed parcels arrive within 24 to 48 business hours of the estimate—if yours is past ETA with no update, contact Support.

### How do CouriersPlease HUBBED collection points and PINs work?

Rather than lockers, CouriersPlease uses over 3,000 HUBBED collection points. Once status reads `Ready for collection` (up to 24 hours after transfer), bring your collection PIN, photo ID and proof of address within 7 days (missing PINs can be requested from HUBBED).

### How do I book a CouriersPlease redelivery or depot pickup?

Book one redelivery on couriersplease.com.au/book/redelivery within 6 business days using your consignment or card number (delivery usually follows in 1 to 2 business days), or ask Support to arrange local depot pickup within 5 business days with photo ID.

### What is the CouriersPlease contact number, and how do I dispute a delivery?

Call 1300 36 1000 (or TIS National on 131 450 for an interpreter) or use the Virtual Assistant and Help Centre at support.couriersplease.com.au. Report missing deliveries within 14 business days of the delivery scan, and ask the sender to lodge damaged-parcel claims within 14 business days.
