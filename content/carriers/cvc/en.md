CVC is a carrier in Bulgaria. Peek's catalog also knows it as Си Ви Си, Си Ви Си ЕООД and e-CVC. To track a CVC parcel, paste its number in the box above and pick CVC as the carrier: Peek can't tell a CVC number from its shape. The number comes from the shop or the person who sent the parcel. CVC's own tracking page is at my.e-cvc.bg/track.

Peek couldn't read CVC's website: on 13 November 2026, e-cvc.bg answered "404 Not Found" to every page Peek asked for. So this page says what Peek does with a CVC parcel, and nothing about CVC's own rules, prices or contacts.

## CVC tracking number: what it looks like

Peek's catalog has no number shape for CVC. Whatever your CVC number looks like, Peek can't recognise it as CVC's, and CVC never appears under "Fits this number" when you add it. This page gives no example: Peek couldn't check what CVC's numbers look like.

| What you paste | What Peek does |
| --- | --- |
| The CVC number alone | It doesn't name CVC. It may list other carriers whose shapes the number fits, or none. Pick CVC yourself, as shown under "Track a CVC parcel with Peek" below. |
| A link to CVC's tracker on my.e-cvc.bg | Peek has no rule for CVC's links, so the link doesn't name CVC either. Copy the number out of it and paste that. |
| A number the shop says is another carrier's | Track it with that carrier: the shop's email is the best clue to who delivers. |

**Where to find it.** In the shop's shipping email or on your order page. For a parcel from a person, ask them. No number at all? See [how to find a tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number), and [tracking number formats](guide:tracking-number-formats) for what other carriers' numbers look like.

## What CVC tracking statuses mean in Peek

Peek doesn't ask CVC's tracker, so its code holds no CVC wordings. It asks tracking services, such as ParcelsApp, Ship24 and 17TRACK, for the number and shows the history of the first one that has the parcel, each line in that service's words. A service can lag behind CVC's own tracker. Peek sorts each line into one of its own statuses:

| Peek shows | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| "Label created" | The sender has registered the shipment. CVC may not have the parcel yet. |
| "Received by carrier" | CVC has taken the parcel in. |
| "In transit" | The parcel is on its way. |
| "Out for delivery" | It's on the delivery round today. |
| "Delivery attempted" | The courier tried and couldn't deliver it. |
| "Ready for pickup" | It's waiting for you to collect it. Ask the shop where and for how long, or look it up on CVC's own tracker. |
| "Delivered" | Delivered, to you or someone who took it for you. |
| "Returned to sender" | It went back to the sender. |
| "Needs attention" | The service reports a problem. Read its line in the history. |

Before any service has a line, Peek shows "Checking for updates" while it asks, then "Waiting for the carrier". "Update unavailable" means two checks in a row failed; Peek keeps trying. More in [tracking statuses explained](guide:tracking-statuses).

## CVC parcel not updating, lost or returned

**Nothing yet.** A tracking service may not know the number yet, or may not follow CVC's parcels at all. Peek then shows "Waiting for the carrier" and keeps asking, each service again at most a day after it last had nothing. If there's still no line two days after you added the parcel, Peek flags it: "Still waiting for the carrier’s first update". Check the number against the shop's email. If nothing comes after a few days, open CVC's own tracker with "Open the CVC website" on the parcel's page, and ask the shop whether the parcel was really sent.

**No news for days.** After four days without a new line, a parcel still showing "Label created", "Received by carrier" or "In transit" gets a flag: "No tracking update for four days". See [why tracking stops updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

**Delivered, but nothing came.** Check around the door and with neighbours, then ask the shop. See [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).

**Lost or damaged.** Tell the shop first: it booked the delivery and can take it up with CVC or send you a new parcel. See [lost parcels](guide:lost-parcel) and [damaged parcels](guide:damaged-parcel).

**Returned.** "Returned to sender" means the parcel went back to the shop or the sender. Ask them for a refund or a new delivery. See [returned to sender](guide:returned-to-sender).

## Track a CVC parcel with Peek

[Peek](/) follows more than 3,500 carriers, CVC among them, but it can't tell a CVC parcel by its number alone. Pick the carrier yourself:

1. Paste the number in the box above.
2. Press "Change" beside the carrier line and search for CVC. "e-CVC" or "Си Ви Си" finds it too, with "Also called e-CVC" or "Also called Си Ви Си" under the name, and so does "Bulgaria".
3. Pick CVC: the line then reads "Chosen by you". Track the parcel.

On a parcel you've already added, use "Change carrier". If Peek lists other carriers under "Fits this number", they only fit the number's shape. Before you choose, the carrier line may say "not found yet · Peek keeps checking": that's normal for a new number.

If you leave the carrier to Peek, it looks the number up with tracking services. If they show the parcel moving and name CVC as its carrier, Peek files the parcel under CVC by itself.

**What you see.** The status, and each line from the tracking service with its date and place when the service gives one. Until a service has the parcel, "Open the CVC website" on the parcel's page opens the number on CVC's own tracker. Once a service shows the history, the link opens that service's page instead, as "Open the ParcelsApp website", for example.

**Another carrier.** In the parcel's first 30 days, Peek also asks the carriers whose number shapes fit yours. If one of them shows the parcel moving under that number, Peek moves the parcel there and says "Swapped automatically from CVC".

**How often.** While someone waits for the parcel (notifications on, or you looked in the last hour), Peek checks it every 15 minutes, the delivery day included. After two days without news (12 hours while it still shows "Label created" or "Received by carrier"), it checks hourly. Notifications can cover "Every scan", "Important steps" or "Delivery only".

Peek can't contact CVC, the shop or the sender for you, change the delivery or file a claim.

## Questions about CVC tracking

### How do I track a CVC parcel?

Paste the number in the box above, press "Change" and pick CVC: Peek can't recognise a CVC number by its shape. Peek then follows the parcel through tracking services. On a parcel you've already added, use "Change carrier". CVC's own tracker is at my.e-cvc.bg/track.

### Is e-CVC the same as CVC?

Yes. Peek's catalog lists e-CVC, Си Ви Си and Си Ви Си ЕООД as other names for CVC, and CVC's tracking page is on my.e-cvc.bg. Searching for any of these names in Peek's carrier list finds CVC.

### Why does my CVC parcel say "Waiting for the carrier"?

No tracking service has a line for the number yet. Peek keeps asking, each service again at most a day after it last had nothing. Check the number with the shop, and look it up on CVC's own tracker with "Open the CVC website".
