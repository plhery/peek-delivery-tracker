DHL eCommerce Iberia is DHL's standard parcel service in Spain and Portugal, still called DHL Parcel in places. Track a parcel in the tracking box on [dhl.com/es-es/ecommerce](https://www.dhl.com/es-es/ecommerce/home/private-shipments/customer-service/receive-a-parcel/tracking-your-shipment.html), or paste the number in the box above. The number comes from the shop, or from DHL's own email or SMS with the expected delivery date.

## DHL eCommerce tracking number: where it is and what it looks like

DHL says its tracking number is 10 to 39 letters and digits. Its system tells capital and small letters apart, so type letters in capitals, without spaces. The shop usually emails it; if the sender gave DHL your email or mobile number, DHL also sends it with the expected delivery date (in Portugal, check your spam folder). No number? Ask the shop, or see [how to find your tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number).

DHL publishes no example number. The shapes Peek's catalog knows (examples invented):

| Shape | Example | Whose it can be |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10 digits | `1234567890` | DHL eCommerce Iberia, but also [DHL Express](carrier:dhl-express), Mondial Relay, Relais Colis, TIPSA and GLS Spain |
| Two letters, 7 digits, then `0` | `AB12345670` | DHL eCommerce Iberia |

If the shop's email names [Mondial Relay](carrier:mondial-relay), [TIPSA](carrier:tipsa) or [GLS Spain](carrier:gls-es) for a 10-digit number, track it there. Two letters, nine digits and `ES` is a [Correos](carrier:correos-spain) number.

## DHL eCommerce tracking statuses explained

DHL's tracking portal (clientesparcel.dhl.es) can show scans in English, Spanish or Portuguese, with the switch at the bottom. The English wordings, in order:

| DHL says | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `Shipment data received` | The sender has registered the parcel. DHL doesn't have it yet. |
| `Picked up` | DHL collected the parcel from the sender; the town follows. |
| `Shipment received at DHL Depot` | The parcel reached a DHL depot. |
| `Arrived at` | Moving between depots; the town follows. `In transit in` and `Departed from` work the same way. |
| `Out for delivery` | On the van today. |
| `The delivery could not be made due to consignee absent` | Nobody answered. See the next section for what happens. |
| `Delivered to DHL ServicePoint, ready for collection by the recipient` | Ready to pick up. The clock starts now. |
| `Parcel picked up by recipient` | You (or someone with your authorisation) picked it up. |
| `Delivered` | Delivered to the address. |
| `Shipment being returned to sender` | A return is under way. |

Some need explaining:

- `Delivered, POD pending`: marked delivered, proof of delivery not in yet. It can appear early: a later delivery round or a failed attempt may still follow.
- `Shipment with incidence If you are the sender, please contact DHL Parcel, if you are the recipient please contact the sender`: the Spanish version says plainly the parcel can't be found. Contact the shop.
- `The expected delivery date might be postponed one working day` is a short delay, not a lost parcel.

## Delivery, ServicePoints and lockers

DHL eCommerce delivers Monday to Friday, except public holidays.

**Nobody home.** DHL tries a neighbour in the same block or next door, leaves a note in your letterbox and emails you the neighbour's details if it has your email address.

**Changing the delivery.** From the expected-date email, or with the shipment number, you can choose another address in the province, a ServicePoint, a neighbour or the concierge, or a day up to 7 working days later. One change only, and once out for delivery only a neighbour or the concierge. Cash-on-delivery parcels and those over 120 x 60 x 60 cm can't go to a ServicePoint.

**ServicePoints.** DHL has more than 3,500 pickup points in Spain and Portugal. An SMS or email names yours. You have 10 calendar days, with a reminder after 7; then it goes back to the sender. Bring your ID (DNI, or Cartão de Cidadão in Portugal); someone else needs a copy of it, their own ID and DHL's signed authorisation.

**Lockers (Spain).** Choose one by changing the delivery before it's out for delivery. You get a PIN and open the locker with the DHL lockers app over Bluetooth. It waits 7 calendar days.

## Parcel delayed, lost or damaged

**No update.** If the status hasn't changed for 2 days, DHL says to contact it or the sender. See [why tracking stops updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

**Lost.** In Spain, DHL tells recipients to contact the sender directly, since the shop holds the contract. In Portugal, DHL eCommerce asks you to contact its customer service.

**Damaged or items missing.** If you told the driver at delivery, contact the sender. Otherwise report it to DHL with the shipment reference and photos, within 7 calendar days for a domestic parcel or 7 working days for an international one.

**Customs.** If customs charges duty, DHL pays it for you and you pay it back, in Spain even if you refuse the parcel. More in the [customs guide](guide:customs).

**CTT Express.** DHL eCommerce Portugal has joined CTT Express, and in Spain, under an alliance with DHL eCommerce, CTT Express focuses on deliveries to consumers. Some parcels carry a CTT Express number: track those with [CTT Express](carrier:ctt-express).

> DHL warns about fake SMS saying you weren't home and asking you to download an app: delete them. DHL asks for payment only for duties and taxes. Report suspicious messages to phishing@dhl.com.

## Tracking DHL eCommerce Iberia parcels with Peek

[Peek](/) asks DHL eCommerce Iberia and [TNT Italy](carrier:tnt-it), whose waybills have the same shape, about two letters, 7 digits and `0`. For 10 digits it asks Relais Colis, TIPSA and DHL eCommerce Iberia, and DHL Express first when its check digit passes; if DHL Express knows the number too, it wins. If carriers of different brands know it, or none does, you pick with "Change carrier". DHL's other numbers (12 or 22 digits, `JJD` plates, postal numbers) work only after picking "DHL eCommerce Iberia" there. No postcode is needed.

Links from clientesparcel.dhl.es can be pasted too, from the portal's Spanish, Portuguese or English version (`/LiveTracking/ModificarEnvio/`, `ModificarMeuEnvio` or `ModifyMyShipment`). They name DHL eCommerce Iberia straight away, whatever the number's shape. A postcode at the end of the link is left out, as none is needed.

DHL's scans carry no time zone: Peek reads them on the clock of the scan's place and lists DHL's own history. Statuses keep DHL's English wording, except `Delivered` and `Out for delivery`, which show as Peek's own. Peek can notify you when the status changes. It shows no expected delivery date or delivery time for DHL eCommerce Iberia.

## Questions about DHL eCommerce tracking

### Is DHL eCommerce the same as DHL Express?

No. DHL eCommerce is DHL Group's standard parcel service, with its own tracking and customer service.

### How long does DHL eCommerce take to deliver?

DHL says 24 to 48 hours within the Iberian Peninsula and 3 to 6 days from elsewhere in Europe.

### Why does my DHL eCommerce parcel have no tracking?

Usually DHL doesn't have the parcel yet: tracking shows only once the sender hands it over, often 24 to 48 hours after you get the number. In Portugal, DHL asks you to contact it if more than 24 hours have passed since your purchase.

### Is a DHL eCommerce text legit?

Real DHL messages link only to DHL's own sites, and DHL asks for payment only for duties and taxes. If a text says you weren't home and asks you to download an app, or asks you to pay for anything else, delete it.
