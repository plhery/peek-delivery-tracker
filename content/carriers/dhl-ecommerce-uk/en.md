DHL eCommerce UK (formerly DHL Parcel UK and UK Mail; announced to rebrand as Evri Premium in 2026 while remaining separate from Evri and DHL Express) is DHL's UK domestic parcel network. Paste your 14-digit shipment number (often starting `6012`, `31`, `41` or `55`) or 9-digit return number above, or track with your postcode or 7-digit calling-card number on [track.dhlecommerce.co.uk](https://track.dhlecommerce.co.uk/).

## DHL eCommerce UK tracking number

A DHL eCommerce UK shipment number has 14 digits. Most of those the tracker shows start with `6012`; others start with 31, 41 or 55. No number at all? Ask the sender; see also [how to find your tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number).

| Number (invented) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `60124829173648` | A UK shipment number: 14 digits, here starting `6012`. |
| `4829173` | The 7-digit number on a driver's calling card, for the tracker's "Card number" box. |
| `948291736` | A return shipment's 9-digit number. |

The tracker also takes a customer reference. A calling-card number or a reference only finds the parcel with the delivery postcode.

Fourteen digits are not unique to DHL. [DPD UK](carrier:dpd-uk) uses them too (a number starting `1550` points to DPD UK), and so do SEUR and BRT. DHL eCommerce UK is also separate from [DHL Express](carrier:dhl-express): DHL eCommerce UK says it can't track or help with DHL Express deliveries. In September 2025, Evri announced that DHL eCommerce UK would be rebranded in 2026 as Evri Premium, a network of DHL kept separate from [Evri](carrier:evri). The tracker and its contact page still carry the DHL eCommerce UK name.

## DHL eCommerce UK tracking statuses explained

The tracker is in English and lists the newest message first. These are the messages Peek receives, roughly in order:

| DHL eCommerce UK says | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `Your shipment has been collected` | DHL has picked the parcel up from the sender. |
| `Your shipment is due to be delivered on Monday 02nd November 2026` | DHL has planned the delivery day, which also heads the tracker as the expected delivery. |
| `Your shipment is at the delivery depot` | It's at the depot that will deliver it. |
| `Your shipment is at the delivery location` | Between the depot and the delivery round: not delivered yet. |
| `We're sorry but the delivery of your shipment has been delayed. We expect to deliver on the next working day` | Running late: DHL expects to deliver the next working day. |
| `We're sorry but we haven't been able to locate the delivery address from the information provided by the sender.` | DHL can't find your address from what the sender gave, so the parcel waits for a better one: check your address with the sender. |
| `Your shipment is out for delivery` | With the driver today. |
| `Your shipment is delivered` | Delivered. The tracker may also show who signed. |
| `Delivery Attempted` | The driver called but couldn't deliver. |
| `We're sorry but we haven't been able to deliver your shipment as our driver was unable to gain access to the delivery address` | The driver couldn't get access to the address, so the delivery failed. |
| `Your shipment is due to be delivered to your ServicePoint on Wednesday 04th November 2026` | After a missed delivery, DHL plans to bring the parcel to a ServicePoint on that day. It isn't there yet. |
| `Delayed` | Running late after a missed delivery; the parcel is still on its way. |
| `Collected` | You collected the parcel yourself. |
| `We're sorry but your shipment was refused on delivery. Please contact your sender for further information` | The parcel was refused at the door; the headline then says it has gone back to the sender. |

Two lines can confuse. `Collected` on its own means you picked the parcel up, while `Your shipment has been collected` at the start means DHL collected it from the sender. And `Your shipment is at the delivery depot` can come back after a delivery or a refusal, when the parcel returns to the depot: the newest line is the one that counts.

## DHL eCommerce UK delivery, ServicePoints and missed deliveries

**Rearranging.** If you've had a notification or a calling card, the tracker lets you rearrange delivery for a day that suits you, or have the parcel sent to your local DHL eCommerce UK ServicePoint. Its Help menu has a ServicePoint Finder and a Depot Finder.

**Collecting from the depot.** Rearrange the delivery to a depot on the tracker. The parcel is ready to collect one working day after you rearrange.

**Customs.** For a parcel from abroad with VAT, duty or delivery charges to pay, GOV.UK says the courier contacts you with a bill and normally holds the parcel for about 3 weeks. If you haven't paid by then, it goes back to the sender. More in [parcel held at customs](guide:customs).

Tracking says delivered but there's nothing? See [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).

## DHL parcel UK delayed, lost or damaged

**Delays.** Check "Live service updates" in the tracker's Help menu.

**Not received.** Citizens Advice says that when you buy from a business, it's the seller's responsibility to make sure the item reaches you; if the seller used a courier, it's up to the seller to chase the courier. If no delivery date was agreed, the seller should usually deliver within 30 days.

**Lost or damaged.** Tell the shop quickly. If the parcel wasn't left where you agreed, Citizens Advice says sorting it out is the seller's legal responsibility: you can ask for it to be redelivered and, in some cases, for a full refund.

**Refused.** DHL's message asks you to contact the sender.

As listed on DHL eCommerce UK's contact page:

## Tracking DHL eCommerce UK parcels with Peek

[Peek](/) recognises a 14-digit number but the shape is shared, so it asks the likely carriers at once. A number starting `6012` goes to DHL eCommerce UK first everywhere. For readers in the UK, a number starting 31, 41 or 55 goes to [DPD UK](carrier:dpd-uk) first and DHL eCommerce UK next; elsewhere Peek doesn't ask DHL eCommerce UK about it, so pick it yourself. If a DPD network also knows the number, you pick the carrier with "Change". A link from track.dhlecommerce.co.uk (with `con=`) or track.dhlparcel.co.uk (with `consignmentnumber=`) can be pasted too. A 9-digit return number is tracked once you pick DHL eCommerce UK with "Change"; calling-card numbers and references aren't tracked, because they need a postcode.

While you have the parcel open, or on its delivery day with notifications on, Peek checks DHL's tracker up to every 10 minutes, or every 2 minutes from 6 a.m. UK time on the day DHL scans the parcel out for delivery, while DHL's own tracker answers; otherwise hourly. Through tracking services, it checks hourly, every 3 hours after a day without a new scan, and up to every 15 minutes on the day the parcel is out for delivery, under the same condition. It can notify you when the status changes. After two days without news (12 hours while the parcel still shows "Label created" or "Received by carrier"), Peek checks hourly. It shows the status, the history, the expected delivery day (until the parcel goes out for delivery) and the delivery time, with DHL's English messages as DHL writes them. Times are read on UK time and shown in your device's time zone. It leaves out who signed and the sender's reference. DHL gives no place for its scans, so nothing appears on the map. Shipments drop out of DHL's tracking some time after delivery, and Peek doesn't follow a parcel to another carrier.

## Questions about DHL eCommerce UK tracking

### Is DHL eCommerce UK the same as DHL Parcel UK or Evri Premium?

Yes: DHL eCommerce UK was formerly DHL Parcel UK (and UK Mail). Evri announced in September 2025 that it would rebrand in 2026 as Evri Premium (a separate network of DHL), while its tracker and contact page still use the DHL eCommerce UK name.

### Can DHL eCommerce UK track or help with a DHL Express parcel?

No. DHL eCommerce UK is a separate UK domestic network and explicitly states it cannot track or help with [DHL Express](carrier:dhl-express) shipments — contact DHL Express instead.

### How do I rearrange a missed DHL eCommerce UK delivery or collect from a depot?

Use your shipment number or 7-digit calling-card number with your postcode on `track.dhlecommerce.co.uk` to choose a new delivery day, send the parcel to a local DHL ServicePoint or rearrange to a depot (ready for collection one working day later).

### What is the DHL eCommerce UK customer service phone number?

Call `02476 937770` (Monday–Friday 7am–8pm, Saturday 8am–6pm) or use the "Send us a message" form on the [contact page](https://track.dhlecommerce.co.uk/contact-us?nav=1) (next-working-day reply outside opening hours). For lost or damaged orders, contact the retailer first.

### Is a DHL eCommerce UK text asking for a payment fee real?

Citizens Advice warns that messages asking you to pay to receive or reschedule a parcel are usually scams. Never tap the link — check your number directly on `track.dhlecommerce.co.uk` or Peek and forward scam texts free to `7726`.
