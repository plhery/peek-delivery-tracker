DHL eCommerce UK, formerly DHL Parcel UK and UK Mail, is DHL's domestic parcel network in the United Kingdom: it delivers for online shops and other businesses, with next-day delivery among its services. Track a parcel on [DHL eCommerce UK's tracker](https://track.dhlecommerce.co.uk/) with the shipment number and, ideally, your postcode, or paste the number in the box above. The number is in DHL's notification or on a driver's calling card; without one, the tracker says to contact your sender.

## DHL eCommerce UK tracking number

A DHL eCommerce UK shipment number has 14 digits. Most of those the tracker shows start with `6012`; others start with 31, 41 or 55. No number at all? Ask the sender; see also [how to find your tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number).

| Number (invented) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `60120000000000` | A UK shipment number: 14 digits, here starting `6012`. |
| `1234567` | The 7-digit number on a driver's calling card, for the tracker's "Card number" box. |
| `912345678` | A return shipment's 9-digit number. |

The tracker also takes a customer reference. A calling-card number or a reference only finds the parcel with the delivery postcode.

Fourteen digits are not unique to DHL. [DPD UK](carrier:dpd-uk) uses them too (a number starting `1550` points to DPD UK), and so do SEUR and BRT. DHL eCommerce UK is also separate from [DHL Express](carrier:dhl-express): DHL eCommerce UK says it can't track or help with DHL Express deliveries. In September 2025, Evri announced that DHL eCommerce UK would be rebranded in 2026 as Evri Premium, a network of DHL kept separate from [Evri](carrier:evri). The tracker and its contact page still carry the DHL eCommerce UK name.

## DHL eCommerce UK tracking statuses explained

The tracker is in English and lists the newest message first. These are the messages Peek receives, roughly in order:

| DHL eCommerce UK says | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `Your shipment has been collected` | DHL has picked the parcel up from the sender. |
| `Your shipment is at the delivery depot` | It's at the depot that will deliver it. |
| `We're sorry but the delivery of your shipment has been delayed. We expect to deliver on the next working day` | Running late: DHL expects to deliver the next working day. |
| `Your shipment is out for delivery` | With the driver today. |
| `Your shipment is delivered` | Delivered. The tracker may also show who signed. |
| `Delivery Attempted` | The driver called but couldn't deliver. |
| `Collected` | You collected the parcel yourself. |
| `We're sorry but your shipment was refused on delivery. Please contact your sender for further information` | The parcel was refused at the door; the headline then says it has gone back to the sender. |

Two lines can confuse. `Collected` on its own means you picked the parcel up, while `Your shipment has been collected` at the start means DHL collected it from the sender. And `Your shipment is at the delivery depot` can come back after a delivery or a refusal, when the parcel returns to the depot: the newest line is the one that counts.

## DHL eCommerce UK delivery, ServicePoints and missed deliveries

**Rearranging.** If you've had a notification or a calling card, the tracker lets you rearrange delivery for a day that suits you, or have the parcel sent to your local DHL eCommerce UK ServicePoint. Its Help menu has a ServicePoint Finder and a Depot Finder.

**Collecting from the depot.** Rearrange the delivery to a depot on the tracker. The parcel is ready to collect one working day after you rearrange.

**Customs.** For a parcel from abroad with VAT, duty or delivery charges to pay, GOV.UK says the courier contacts you with a bill and normally holds the parcel for about 3 weeks. If you haven't paid by then, it goes back to the sender. More in [parcel held at customs](guide:customs).

Tracking says delivered but there's nothing? See [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).

## DHL parcel UK delayed, lost or damaged

**Delays.** Check "Live service updates" in the tracker's Help menu.

**Not received.** Citizens Advice says that when you buy from a business, it's the seller's responsibility to make sure the item reaches you; if the seller used a courier, it's up to the seller to chase the courier. If no delivery date was agreed, the seller should usually deliver within 30 days.

**Lost or damaged.** Tell the shop quickly. If the parcel wasn't left where you agreed, Citizens Advice says sorting it out is the seller's legal responsibility: you can ask for it to be redelivered and, in some cases, for a full refund.

**Refused.** DHL's message asks you to contact the sender.

## Tracking DHL eCommerce UK parcels with Peek

[Peek](/) recognises a 14-digit number but the shape is shared, so it asks the likely carriers at once. A number starting `6012` goes to DHL eCommerce UK first, and so does any 14-digit number for readers in the UK. If a DPD network also knows the number, you pick the carrier with "Change". A link from track.dhlecommerce.co.uk (with `con=`) or track.dhlparcel.co.uk (with `consignmentnumber=`) can be pasted too. A 9-digit return number is tracked once you pick DHL eCommerce UK with "Change"; calling-card numbers and references aren't tracked, because they need a postcode.

Peek checks DHL's tracker every 10 minutes by day, every 2 minutes once the parcel is out for delivery, and every hour or two overnight, and can notify you when the status changes. It shows the status, the history, the expected delivery day and the delivery time, with DHL's English messages as DHL writes them. Times are read on UK time and shown in your device's time zone. It leaves out who signed and the sender's reference. DHL gives no place for its scans, so nothing appears on the map. Shipments drop out of DHL's tracking some time after delivery, and Peek doesn't follow a parcel to another carrier.

## Questions about DHL eCommerce UK tracking

### Is DHL eCommerce UK the same as DHL Parcel UK?

Yes. DHL eCommerce UK was formerly known as DHL Parcel UK (and UK Mail); it's the same network under a new name. In 2026 it's due to become Evri Premium, still a network of DHL.

### Can I collect a DHL eCommerce UK parcel from the depot?

Yes. Rearrange the delivery to a depot on DHL's tracker; the parcel is ready to collect one working day later.

### Can DHL eCommerce UK help with a DHL Express parcel?

No. DHL eCommerce UK says it can't track or help with DHL Express deliveries, so contact DHL Express.

### Is a DHL eCommerce UK text legit?

Be careful. Citizens Advice says a text asking you to pay to get a parcel or reschedule its delivery is usually a scam. Check the number on DHL's tracker or in Peek instead of tapping the link, and forward the text to 7726.
