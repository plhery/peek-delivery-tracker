DK Logistics (DKL, the Estonian company DK Logistics OÜ) is an international courier and freight forwarder with its head office in Tallinn. It carries express mail, small parcels and freight to and from Estonia, Latvia and Lithuania, and works with courier and transport companies in Europe and the CIS countries to reach the rest of the world. Track a shipment with its waybill number on dkl.ee, or paste the number in the box above.

## DK Logistics tracking number: what it looks like

DK Logistics publishes no number format, and no public page shows a whole DKL number. Its tracking page, "TRACK&TRACE" in English and "Jälgimine" in Estonian, asks "Check your HAWB" and lets you search in one of two ways:

| What you enter | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Waybill number (Estonian: *saatelehe number*) | The number of DKL's waybill for the shipment, its house air waybill (HAWB). The page picks this option by default. |
| Reference (*viide*) | The shipment's reference, the other option the page offers. |

Pick the option that matches what you have before you search.

**Where to find it.** If you sent the shipment with DKL, the number is on your waybill. If a company or a person sent it to you, ask them for the waybill number: DKL's pages don't say that it sends the number to the recipient. If you can't find any number, see [how to find your tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number).

## What DK Logistics carries and how it delivers

As read on dkl.ee, DKL offers:

- **Express mail and small parcels** to any country in 1 to 3 working days, and to the main European cities on the next working day, door to door.
- **Air freight**, export and import, between Estonia, Latvia or Lithuania and any country in the world. DKL has its own representatives in Latvia and Lithuania.
- **Road freight**, full and part loads, plus multimodal and sea freight.
- **Special cargo**: dangerous goods (the site lists the codes DGR, ADR, HUM and AVI), and frozen and chilled goods.
- **Other services**: guarded escort within Estonia, gifts and flowers, warehousing and customs warehousing.

It also lists a free courier call, insurance if the customer asks for it, pickup anywhere in the world with the customer's account number, and payment by the recipient if asked for in advance. Senders book a pickup with the courier order form on dkl.ee. Orders are taken on weekdays until 16:00; orders placed later go out on the next working day.

After delivery, DKL emails the sender a proof of delivery (POD) with the recipient's surname and the date and time of delivery.

DKL doesn't publish how many delivery attempts it makes, whether it leaves a notice card, or how long it holds a parcel it couldn't deliver. Its site mentions no parcel lockers or pickup points. Abroad, it works with partner courier and transport companies that it doesn't name, so the driver who hands over your parcel may not be from DKL.

## Customs: shipments from outside the EU

DKL brings goods by air from any country in the world to Estonia, Latvia and Lithuania. It offers customs broker services (customs declarations and shipping documents) and customs warehousing. A shipment from outside the EU goes through customs before it is delivered.

Since 1 July 2026, goods from outside the EU worth up to €150 pay an EU customs duty of €3 per item. Since 1 November 2026, a Union handling fee of €2 per item comes on top, whatever the value. The Estonian Tax and Customs Board (EMTA) says that a gift from one private person to another doesn't pay the handling fee.

DKL says it has no fixed price list for import deliveries, and it doesn't say how it passes duties and fees on to the recipient. If you expect a shipment from outside the EU, ask DKL or the sender who clears it and who pays. More in [parcels held at customs](guide:customs).

## DK Logistics shipment late, stuck or lost

**No update.** Check DKL's own tracking first, with the right option (waybill number or reference). Tracking services may know less, or nothing, about a DKL shipment. If the waybill shows no progress, call customer service on a weekday. More in [why tracking stops updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

**Delivered but not there.** The sender gets a proof of delivery by email with the recipient's surname, date and time. Ask the sender for it: it shows who took the parcel. Then see [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).

**Lost or damaged.** DKL publishes no claims procedure or deadline on its site. It insures goods for the whole delivery when the customer asks for it. Tell customer service as soon as you can. If a shop sent you the parcel, ask the shop for a refund or a new parcel: see [lost parcels](guide:lost-parcel) and [damaged parcels](guide:damaged-parcel).

## Track a DK Logistics shipment with Peek

[Peek](/) can't tell a DK Logistics shipment by its number alone: DKL publishes no number shape, and Peek's catalog links none to it, so DKL never shows under "Fits this number". Paste the number all the same: Peek follows more than 3,500 carriers. Then pick DK Logistics with "Change" (on a parcel you've already added, "Change carrier"). Typing "DKL" or "DK Logistics OÜ" finds it too, and so does "Estonia". A number of six digits alone first shows "That looks like a pickup code": once you pick DK Logistics, Peek takes it as the tracking number. A link to dkl.ee doesn't help: the tracking form there gives no link with the number in it, so paste the number itself.

If you leave the carrier open, Peek looks for it through tracking services. When the number looks like other carriers' numbers, Peek asks the services while you add it ("Checking tracking services…"), and if none knows it yet, the carrier list says "Not found by tracking services yet. Peek will keep checking after you add it." A number that looks like no other carrier's isn't checked while you add it: the carrier list says "Peek will look it up after you add it." When the services name DK Logistics or DKL as the carrier and show the parcel moving, Peek files it under DK Logistics.

DK Logistics has no tracker for Peek to read. So for a parcel filed under DK Logistics, Peek asks ParcelsApp, Ship24 and 17TRACK in turn (Ship24 first for a ten-digit number) and keeps the first one that has the parcel. It shows the status and history that service holds, in its wording. That history can lag behind DKL's own, and for some shipments there's nothing at all. Once a service has the parcel, its tracking link opens that service's page for the number; until then, the parcel has no tracking link. Peek doesn't pass your IP address or country on to the services.

Peek checks every 15 minutes, out for delivery too; a service that didn't know the number is asked again a day later. It can notify you of every scan, important steps only or "Delivery only". It can't contact DK Logistics or the sender for you.

## Questions about DK Logistics tracking

### What is DK Logistics?

DK Logistics OÜ (DKL) is a courier and freight forwarder based in Tallinn, Estonia. It carries express mail, small parcels and air, road and sea freight to and from Estonia, Latvia and Lithuania, with partner companies in Europe and the CIS countries.

### Where do I track a DK Logistics shipment?

On DKL's tracking page at dkl.ee: choose "Waybill number" or "Reference" and enter it. You can also paste the number into Peek and pick DK Logistics as the carrier.

### Which number do I need to track DKL?

The waybill number (HAWB) of the shipment, or its reference. DKL doesn't publish what its numbers look like. If you didn't send the shipment yourself, ask the sender for the waybill number.

### Does DK Logistics deliver on Saturdays?

DKL's office is open Monday to Friday, 09:00–17:00, and closed at weekends and on public holidays. It doesn't publish weekend delivery.

### How do I contact DK Logistics?

Call customer service on +372 640 4014 or write to info@dkl.ee, Monday to Friday, 09:00–17:00. The office is at Kesk-Sõjamäe 2 in Tallinn.
