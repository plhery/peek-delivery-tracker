**DPD Hungary** (*DPD Hungary Kft.*, part of Geopost's DPD network) delivers parcels in Hungary and sends them abroad ([dpd.com/hu/en/](https://www.dpd.com/hu/en/)). It offers domestic and international shipping, cash on delivery, Pickup parcelshops, the DPD PickApp for sending parcels and, with **FOXPOST**, delivery to FOXPOST parcel lockers; its business form lists parcels up to 31.50 kg. You can track a parcel on [dpd.com/hu/hu/](https://www.dpd.com/hu/hu/) or paste your 14-digit DPD parcel number into the box above.

## DPD Hungary tracking numbers (14-digit parcel format)

DPD Hungary parcel numbers are **14 digits** long; as noted on [dpd.com/hu/en/contact/](https://www.dpd.com/hu/en/contact/), Hungarian-origin DPD parcel numbers typically begin with **`1638`** or **`1640`**:

| Format (invented) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `16384829173605` | 14-digit Hungarian-origin DPD parcel number starting with `1638`. |
| `16407391846205` | 14-digit Hungarian-origin DPD parcel number starting with `1640`. |

Other DPD companies use 14-digit parcel numbers too, so a bare number can belong to any of them (see below). See [tracking number formats](guide:tracking-number-formats).

## What DPD Hungary tracking statuses mean

DPD Hungary uses the unified myDPD status vocabulary covering handover, depot sorting, local delivery rounds, Pickup ParcelShop collection and missed attempts:

| myDPD code & wording | What it means for you | Peek shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `PARCEL_HANDED` (`Parcel handed to DPD`) | Physical parcel received from the sender into DPD's network. | "In transit" |
| `IN_TRANSIT` (`Your parcel is on its way`), `AT_DELIVERY_CENTER` (`At delivery center`) | Moving between DPD hubs and arrived at your regional Hungarian delivery depot. | "In transit" |
| `PARCEL_OUT_FOR_DELIVERY` (`Parcel out for delivery`) | Loaded onto the local DPD courier's van for doorstep delivery today. | "Out for delivery" |
| `AVAILABLE_FOR_COLLECTION` (`Ready for collection`) | Ready to pick up at a DPD Pickup ParcelShop or parcel locker. | "Ready for pickup" |
| `UNSUCCESSFUL_DELIVERY_ATTEMPT` (`Unsuccessful delivery attempt`) | Delivery failed; the driver tries again the next day, and after a second failed attempt the parcel waits five working days at the depot named in the notification. | "Delivery attempted" |
| `DELIVERED` (`Delivered`) | Delivered to the recipient or collected from the Pickup point/locker. | "Delivered" |

## Rescheduling, FOXPOST lockers and cross-border EU delivery

According to [dpd.com/hu/hu/](https://www.dpd.com/hu/hu/) and [dpd.com/hu/en/contact/](https://www.dpd.com/hu/en/contact/):

- **Changing the address or day:** after one unsuccessful delivery, the driver tries again the next day. You can change the address or the date through the link in DPD's notification or in the myDPD app, and choose a pickup point or a parcel locker there. If the second attempt fails too, you can collect the parcel within five working days at the depot named in the notification.
- **FOXPOST parcel locker partnership:** thanks to DPD Hungary's partnership with **FOXPOST**, DPD parcels can be delivered to FOXPOST parcel lockers across Hungary. For parcels directed to a FOXPOST locker, **FOXPOST handles recipient customer service** on `+36 1 999 0 369` (Mon/Tue/Wed/Fri 08:00–18:00, Thu 08:00–20:00).
- **Parcels from outside the EU:** see [parcels held at customs](guide:customs).

## DPD Hungary parcel delayed, missed or damaged

- **Missing or delayed parcel:** contact your online seller first if a shipment stalls between depots, or submit a domestic/international parcel inquiry on [dpd.com/hu/hu/kapcsolat/](https://www.dpd.com/hu/hu/kapcsolat/) / [dpd.com/hu/en/contact/](https://www.dpd.com/hu/en/contact/) using your 14-digit parcel number. See [why tracking stops updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).
- **Damage or loss claims:** DPD Hungary's contact page includes a dedicated online claim form requesting the 14-digit parcel number, item contents, invoice value, supporting documents and bank details — always notify the sender first too. See [damaged parcel](guide:damaged-parcel), [lost parcel](guide:lost-parcel), [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received) and [returned to sender](guide:returned-to-sender).

## DPD Hungary customer service and contacts

Official contact details published on [DPD Hungary Contact (English)](https://www.dpd.com/hu/en/contact/) and [DPD Hungary Kapcsolat](https://www.dpd.com/hu/hu/kapcsolat/):

- **Customer service phone:** **`+36 1 501 6200`** (Monday `07:00–19:00`, Tuesday–Friday `08:00–16:30`).
- **Online web forms (replacing direct email):** [dpd.com/hu/en/contact/](https://www.dpd.com/hu/en/contact/) and [dpd.com/hu/hu/kapcsolat/](https://www.dpd.com/hu/hu/kapcsolat/) (written inquiries & Facebook chat answered weekdays `08:00–16:30`; in-person support Monday–Friday `08:00–16:30`).
- **FOXPOST locker deliveries support:** `+36 1 999 0 369` (Mon/Tue/Wed/Fri `08:00–18:00`, Thu `08:00–20:00`).

> Beware of fake text messages asking you to pay a small online fee to reschedule a DPD Hungary parcel. Always verify delivery changes directly through [dpd.com/hu/hu/](https://www.dpd.com/hu/hu/) or call `+36 1 501 6200`. See [delivery scam texts](guide:delivery-scam-texts).

## Track a DPD Hungary parcel with Peek

A 14-digit number (`16384829173605` or `16407391846205`, invented) fits several DPD companies and other carriers. In Hungary, [Peek](/) asks DPD Hungary first; elsewhere it asks other DPD companies, Chronopost, SEUR and BRT first. It shows "DPD Hungary has this parcel." once DPD Hungary confirms it. Peek also recognises pasted `dpdgroup.com/hu/...?parcelNumber=` and `dpd.com/hu/...?parcelNumber=` links as DPD Hungary's. To choose DPD Hungary yourself, tap "Change" while adding or "Change carrier" on the parcel screen.

Peek reads DPD Hungary's guest tracking, which needs no postcode, and shows the stage ("In transit", "Out for delivery", "Ready for pickup", "Delivery attempted", "Delivered", or another stage when DPD reports one), the estimated delivery day when DPD gives one, the delivery time, and the parcelshop's name while the parcel waits there. Without a postcode, DPD gives each step once, with its newest date and no place, so the scans between steps are missing. DPD's times carry an offset or a time zone, else Peek dates them on Budapest time, and Peek shows them on your device's clock. When DPD places the parcel at a delivery depot, or out for delivery, in a country whose DPD company Peek follows (such as [DPD Germany](carrier:dpd-de), [DPD Austria](carrier:dpd-at), [DPD Poland](carrier:dpd-pl), SEUR in Spain or BRT in Italy), Peek checks that it has the parcel: the parcel then shows "Delivery with" and the carrier's name, and lists both numbers. Peek doesn't show DPD's status codes.

While you have the parcel open in Peek, or on its delivery day with notifications on, Peek checks up to every 10 minutes; on the day DPD shows `Parcel out for delivery`, every 2 minutes from 6 a.m. on the newest scan's clock, while DPD Hungary's tracking answers. Otherwise Peek checks hourly. After 12 hours with only "Label created" or "Received by carrier", or two days without news, it checks hourly however you follow it, and every 6 hours after seven days at "Label created". When DPD Hungary's tracking doesn't answer and Peek follows the parcel through tracking services alone, it checks hourly, every 3 hours after a quiet day, and up to every 15 minutes on the out-for-delivery day.

When the newest scan is four days old and the parcel is still at "Label created", "Received by carrier" or "In transit", Peek flags it with that scan's day, such as "No tracking update since Mon 12 Oct". The parcel's page then says "Parcels often go quiet on long flights, at customs or over a weekend. Peek keeps asking DPD Hungary.", with a "Get help from DPD Hungary" link to DPD Hungary's contact page. After three weeks with no new scan, it says "Nothing new for over three weeks. If it still hasn’t arrived, ask the seller. Peek keeps asking DPD Hungary."

You can pick alert presets for "Every scan", "Important steps" or "Delivery only". Peek monitors tracking updates only and cannot redirect parcels to a ParcelShop or FOXPOST locker or contact DPD Hungary for you.

## Questions about DPD Hungary tracking

### What does a DPD Hungary parcel number look like?

DPD Hungary parcel numbers are 14 digits long — parcels dispatched in Hungary typically start with `1638` or `1640` (for example `16384829173605`, invented).

### Who handles DPD Hungary parcels delivered to a FOXPOST locker?

Per [dpd.com/hu/hu/](https://www.dpd.com/hu/hu/), FOXPOST manages customer support and tracking assistance for DPD parcels sent to FOXPOST parcel lockers (phone `+36 1 999 0 369`, Mon/Tue/Wed/Fri 08:00–18:00, Thu 08:00–20:00).

### What are DPD Hungary's customer service phone hours?

DPD Hungary's phone line `+36 1 501 6200` operates **Monday 07:00–19:00** and **Tuesday to Friday 08:00–16:30**.

### Does Peek need my postcode to track a DPD Hungary parcel?

No. Peek reads DPD Hungary's guest tracking with your 14-digit parcel number alone. Without a postcode, DPD gives each step once and without its place.
