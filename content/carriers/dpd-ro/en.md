DPD Romania (Dynamic Parcel Distribution S.A.) is the Geopost group's courier in Romania, active there since 2008, with its head office in Mogoșoaia, Ilfov. Each parcel gets its own waybill number: track it on DPD's tracker, tracking.dpd.ro, which has an English version, through the Smart Control link in DPD's delivery SMS, or in the box above.

## DPD Romania tracking number: where to find it

DPD calls it the waybill number, or AWB. DPD's pages give no length rule; the waybills Peek knows as DPD Romania's are 11 digits starting with 8:

| Number (invented) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `80000000000` | 11 digits starting with 8: a DPD Romania waybill. The shape also fits [GLS Germany](carrier:gls-de), [GLS France](carrier:gls-fr), [GLS Switzerland](carrier:gls-ch), [GLS Austria](carrier:gls-at), [PPL](carrier:ppl) and others. |
| `01200000000000` | 14 digits: the shape of parcel numbers from other DPD companies, such as [DPD Germany](carrier:dpd-de), [DPD UK](carrier:dpd-uk), [DPD France](carrier:dpd-fr) or [DPD Portugal](carrier:dpd-pt). Peek tracks DPD Romania by its 11-digit waybill only. |

Look in the shop's shipping email or on your order page. DPD's mobile app has a recipient dashboard; you sign in with a code sent by SMS or email. If the shop names [Cargus](carrier:cargus), [FAN Courier](carrier:fan-courier) or [Sameday](carrier:sameday) instead, the number is theirs. No number at all? See [how to find your tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number).

## What DPD Romania's tracking statuses mean

DPD's tracker answers in Romanian or English; its Romanian scans come in an older, short wording or a newer sentence. In the order a parcel usually meets them:

| DPD says | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `Shipment data received`, `Receptionare informatii colet`, `Expedierea ta a fost înregistrată de către expeditor.` | The sender has created the waybill. DPD has the details, not yet the parcel. |
| `Expedierea nu a putut fi preluată de către curierul DPD.` | The courier couldn't collect it from the sender; a reason follows. |
| `Courier Pick-up`, `Expedierea ta a fost preluată de la expeditor de către curierul DPD.` | A DPD courier collected it from the sender. |
| `Received in Office`, `Receptie la sediu` | A DPD office took it in, from a courier or at its counter. |
| `Departure Scan`, `Arrival Scan`, `Intrare in depozit`, `Expedierea ta a fost recepționată în depozitul DPD.`, `Expedierea ta a fost procesată în depozitul DPD.` | Leaving, reaching or processed at a DPD depot. |
| `Expedierea ta se află în tranzit spre depozitul de destinație.` | On its way to the depot that delivers it. |
| `Expedierea ta a ajuns în depozitul de destinație.` | At the depot that delivers it: delivery comes next. |
| `Predict`, `Predict – s-au trimis informațiile cu privire la intervalul orar de livrare.` | DPD has sent you the delivery time window. A message, not a scan. |
| `Out for Delivery`, `Iesire in livrare`, `Expedierea ta a fost preluată spre livrare de către curierul DPD.` | With the courier for delivery. |
| `Livrare nereusita`, `Expedierea ta nu a putut fi livrată.` | Not delivered. The reason follows, such as a wrong address or a refusal. |
| `Expedierea ta a fost returnată în depozitul DPD.` | Back at the depot after a failed delivery round, not a return. |
| `Delivered`, `Livrat` | Delivered. The tracker adds the name of the person who took it. |
| `Retur catre expeditor` | Going back to the sender, under a return waybill given in the note. |
| `Retur livrat catre expeditor` | The sender has it back. |
| `Canceled`, `Expedierea ta a fost anulată.` | The waybill was cancelled: ask the shop. |

`Shipment data received` often shows the expected delivery date. Scans at DPD depots abroad name the depot and its country.

## DPD delivery in Romania: Smart Control, offices and lockers

**When.** DPD Standard delivers anywhere in Romania within 1 to 2 working days. You pay any cash-on-delivery amount to the courier, in cash or, in urban areas, by card.

**Smart Control.** Before delivery, DPD sends an SMS with the delivery date and a Smart Control link. It's free, and from it you can confirm or change the address, see where your parcel is, send it to a DPD warehouse, office, locker or DPD Shop, or postpone delivery to the next working day. Redirection is free within the same city. Delivery is contactless: the electronic signature is no longer required. DPD may check who you are with your ID and a PIN sent by SMS or email.

**Missed delivery.** DPD tries twice, on consecutive working days. After the second try it leaves a notice and keeps the parcel free of charge at a staffed DPD point for 5 calendar days, during which you can agree a last delivery. Express and Livrare specială parcels get one try, then go back to the sender. DPD doesn't deliver to letterboxes. If you refuse a parcel, or refuse to pay its cash-on-delivery amount, DPD starts the return to the sender that day, without holding it for you.

**Offices, lockers and Parcel Shops.** Collection periods, as DPD gives them:

- DPD office: 7 calendar days; pay in cash, by card or Pay by Link.
- DPD locker: 3 calendar days (72 hours from the notice, in DPD's terms); outdoor lockers open 24/7; card or Pay by Link.
- DPD Parcel Shop: 5 calendar days; Pay by Link or card (no card at Pay Point locations).

After the collection period, the parcel goes back to the sender within 5 working days (10 for an international parcel).

## DPD Romania parcel late, lost, damaged or at customs

For a refund or a new parcel, ask the shop: it holds the contract with DPD.

**No update.** If the status stays the same for a long time, or the parcel misses its time, DPD asks you to contact client@dpd.ro or 031 824 90 90. See [tracking that isn't updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

**Delivered but not received.** DPD's tracker names the person who took the parcel: ask them first, then contact DPD and the shop. More in [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).

**Returned.** `Retur catre expeditor` means the parcel is going back. Ask the shop to send it again. See [returned to sender](guide:returned-to-sender).

**Complaints.** The sender and the recipient can both complain within 6 months of posting, on DPD's form: by email to reclamatii@dpd.ro, online, at a staffed DPD point or by registered post. DPD settles within 3 months and pays within 15 days of settling. For a lost or damaged parcel sent without a declared value or cash on delivery, DPD pays 5 times the service price. Within 1 year of posting, you can also go to ANCOM, Romania's communications regulator, or to court. See [lost parcels](guide:lost-parcel).

**Customs.** Goods bought online from outside the EU carry a €3 EU customs duty per item on parcels worth up to €150, since 1 July 2026, and a €2 Union handling fee per item, whatever the value, since 1 November 2026. DPD Romania's pages don't say how it passes these on: ask the shop whether your price included them. Its notice of 22 December 2025 also explains Romania's fixed 25 RON fee on each parcel from outside the EU declared under €150, introduced on 1 January 2026: the seller, sender or marketplace pays it, and DPD collects it from them. See [parcels held at customs](guide:customs).

## DPD Romania contact and customer service

Peek can't contact DPD or the shop for you. As read on 1 November 2026, on DPD Romania's English contact page:

- **Parcels within Romania:** 031 824 90 90, Monday to Friday, 8:30 am to 4:30 pm. Requests to client@dpd.ro.
- **International parcels:** 031 824 90 32 or international@dpd.ro, same hours.
- **Complaints:** reclamatii@dpd.ro or the complaint form.
- **Post:** Dynamic Parcel Distribution S.A., Str. Buiacului nr. 2, LogIQ Logistic Park, Corp A (A1-A2), 077135 Mogoșoaia, Ilfov.

DPD doesn't say whether its staff speak English.

> DPD Romania warns of fake SMS and emails in its name, with links to sites copying DPD's that ask for a payment or your card details. DPD Romania never asks for bank details by SMS or email. Don't open the link, report the message as spam and tell DPD. See [delivery scam texts](guide:delivery-scam-texts).

## Tracking a DPD Romania parcel with Peek

Paste the 11-digit waybill in [Peek](/). Other carriers share that shape, so Peek asks the likely ones, by your country (your iPhone's region, or on the web where you connect from). In Romania it asks DPD Romania first, and you'll see "DPD Romania has this parcel." when DPD knows the number. Elsewhere it asks other carriers: if you see "not found yet · Peek keeps checking", press "Change" and pick DPD Romania, or use "Change carrier" on a parcel already added. Paste it all the same: Peek follows more than 3,500 carriers. A tracking.dpd.ro link with `shipmentNumber=` works too.

Peek asks DPD for its English tracking and shows the status, the history in DPD's words with each scan's town (and country), the expected delivery date while the parcel is on its way, and the delivery time. It leaves out who received it, the signature and the return waybill; DPD gives no weight or sender.

When DPD hands a parcel to [ACS Courier](carrier:acs-courier) for the last leg, to Cyprus for example, it names ACS and the ACS number. If that number has ten digits, as ACS's vouchers do, Peek asks ACS with it and follows ACS once it has a scan as recent as DPD's latest. Until then, or for a number of another shape, Peek follows DPD, and the parcel's card already names ACS and shows its number.

Peek checks every 10 minutes, or every 2 minutes from 6 a.m. local time on the day DPD scans the parcel out for delivery, while DPD's own tracker answers (through tracking services, every 15 minutes). It notifies you as you choose: "Every scan", "Important steps" or "Delivery only". After two days without news (12 hours while the parcel still shows "Label created" or "Received by carrier"), Peek checks hourly. To change the delivery, use Smart Control.

## Questions about DPD tracking in Romania

### How long does DPD take to deliver in Romania?

1 to 2 working days with DPD Standard. DPD's terms allow up to 7 calendar days for places with fixed visit days.

### How long does DPD keep a parcel at an office or locker?

7 calendar days at an office, 5 at a Parcel Shop, 3 in a locker, and 5 at a DPD point after two missed deliveries. Then it goes back to the sender.

### Can I track a DPD Romania parcel in English?

Yes. DPD's tracker has an English version, and Peek shows DPD's English wordings.

### What is the DPD Romania phone number?

031 824 90 90 for parcels within Romania and 031 824 90 32 for international ones, Monday to Friday, 8:30 am to 4:30 pm.
