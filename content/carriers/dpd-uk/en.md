DPD UK is a British parcel company that delivers for many online shops. Track a parcel on [DPD's tracking page](https://track.dpd.co.uk/) with the parcel number and your postcode, or paste the number in the box above. The number comes from the shop, from DPD's SMS or email, or from the "sorry we missed you" card.

## Where is my DPD tracking number?

The shop that sent the parcel has the number. You can also tap the link in DPD's email or SMS. If the driver left a "sorry we missed you" card, the number is beside the barcode, and DPD's tracking page accepts the calling card number too. Lost it? See [how to find your tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number).

DPD's own pages don't describe the number's format. The parcel numbers seen on DPD UK's tracking have 14 digits, and many start `1550` or `1597`; DPD's page may print a check character after them.

| Number (invented) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `15501234567890` | A DPD UK parcel number: 14 digits starting `1550` (or `1597`). |
| `15501234567890Q` | The same number with DPD's check character on the end. |
| `19999999999980` | Any other 14 digits: could be DPD UK, another DPD country or another carrier. |

Other DPD countries also use 14 digits, and so do [DHL eCommerce UK](carrier:dhl-ecommerce-uk), SEUR and BRT, so the digits alone don't prove who has your parcel. DPD's messages do: its SMS now comes from "DPD UK" (or "DPD Local" or "DPDgroup UK"), with the shop's name in the text. DPD Local is DPD's sister brand. In Ireland, parcels go with [DPD Ireland](carrier:dpd-ie), which has its own numbers.

## DPD tracking statuses explained

DPD UK's tracking is in English only. These are the lines most parcels show, in order:

| DPD says | What it means for you | Peek shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `We've received your order details, but have not yet received your parcel` | The shop has booked the delivery. DPD doesn't have the parcel yet. | "Label created" |
| `We've received your order details, and we're expecting your parcel shortly` | The label is booked, and DPD expects the sender to hand the parcel in at a DPD Pickup Shop. | "Label created" |
| `Your parcel has been dropped off at a DPD Pickup Shop` | The parcel was handed in at a DPD Pickup Shop. | "Received by carrier" |
| `We have your parcel and it's on its way to our depot`, `We have your parcel and it's on its way to you` | DPD has collected it and is taking it to a depot, or on towards you. | "In transit" |
| `Your parcel has arrived at our depot`, `Your parcel is at our depot` | At a DPD depot, being sorted. | "In transit" |
| `Your parcel is at our depot ready for delivery` | At the depot, ready for its delivery round. Later the same line reads `Your parcel is at our depot`. | "In transit" |
| `A notification has been sent to advise you when the delivery will take place` | DPD has sent you its SMS or email with the delivery day or one-hour window. | The status before it stays |
| `As requested, your parcel will now be delivered on Thu 05 November 2026 at 11:00` | You chose another delivery day, sometimes with an hour. The day and hour vary. | The status before it stays |
| `Your parcel will be with you today`, `Your parcel will be with you today between 14:18 and 15:18` | Out for delivery today, with the hour DPD expects to come when it gives one. The hours vary. | "Out for delivery" |
| `As requested, your parcel will now be left in your selected safe place` | You (or your DPD app settings) chose a safe place, and the driver will leave it there. | The status before it stays |
| `We missed you. Your driver won't be back today but we'll be back Wed 28 October with your parcel` | The driver couldn't deliver and comes back on the day DPD names; the day varies. DPD's line also gives the driver's name, which Peek leaves out. | "Delivery attempted" |
| `Your parcel has been delivered` | Delivered. The headline adds the time and the day. | "Delivered" |
| `Your parcel is waiting for you at home (left in safe place)` | Delivered and left in your safe place. | "Delivered" |
| `Your delivery has been cancelled` | The sender cancelled the delivery. DPD asks you to contact the shop it names. | "Needs attention" |

`We have X-rayed your parcel as part of our normal security procedures` is a routine scan: nothing to do ("In transit" in Peek). On a parcel DPD UK sends abroad, `Your parcel has left our sortation facility and is on it's onwards journey` (DPD's spelling) means it has left the UK, and the history then carries the delivering partner's lines: `The parcel is in transit to its final destination`, `The parcel is undergoing customs clearance` and `Duties and Taxes paid` ("At customs" in Peek), `The parcel has now cleared customs`, `The parcel has arrived at the delivery depot`, `The parcel is on the vehicle for delivery` ("Out for delivery") and `The parcel has been delivered` ("Delivered"). The lines without a step in brackets show as "In transit".

## DPD delivery options, Pickup shops and missed deliveries

**Predict.** DPD sends a one-hour delivery window by SMS and email. It arrives on the day once the driver has loaded the van, usually by 12 noon. A live map then narrows it to a 15-minute slot. You can't change the slot itself.

**Changing the delivery.** The night before and on the day, the SMS, email or DPD app lets you pick another day, a neighbour, a safe place or a DPD Pickup Shop, or upgrade to an earlier, afternoon or weekend delivery. If the sender asked for a signature, some of these options aren't offered. To send it to [a different address](guide:change-delivery-address), contact the shop.

**Not in.** DPD automatically tries a neighbour. Its terms define one as someone living or working within 50 metres' walk. You can name a preferred neighbour or a safe place in advance. Otherwise the driver leaves a card, and you can choose a new date, a safe place, a neighbour or a Pickup Shop with your reference number and postcode. DPD's pages don't say how many attempts it makes. If you were in and the driver didn't come, DPD's tracking page shows where the driver left the "sorry we missed you" card and lets you choose a redelivery or collection; the driver can't come back the same day. Tracking says delivered but you can't find it? Check your safe place and your neighbours first, since DPD tries a neighbour automatically, then see [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).

**Pickup shops.** DPD has more than 6,000 Pickup shops, including Sainsbury's, Post Office, Co-op, Morrisons, Argos and Matalan branches. A parcel you redirect there is usually ready the next day. Bring two pieces of ID: photo ID and something with your address, such as a utility bill. DPD's tracking page shows the date by which you must pick it up; DPD doesn't publish a set number of days. The DPD app also offers delivery to a locker.

## DPD parcel not delivered, delayed or damaged

**Delays.** DPD's delivery times are estimates. When there's a delay in your area, the tracking page can show it with a new estimated date, and DPD's [Service Disruption page](https://www.dpd.co.uk/content/products_services/uk_issues.jsp) checks your postcode. If nothing moves for days, read [why tracking stops updating](guide:tracking-not-updating) and ask the shop.

**Lost or damaged.** The shop holds the contract with DPD, so it makes the claim. Under DPD's terms the sender must report loss or damage within 14 days of despatch and claim within a year. Keep the packaging and take photos. For a lost parcel, DPD may ask you to sign a letter confirming it never arrived.

**Not delivered.** DPD may return a parcel it can't deliver after at least 5 calendar days.

**Import charges.** Parcels from outside the UK can carry VAT or duty, billed by the delivery company. GOV.UK says that company normally holds the parcel for about 3 weeks, then returns it to the sender if unpaid.

> DPD never asks you to pay a "redelivery fee" through a link in an email, text or iMessage. A message from an email address or a random number instead of "DPD" is a scam. Forward suspicious texts to 7726. DPD says its automatic alerts come by SMS, not iMessage, and its links only use its own sites, dpd.co.uk, dpdlocal.co.uk and dpdgroup.co.uk: look-alike spellings are scams. Report suspicious emails to report@phishing.gov.uk.

## Tracking DPD UK parcels with Peek

[Peek](/) recognises a 14-digit DPD number, but the shape is shared, so Peek asks the carriers that use it whether they know the number: DPD UK first when the number starts `1550` or `1597`, otherwise the carriers serving your country first. When DPD UK knows it, the carrier line says DPD UK "has this parcel" and Peek follows it there. If the line says "not found yet · Peek keeps checking" or names another carrier, press "Change" and pick "DPD UK". A track.dpd.co.uk or tracking.dpd.co.uk link with `reference=` or `parcelNumber=` names DPD UK straight away. Order and collection references that need a postcode aren't tracked.

DPD UK's scans carry no time zone: Peek reads them on UK time and lists DPD's own history. Statuses keep DPD's English wording, and a cancelled delivery shows as "Needs attention". Peek can notify you when the status changes.

DPD gives no place for its scans, and Peek doesn't read DPD's expected day or Predict window. It drops your details, references and delivery photos. Peek shows the shop that booked the delivery as "From …", as DPD's page names it, except on a parcel its sender handed in at a Pickup Shop: the account that booked that label didn't send it (on a return label, it's the shop the parcel goes back to). DPD reuses numbers after some months, so an old number can show a newer parcel.

Peek checks every 10 minutes, or every 2 minutes from 6 a.m. UK time on the day DPD says the parcel is out for delivery, while DPD UK's own tracker answers (through tracking services, every 15 minutes). After two days without news (12 hours while the parcel still shows "Label created" or "Received by carrier"), Peek checks hourly. Its alerts can cover every scan, important steps only (delivery, pickup, customs, missed attempts and returns) or delivery day only. It can't change the delivery for you: use DPD's tracking page or app.

## Questions about DPD UK tracking

### Is DPD part of Royal Mail?

No. DPD UK is run by DPDgroup UK Ltd and belongs to Geopost, which is part of the French [La Poste](carrier:la-poste) group.

### How long does DPD take to deliver in the UK?

It depends on the service the shop chose. DPD Next Day aims for the next working day; the Channel Islands, Isle of Man and Scilly take two days as standard.

### Where is my DPD driver?

On the delivery day, DPD's Predict SMS or email gives the driver's name and your place in their queue, and a live map follows the delivery.

### How long does DPD hold parcels at a Pickup shop?

DPD UK doesn't publish a set period. The tracking page shows the date by which you must pick the parcel up.
