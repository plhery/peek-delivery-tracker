**E24** (*Metro E24*, *Metro E24 Solutions*) is listed in Peek as a Canadian delivery carrier alongside **Metro Supply Chain** ([metroscg.com](https://www.metroscg.com/), whose website doesn't use the name E24). Based in Montreal, Metro Supply Chain operates 190 sites across 6 countries with 22.5 million square feet of warehousing and completes 7 million last-mile deliveries a year in North America, including White Glove, Last-Mile and Time Critical Logistics. Because Peek has no fixed number pattern for E24, paste the retailer's tracking reference above and choose **E24** manually.

## E24 (Metro Supply Chain) tracking numbers

Peek has no number pattern or tracking link for E24:

- **Where to find your number:** use the tracking reference in your retailer's shipping confirmation or order page.
- **Choosing E24:** Peek never picks E24 from the number's shape, so enter the reference and select **E24** yourself. See [tracking number formats](guide:tracking-number-formats).

## Metro Supply Chain's services

According to [Metro Supply Chain](https://www.metroscg.com/):

- **Fulfillment:** Metro Supply Chain lists warehousing, e-commerce, direct-to-consumer and cross-border fulfillment and contract packaging among its services.
- **Delivery:** its transportation services include White Glove Delivery, Last-Mile Delivery and Time Critical Logistics.
- **Returns:** Metro Supply Chain lists Reverse Logistics among its services.

## What E24 tracking stages mean

E24 publishes no list of its tracking statuses. Peek follows E24 through tracking services, so the scans carry their wording, and Peek gives each scan the stage its wording says.

## E24 parcel delayed, missed or missing

For delivery support, Metro Supply Chain's FAQ says to use its [contact page](https://www.metroscg.com/contact-us/) and choose "find my package" from the drop-down. For a refund or a damaged item, contact the retailer you ordered from.

- **Tracking not updating:** see [why tracking stops updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).
- **Marked delivered or damaged:** check around porches, garages, building concierges and neighbours first, review any delivery confirmation sent by your retailer, and report missing or damaged goods to the store promptly. See [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received), [damaged parcel](guide:damaged-parcel), [lost parcel](guide:lost-parcel) and [returned to sender](guide:returned-to-sender).

## Track an E24 parcel with Peek

Peek has no number pattern or link rule for E24, so enter your reference in [Peek](/), tap "Change" while adding (or "Change carrier" on the parcel screen) and select **E24** (searching "Metro E24", "Metro E24 Solutions", "e24 Metro" or "Metro Supply Chain" finds it too).

Peek has no direct E24 lookup and follows E24 shipments through tracking services, showing the stage ("Label created", "Received by carrier", "In transit", "Out for delivery", "Delivery attempted", "Needs attention", "Returned to sender", "Delivered") and the scans whenever they have updates for your reference.

While Peek follows the parcel through tracking services, it checks hourly, every 3 hours once a day passes without a new scan, and up to every 15 minutes on the day the parcel is out for delivery, while you have it open or notifications are on.

When the newest scan is four days old and the parcel is still at "Label created", "Received by carrier" or "In transit", Peek flags it with that scan's day, such as "No tracking update since Mon 12 Oct". The parcel's page then says "Parcels often go quiet on long flights, at customs or over a weekend. Peek keeps asking E24." After three weeks with no new scan, it says "Nothing new for over three weeks. If it still hasn’t arrived, ask the seller. Peek keeps asking E24."

You can pick alert presets for "Every scan", "Important steps" or "Delivery only". Peek monitors tracking updates only and cannot book a delivery, edit your address or contact Metro Supply Chain or your retailer for you.

## Questions about E24 (Metro Supply Chain) tracking

### What is E24 (Metro E24 / Metro Supply Chain)?

Peek lists E24 as a Canadian delivery carrier under the aliases Metro E24, Metro E24 Solutions and Metro Supply Chain. Metro Supply Chain is a Montreal-based 3PL operating 190 sites in 6 countries and handling 7 million last-mile deliveries per year in North America; its own website doesn't use the E24 name.

### Why doesn't Peek auto-detect E24 from my tracking number?

Peek has no number pattern or tracking-link rule for E24. Paste your retailer's reference, tap "Change" (or "Change carrier" later) and select **E24** (searching "Metro E24" or "Metro Supply Chain" also finds it).

### How do I track or reschedule a Metro Supply Chain (E24) delivery?

Track updates in Peek via tracking services after selecting E24, or for delivery support go to [metroscg.com/contact-us/](https://www.metroscg.com/contact-us/) and select "find my package" from the drop-down menu as Metro Supply Chain's FAQ instructs.

### Who should I contact if my E24 parcel is missing or damaged?

Use "find my package" on Metro Supply Chain's contact page for delivery help, and contact the retailer you bought from for a replacement, damage claim or refund.

### Where is Metro Supply Chain's Canadian headquarters?

Metro Supply Chain Inc. is headquartered at 1002 Sherbrooke St West, Suite 2000, Montreal, Quebec, Canada, with a European office at Saxham Business Park in Bury St Edmunds, UK.
