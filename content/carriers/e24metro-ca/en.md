**E24** (*Metro E24* / *Metro E24 Solutions*, part of **Metro Supply Chain Inc.**) is the Canadian final-mile, e-commerce fulfillment and white-glove home-delivery arm of Montreal-headquartered third-party logistics provider **Metro Supply Chain** ([metroscg.com](https://www.metroscg.com/)). Operating 190 logistics sites across 6 countries with 22.5 million square feet of warehousing and completing roughly 7 million North American last-mile deliveries each year, Metro Supply Chain / E24 handles direct-to-consumer e-commerce orders, big-and-bulky white-glove home delivery, time-critical logistics and returns for major Canadian and omni-channel retailers. You can paste your retailer-provided E24 tracking reference into the box above and choose **E24** to monitor updates.

## E24 (Metro Supply Chain) tracking numbers

Unlike national postal carriers with one fixed barcode prefix, E24 / Metro Supply Chain generates client- and merchant-specific waybill references across its 3PL fulfillment and final-mile delivery systems:

- **Where to find your number:** check your retailer's shipping confirmation email, SMS delivery notice or online order-history page — retailers using Metro Supply Chain's E24 last-mile service typically label the carrier as *E24*, *Metro E24* or *Metro Supply Chain* next to the order or waybill code.
- **No single universal prefix:** because reference formats vary by retail partner and fulfillment channel, Peek does not guess E24 from a barcode shape alone; paste the exact reference provided by your retailer and select **E24** manually. See [tracking number formats](guide:tracking-number-formats).

## How E24 final-mile and white-glove deliveries work in Canada

According to [Metro Supply Chain](https://www.metroscg.com/):

- **Fulfillment-to-doorstep flow:** Metro Supply Chain manages warehousing, picking, contract packaging, cross-border fulfillment and final-mile dispatch out of regional Canadian hubs, meaning a tracking number is often issued at the fulfillment warehouse before the local delivery van departs.
- **Standard parcel vs. white-glove delivery:** small e-commerce orders move through regional last-mile routes, whereas appliances, furniture and oversized retail orders dispatched via Metro Supply Chain's **White Glove Delivery** service often require scheduling a delivery window with the consignee before final drop-off.
- **Reverse logistics & returns:** participating retailers also route customer pickups and return authorizations back through Metro Supply Chain's reverse-logistics network.

## E24 parcel delayed, missed or missing

Because Metro Supply Chain / E24 operates as a B2B third-party logistics (3PL) partner on behalf of retail brands rather than a walk-in consumer parcel shop, **always contact the retailer where you placed your order first** for delivery-window edits, address corrections, missing packages or damaged items.

- **Quiet gap after order confirmation:** fulfillment orders frequently show no movement while picking, packing or transferring from a central distribution warehouse to your local Canadian delivery hub (especially on bulky or cross-border orders). See [why tracking stops updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).
- **Marked delivered or damaged:** check around porches, garages, building concierges and neighbours first, review any delivery confirmation sent by your retailer, and report missing or damaged goods to the store promptly. See [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received), [damaged parcel](guide:damaged-parcel), [lost parcel](guide:lost-parcel) and [returned to sender](guide:returned-to-sender).

## Track an E24 parcel with Peek

Because E24 uses merchant-specific references with no standalone public tracking-link rule or exclusive barcode pattern, paste your E24 reference into [Peek](/), tap "Change" while adding (or "Change carrier" on the parcel screen) and select **E24** (searching "Metro E24", "Metro E24 Solutions", "e24 Metro" or "Metro Supply Chain" finds it too).

Peek tracks E24 shipments through its supported tracking services rather than a direct Metro Supply Chain API, displaying the mapped stage ("Label created", "Received by carrier", "In transit", "Out for delivery", "Delivery attempted", "Needs attention", "Returned to sender", "Delivered") and scan history whenever tracking services return updates for your reference.

Because E24 runs through Peek's tracking services, Peek checks hourly, slows to every 3 hours after a day without change, and checks up to every 15 minutes on delivery day. After 4 days in transit with no new scan, Peek flags the pause (for example "No tracking update since Mon, Oct 12") and shows "Parcels often go quiet on long flights, at customs or over a weekend. Peek keeps asking E24." After more than three weeks without an update, Peek shows "Nothing new for over three weeks. If it still hasn’t arrived, ask the seller. Peek keeps asking E24."

You can pick alert presets for "Every scan", "Important steps" or "Delivery only". Peek monitors tracking updates only and cannot schedule white-glove delivery windows, edit your address or contact Metro Supply Chain or your retailer for you.

## Questions about E24 (Metro Supply Chain) tracking

### What is E24 (*Metro E24*)?

E24 is the Canadian final-mile and e-commerce delivery division of **Metro Supply Chain Inc.**, a Montreal-based 3PL operator handling fulfillment, white-glove home delivery and roughly 7 million North American last-mile deliveries per year.

### Why didn't Peek auto-detect E24 from my tracking number?

E24 uses retailer-specific order and waybill references without one exclusive public prefix, so Peek requires you to select **E24** manually via "Change" or "Change carrier".

### Who should I contact to reschedule an E24 delivery?

Contact the online retailer where you bought the item first — Metro Supply Chain operates as a 3PL for retail clients and processes consumer delivery changes and claims through the originating merchant.

### Where is Metro Supply Chain headquartered?

Metro Supply Chain's Canadian headquarters is located at 1002 Sherbrooke St West, Suite 2000, Montreal, Quebec, Canada ([metroscg.com/contact-us/](https://www.metroscg.com/contact-us/)).
