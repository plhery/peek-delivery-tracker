Early Bird is a Swedish parcel company. Its couriers bring online orders with the morning newspaper, so your parcel usually comes at night or early in the morning, to your door or your mailbox. Track it on [Early Bird's Track & help page](https://earlybird.se/en/track-and-help) (in Swedish at earlybird.se/spara-och-hjalp), with the link in Early Bird's SMS or email, or paste the number in the box above. The number is in Early Bird's message or in the shop's order information.

## Early Bird tracking number format

The number shape Peek's code knows for Early Bird (digits invented):

| Number (invented) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `00373402216000000004` | 20 digits: `00`, any digit, `73402216`, then nine digits. The last one is a check digit. A parcel barcode number. |
| Another reference | 8 to 30 letters and digits. Peek can track it at Early Bird, but can't tell Early Bird from its shape. |

[PostNord](carrier:postnord) and [Bring](carrier:bring) also use 20-digit numbers starting `00`, so the shape alone doesn't prove the parcel is with Early Bird. Gave the wrong mobile number? You can still track with the parcel number from the shop's order confirmation. More in [how to find your tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number).

## Early Bird delivery in Sweden: night, mailbox and lockers

**Who brings it.** Newspaper carriers, on their rounds. Early Bird is owned by Sweden's leading media companies and reaches almost all of Sweden, towns and countryside. Your options depend on where you live and what the shop chose.

**When.** At night or early in the morning. Early Bird says deliveries usually happen between 01:00 and 07:00, and your parcel is there by 07:00. You don't need to be home or awake. It delivers up to six days a week, weekends included, depending on the area.

**How long.** Home Delivery takes 1 to 3 days from when Early Bird receives the parcel, not from the day you ordered. Early Bird Express, where the shop offers it, comes the same night: by 07:00 the morning after you order. The planned delivery day is in the link in Early Bird's SMS or email.

**Where it's left.** Outside your door or in your mailbox. Couriers often deliver newspapers in your area, so they can get into many apartment buildings. A message tells you where the parcel was left.

**If delivery fails.** Tracking and a message tell you the next step. Depending on the service the shop booked, Early Bird tries again, sends the parcel to a parcel locker, or hands it to another carrier for delivery to a service point, which tells you when it's ready. See [missed delivery](guide:missed-delivery).

**Parcel lockers (Early Bird Box).** If you chose Early Bird Box at checkout, the parcel goes to an outdoor iBoxen locker, open around the clock. You open it with the iBoxen app and Mobile BankID; register the app with the same phone number as your order.

- The parcel waits five days. That normally can't be extended. Then it goes back to the shop: see [uncollected parcels](guide:uncollected-parcel).
- If your locker is full, Early Bird uses one nearby; the message and the app show where.
- Where the iBoxen app offers it, you can share collection with someone else. They register in the app and identify themselves, usually with BankID.

**Changes.** Once the parcel is on its way, Early Bird can't change the day or the address, and normally can't change the delivery method or cancel. Ask the shop before it ships. See [changing a delivery address](guide:change-delivery-address).

## Early Bird parcel late, missing or damaged

- **Only "booked".** The parcel hasn't reached Early Bird yet. Until it does, Early Bird says the shop is best placed to answer. See [tracking that isn't updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).
- **No news after a failed delivery.** If nothing comes within the time Early Bird gave, contact its customer service.
- **Marked delivered, but not there.** Check the address in your order confirmation. Look in and beside your mailbox, and outside your door. Ask your household and neighbours. Then use the "Missing package despite confirmation" form. See [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).
- **Damaged.** Use the "Damaged package" form with a clear photo. Contact the shop too: Early Bird says the shop answers for the item and any compensation. See [damaged parcels](guide:damaged-parcel).

## Early Bird customer service and contact

As read on 2 November 2026, Early Bird's [Contact us page](https://earlybird.se/en/contact-us) lists no phone number or email address. You pick a subject, such as "Damaged package", "Incorrect delivery" or "Received someone else's package", and fill in its form with your tracking number and a short description. For a refund or a return, contact the shop. Peek can't contact Early Bird or the shop for you.

> Early Bird doesn't ask for an extra fee to release a parcel: delivery is normally paid when you buy. Don't pay or click links in an unexpected message; check the parcel on Early Bird's own tracking page. More in [delivery scam texts](guide:delivery-scam-texts).

## Track an Early Bird parcel with Peek

- **A 20-digit Early Bird number.** Paste it in [Peek](/). Because PostNord numbers look alike, Peek shows "Checking tracking services…" and asks Early Bird first, with PostNord and other carriers the number fits. When Early Bird knows it, Peek says "Early Bird has this parcel." If no carrier knows it yet, Peek names Early Bird as the likely carrier: the parcel shows "Probably Early Bird" and "Waiting for Early Bird’s first scan", and Peek keeps asking. To file it under Early Bird, press "Change" before tracking and pick Early Bird under "Fits this number", or use "Change carrier" later.
- **A link to Early Bird's tracking.** An earlybird.se link to `spara-och-hjalp`, `en/track-and-help` or `tracking` with `id=`, or a track.earlybird.se link with `a=`, names Early Bird at once, whatever the number.
- **Another reference.** Paste it all the same: Peek follows more than 3,500 carriers. To track it at Early Bird, press "Change" and search for Early Bird, or use "Change carrier" later. For Early Bird, Peek takes 8 to 30 letters and digits, and a 20-digit number starting `00` needs a valid check digit. Otherwise it says Early Bird "doesn’t issue numbers like this one".
- **Not listed yet.** A parcel filed under Early Bird shows "Waiting for the carrier" until Early Bird lists it, and Peek keeps asking. If Early Bird says the number is wrong, Peek shows "Check the tracking number".

**What Peek shows.** Each step in Early Bird's English wording (older lines stay Swedish), with its time, the place each terminal scan names and the delivery time. The booking shows as "Label created", the receipt as "Received by carrier", terminals and the way to a locker as "In transit", failed deliveries as "Delivery attempted", the locker as "Ready for pickup", and delivery or collection as "Delivered". A new attempt keeps the step before it. Peek shows no planned delivery day and doesn't name the locker. The planned day is in the link in Early Bird's SMS or email; the locker is in its message and the iBoxen app.

**When another carrier takes over.** Early Bird's tracking then links to that carrier's. Peek's code knows three: PostNord, DSV and DB Schenker, which Peek files under [DSV](carrier:dsv). The card says "Delivery with PostNord" or "Delivery with DSV", and Peek follows that carrier under the same number once it shows fresh scans. While that carrier has only the announcement of the parcel, with no scan of its own, the card says "Announced to PostNord, not received yet" or "Announced to DSV, not received yet" instead.

Peek checks about every 10 minutes and can notify you of "Every scan", "Important steps" or "Delivery only". After two days without news (12 hours while the parcel still shows "Label created" or "Received by carrier"), Peek checks hourly.

## Questions about Early Bird tracking

### What time does Early Bird deliver?

Usually between 01:00 and 07:00, up to six days a week depending on the area. You don't need to be home or awake.

### Why does my Early Bird tracking only say the delivery is booked?

The shop booked the delivery, but Early Bird hasn't received the parcel yet. Tracking updates, with a planned delivery day, once it has. Until then, ask the shop.

### How long does Early Bird keep a parcel in the locker?

Five days, normally with no extension. Then it goes back to the shop.

### Is Early Bird legit?

Yes. It's a Swedish parcel company owned by Sweden's leading media companies, and its parcels travel with newspaper carriers. It doesn't ask for an extra fee to release a parcel.

### How do I contact Early Bird customer service?

Through the forms on its Contact us page, with your tracking number. It lists no phone number or email.
