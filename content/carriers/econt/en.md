Econt Express is a Bulgarian courier, founded in 1993, that delivers to your door, to its offices and to Econtomat parcel lockers. Track a parcel with "Track a shipment" on [econt.com](https://www.econt.com/en/services/track-shipment), with the link in Econt's email or SMS, or by pasting the shipment number in the box above. The number comes from Econt's notification or the shop's shipping email.

## Econt tracking number: where to find it

Econt calls it the shipment number or waybill number. Econt's emails and SMS carry a link that opens the tracking; SMS is an option the sender pays for, so not every parcel gets one. No message? Ask the shop, or see [where else to find a tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number).

Econt publishes no format; Peek reads its shipment numbers as 13 digits, like the invented `1111111111111`.

Thirteen digits also fit other carriers, among them [DPD France](carrier:dpd-fr), Ukraine's [Ukrposhta](carrier:ukrposhta) and Romania's [FAN Courier](carrier:fan-courier), so check which carrier the shop's email or the SMS names.

## What Econt's tracking statuses mean

econt.com opens in Bulgarian, with a switch to English. The English site shows each scan's label in English and its place in Latin script, plus a summary status at the top. These are its English labels, in the order a parcel meets them.

| Econt's status | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `Awaiting delivery to Econt` | The sender has [made the label](guide:tracking-statuses) but not handed the parcel over yet. |
| `Submitted to courier` | First in the list: a courier has collected it from the sender. Later on: it's out for delivery. |
| `Arrival at an office` | It reached the Econt office or logistics centre named beside it, possibly your pickup office. |
| `Received by line-haul` | It's on a trunk route between centres; the place shows the route, town to town. |
| `Change delivery terms` | Someone changed the delivery, for example the office. The parcel hasn't moved. |
| `Shipment forwarding` | It's being sent on to another place. |
| `Failed delivery` | The courier couldn't deliver and took it back to an office. |
| `Delivered` | Delivered to you, or picked up. |
| `Delivery return` | It's going back to the sender. |
| `Return receipt: Delivered` | The signed return receipt reached the sender; it says nothing new about your parcel. |

`Submitted to courier` is the confusing one: the same words mean a pickup at the start and the last mile at the end, so read what came before it. `Failed delivery` gives a reason in brackets. A `Change delivery terms` line names what changed.

On the Bulgarian site the summary reads `В офис` when the parcel waits at an office (ready for pickup once Econt names the office storing it) and `Доставена` when delivered. `Information` is a note without a step.

## Econt delivery, offices and Econtomats

**At the door**, within a town, Econt delivers to the address by 6pm on the next working day; elsewhere, on its own schedule. An email or SMS tells you where the parcel is and which office or courier has it. The sender can book a delivery time slot, or Saturday delivery for a fee.

**At an office**, a parcel sent for the next working day is there early in the morning, when the office opens. Econt charges a storage fee per day once a parcel has waited more than seven days.

**At an Econtomat**, open 24/7: choose "Receive a shipment", scan the QR code or enter the two PIN codes, pay by card in the Econt app if anything is due, and take the parcel from the open box. Econtomats take parcels up to 61 x 44 x 37 cm.

**Changing the delivery.** Econt says you can change the recipient, place and time of delivery at any time: by phone, through the email notification or in your e-Econt profile. Options include another office, address, town or day, or leaving it with a neighbour.

**Checking the goods.** Econt's price list has three free options a sender can add for you: Review, Review and Test, and Review, Test and Select.

## Econt parcel late, lost or damaged

Econt gives the next working day for places with an Econt office, up to three working days for places without one, and one to two working days from an Econtomat, depending on when the parcel was sent. No movement for days? See [tracking that isn't updating](guide:tracking-not-updating), then ask Econt or the shop.

**Damaged, missing or lost.** File a claim through "I want to file a claim" on Econt's customer service page, and tell the shop.

## Tracking an Econt parcel with Peek

[Peek](/) can't tell an Econt parcel from its 13 digits: it offers them to other carriers, such as Delhivery, never Econt. If none knows it, you'll see "not found yet · Peek keeps checking": pick "Econt" with "Change". Or paste an econt.com link with `/services/track-shipment/`, `/tracking/` or `/tracking/?num=` and the number, which names Econt straight away.

It then shows its own status, set from Econt's summary when that settles one, the history with the office of each office scan on a map, and the delivery time. While the parcel waits, it shows the name of the Econt office storing it. Econt gives no expected delivery day or weight. The parcel count, courier's and recipient's names, sender and order references are left out.

Scans keep the English labels above, except `Delivered`, shown in Peek's own words; `Return receipt: …` lines change nothing. Times are read as Sofia time; a scan in the hour the clocks repeat in late October shows in the history once a later scan arrives, in order, though not at its exact time. It checks every 10 minutes, or every 2 minutes once the parcel is out for delivery, and can notify you of every scan, important steps or on delivery day only. After two days without news (12 hours while the parcel still shows "Label created" or "Received by carrier"), Peek checks hourly.

## Questions about Econt tracking

### Can I see Econt tracking in English?

Yes: econt.com's English version shows scans in English and places in Latin script.

### How long does Econt delivery take?

In Bulgaria, the next working day where the town has an Econt office, up to three working days where it doesn't, and one to two working days from an Econtomat, depending on when it was sent, says Econt.

### How do I track an Econt parcel by its waybill number?

Paste the number in the box above and pick "Econt" with "Change": Peek can't tell Econt from the digits alone. Or enter it under "Track a shipment" on econt.com.
