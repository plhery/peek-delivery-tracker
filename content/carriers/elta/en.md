ELTA Hellenic Post (ΕΛΤΑ) is Greece's national post, and ELTA Courier is now part of it. Track an item on ELTA's [English tracking page](https://www.elta.gr/en/track), which takes up to five numbers at once, one per line, or paste the number in the box above. An ELTA Courier parcel can also be looked up on elta-courier.gr's [search](https://www.elta-courier.gr/search), which takes several voucher numbers, one per line. The number comes from the sender: the receipt of posting, the courier voucher or the shop's message. In short: the status table below says what each line means; a parcel ELTA couldn't deliver waits at the post office or courier store the tracking names (a domestic parcel for as long as the notice or SMS says, an item cleared through customs 20 days from the notice); and ELTA's call centre is 800-11-82000 from a Greek landline, 212-0000-800 from a mobile, +30 212-0000-800 from abroad.

## ELTA tracking number

ELTA's tracking numbers have 13 characters: [two Latin letters, nine digits and two Latin letters](guide:tracking-number-formats). Items posted in Greece end in `GR`; ELTA's own example is `PP123456785GR`, a sample Peek refuses. The last of the nine digits is a check digit, so one mistyped digit usually makes the number invalid. ELTA Courier numbers mostly use the same layout, printed at the top right of the courier voucher. Simple tracked letters, whose numbers start with `U`, `L` or `TR` (`LL` within Greece), are left at the address without a signature.

| Shape (invented) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `RR482910576GR` | A postal item from Greece. Spaces and lower case don't matter (`RR 482 910 576 GR`). |
| `482910573649` | Some items from Germany carry a 12-digit barcode instead, which ELTA's tracker also accepts. |
| `RR482910576CN` | An item from abroad keeps the foreign post's number, ending in the country it came from. |

Lost the number? See [how to find a tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number).

Lookalikes: a number ending `CY` belongs to [Cyprus Post](carrier:cyprus-post). A plain 10-digit voucher is not ELTA's: it is the shape [ACS Courier](carrier:acs-courier) and [Geniki Taxydromiki](carrier:geniki-taxydromiki) use.

## What ELTA tracking statuses mean

ELTA's tracker shows every scan in Greek or English (a language switch), with a date, a time and the station: the post office or courier agency. Here are the English wordings, roughly in the order a parcel meets them.

| ELTA's status | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `Label printed by customer` | The sender printed the label; ELTA doesn't have the item yet. |
| `Item deposited at ELTA` | The sender handed the item in at the post office the scan names. |
| `Shipment in transit` | Sent on from the station the scan names. |
| `Arrival in` | Arrived at the post office or courier agency named after these words. |
| `Arrival at the airport` | An item leaving Greece reached ELTA's hub at Athens airport. |
| `For dispatch abroad` | At the Athens airport hub, ready to leave Greece. |
| `With Courier for delivery` | Out for delivery to your address. |
| `Notification of attempted delivery` | Nobody could take it: a notice was left, with the reason in the next line. |
| `ADDRESSEE NOT AVAILABLE AT TIME OF DELIVERY` | Nobody was home. |
| `ADDRESSEE REQUEST OWN PICK-UP` | The attempt ended because you will collect it yourself. |
| `Item at post office - Can be collected` | Waiting for you at the post office or agency named. |
| `SMS text message sent for item to be collected` | ELTA texted you a pickup reminder. Nothing moved. |
| `Shipment delivered` | Delivered (seen on courier parcels collected from an agency). |
| `Item delivered` | Delivered (seen on parcels collected from a post office). |

`Arrival in` is followed by a gap of spaces and then the station, so it can look cut off. `Notification of attempted delivery` and its reason arrive at the same minute.

If the tracker answers "No Tracking History", the item has no scans yet or is too old: ELTA keeps a history for six months from the date the item was sent.

## Delivery, PostBox lockers and picking up from ELTA

ELTA delivers a domestic parcel to your home, to an ELTA store (you get an SMS or a notice) or to a PostBox locker where your postcode allows it. ELTA's domestic parcel page promises delivery in 3 working days across Greece.

**Missed delivery.** A parcel from abroad that can't be handed over leaves a notice asking you to collect it at the nearest post office. ELTA's charter gives no collection period for such a parcel; one that went through customs waits 20 days from the notice (see Customs below). If the postman finds nobody in, ELTA may also take the item back to the nearby store and put it in that store's PostBox, so you can collect it whenever you like, without queuing. Items with customs charges are delivered only against a signature. A domestic parcel that isn't delivered to your home is collected at the post office counter after a phone call, a notice slip or an SMS, and that notice or SMS states how long the parcel stays at the post office. Collect it within that time: an item nobody collects goes back to the sender (at no extra charge within Greece), and one the sender doesn't take back either is kept as unclaimed until at least six months after it was posted.

**ELTA Courier** makes two delivery attempts. After the first notice, call your local courier store to schedule delivery; after two notices, collect it there or book a new delivery, which may cost extra. Courier stores open Monday to Friday 08:00 to 20:00 and Saturday 08:00 to 14:00, which may vary by area. A door-to-door courier item that can't be delivered goes back to the sender, within two weeks of being sent unless the sender's contract says otherwise.

**PostBox lockers.** ELTA's smart lockers are open 24/7 (stations in the Metro and ISAP keep station hours). When your item is placed, a Viber or SMS message gives a 6-digit PIN and the locker's location. Collect within 48 hours; after that it moves to the nearest ELTA store and you get a new message. Items with duties or taxes don't go to a PostBox. You can ask for one with "Θέλω από PostBox" in ELTA's SMS or Viber; for an item from abroad, whether you can still choose one depends on when ELTA sorts it. A PostBox takes items up to 62 × 36 × 60 cm and 20 kg, and collecting from one costs nothing extra.

**Changing the address.** For a courier item only the sender can, in writing to customerservice@elta-courier.gr. For a fully tracked postal item, the sender can file a change request at a store, in person with ID.

## ELTA parcel delayed, lost or damaged

If tracking stops moving for days, see [tracking that isn't updating](guide:tracking-not-updating) first.

**Customs.** For parcels from outside the EU, duties are paid only at an ELTA store when you collect. In Greece, an online order shipped from outside the EU and worth up to €150 carries a temporary €3 customs duty per category of items since 1 July 2026, and since 1 November 2026 distance sales of any value also carry a €2 Union handling fee per category of items. Greek import VAT is added, except on Mount Athos, which is outside the EU VAT area, unless the VAT was paid at purchase through IOSS. ELTA sends a notice that lists the charges and the post office to collect from. A cleared item waits 20 days at the post office from the notice; you, or someone you authorise, bring the notice and the ID of whoever collects (ID card, passport or driving licence), and pay the charges there. After 20 days it goes back to ELTA's customs centre, where it is declared unclaimed or, in some cases, returned to the sender. If you dispute the charges, don't collect: file a reasoned request at the store with proof of value. More in [parcels held at customs](guide:customs).

**Lost.** The sender or recipient can file a search request at an ELTA store, with ID and the receipt of posting, up to six months after posting. It covers fully tracked items only. ELTA answers by registered letter within 8 weeks (4 weeks for some items abroad).

**Damaged courier parcel.** Accept it with reservation and declare it within one working day, through the ELTA Courier contact form or at the store. Without that, ELTA bears no liability. The compensation form goes to apozimiwseis@elta-courier.gr with the voucher, the invoice and photos of the item and packaging. Compensation goes to the sender, or to you if the sender waives it in writing: for an online order, ask the shop. For questions, call ELTA Courier's compensation department on +30 210-6073066.

**Complaints.** ELTA must answer within 15 days. If you're not satisfied, ask Customer Service in writing to refer it to its Dispute Resolution Committee.

## ELTA contact number and email

ELTA's channels, as elta.gr and elta-courier.gr list them. ELTA's tracking site gives the call centre for questions about an item; ELTA Courier gives its own line for courier tracking help.

- **Call centre:** 800-11-82000 from a Greek landline, 212-0000-800 from a mobile, +30 212-0000-800 from abroad; Monday to Friday 08:00 to 18:00.
- **New service points:** 212 00 00 700, a line only for questions about ELTA's new service points, Monday to Friday 08:00 to 18:00, at local call rates.
- **E-mail:** service@elta-net.gr, or the form on [elta.gr/en/contact](https://www.elta.gr/en/contact).
- **ELTA Courier:** 210 6073000 (charged as a normal call) or 11120 (charged at your phone provider's rates), tracking help Monday to Friday 08:00 to 16:00 (at weekends and on public holidays, online tracking only); info@elta-courier.gr and customercare@elta-courier.gr. Written complaints can also go by post to 200 Ionias Ave. & 61 Iakovaton St., 111 44 Athens, for the attention of the Customer Service Department.
- **By post:** Hellenic Post Customer Service, Adrianoupoleos 45, 16070 Kessariani.
- **In person:** any ELTA store (store finder on elta.gr).

> ELTA never asks for your bank or card details, never asks by SMS or e-mail for a fee to deliver a parcel, and never calls from numbers outside Greece. Customs charges are paid only at an ELTA store. Fake e-mails titled "Ειδοποίηση αποτυχίας παράδοσης" are not from ELTA.

## Tracking ELTA parcels with Peek

For a number ending `GR` with a valid check digit, [Peek](/) asks ELTA (and [Chronopost](carrier:chronopost)) at once and shows "ELTA Hellenic Post has this parcel." when ELTA knows the number, or "Not found at … yet. Peek will keep checking after you add it." With a wrong check digit ELTA isn't asked, and picking it by hand doesn't help (Peek says it "doesn’t issue numbers like this one"): check the number with the sender. A 12-digit code from Germany, typed, goes to other carriers, never ELTA: use "Change" or paste an ELTA link. Links from elta.gr's tracker and elta-courier.gr's search always name ELTA.

An item from abroad is followed at its foreign post first. When that post says the item is going to Greece (or names no destination and you're in Greece), Peek also asks ELTA with the same number, and moves to it once it has a fresh scan; the earlier history stays. To follow it at ELTA straight away, pick "ELTA Hellenic Post" with "Change".

ELTA gives each scan a date and time but no time zone: Peek reads them on the clock of a place the history names, Greek time for a history in Greece, and lists ELTA's own history with ELTA's English wording. Times show in your device's time zone; on peektracker.com and in the iPhone and Android apps, the history ends with "Times are in your time zone:" and its name. It can show the post office or courier agency where the parcel waits and notify you when the status changes. ELTA gives no expected delivery day and no weight.

## Questions about ELTA tracking

### Can I see ELTA tracking in English?

Yes. elta.gr/en/track shows the form and every scan in English; the plain elta.gr/track opens in Greek with a language switch.

### Can I track an international parcel with ELTA?

Yes, with the postal number the sender gave you, which ends in the sending country's code: ELTA's tracker takes it.

### What is ELTA Courier?

ELTA's courier service, with its own site, elta-courier.gr, for door-to-door delivery in Greece, EMS and parcels to Cyprus. It merged into ELTA in 2024.

### Where is my nearest ELTA post office?

Use the store finder on elta.gr; ELTA Courier stores are on elta-courier.gr's network page.
