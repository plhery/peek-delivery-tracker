Estafeta (*La Fuerza Roja*) is one of Mexico's largest domestic and international logistics operators, founded on 8 August 1979 as the first company to offer door-to-door courier and parcel delivery in Mexico. Serving all of Mexico alongside international shipping to more than 220 countries and territories, Estafeta handles *Terrestre* (ground), *Día Siguiente* (next-day), *Garantía 12:30*, LTL/FTL freight, reverse logistics and retail service points across the country. You can track a shipment on [Estafeta's tracking page](https://www.estafeta.com/rastrear-envio?rastreo=true) or [cs.estafeta.com](https://cs.estafeta.com/), or paste your code into the box above.

## Estafeta tracking numbers: 10-digit code vs 22-character guide

Every Estafeta shipment carries both a short 10-digit **tracking code** (*código de rastreo*) and a full 22-character **waybill / guide number** (*número de guía*). Either works on Estafeta's tracker:

| Format (invented) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `482917364058E704829173` | 22-character full *guía* with a letter in the 13th place (for example `E`, `A` or `C`). |
| `4829173640580D48291736` | 22-character two-day full *guía* with a service letter (such as `D`) in the 14th position. |
| `4829173640582917364058` | 22-digit all-numeric full *guía*. Shares this 22-digit length with [Austrian Post](carrier:austrian-post) and [USPS](carrier:usps). |
| `4829173640` | 10-digit short *código de rastreo*. Shares 10-digit numeric length with [DHL](carrier:dhl), [Mondial Relay](carrier:mondial-relay), [Relais Colis](carrier:relais-colis), [Packeta](carrier:packeta) and others. |

If your Mexican delivery carries a `JMX` waybill or a branch-coded *guía* (such as `MEX...`), check [J&T Express Mexico](carrier:j-and-t-mx) or [Paquetexpress](carrier:paquetexpress); for other courier patterns, see [tracking number formats](guide:tracking-number-formats).

## What Estafeta tracking statuses mean

Estafeta's tracker shows Spanish status descriptions with the scanning city or hub and the local time of the scan:

| Estafeta says | What it means for you | Peek shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `La guía ha sido generada sin embargo el envío aún no es depositado en Estafeta` | The sender generated the waybill electronically, but the parcel has not been dropped off or collected yet. | "Label created" |
| `Recibido por estafeta` | Estafeta accepted the physical parcel at a branch or pickup. | "Received by carrier" |
| `En tránsito`, `[City] Llegada a centro Operativo plaza Destino`, `Pendiente programacion para salida a ruta` | Moving through Estafeta's national hubs, arrived at the destination city's operating centre or queued for route dispatch. | "In transit" |
| `En proceso de entrega a domicilio` | Loaded on the local Estafeta delivery van for doorstep delivery today. | "Out for delivery" |
| `CARGA DEMORADA POR BLOQUEO O ACCIDENTE EXTERNO EN VIA FEDERAL` | Road freight delayed by an external highway blockade or accident on a federal route. | "Needs attention" |
| `Entregado` | Delivered to the recipient or collected at a branch. | "Delivered" |

## Estafeta delivery hours, 2 attempts and 10-day branch storage

According to Estafeta's [FAQ](https://www.estafeta.com/preguntas-frecuentes) and [Service Contract](https://www.estafeta.com/contrato-de-servicios):

- **Delivery windows:** *Terrestre* (ground) shipments deliver on an open schedule from **09:00 to 18:00**; *Garantía 12:30* delivers the next day by 12:30. Rates use the greater of actual or volumetric weight (`length × width × height in cm ÷ 5000`, Clause 2).
- **Two delivery attempts and 10-day storage (Clause 7):** if Estafeta cannot deliver on the first visit, the driver leaves a notice and schedules a **second attempt at no extra charge** according to your postcode's service frequency. If the second attempt also fails, Estafeta holds the parcel at the **nearest Estafeta point of sale (*punto de venta*)** with a notice for **up to 10 calendar days** (*10 días naturales*) before returning it to the sender.
- **Delay remedy (Clause 7):** if Estafeta misses a committed delivery window for reasons attributable to Estafeta, the shipper may request in writing within **20 working days** a complimentary equivalent shipment usable within 12 months.

## Delayed, damaged or missing Estafeta parcels

For an online purchase, the store booked the shipment. Estafeta's [terms](https://www.estafeta.com/terminos-y-condiciones) let the sender or the recipient, under conditions, change the address within the same city, switch to an Estafeta branch, change the delivery date or ask for extended hours (18:00 to 22:00), and let the sender stop the parcel, for deliveries in Mexico City and its metropolitan area, Guadalajara and Monterrey; ask while tracking the parcel on Estafeta's channels. For a new shipment or a refund, contact the store.

- **Strict 24-working-hour claims window (Clause 10):** under Estafeta's [Service Contract](https://www.estafeta.com/contrato-de-servicios), written claims for damage, partial loss or confirmed total loss must be submitted within **24 working hours** (*24 horas hábiles*) after receipt or confirmation of loss, addressed to Estafeta's Customer Care department in Mexico City.
- **Liability cap (Clause 6):** without Declared Value insurance, Estafeta's maximum carrier liability is **30 UMA** (upon presenting proof of commercial value or repair cost).
- **External delays and missing parcels:** check [estafeta.com](https://www.estafeta.com/) for active Mexican highway or holiday disruption alerts. For stuck scans or missing items, see [why tracking stops updating](guide:tracking-not-updating), [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received), [damaged parcel](guide:damaged-parcel), [lost parcel](guide:lost-parcel) and [returned to sender](guide:returned-to-sender).

- **Online contact form:** [estafeta.com/contacto](https://www.estafeta.com/contacto) and the **Estafeta** mobile app (iOS / Android).

## Track an Estafeta parcel with Peek

[Peek](/) automatically recognises 22-character lettered Estafeta guides (`482917364058E704829173` and `4829173640580D48291736`, invented) with high confidence, and also recognises pasted `cs.estafeta.com` links carrying `wayBill=`. For a 10-digit tracking code (`4829173640`) or a 22-digit all-numeric guide (`4829173640582917364058`), Peek asks Estafeta first when you track from Mexico, and elsewhere only once the other likely carriers don't know the number; it shows "Estafeta has this parcel." when Estafeta confirms it. You can also tap "Change" while adding or "Change carrier" on the parcel screen to pick Estafeta yourself.

Peek reads Estafeta's tracker directly and shows the stage ("Label created", "Received by carrier", "In transit", "Out for delivery", "Needs attention", "Delivered") and the Spanish scan descriptions with their city or hub. Estafeta gives each scan's time without a zone: Peek dates it on its city's time zone and shows it on your device's clock. The service name stays out of every Peek app. When you entered a 10-digit code, Peek shows the full 22-character guide under "Estafeta tracks this as"; a guide with only its label shows Estafeta's registration notice until the first scan. Peek leaves out recipient names, postcodes, signatures, proof-of-delivery images, downloadable shipment reports and scheduled delivery dates.

While you have the parcel open in Peek, or on its delivery day with notifications on, Peek checks up to every 10 minutes; on the day Estafeta shows `En proceso de entrega a domicilio`, every 2 minutes from 6 a.m. on the clock of the newest scan's city, while Estafeta's own tracker answers. Otherwise Peek checks hourly. After 12 hours with only "Label created" or "Received by carrier", or two days without news, it checks hourly however you follow it, and every 6 hours after seven days at "Label created". When Estafeta's tracker doesn't answer and Peek follows the parcel through tracking services alone, it checks hourly, every 3 hours after a quiet day, and up to every 15 minutes on the out-for-delivery day.

When the newest scan is four days old and the parcel is still at "Label created", "Received by carrier" or "In transit", Peek flags it with that scan's day, such as "No tracking update since Mon 12 Oct". The parcel's page then says "Parcels often go quiet on long flights, at customs or over a weekend. Peek keeps asking Estafeta.", with a "Get help from Estafeta" link to Estafeta's contact page. After three weeks with no new scan, it says "Nothing new for over three weeks. If it still hasn’t arrived, ask the seller. Peek keeps asking Estafeta."

You can pick alert presets for "Every scan", "Important steps" or "Delivery only". Peek monitors tracking updates only and cannot reschedule an Estafeta delivery or file a 24-hour claim on your behalf.

## Questions about Estafeta tracking

### What is the difference between Estafeta's 10-digit code and 22-character guide?

The 10-digit number is the short tracking code (*código de rastreo*) and the 22-character string (often containing a letter in the 13th or 14th position, such as `482917364058E704829173`, invented) is the full waybill (*número de guía*). Both track the same shipment on Estafeta and in Peek.

### How many delivery attempts does Estafeta make?

Under Clause 7 of Estafeta's [Service Contract](https://www.estafeta.com/contrato-de-servicios), Estafeta makes **two delivery attempts**. After a second unsuccessful attempt, it holds the parcel at the nearest *punto de venta* for **up to 10 calendar days** before returning it to the sender.

### What are Estafeta's delivery hours?

Estafeta's [FAQ](https://www.estafeta.com/preguntas-frecuentes) states that *Terrestre* (ground) deliveries operate on an open schedule from **09:00 to 18:00**, while *Garantía 12:30* delivers the next day by 12:30.

### How do I contact Estafeta or file a claim?

Call `800 3782338`, use the form on [estafeta.com/contacto](https://www.estafeta.com/contacto) or chat with Beatriz (24/7) on [estafeta.com](https://www.estafeta.com/). Written claims for damage or loss must be filed within **24 working hours** of receipt or confirmed loss.
