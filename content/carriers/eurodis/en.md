EURODIS is a European network for parcels and pallets. In each country a partner company collects and delivers the goods: trans-o-flex in Germany, Österreichische Post (Austrian Post) in Austria. EURODIS's [Track & Trace page](https://eurodis.com/track-trace/) tracks a shipment across the network (more below). If the partner gave you its own tracking number, paste that in the box above.

## EURODIS tracking number: what it looks like

Peek's code knows two kinds of EURODIS number: one for the whole shipment, and a barcode for each package or pallet in it. Here they are with invented digits:

| Number (invented) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `7394028156342` | 13 digits: the EURODIS shipment number. It covers every package and pallet in the shipment. |
| `849203716482910375618294` | 24 characters: the barcode of one package or pallet. Mostly digits; some partners put letters in front. |
| `eurodis.com/track-trace?trackCollo-input-of=849203716482910375618294` | A link to EURODIS's tracker with a number in it. Peek takes the number from it and files it under EURODIS. |

The partner that delivers the shipment can have its own number for it. EURODIS's tracker shows that number beside EURODIS's. If you have the partner's number, track it with that partner, or add it to Peek under that partner.

Several other carriers use 13-digit and 24-digit numbers, so the shape alone doesn't prove that a number is EURODIS's. A 24-digit number also fits one of [Austrian Post](carrier:austrian-post)'s shapes. No number at all? Ask the sender, or see [how to find your tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number).

In other countries, such as Belgium, Denmark, France, Ireland, Poland, Sweden and the UK, a delivery partner covers EURODIS shipments. trans-o-flex, the German partner, says that with EURODIS it ships within Germany and to 38 other European countries. It delivers to businesses and to private homes. Parcels and pallets can travel together as one consignment.

### Delivery in Germany: trans-o-flex

trans-o-flex is an express and logistics company based in Weinheim. Its own pages say this about deliveries:

- **Tracking.** Recipients track with trans-o-flex insight, an app and a web page. Without an account, you enter the consignment number, your postcode and your house number.
- **When.** Regular deliveries come Monday to Friday between 8:00 and 17:00, and on Saturdays between 7:00 and 14:00, German time. The delivery time in the tracking is an estimate. Customer service can't change it during the round and can't call the driver.
- **Notice.** If the sender books it, trans-o-flex tells you about the delivery by text message or email.
- **Not at home.** The driver may leave the goods only if you gave written permission in advance. You can do that in trans-o-flex insight, but only for shipments where the sender agreed. It doesn't work for dangerous goods, cash on delivery, island deliveries or confidential shipments. See also [missed delivery](guide:missed-delivery).
- **Another day, another address, a second try.** Only the sender can ask for a new address. In Germany, a second delivery attempt for a shipment that isn't refrigerated also goes through the sender, and insight has no way to pick a delivery day: ask the sender.
- **Collect it yourself.** You can usually pick up a shipment at a trans-o-flex branch, unless it is a refrigerated (2–8 °C) one. Contact customer service first so they can get it ready.

### Delivery in Austria: Österreichische Post

In Austria, EURODIS's partner is Österreichische Post. Its notices, pickup points and lockers are on our [Austrian Post](carrier:austrian-post) page.

## EURODIS tracking statuses

EURODIS's [Track & Trace page](https://eurodis.com/track-trace/) tracks a shipment by its 24-digit barcode, its 13-digit EURODIS shipment number, the shipper's reference with the destination country and postcode, or the dispatch partner's number with the dispatch country. In Germany, trans-o-flex also has its own tracking for recipients, trans-o-flex insight (see above). For Austria, see our [Austrian Post](carrier:austrian-post) page.

This page doesn't list EURODIS's exact wordings: Peek's code knows them only from old archived copies of EURODIS's tracker, so we couldn't confirm any of them. What the usual steps mean, whoever the carrier: [tracking statuses](guide:tracking-statuses).

## EURODIS shipment late, damaged or not delivered

**Not moving.** If nothing changes for days, contact the partner in your country with your number (contacts below), or ask the sender. See [tracking that isn't updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

**Late.** If you bought the goods as a consumer in the EU, the shop has to deliver within 30 days, unless you agreed on another time, says the EU's Your Europe site. After that, give the shop extra time. If it still doesn't deliver, you can cancel and get your money back.

**Damaged or lost.** trans-o-flex says only the shipper can claim for damage or loss in transport, so contact the sender. For consumers, Your Europe says the seller is responsible for damage from dispatch until you receive the goods, and to contact the seller first. See [damaged parcel](guide:damaged-parcel) and [lost parcel](guide:lost-parcel).

**A complaint about the delivery.** trans-o-flex wants complaints in writing, through its contact form. For an urgent concern or a complaint, the form promises an answer within 48 hours (working days).

**Sent back.** If the shipment goes back to the sender, ask the sender for a refund or a new delivery. See [returned to sender](guide:returned-to-sender).

## EURODIS contact and customer service

- **EURODIS.** Its [contact page](https://eurodis.com/contact/) has a "Shipment request" form: choose the dispatch and delivery countries to find the contact for your shipment. For business enquiries and general questions, email contact@eurodis.com.
- **Germany: trans-o-flex.** Recipient Customer Service +49 6201 988 222, Monday to Friday from 7:00 to 17:00, German time. Or use the form "[Are you awaiting a consignment?](https://www.trans-o-flex.com/en/contact-persons/are-you-awaiting-a-consignment/)" and add your tracking number.
- **Austria: Österreichische Post.** Its hotline and forms are on our [Austrian Post](carrier:austrian-post) page.
- **The sender.** With trans-o-flex, only the sender can change the address or claim for damage or loss. The sender can also ask for another delivery attempt.

## Track a EURODIS shipment with Peek

**EURODIS's tracker.** Peek gets a EURODIS shipment's steps from EURODIS's tracking page on eurodis.com. If that page doesn't answer, Peek asks tracking services for the number instead. If none has it, the parcel reads "Waiting for EURODIS", and from Peek's second check on, its page says "EURODIS couldn’t be reached yet. Peek keeps trying." Peek keeps asking EURODIS and the tracking services on its own, but it can't contact EURODIS, its partners or the sender for you.

**The partner's number.** If the partner in your country gave you its own tracking number, add that to Peek as a parcel of its own: Peek follows more than 4,500 carriers, Austrian Post and Express One (Hungary) among them. If Peek doesn't name the partner, press "Change" and pick it. A trans-o-flex tracking link (trans-o-flex.com/sendungsverfolgung/tracking with `ref=` and the number) files the parcel under trans-o-flex, and Peek asks tracking services about it.

**A EURODIS number.** Peek can't tell a EURODIS number by its shape: no shape is EURODIS's alone. For 13 digits, Peek asks [FAN Courier](carrier:fan-courier) first, then tracking services ("Checking tracking services…"), and Delhivery, Econt, Ozon and Paquetexpress only if none knows the number; for 24 digits, [Austrian Post](carrier:austrian-post), InPost and Ciblex, then tracking services, and Express One only if none knows it; never EURODIS. If none knows it, the parcel reads "No tracking history yet". To file it under EURODIS, press "Change", search for EURODIS and pick it. On a parcel you've already added, use "Change carrier". A link to EURODIS's tracker on eurodis.com files the parcel under EURODIS at once, as the "Detected carrier". For EURODIS, Peek takes 13 digits, or 24 letters and digits with at least one digit. For anything else it says EURODIS "doesn’t issue numbers like this one".

**Hand-over, pace and alerts.** When EURODIS names Express One or Austrian Post for the delivery, Peek shows that member and its number, switches to its tracking once it shows a scan no older than EURODIS's newest, and then follows its pace; it doesn't follow the other members. While the parcel is open in Peek, or on its delivery day with notifications on, Peek checks up to every 10 minutes, and every 2 minutes from 6 a.m. on the latest scan's clock on the day the parcel is out for delivery, while EURODIS's tracker answers. After two days without news (12 hours while the parcel still shows "Label created" or "Received by carrier"), Peek checks hourly, and after seven days without news at "Label created", every 6 hours. After four days without a scan on a parcel at "Label created", "Received by carrier" or "In transit", Peek shows "No tracking update since …" and the note "Parcels often go quiet on long flights, at customs or over a weekend. Peek keeps asking EURODIS." and a "Get help from EURODIS" link. Notifications can follow "Every scan", "Important steps" or "Delivery only".

## Questions about EURODIS tracking

### How do I track a EURODIS shipment?

On EURODIS's [Track & Trace page](https://eurodis.com/track-trace/), with the 24-digit barcode, the 13-digit shipment number, the shipper's reference or the dispatch partner's number. Or paste the 13- or 24-digit number in Peek and pick EURODIS with "Change".

### Who delivers EURODIS shipments in Germany?

trans-o-flex, an express and logistics company based in Weinheim and EURODIS's member for Germany. Its Recipient Customer Service is on +49 6201 988 222, Monday to Friday from 7:00 to 17:00, German time. Its own tracking for recipients is trans-o-flex insight.

### Who delivers EURODIS shipments in Austria?

Österreichische Post (Austrian Post), says EURODIS's partner list. Its tracking and contacts are on our [Austrian Post](carrier:austrian-post) page.

### How do I contact EURODIS?

Use the "Shipment request" form on EURODIS's [contact page](https://eurodis.com/contact/): choose the dispatch and delivery countries to find the contact for your shipment. For business enquiries and general questions, email contact@eurodis.com.
