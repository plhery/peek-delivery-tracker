Evri is the UK parcel carrier known as Hermes UK until March 2022. Track a parcel on [evri.com](https://www.evri.com/track-a-parcel) with its 16-character tracking number or a calling card's 8-digit number, or paste the barcode or Evri's tracking link in the box above. The sender usually emails you the number; Evri's texts and emails carry it too.

## Evri tracking number: where to find it

Evri calls it a 16-character code or a 16-digit number, though its own example has letters. Look in the shop's shipping email or Evri's texts (from "Evri") and emails.

| Shape (invented) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `H00ABC1234567890` | Evri's barcode: `H` (sometimes `T`), five letters or digits, ten digits. |
| `1234567890123456` | Sixteen digits: may be Evri's; TNT and [DHL eCommerce](carrier:dhl-ecommerce) use them too. |
| `12340000` | A calling card number, which works on evri.com. |

An `H` followed only by digits (`H123456789012349`) also fits [Hermes in Germany](carrier:hermes-de): go by the carrier the shop names. International parcels need Evri's [international tracker](https://globaleco.app/track/). No number? See [how to find a tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number).

**Sent it yourself?** Evri says every parcel in its network is tracked, Standard delivery included. Your 16-digit number is in Evri's email and in your order history on evri.com. At a ParcelShop, give your email to get the digital receipt with tracking: without it you get neither, so photograph the label or note the barcode. Add your recipient's contact details when booking and Evri notifies them too. If tracking hasn't moved in 24 hours on Next Day, or 5 days on Standard, Evri asks senders to start a chat.

## What Evri's tracking statuses mean

Evri's tracking shows each stage of a parcel's journey, from collection to delivery. What Evri's own pages say about its statuses:

| Evri says | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `We're expecting it` | Evri hasn't received the parcel from the sender yet. |
| `on its way` (ParcelShop delivery) | It's moving through Evri's depots to your ParcelShop. You get a text or email when it's ready to collect. |
| `On its way back to the retailer` | A return's last update: Evri has handed it back to the shop and sends no more. |

If your tracking mentions an `Evri gateway`, the parcel came from abroad: Evri holds it there while it clears customs, then sends it on to a hub (see [parcels held at customs](guide:customs)). If Evri asks you to contact it, it needs more information to complete the delivery: start a chat on evri.com.

## Missed Evri delivery: calling cards, ParcelShops and lockers

**On the day.** The courier sets the expected time that morning. Once the parcel is out, Evri sends a window: its homepage says 1 hour, its help centre 2 hours.

**If you're out.** Where possible the courier leaves it in a safe place, even one you didn't choose, or posts it through the letterbox. Otherwise you get a calling card with an 8-digit number and the next attempt, normally the next day, up to three in all. After the last, Evri's safe-place guide says the parcel goes back to the depot; its terms, to the sender; and its sending page, for parcels sent through evri.com, to your nearest ParcelShop.

**Changing it.** On Evri's tracking page, enter the tracking or calling card number and your postcode to choose a safe place or neighbour or, where available, a ParcelShop or Locker, if the sender allows, until the courier reaches your doorstep. Only your last request counts. When the shop requires a signature there's no diversion. Evri can't change the day; only the sender can change the address.

**At a ParcelShop**, a local shop or Post Office branch, you get a text or email when the parcel is ready. Bring the sender's confirmation email or text if you have one, plus ID or the PIN the sender emails. Evri's ParcelShop page, written for senders, lists a debit or credit card, passport, UK driving licence or utility bill as accepted ID, and says a parcel waits 10 days, then goes back.

**Lockers** outside shops and supermarkets are open 24/7. Evri emails you when the parcel is ready, with a code or barcode to scan or key in at the Locker. You have 3 days to pick up. Stuck? Press "help" on the terminal.

**Collecting a home delivery** isn't possible: while a courier has your parcel, Evri says you can't pick it up from a ParcelShop or depot. The courier tries three times instead, or you divert it as above.

## Evri parcel late, lost or damaged

**No tracking yet** just means Evri hasn't received the parcel. If `We're expecting it` doesn't move, ask the sender; for any other status unchanged for 24 hours, start a chat. See [why tracking stops updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

**Still waiting** after 7 days with Evri? Tell the sender, who holds the contract and contacts Evri. If you bought from a business, Citizens Advice says delivery is the seller's responsibility.

**Delivered but not there.** Ask your household and neighbours, then start a chat so your local depot can investigate. More in [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).

**Damaged or lost.** Ask the sender for a refund or replacement: Evri can't give either, and [only the sender can claim](guide:damaged-parcel). Evri recommends senders claim within 28 days of the order for UK services, 45 for international.

## Evri contact number and customer service

Evri lists these UK channels (read 10 October 2026), and none for Ireland or elsewhere:

- **Chat** 24/7 on [evri.com/contact-us](https://www.evri.com/contact-us), with a reply in 24 hours or less.
- **Phone** 0330 808 5456, open 24/7, with advisors Monday to Friday 8am to 4pm and Saturday 8am to 2pm (other Evri pages differ). Call from the number linked to the parcel, or key in the tracking number's last 8 digits or the calling card number. GOV.UK says 03 numbers cost about the same as 01 and 02 numbers.
- **Tell us straight:** if Customer Services couldn't help, email noreply.priority@evri.com with that subject to reach Evri's senior team.

> Evri's emails typically come from @evri.com, @hermes-europe.co.uk or @myhermes.co.uk; its full list adds @custhelp.com, @globalco.uk, @globaleco.app and sub-domains of all six. Its texts come only from "Evri", never a mobile number, never ask you to reschedule a delivery, pay to do so or install an app, and link only to `evri.link` tracking, which Evri says can still be faked. Forward a suspect text to 7726 for free. More in [parcel scam texts](guide:delivery-scam-texts).

## Tracking an Evri parcel with Peek

[Peek](/) asks both Evri UK and Evri International about a typed barcode whose check digit adds up, as they use the same shape, and names the one that holds the parcel; for `H` and 15 digits it asks Hermes Germany too. If neither knows it yet, press "Change" and pick "Evri UK" under "Fits this number" (the "Evri" listed first is Evri International), or paste the `evri.com/track/parcel/…/details` link from Evri's page or email, which Peek reads as Evri UK at once. For 16 digits it asks Evri UK along with other carriers when the last digit checks out, and only the others when it doesn't. No postcode needed, but Peek can't track calling card numbers.

You see the status, the history, the sender's name when it's a business and, while out for delivery, Evri's window in UK time; no earlier date. Evri gives no scan places, so the map stays empty. A courier delivery shows as "Delivered", without where it was left. Any other scan naming the recipient reads "Delivery update", and the rest keep Evri's own sentence, not the headlines above. A parcel waiting at a ParcelShop or Locker shows as "Ready for pickup", and one collected from a ParcelShop as "Delivered". Your name, address, the delivery photo and collection codes are dropped.

Peek checks every 10 minutes by day, every 2 once out for delivery, and hourly overnight, and can notify you of every scan, important steps only or delivery day only. It doesn't follow parcels to other carriers.

## Questions about Evri tracking

### Is Evri the same as Hermes?

Yes: Evri is the former Hermes UK, renamed in March 2022. Hermes in Germany is a separate carrier.

### Is Evri part of DHL or Royal Mail?

Neither. In the deal the UK competition authority cleared in September 2025, Evri, then owned by funds managed by Apollo, was to buy DHL eCommerce UK from DHL Group, and DHL Group was to take a minority stake in Evri. Evri completed the merger on 1 October 2025, forming the Evri Group, and said DHL eCommerce UK would be renamed Evri Premium, a network of DHL, in 2026 and stay a separate network. Evri's tracker asks for a 16-digit tracking number or an 8-digit calling card number and says nothing about DHL parcels: for those, see [DHL eCommerce UK tracking](carrier:dhl-ecommerce-uk).

### Is an email from noreply.priority@evri.com genuine?

Evri gives it as an address to write to. On its contact page, the "Email us" button under Tell us straight opens an email to it: that's how you reach Evri's senior team once Customer Services has tried to help. Evri says it acknowledges those emails within one hour in normal office hours and, if it has to investigate, updates you every day. Its pages on genuine messages don't mention this address, so the address alone doesn't prove an email is from Evri. Scam emails often use a slightly misspelt address, so check it letter by letter. If you have any doubt, Evri says don't respond: contact it with the details on its website, not those in the message.

### How long does Evri take to deliver?

Evri says typically 1 to 3 days once it has the parcel, depending on the sender's service; its help centre says 1 to 2 days for next-day parcels, 3 to 5 for standard.

### Can I track an Evri parcel by postcode?

No. Evri asks for the tracking or calling card number; the postcode comes on top, to divert the parcel or see more.
