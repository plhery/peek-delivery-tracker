FAN Courier International is the cross-border service of the Romanian courier FAN Courier, built for online shops that sell across Europe. It carries parcels by road through its own partner network, and has its own tracker: enter the AWB number on [fancourier.eu's Track Delivery page](https://fancourier.eu/track-order). The AWB comes from the shop that sent the parcel, usually in its shipping email.

## FAN Courier International AWB: the tracking number

Like FAN Courier at home, FAN Courier International calls its tracking number the AWB (air waybill). The numbers its tracker answers are 21 characters: 16 digits, the letter `F` and 4 more digits.

| Number (made up) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `1234567890123456F0001` | FAN Courier International AWB: 16 digits, `F`, 4 digits. Track it on fancourier.eu. |
| `7000000000000` | FAN Courier AWB: 13 digits, no letter. This is a [FAN Courier](carrier:fan-courier) parcel, tracked on fancourier.ro. |

Look for it in the shop's shipping email or on your order page. FAN Courier International works only with businesses under contract, so the shop books the delivery and gets the AWB, and only the shop can give it to you. No number at all? See [how to find a tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number). For other shapes, see [tracking number formats](guide:tracking-number-formats).

If fancourier.eu doesn't find your AWB, FAN's FAQ explains why: the courier hasn't picked the parcel up yet, so nobody has scanned it. The shipment shows up once FAN collects it from the sender.

## What FAN Courier International's statuses mean

The tracker on fancourier.eu writes each step as a short English sentence. These are the ones you are most likely to see, roughly in order:

| FAN Courier International's status | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `Shipment data has entered the system` | The shop has created the AWB. FAN doesn't have the parcel yet. |
| `Shipment was successfully created` | Same: the shipment exists, but nobody has collected it. |
| `The shipment is ready to be picked up` | Ready for FAN's courier to collect **from the sender**. It is not waiting for you. |
| `The shipment is picked up` | FAN has collected the parcel from the shop. |
| `Received code: In Transit` | On its way between countries or depots. |
| `The shipment arrived in the hub: Timisoara` | Scanned at one of FAN's hubs. The town changes along the way. |
| `Received code: Delivery attempt` or `Delivery attempt failed` | The courier came but couldn't deliver. |
| `Received code: Postponed delivery` | The delivery was moved to another day. |
| `Received code: Delivery attempt - refused` | Delivery refused; FAN's tracker links this one to a refused cash-on-delivery payment. The parcel goes back to the shop. |
| `Received code: Damaged` | FAN has recorded damage. Contact the shop. |
| `The shipment has been canceled` | The shipment was cancelled. Ask the shop what happens to your order. |
| `The shipment has been delivered` | Delivered. |

The two "created" lines and "ready to be picked up" all mean the same thing for you: the parcel is still with the shop. If one of them doesn't move for several days, ask the shop when it will hand the parcel over. More about [what tracking statuses mean](guide:tracking-statuses).

## Delivery, lockers and missed deliveries

FAN Courier International lists the countries it delivers to on its price list: Romania, Bulgaria, Greece, Hungary, Czechia, Slovakia, Slovenia, Italy, Croatia, Lithuania, Latvia, Estonia, Moldova, Cyprus, Poland, Austria, Germany, the Netherlands, Belgium, France, Sweden, Denmark, Luxembourg, Spain, Ireland, Finland and Portugal. Its site also lists its own international locations, in Romania (including an international hub in Arad), Moldova, Bulgaria, Hungary, Slovenia, Greece, Czechia, Slovakia, Croatia, Poland, Austria and Germany.

FAN's general conditions describe three ways the parcel reaches you:

- **At your address**, to you or to someone allowed to receive it. You prove who you are with your ID card. For parcels delivered in Romania, FAN also sends a PIN code by SMS; in other countries it doesn't use one.
- **At a staffed contact point**, after a notice telling you the parcel has arrived.
- **In a locker.** Lockers are served every day of the week. FAN keeps the parcel there for at least 36 hours from the notice, and the notice tells you the exact deadline. If the locker is out of order, the deadline moves on by the time it was down.

**Not home.** If the courier can't find you, or you don't collect the parcel by the deadline, FAN notifies you electronically and keeps it for 5 calendar days at a locker or a staffed point. After that it goes back to the shop: FAN allows itself 12 working days for the return within the EU and EEA, 14 outside them.

**Changing the address.** FAN treats a new address as a new delivery, which takes 24 hours on average, so it won't arrive the same day.

**Paying the courier.** Cash on delivery is paid in the local currency of the country it's delivered to. You can't open the parcel before you pay, unless the shop paid for the "Opening on delivery" option. If you refuse the parcel or the payment, FAN sends it straight back to the shop without holding it for you.

**Saturday.** FAN's Special Delivery service can bring the parcel to your address or a staffed point on a Saturday, when the shop asked for it.

**Customs.** Moldova is the only country on the list outside the EU, and FAN's conditions say its international parcels follow customs law. If a parcel comes into the EU from Moldova, it goes through customs: one worth up to €150 pays a €3 customs duty per kind of item since 1 July 2026, and a €2 Union handling fee per kind of item since 1 November 2026, whatever its value, owed by whoever declares the goods. See [parcels at customs](guide:customs).

## Parcel delayed, lost or damaged

FAN Courier International promises delivery within 12 working days in the EU and EEA and 14 working days elsewhere, Moldova included. Public holidays in the sending and receiving countries don't count. If the tracking hasn't moved for days, write to the shop first: it holds the contract with FAN and can chase the parcel. See [tracking that isn't updating](guide:tracking-not-updating) and [lost parcels](guide:lost-parcel).

**Damaged.** Keep the box, the packaging and the damaged item, and take photos: FAN asks for that kind of evidence with a complaint, along with a copy of the AWB.

**Complaints** can come from the sender or the recipient, within 6 months of the day the parcel was posted. Send yours by email to customer@fancourier.eu, or by post (ideally with confirmation of receipt) to FAN Courier International, Strada Linia de Centura nr. 66, Ștefăneștii de Jos, Ilfov, Romania. Describe what happened, add the AWB and any photos. FAN must resolve it within 3 months, and pays compensation to the sender within 30 days of agreeing to it. If you aren't satisfied, you can complain to the postal regulator or go to court within a year of the posting date. If the parcel came back to the shop, see [returned to sender](guide:returned-to-sender).

## FAN Courier International contact

FAN Courier International's channels, as listed on fancourier.eu on 14 November 2026:

- **Email** customer@fancourier.eu, for questions about a shipment and for complaints.
- **Contact form** on [fancourier.eu/contact](https://fancourier.eu/contact). It asks for the AWB number.
- **Hours** Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00.

fancourier.eu gives an email address and a form, not a phone number. For a parcel sent with a 13-digit FAN Courier AWB, use [FAN Courier's](carrier:fan-courier) own channels instead.

## Track a FAN Courier International parcel with Peek

Paste the AWB in the box above. A number of 16 digits, `F` and 4 digits fits only FAN Courier International, so [Peek](/) asks it while you add the parcel: the carrier line reads "asking FAN Courier International…", then "FAN Courier International has this parcel." once FAN knows the number. A brand-new AWB may show "not found yet · Peek keeps checking", and Peek goes on asking after you add it. To pick the carrier yourself, press "Change": FAN Courier International is listed under "Fits this number", and the search also finds it as "FAN Courier (EU)".

A 13-digit AWB is a FAN Courier number: Peek asks [FAN Courier](carrier:fan-courier) about it, along with other carriers whose numbers can look the same, never FAN Courier International. If you choose FAN Courier International for one anyway, the carrier line says it "doesn’t issue numbers like this one", and Peek doesn't ask it. A link copied from fancourier.eu holds no number Peek can read, so paste the AWB itself.

Peek shows each step with FAN's English wording, the hub it names and its time, in your device's time zone; on peektracker.com, the history ends with "Times are in your time zone:" and its name. It checks every 10 minutes while FAN's tracker answers (every 15 minutes if it has to go through tracking services), and can notify you when the status changes. After 12 hours with only "Label created" or "Received by carrier", or two days without news, it checks hourly.

Peek shows no expected delivery day and no locker name, and leaves out the sender's and recipient's names and the parcel's weight. It doesn't pick up a return number from FAN's tracker: if the parcel goes back under a new number, add that number to follow it. Peek can't contact FAN or the shop for you.

## Questions about FAN Courier International tracking

### Why can't fancourier.eu find my AWB?

FAN hasn't scanned the parcel yet: its FAQ says an AWB enters its system only once the courier picks the parcel up from the sender. Check the number for typos, then wait a day or ask the shop when it will hand the parcel over.

### Does "The shipment is ready to be picked up" mean I can collect it?

No. It means the parcel is ready for FAN's courier to collect from the shop. Your parcel hasn't left yet. When a locker or contact point holds it for you, FAN sends you a notice.

### Is FAN Courier International the same as FAN Courier?

It's FAN Courier's cross-border service, run by FAN Courier International S.R.L. with its own site, tracker and AWBs. Its AWBs have 16 digits, an `F` and 4 digits, and you track them on fancourier.eu. A 13-digit AWB belongs to [FAN Courier](carrier:fan-courier) and is tracked on fancourier.ro.

### How long does FAN Courier International take?

At most 12 working days within the EU and EEA, and 14 outside them, Moldova included, according to FAN's conditions. Public holidays don't count.

### Can I open the parcel before paying?

Not usually. FAN hands over sealed parcels, and opening one before you pay isn't allowed, unless the shop paid for "Opening on delivery".
