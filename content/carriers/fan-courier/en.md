FAN Courier is a Romanian courier founded in 1998, with FANbox lockers across the country; it calls itself Romania's courier leader since 2006. Track a parcel with its AWB number on [fancourier.ro's AWB Tracking page](https://www.fancourier.ro/en/awb-track/), with the link in FAN's SMS or in the FAN Courier app. The AWB comes from the shop or person who sent the parcel.

## FAN Courier AWB: the tracking number

AWB (Air Waybill) is FAN's word for the shipment number. FAN doesn't publish its length, but the AWBs its tracker answers are 13 digits, like the invented `2123456789012`.

Where to find it:

- **The shop's shipping email** or order page. FAN's advice is to ask the sender or the store.
- **FAN's SMS.** When the courier collects the parcel, FAN texts you a secure link to follow the delivery, which also shows your PIN. On the delivery day another SMS gives an estimated time and the courier's contact.

No number at all? See [how to find a tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number).

`The AWB has been registered by sender` means the shop has created the AWB but hasn't handed the parcel over yet. `The AWB was not found` can mean a typo, or a parcel FAN hasn't collected: FAN registers it automatically once it collects it from the sender.

Lookalikes: [Econt](carrier:econt) numbers are also 13 digits, [Cargus](carrier:cargus) calls its numbers AWBs too, and [Sameday](carrier:sameday) AWBs start with `1` and letters (`1ABC123456789`). Go by the courier the email names. More in [tracking number formats](guide:tracking-number-formats).

## What FAN Courier's tracking statuses mean

FAN's tracking opens in Romanian with an English switch. The page shows one "Shipment status" line and four milestones (`Picked up`, `In transit`, `In delivery`, `Delivered`), each with a date. These are the English wordings, in the order a parcel meets them:

| FAN Courier's status | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `The AWB has been registered by sender` | The shop [made the AWB](guide:tracking-statuses); FAN doesn't have the parcel yet. |
| `Delivered to FANbox` | The sender dropped it at a FANbox for a courier to collect. Not your delivery. |
| `Picked up` | FAN collected it from the sender. |
| `In transit` | Moving between FAN's depots. |
| `On hold. The shipment will leave the country on the next transport.` | Going abroad, waiting for the next transport. |
| `In delivery` | With the courier on today's round. |
| `Recipient notified` | Not delivered: the courier tried and FAN notified you. |
| `Redirected` | Sent to a new address or locker under a new AWB, shown on the page. |
| `Delivered` | Delivered. |
| `Rejected delivery` | The recipient refused it; it goes back to the sender. |
| `Returned` | On its way back to the sender, under a return AWB of its own. |

Two trip people up. `Picked up` is FAN picking the parcel up from the sender, not you collecting it. `Delivered to FANbox` (and `Delivered to Collect Point`) is the start of the journey: the page's tooltip says the order to pick it up from the locker has been placed with the courier. FAN's tracking has no step saying the parcel waits for you in a FANbox; that news comes by SMS, email or the app, with your PIN.

Each scan has its own wording, such as `Shipment sorted`, or `Shipment taken over for delivery` and `Delivery in process` (both out for delivery), which the page sums up as above. It hides anything after a delivery.

## FAN Courier delivery, FANbox and missed deliveries

Standard parcels arrive in 1 to 3 working days from collection, FAN says. Couriers deliver Monday to Friday 9:00 to 17:00, and on Saturday 9:00 to 14:00 only when the sender chose Saturday delivery.

**Not home.** FAN includes two delivery attempts. The courier tries again the next day; with the link in the SMS or in the app you can pick another day, send the parcel to a FANbox or change the address. A new address counts as a new delivery and usually takes 24 to 48 hours, and needs the sender's consent. You can also ask to pick it up at a FAN office, with your ID, a passport or the SMS PIN.

If FAN can't deliver, its general conditions say it notifies you and keeps the parcel at a locker or staffed point for 5 calendar days from the notice (its Standard page says up to 7).

**FANbox lockers.** Outdoor lockers open 24/7. The SMS holds a secure link and a four-digit code that shows your PIN; the email and app show the PIN and a QR code to scan at the locker. You have 48 hours from the notification. The FAN Courier app sells extensions of 24, 48 or 72 hours or 5 days, before time runs out. A parcel sent straight to a FANbox and not picked up goes back to the sender; one redirected there waits at the nearest FAN branch. Cash on delivery is paid by card at the locker, and once the door opens after paying you can't refuse the parcel.

**Collect Point** (PayPoint Colet Expres shops): prepaid parcels only, up to 10 kg. Bring ID and the 5-character code from the SMS or email; the shop keeps it 7 calendar days.

**Paying the courier.** Card works only if the shop allows it. You can't open the parcel before paying unless the sender bought "Check on Delivery".

## FAN Courier parcel delayed, lost or damaged

FAN gives no number of days after which to worry. If nothing moves, ask the shop: it holds the contract with FAN and can open a "Delivery Delay" ticket or email FAN with the AWB, contents and value. See [tracking that isn't updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

**Damaged at the door:** ask the courier for a damage report, then accept or refuse the parcel. Wrong item inside? Contact the sender: FAN doesn't check contents.

**Complaints** can come from the sender or the recipient, by phone, email, in writing at any FAN location or by registered post. FAN's conditions set the deadlines: complain within 6 months of posting, FAN answers within 3 months and pays within 30 days of agreeing. Compensation goes to the sender. If you're not satisfied, you can go to ANCOM, Romania's postal regulator, or to court within a year of posting. ANCOM can look into FAN's conduct and sanction it, but can't make it pay compensation.

## Tracking FAN Courier parcels with Peek

[Peek](/) asks FAN Courier itself about a 13-digit number starting with `2`, `4` or `7000`, along with [La Poste](carrier:la-poste) and Delhivery, whose numbers can look the same. If only FAN knows it, you'll see "FAN Courier has this parcel."; if another carrier knows it too, you choose. An AWB of any other shape isn't recognised: paste it all the same (Peek follows more than 3,500 carriers), then press "Change" and pick "FAN Courier". A link from FAN's tracking page, Romanian (`/awb-tracking/`) or English (`/en/awb-track/`), with `tracking=` or `awb=`, goes straight to FAN Courier.

Peek shows each of FAN's scans with its town and time. FAN's times carry no time zone, so Peek reads them as Romanian time. Scans keep FAN's English wording, except `Shipment picked up`, which shows as "Shipment collected". Peek checks every 10 minutes by day, every 2 while the parcel is out for delivery, and hourly overnight, and can notify you when the status changes.

It shows no expected delivery day and no FANbox name, and leaves out the recipient's name, contents, weight and payment details. A return travels under its own AWB, so this one never shows as returned.

## Questions about FAN Courier tracking

### Is FAN Courier international?

Yes: FAN offers road transport to most EU countries and air transport worldwide, and has offices in Moldova. Its conditions allow up to 12 working days within the EU and EEA, 14 elsewhere.

### Does FAN Courier deliver on Saturday?

Only if the sender chose the Saturday delivery option, between 9:00 and 14:00.

### Can I track a FAN Courier parcel without the AWB?

Not on FAN's tracker, which needs the AWB or a reference. Ask the shop, or open FAN's SMS link.
