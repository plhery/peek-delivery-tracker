To track a **FedEx Poland** domestic parcel, paste your 13-digit number starting with `6` (or a FedEx Poland domestic tracking link) into the box above, or use FedEx's Polish domestic tracking page on `fedex.com`. FedEx's worldwide tracker does not accept 13-digit FedEx Poland numbers, and Peek reads FedEx's Polish branch scans and delivery-day time windows directly.

## FedEx Poland tracking number: 13 digits

The number is in the shop's shipping email or on your order page. A FedEx Poland number has 13 digits, starts with 6 and ends in a check digit. FedEx's domestic tracking page takes only 13-digit numbers, and FedEx's worldwide tracking page doesn't take them at all.

| Shape (invented) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `6123456789016` | 13 digits starting with 6: a FedEx Poland domestic parcel. |
| `784291736480` | 12 digits: a number for FedEx's worldwide tracking page, not FedEx Poland. See the [FedEx](carrier:fedex) page. |

No number? Ask the shop. More in [where to find a tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number).

## What FedEx Poland statuses mean

FedEx writes its domestic scans in Polish, each with its branch. Next to the number, FedEx's tracking page sums up the parcel in a one-line status: `W ODDZIALE FEDEX` (at a FedEx branch), `WYDANA DO DORĘCZENIA` (out for delivery) or `DORĘCZONA` (delivered). The scans, in order:

| FedEx says | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `Przesyłka przygotowana przez nadawcę do odbioru przez kuriera FedEx.` | The sender has made the label and is waiting for a FedEx courier. FedEx doesn't have it yet. |
| `Kurier odebrał przesyłkę od nadawcy.` | A FedEx courier picked it up from the sender. |
| `Przesyłka w oddziale FedEx.` | It's at a FedEx branch. Every branch scan reads the same, so it can repeat. |
| `Przesyłka wydana do doręczenia kurierowi FedEx.` | It's with your courier for delivery today. |
| `Dyspozycja zmiany terminu doręczenia` | A request to change the delivery date. The parcel didn't move. |
| `Nieudana próba doręczenia. Przesyłka awizowana.` | The courier missed you and left a notice. |
| `Nieudana próba doręczenia. Odbiorca odmówił przyjęcia przesyłki.` | Delivery failed: the recipient refused the parcel. |
| `Zwrot przesyłki przez kuriera do oddziału FedEx.` | The courier took it back to the branch undelivered. It can go out again. |
| `Kurier doręczył przesyłkę do odbiorcy.` | Delivered. `Przesyłka doręczona do odbiorcy.` says the same. |
| `Zwrot przesyłki do nadawcy.` | It's going back to the sender. |

On delivery day, FedEx may add a planned slot under the out-for-delivery scan: `Planowane doręczenie w przedziale godzinowym` and two times, the window when the courier expects to reach you. The general stages are explained in [tracking statuses](guide:tracking-statuses), and what to do after a notice in [missed deliveries](guide:missed-delivery).

## FedEx Poland parcel not updating, lost, damaged or returned

**Only the label scan.** The sender has a label but FedEx doesn't have the parcel. Ask the shop when it will hand it over.

**No movement for days.** Ask the shop that sent it, and see [why tracking stops updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

**Bought online?** Until you receive the parcel, the shop is responsible if it's lost or damaged, as long as the shop chose FedEx or offered it among its delivery options. If FedEx damages it, you complain to the shop, which should then claim from FedEx. For a lost parcel, only the sender can complain to FedEx itself, unless the sender hands the right to claim over to you.

**Damaged.** If the damage shows on the outside, you can refuse the parcel, or accept it and open it in front of the courier to write a damage report (protokół szkody), which then backs your complaint. Damage you couldn't see from outside must be reported to FedEx within 7 days of receiving the parcel. More in [damaged parcels](guide:damaged-parcel).

**Refused or returned.** After a refused delivery, `Zwrot przesyłki do nadawcy.` shows the parcel going back to the sender. See [returned to sender](guide:returned-to-sender).

**Delivered but not there.** Ask the people you live with. FedEx's proof of delivery needs the payer's account number, so ask the sender for the proof. More in [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).

**Complaints.** In Poland, courier parcels fall under postal law, overseen by UKE, the postal regulator. You can complain to FedEx in writing, electronically (for example through a form on the operator's website) or orally, within 12 months of posting. The sender can complain, and so can the recipient once the parcel is delivered or the sender hands over the right. FedEx has 30 days to answer; no answer in time means the complaint is accepted. You can appeal a refusal within 14 days. If you're still not satisfied, ask the President of UKE to intervene, use UKE's free out-of-court dispute resolution (ADR), or go to court.

## Track a FedEx Poland parcel with Peek

[Peek](/) can't tell a FedEx Poland number by its shape: 13 digits starting with 6 also fit Correos Chile, Delhivery, Yunda and Korea Post. Paste it all the same: Peek follows more than 4,500 carriers. When you add it and the last digit passes the number's check, Peek asks FedEx Poland first and shows "FedEx Poland has this parcel." once it knows the number. If none knows it yet or several do, pick FedEx Poland with "Change"; typing "Opek" or "FedEx Polska" in the search finds it too. Peek suggests FedEx Poland only when the last digit passes the number's check, but you can pick it by hand.

A link from FedEx's domestic tracking page, on fds2tracking.emea.fedex.com or fedex.com, or a poland.fedex.com link with `packageId=`, opens as FedEx Poland straight away.

Peek reads FedEx's domestic tracking service directly. It shows the status, each scan in FedEx's Polish words with its branch and exact time, the courier's time slot on delivery day, the delivered time and the weight. It drops the number of pieces, the sending and delivering branches, the dispatch date, the return number and the proof of delivery; FedEx shows no sender, name or address there anyway. While you have the parcel open in Peek, or on its delivery day with notifications on, Peek checks up to every 10 minutes, and up to every 2 minutes from 6 a.m. Polish time on the day FedEx scans the parcel out for delivery, while FedEx's own tracking answers; when FedEx gives a slot, from half an hour before it opens. Otherwise, and after two days without news (12 hours while the parcel still shows "Label created" or "Received by carrier"), Peek checks hourly, and every 6 hours after seven days without news at "Label created". It can notify you of every scan, important steps or delivery day only. Peek can't change a delivery or complain for you: that's done with FedEx or the sender.

## Questions about FedEx Poland tracking

### Why does my FedEx Poland number have 13 digits?

Because it's a FedEx Poland domestic parcel (13 digits starting with `6` and ending in a check digit). FedEx's worldwide tracking page doesn't take 13-digit domestic Polish numbers — only international formats such as 12-digit [FedEx](carrier:fedex) numbers work there.

### Why doesn't FedEx Poland's tracker recognise my number?

FedEx's domestic page reports unknown or very old delivered numbers as not recognised, so check all 13 digits against the shop's email or ask the sender. If the message refers you to customer service instead, the parcel may exist without public web tracking.

### What do Polish scans like `Przesyłka awizowana` or `Wydana do doręczenia` mean?

`Wydana do doręczenia` (`Przesyłka wydana do doręczenia kurierowi FedEx.`) means the parcel is out with the courier today (often with a planned time window under `Planowane doręczenie w przedziale godzinowym`). `Nieudana próba doręczenia. Przesyłka awizowana.` means the courier missed you and left a notice, while `Zwrot przesyłki do nadawcy.` means it is returning to the sender.

### How do I complain about a lost or damaged FedEx Poland parcel?

If you bought online, contact the shop first — under Polish law (UOKiK), the shop stays responsible until delivery when it chose or offered FedEx. Report hidden damage within 7 days of delivery (for visible damage, refuse it or open it with the courier to write a *protokół szkody*). Under UKE postal rules, formal complaints can be filed within 12 months of posting; FedEx has 30 days to reply, with 14 days to appeal and free UKE ADR afterwards.

### Is a text asking me to pay for a FedEx delivery real?

It may be a scam. CERT Polska (CSIRT NASK) warns of fake courier texts linking to phishing sites that demand an extra redelivery fee to steal card details. Type your number into the box above instead of tapping links, contact FedEx only via its official website, forward suspicious texts to `8080`, and alert your bank immediately if you entered card details. See [delivery scam texts](guide:delivery-scam-texts).
