Foxpost is a Hungarian parcel service that delivers to its parcel lockers, and to the door, across Hungary; since January 2026 it has also absorbed Packeta Hungary. Track a parcel on [foxpost.hu](https://foxpost.hu/csomagkovetes/) or paste its number in the box above. The number is at the end of the email Foxpost sends when the parcel is created, and usually starts with `CLFOX`.

## Foxpost tracking number

Foxpost's tracking box asks for one of three kinds of ID:

| Number | Whose it is |
| --- | --- |
| `CLFOX123456789012345` | `CLFOX` and digits: a parcel going to a Foxpost locker or pickup point. Foxpost also calls it the parcel's barcode. |
| `Z1234567890` | `Z` and digits: a Packeta parcel. Foxpost's tracker sends a Z number it doesn't know on to Packeta's tracking. |
| `6663900000` | Digits starting `66639`: a parcel you chose to have delivered to your door. |

Foxpost's email to you and the sender ends with the parcel ID. With a Foxpost account, it also appears under "Bejövő csomagjaim" (incoming parcels). When the parcel reaches the locker, the opening code comes by Viber (else SMS) and email.

Can't find any of it? Ask the shop, and see [how to find your tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number). If the number starts with `Z`, it's a [Packeta](carrier:packeta) number.

## What Foxpost tracking statuses mean

Foxpost's tracker shows each step as a short title, a date and time, and a sentence. The site is Hungarian; switch it to English in the menu and the same steps appear in English, such as `Your package has arrived at the (return) parcel locker you selected and is now ready for pickup!`. The Hungarian sentences, in the order a parcel meets them:

| Foxpost's status | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `Csomagod létrejött a rendszerünkben, a feladó még nem adta át azt a FOXPOST részére.` | [The label exists](guide:tracking-statuses); the sender hasn't handed the parcel over yet. |
| `Csomagod fel lett adva, a csomagautomatában el lett helyezve. A neked címzett (feladott/visszaküldött) csomagot hamarosan kézbesítjük.` | The sender dropped it in a locker; it isn't in yours yet. |
| `Csomagodat futárunk kivette a csomagautomatából.` | A courier took it out of the drop-off locker. |
| `Csomagod beérkezett a FOXPOST raktárába. A feladott/visszaküldött csomag hamarosan kézbesítésre kerül.` | At Foxpost's warehouse. |
| `Csomagodat az automata telítettsége / műszaki hibája miatt átírányítottuk egy másik csomagautomatába` | Sent to another locker because yours is full or broken; its name follows. |
| `Csomagod megérkezett az általad választott (vissza)kézbesítési automatába, most már átveheted!` | Ready for pickup: use the code from the message. |
| `Csomagod (vissza)kézbesítve lett, a címzett/feladó átvette.` | Picked up: delivered. |
| `Csomagod nem került átvételre a tárolási idő alatt, ezért a küldeményt visszaküldjük a feladónak.` | Not picked up within the hold time; it's going back to the sender. |
| `Csomagot a címzett nem vette át, a csomag vissza lett küldve a feladónak.` | Not collected and sent back to the sender. |

Read the sentence, not the title: "Automatában" (in parcel locker) covers both the sender's drop-off and your parcel waiting for you. The "(vissza)" and "címzett/feladó" are there because the same text serves returns, so "received by the recipient/sender" means you picked it up.

## Foxpost lockers, pickup and missed deliveries

**Picking up.** At an A-BOX locker, enter the code from the message on the screen. At a Z-BOX (keypad), type the 6-digit opening code. Anyone with the code can open the door. At a staffed Z-Pont pickup point, give the password; anyone you send can pick it up, with no ID unless the shop asks for it.

**How long it waits.** Foxpost keeps a parcel in a locker for 5 calendar days, weekends and public holidays included; the exact deadline, to the minute, is in the message. It can't extend that. A broken locker extends it automatically. At a Z-Pont, Foxpost's terms give 7 days, 5 days from 1 October to 20 January (its pickup page says 7 days all year, so go by the date in the message), and customer service can extend it by phone. After the deadline the parcel goes back to the sender; if you bought it from a shop, contact the shop.

**Cash on delivery.** An A-BOX takes card only, at its terminal. A Z-BOX takes payment only through the link in Foxpost's emails, and the opening code comes after you pay. A Z-Pont takes cash or card.

**Full or broken locker.** Foxpost may send the parcel to another locker nearby or a Z-Pont; you can accept or refuse that in its notice. If the code doesn't open the door, start again, then call customer service.

**DPD parcels.** Since October 2026 you can choose a Foxpost locker for a parcel DPD handles, at the shop's checkout. Foxpost notifies you once DPD has handed it over and it has reached Foxpost's warehouse. Track it with DPD's 14-digit number on DPD's tracking, or with its `CLFOX` number on Foxpost's. It waits 5 calendar days like any other, then goes back to DPD.

**Home delivery.** A partner courier makes two attempts on consecutive working days, announced by SMS and email. The email's link changes the day or address but uses up one attempt. A failed attempt brings an email and SMS, not a paper card. If the parcel still isn't taken, it goes back to the sender.

## Foxpost parcel delayed, damaged or lost

**No update.** Foxpost gives no delivery guarantee: locker to locker can take as little as one working day, home delivery usually two or three. Once a status hasn't changed for 3 days, file a parcel enquiry ("Csomag utáni érdeklődés") on Foxpost's contact forms: it starts a search only then and looks into it within 30 days. See also [tracking that isn't updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

**Damaged.** Note visible damage at delivery. Otherwise report it in writing within 3 working days of delivery. Photograph the packaging, the inside and the item, and send them with Foxpost's claim form.

**Lost.** A domestic parcel not delivered within 15 days of posting needs a complaint from the sender. The sender claims, unless the parcel was delivered to you, the sender assigned you the claim in writing, or you can show the order confirmation with the full price paid. Claims for loss or damage are due in writing within 6 months, counted from the 15th day after posting. If you disagree with Foxpost's answer, you have 30 days to turn to the NMHH (Hungary's communications authority) or, as a consumer, a conciliation board.

- **In person:** 1097 Budapest, Könyves Kálmán krt. 12-14, green staircase, 2nd floor, Thursdays only, 8:00 to 20:00.
- **Written:** the [contact forms](https://foxpost.hu/kapcsolatfelveteli-urlapok?pageType=c2c), including an enquiry about a parcel ("Csomag utáni érdeklődés"), and the [claim form](https://foxpost.hu/reklamacio?pageType=c2c). Foxpost publishes no customer email address. For a Packeta parcel, use Packeta's help instead.

> Foxpost writes only from foxpost.hu addresses. A link that doesn't end in foxpost.hu is a scam, Foxpost never asks for bank login details, and it doesn't phone to ask you to "confirm delivery": hang up.

## Tracking Foxpost parcels with Peek

[Peek](/) names Foxpost straight away for a `CLFOX` number. A 10-digit home-delivery number is offered to other carriers, never Foxpost: pick "Foxpost" with "Change", or paste a foxpost.hu tracking link with `code=` (from /csomagkovetes/ or /en/parcel-tracking), which names Foxpost whatever the number. A `Z` number goes to Packeta.

Peek lists Foxpost's own history, reading its times as Budapest time. Statuses stay in Foxpost's Hungarian, whatever language you read Peek in. Peek can show the sender (usually the shop) and the locker the parcel is heading to or waits at, and can notify you when the status changes. There's no expected delivery date or weight, and the order number and payment details are left out.

## Questions about Foxpost tracking

### Is Foxpost the same as Packeta?

They're now one company in Hungary: Packeta Hungary merged into FoxPost Kft. on 1 January 2026, and the site calls itself "FOXPOST – Packeta Group".

### Is there Foxpost in Poland?

No. Foxpost delivers in Hungary only, and its FAQ says sending a Foxpost parcel abroad to a Packeta point isn't possible yet.

### Can I pay cash on delivery at a Foxpost locker?

Not in cash. An A-BOX takes card at its terminal and a Z-BOX takes payment through the link in Foxpost's email; only a Z-Pont or the home-delivery courier takes cash.

### How long does Foxpost delivery take?

Foxpost says locker to locker can take as little as one working day after collection and home delivery usually two or three, without guaranteeing either.
