GLS Germany delivers on weekdays and, by its group's count, has 9,000 ParcelShops and 200 Parcelstations in Germany. Track a parcel on [GLS's tracking page](https://gls-group.com/EU/en/parcel-tracking) with its parcel number and the recipient's postcode, or paste the number in the box above. The number is in the shop's email, and GLS's notification card carries a Track-ID.

## GLS tracking number and Track-ID: where to find them

Check the shop's shipping email first. GLS's notification card, on paper or by email, carries a Track-ID of six letters, which works like the parcel number in GLS's tracking.

Peek knows these shapes:

| Number (invented) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `11111111111` | 11 digits. Also fits [GLS Switzerland](carrier:gls-ch), [GLS France](carrier:gls-fr), GLS Austria, GLS Poland, PostLogistics and others. |
| `123456789011` | 12 digits, the last a check digit. Also fits [BRT](carrier:brt), [Mondial Relay](carrier:mondial-relay), Colis Privé and others. |
| `AB123456` | 8 letters and digits. Also fits GLS Switzerland, GLS France and Loggi. |
| Track-ID | Six letters, on the notification card. Peek reads it like a parcel number; if it doesn't take yours, use the parcel number. |

Number refused? If the shop's email names another carrier, the number is theirs: see [tracking number formats](guide:tracking-number-formats).

## What GLS tracking statuses mean

GLS's English wordings, in the order a parcel meets them:

| GLS says | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `Preadvice` | GLS has the sender's data, not yet the parcel. |
| `The parcel was handed over to GLS.` | GLS has it. |
| `In transit` | Moving between GLS parcel centres. |
| `Final parcel center` | At the centre that delivers it: delivery is next. |
| `In delivery`, `The parcel is in delivery.` | In the GLS van, expected today. |
| `Not delivered` | A delivery attempt failed, for example because nobody was home. |
| `The parcel is ready for collection at the ParcelShop.` | Waiting for you at a ParcelShop or Parcelstation. |
| `The parcel has been delivered.`, `Delivered` | Delivered to you or to someone who took it in for you, such as a neighbour. |

GLS counts a ParcelShop arrival as delivered, though the parcel is still waiting for you there. `Preadvice` lasts until the sender hands the parcel over, so ask the sender.

## GLS delivery times, ParcelShops and parcel lockers

**When.** Monday to Friday, never Saturday, within estimated delivery times that GLS doesn't guarantee.

**Nobody home.** The driver may hand the parcel to someone at your home or business, or to a neighbour in the same or the nearest building, unless you or the sender said otherwise; a card or email names the neighbour. Otherwise GLS asks you to collect it from the nearest ParcelShop, depot or Parcelstation. GLS makes at most two delivery attempts.

**Changing it.** Where GLS offers delivery options, you can pick another day or address, a ParcelShop or collection at the depot; GLS's consumer information from January 2018 says they're free for recipients. A drop-off permit lets the driver leave parcels at a place you named, and GLS tells you by card or email when it has.

**ParcelShops** keep a parcel left after a missed delivery at least seven working days, say GLS's terms for business senders. Uncollected parcels go back to the sender.

**Parcelstations** are GLS's lockers, and you need your own device to use one. You can object to GLS leaving a parcel in one, once or for good: the missed-delivery notice says how.

## GLS parcel not delivered, late or damaged

GLS's contract is with the sender, so refunds go through the shop.

**No update.** Ask the sender, and see [why tracking stops updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

**Delivered but not there.** Check the tracking for a ParcelShop, and your letterbox and email for GLS's card naming a neighbour or drop-off place. More in [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).

**Damaged.** German law (Commercial Code, § 438) wants visible damage noted at delivery, hidden damage reported within 7 days and delay within 21, in text form. A shop selling to you carries the transit risk unless you hired the carrier yourself (Civil Code, § 475), so ask the shop first.

**Disputes.** Loss or damage you can't settle with GLS can go to the Bundesnetzagentur's Schlichtungsstelle Post.

## GLS Germany customer service and contact

For a parcel from a shop, the shop is your first contact. GLS Germany's own contact options are on gls-pakete.de, its German site.

> GLS's group warns of fake emails, SMS and websites in its name. Its main website is gls-group.com, and only that site and the sites it links to, such as gls-pakete.de, are genuine. GLS never asks recipients by email to pay for goods by card, transfer or online payment. Send suspicious emails as an attachment to security@gls-group.com.

## Tracking a GLS Germany parcel with Peek

[Peek](/) asks the likely carriers about the number shapes above. GLS answers for German and Swiss parcels alike, and Peek names GLS Germany ("GLS Germany has this parcel"), or GLS France if it knows the parcel. In Switzerland or Liechtenstein (by your iPhone's region or web connection) it names GLS Switzerland, with a 4-digit postcode field: for a parcel to Germany, press "Change" and pick GLS Germany. A pasted gls-pakete.de link, or a gls-group one with /DE/, /EU/ or /GROUP/, also names GLS Germany.

GLS needs the delivery postcode for the detailed history, so the "Delivery postcode" field is required: without it Peek only learns whether GLS knows the number, and if GLS doesn't find the parcel with the one you gave, Peek says so and lets you change it. Any country's format works. It goes to GLS and, when needed, to tracking services.

You then see the status, each scan's town and country on a map, the expected day, the weight, the delivery time and, at a ParcelShop, its name and address. Your name, address and contact details, the signature and the sender's reference are dropped. GLS's ParcelShop status shows as ready for pickup. Times are read as German time. Peek checks GLS about once an hour by day and every hour or two overnight, delivery day included (4 hours after a failed check), and can notify you of each change. Only a parcel GLS hands to Swiss Post is followed to another carrier.

## Questions about GLS tracking in Germany

### Where is my GLS package?

GLS's tracking shows it once you enter the number and the recipient's postcode: `In delivery` means it's in the van today.

### What is the GLS Track-ID?

The six-letter code on GLS's notification card. It works like the parcel number in GLS's tracking.

### How long does GLS take to deliver?

GLS lists estimated delivery times on gls-pakete.de and doesn't guarantee them. Only a parcel the sender booked with GLS's Guaranteed24Service is due the next working day (Monday to Friday) within Germany, except on its islands.

### How long does GLS keep a parcel?

After a missed delivery, the ParcelShop, depot or Parcelstation keeps it at least seven working days. Then it goes back to the sender.

### Is GLS legit?

Yes: GLS Germany is General Logistics Systems Germany GmbH & Co. OHG, of Neuenstein. Fake messages use its name, though: see the warning above.
