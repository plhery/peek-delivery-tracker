GLS Spain is the Spanish arm of the GLS Group, the parcel network that took over ASM Transporte Urgente in 2016. To track a parcel, open "Track your shipment" on GLS Spain's site or the My GLS app and enter the tracking number together with the delivery postcode: GLS asks for both. The number comes from the shop that sent the parcel.

## GLS Spain tracking number and where to find it

The shop gives you the number: look in its shipping email or order page, then in GLS's emails, SMS, WhatsApp messages and the My GLS app. If the courier missed you, the paper note left at your door, the "nota de paso", carries it too. Still nothing? Ask the shop, as GLS suggests, or see [how to find a tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number).

GLS publishes no length or format for its Spanish numbers. The Spanish form calls the field "N° de envio / Albarán". Shipment numbers have nine or ten digits; those seen by Peek carried 10 starting with 1, like the invented `1234567890`. GLS's tracking also finds a parcel by the longer barcode on its label or by the shop's reference.

GLS's tracking page first asks whether the shipment is national or international; either way, without the destination postcode it shows nothing.

Ten digits also fit other carriers, such as [DHL eCommerce Iberia](carrier:dhl-ecommerce-es) and [TIPSA](carrier:tipsa): if GLS doesn't find the number with the right postcode, check which carrier the shop's email names.

## What GLS Spain's tracking statuses mean

GLS Spain's tracking speaks Spanish or English, as you choose. These are its English wordings, in the order a parcel usually meets them:

| GLS's status | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `Label created. The shipment has not yet been delivered to GLS by the sender` | The shop has registered the parcel, but GLS doesn't have it yet. |
| `Parcel picked up by GLS. Pending to arrive to final destination` | GLS has collected the parcel and its journey has begun. |
| `At delivery agency` | It's at the depot that delivers it. This can repeat. |
| `Out for delivery` | It's with a courier today. GLS shows an estimated time slot. |
| `Absent` | The courier came and nobody was in. |
| `Received in <point>` | It's waiting for you at the Parcel Shop or locker named. |
| `Delivered` | Handed to you or someone you authorised, or picked up from a GLS point. |
| `Refused by the consignee` | Refused at the door. A return follows. |
| `Returned to the shipper` | Back with the sender, after `Return delivery process` on the way. |

GLS's help pages warn that for a parcel sent to a Parcel Shop, `Delivered` can appear when it reaches the shop.

A parcel stuck on "Label created" for days usually hasn't been handed over yet. GLS says the sender has sent the data but not the parcel, and it can't give a delivery time until it has it, so ask the shop.

## GLS delivery in Spain: missed parcels, Parcel Shops and lockers

GLS delivers Monday to Friday, roughly from 9am to 8pm, plus Saturday mornings for its Express Saturday service.

**If you're out.** The courier leaves a nota de paso saying where the parcel is. EconomyParcel shipments get one attempt and then go to the nearest Parcel Shop. Other services may get a second attempt; a parcel still not delivered waits at the depot and goes back to the sender 7 calendar days after the first attempt.

**Parcel Shops.** GLS says it has more than 10,500 GLS Points in Spain. Its pages give a parcel 7 calendar days there, or 9 after a missed delivery. Bring an official ID. Someone else can pick it up with GLS's signed authorisation form and a copy of your DNI or NIE.

**Parcel Lockers.** GLS sends a PIN by SMS, email or in My GLS. The parcel stays 7 calendar days from its arrival, and GLS recommends picking it up within 48 hours.

**Changing the delivery.** In the tracking, choose "Pick up at convenience point", then "Pick up at this Parcel Shop", for free. You can change the address yourself only within the same postcode; otherwise the sender has to ask GLS. After a one-time consent in My GLS, GLS sends the delivery date by WhatsApp and lets you redirect the parcel to a Parcel Shop from the chat.

## GLS parcel not delivered, late or damaged

First check the tracking and any nota de paso. If that doesn't explain it, contact GLS (below) with the number to hand. When tracking stops moving for days, see [tracking that isn't updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

If the packaging is damaged at the door, GLS says you can refuse the parcel: it goes back and GLS tells the sender. Damage inside the box goes to the sender, because GLS doesn't check what parcels contain. GLS's general terms, written for the shops that send with it, give 7 calendar days to report hidden damage or loss within Spain.

For a formal complaint, GLS Spain has an official complaint sheet (hoja de reclamaciones) at its offices open to the public, or Madrid's official form sent to customersupport@gls-spain.com. GLS says it answers within 30 days and doesn't belong to any consumer arbitration scheme.

For a parcel from outside the EU, GLS Spain or an authorised agent will probably contact you by email, SMS or letter to pay duties and taxes. See [parcels held at customs](guide:customs).

> GLS Spain warns that it never asks for payment details by email or SMS to confirm a delivery, never asks for payment through online payment platforms (cash on delivery aside) and uses no 807 numbers. Its emails come from @gls-spain.es, @mail.gls-spain.es, @comunicaciones.gls-spain.com or @gls-spain.com.

## Tracking GLS Spain parcels with Peek

[Peek](/) reads GLS Spain's own tracking, without an account. Like GLS, it needs the delivery postcode: once GLS Spain is the carrier, a "Delivery postcode" field appears, filled in if you gave one before. It goes to GLS and, when needed, to tracking services. A wrong postcode looks like an unknown number.

You then see each scan's time and GLS's English wording, such as `At delivery agency`, under a headline in Peek's words ("Ready for pickup"), with the expected delivery day, the weight and the Parcel Shop or locker where the parcel waits. GLS gives no scan places, and the pickup code stays on its page. Peek's link to GLS opens its form without the number. Checks run every 10 minutes by day, every 2 once the parcel is out for delivery, and every hour or two overnight, and you can be notified of changes.

The number alone doesn't name GLS Spain, and GLS answers nothing without the postcode, so Peek asks TIPSA, Relais Colis, DHL eCommerce Iberia and, when its check digit fits, [DHL Express](carrier:dhl-express) about a 10-digit number. Press "Change", pick "GLS Spain" and add the postcode. A mygls.gls-spain.es link, or a gls-group.com or gls-group.eu link with `/ES/`, names GLS Spain at once, though the postcode is still asked. A parcel another carrier hands to GLS Spain isn't followed there automatically.

## Questions about GLS tracking in Spain

### Where is my GLS package?

The latest status in GLS's tracking tells you, once you enter the number and the delivery postcode. If the courier missed you, the nota de paso says where the parcel waits, usually the nearest Parcel Shop.

### How long does GLS take to deliver in Spain?

GLS gives a standard 24 working hours within Spain once it has the parcel, islands excluded, and 24 to 96 hours for Europe.

### How long does GLS keep a parcel?

About a week: 7 calendar days at Parcel Shops and lockers, 9 after a missed home delivery, by GLS's pages. A parcel left at the depot goes back after 7 days.

### Is GLS Spain the same as ASM?

GLS bought ASM: in April 2016 it announced the purchase of ASM Transporte Urgente, then the country's second-largest express network.
