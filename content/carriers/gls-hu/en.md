GLS Hungary (*GLS General Logistics Systems Hungary Csomag-Logisztikai Kft.*, based in Alsónémedi near Budapest) is the Hungarian arm of the pan-European GLS parcel network. Operating standard next-working-day delivery across Hungary alongside nearly 1,000 GLS Parcel Shops, GLS Parcel Lockers and European cross-border routes, GLS Hungary serves both online retailers and private senders (via *eCsomag* and Parcel Shops). You can check full scan history and redirection options on [GLS Hungary's English tracker](https://gls-group.com/HU/en/parcel-tracking/) or [Hungarian tracker](https://gls-group.com/HU/hu/csomagkovetes/), or paste your parcel number into the box above.

## GLS Hungary parcel numbers: 10- and 11-digit formats

Check your webshop dispatch email, FlexDeliveryService notification, Viber/SMS message or the card left by the courier. GLS Hungary parcel numbers are 10 or 11 digits long:

| Format (invented) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `3482917364` | 10-digit Hungarian national GLS parcel number. |
| `03482917364` | 11-digit Hungarian national GLS parcel number written with a leading zero. |
| `94829173640` | 11-digit GLS international parcel number travelling into or out of Hungary. |

Ten- and 11-digit numbers are also used by other GLS companies (such as [GLS Austria](carrier:gls-at), [GLS Germany](carrier:gls-de), [GLS Poland](carrier:gls-pl) and [GLS Italy](carrier:gls-it)) and by other couriers, so choose GLS Hungary yourself or paste a `gls-group.com/HU/...` tracking link. See [tracking number formats](guide:tracking-number-formats).

## What GLS Hungary tracking statuses mean

On [gls-group.com/HU/en/parcel-tracking/](https://gls-group.com/HU/en/parcel-tracking/), entering the parcel number alone shows GLS's summary progress stage, while adding the delivery postcode reveals detailed depot scans, locations, ETA and pickup-point address:

| GLS Hungary status code | What it means for you | Peek shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `PREADVICE` | Parcel data submitted electronically by the sender; GLS has not yet collected the physical parcel. | "Label created" |
| `INPICKUP` | GLS collected the parcel from the sender or Parcel Shop. | "Received by carrier" |
| `INTRANSIT` | Moving through GLS Hungary's depots and sorting hubs (or cross-border European hubs). | "In transit" |
| `DELIVERED` | Delivered to the recipient, neighbour, Parcel Shop or Parcel Locker. | "Delivered" |
| `CANCELED` | The collection was cancelled. | "Returned to sender" |

## GLS Hungary delivery times, limits, Parcel Shops and Lockers

According to [GLS Hungary's Home](https://gls-group.com/HU/en/home/) and [GLS Hungary FAQ](https://gls-group.com/HU/en/faq/):

- **Transit times:** domestic parcels within Hungary generally arrive on the **next working day** (`< 24 hours`), Monday to Friday during business hours, with **Saturday delivery in Budapest** and optional *Guaranteed24Service*. Cross-border European deliveries take **24–48 hours** to neighbouring countries and **48–120 hours** (typically 72–120 hours) to more distant European countries.
- **Weight and size caps:** up to **40 kg** (national and export); maximum dimensions **200 cm length × 80 cm width × 60 cm height** with a maximum combined length and girth of **300 cm** (`2 × height + 2 × width + 1 × longest side`).
- **Missed delivery and neighbour drop-off:** if nobody is home on the first attempt, GLS first checks whether a neighbour can accept and sign for the parcel; otherwise the courier leaves a notification card and returns the parcel to the depot. A **second attempt** is arranged, and a **third attempt** is available on request within the **5-working-day storage period** before return to sender.
- **FlexDeliveryService, Parcel Shops and Lockers:** where the sender activates *FlexDeliveryService* (passing your email address to GLS), you can pick a new delivery day, alternate address, depot pickup (**5 working days**), nearly 1,000 **GLS Parcel Shops** (**5 working days** storage) or a **GLS Parcel Locker** (**5 days** storage; the notice with its access code comes by email and Viber, or by email and SMS if you do not use Viber).
- **COD payment:** cash-on-delivery parcels can be paid by bank card where the sender enabled *BankCardService* alongside *CashService* (and at supported Parcel Shops).
- **Non-EU imports:** orders shipped from outside the EU carry the €3 customs duty on consignments up to €150 and the €2 EU handling fee per item under the [EU Customs Reform](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/customs/eu-customs-reform_en) unless settled at checkout; see [parcels held at customs](guide:customs).

## GLS Hungary parcel delayed, damaged or missing

GLS Hungary's [FAQ](https://gls-group.com/HU/en/faq/) advises inspecting the package upon receipt:

- **External damage on arrival:** if the outer box is visibly damaged (such as a hole), you may **refuse the parcel**, and GLS returns it to the sender. With damaged packaging you can also check the contents on delivery; the driver records the check in a damage report. If the parcel looked intact and the contents turn out damaged, complain to the sender.
- **Missing or delayed parcel:** check your notification card (in case a neighbour signed for it) and your FlexDeliveryService email/Viber notice, then call GLS Hungary's recipient hotline (`+36 29 886-700`) with your parcel number and notify the seller. See [why tracking stops updating](guide:tracking-not-updating), [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received), [damaged parcel](guide:damaged-parcel), [lost parcel](guide:lost-parcel) and [returned to sender](guide:returned-to-sender).

## Track a GLS Hungary parcel with Peek

[Peek](/) recognises a pasted `gls-group.com/HU/...` or `gls-group.eu/HU/...` tracking link (with `match=`, `parcelNumber=` or `matchParcelNumber=`) as GLS Hungary. A 10-digit number starting with 33 or 34, such as `3482917364` (invented), fits other carriers too, so Peek asks them first and GLS Hungary only once none of them knows it; it then shows "GLS Hungary has this parcel." unless the parcel is more than two months old. Other numbers, such as `03482917364` or `94829173640` (invented), are never matched to GLS Hungary. If Peek doesn't name GLS Hungary, tap "Change" while adding or "Change carrier" on the parcel screen and choose GLS Hungary (searching "GLS Magyarország" finds it too).

Peek asks GLS Hungary for the overview its tracking page shows without the postcode, and shows its stage ("Label created", "Received by carrier", "In transit", "Returned to sender", "Delivered") with GLS's wording. The overview holds no scans, places, expected day or Parcel Shop and Locker details: GLS shows those only with the delivery postcode, so open [gls-group.com/HU/en/parcel-tracking/](https://gls-group.com/HU/en/parcel-tracking/) with it for the scans, pickup addresses or a FlexDeliveryService redirection.

Peek checks a GLS Hungary parcel at most once an hour, a refresh you start included, and waits 4 hours after a check that failed. After seven days at "Label created", it checks every 6 hours.

When GLS Hungary's status hasn't changed for four days and the parcel is still at "Label created", "Received by carrier" or "In transit", Peek flags it with the day Peek last saw it change, such as "No tracking update since Mon 12 Oct". The parcel's page then says "Parcels often go quiet on long flights, at customs or over a weekend. Peek keeps asking GLS Hungary.", with a "Get help from GLS Hungary" link to GLS Hungary's contact page. After three weeks with nothing new, it says "Nothing new for over three weeks. If it still hasn’t arrived, ask the seller. Peek keeps asking GLS Hungary."

You can pick alert presets for "Every scan", "Important steps" or "Delivery only". Peek monitors tracking status only and cannot redirect a parcel via FlexDeliveryService, extend storage or contact GLS Hungary for you.

## Questions about GLS Hungary tracking

### How long does GLS Hungary hold a parcel in a Parcel Shop or Parcel Locker?

GLS Hungary holds parcels for **5 working days** in a GLS Parcel Shop or depot and for **5 days** in a GLS Parcel Locker before returning them to the sender.

### How many delivery attempts does GLS Hungary make?

If you are absent on the first attempt (and no neighbour accepts it), GLS leaves a card and arranges a second attempt; a **third attempt** can also be requested within the 5-working-day storage period.

### What is the maximum weight and size for GLS Hungary?

GLS Hungary accepts parcels up to **40 kg**, with maximum dimensions of **200 cm (length) × 80 cm (width) × 60 cm (height)** and maximum length + girth of **300 cm**.

### What is GLS Hungary's customer service phone number?

Recipients can call **`+36 29 886-700`** (senders: `+36 29 88 66 70`), email `info@gls-hungary.com` or use the contact form at [gls-group.com/HU/hu/kapcsolat/](https://gls-group.com/HU/hu/kapcsolat/).
