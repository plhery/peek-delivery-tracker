GLS US (General Logistics Systems US, Inc.) is GLS Group's parcel company in the United States, delivering across the Western U.S. Peek's catalog also knows it as GLS USA, GSO and Golden State Overnight, the company GLS bought in 2016. To track a GLS US parcel, paste its number in the box above or enter it on [GLS's US tracking page](https://gls-group.com/US/en/send-and-receive/track-a-parcel/).

## GLS US tracking number: what it looks like

Peek's catalog knows one shape for GLS US numbers. With invented digits:

| What you paste (invented) | What Peek does |
| --- | --- |
| `SH000000000000000000` | SH and 18 digits, 20 characters in all. Only GLS US uses this shape in Peek's catalog, but the match is weak, so Peek asks GLS US before naming it. |
| `sh 0000 0000 0000 0000 00` | The same number. Lower case, spaces and dashes don't matter. |
| A GLS US number of another shape | Peek doesn't recognize it. Pick GLS US yourself (see "Track a GLS US parcel with Peek" below). |
| A tracking link from gls-group.com with `/US/` in it, or from gls-us.com or gso.com | Peek takes the number out of the link and names GLS US at once ("Detected carrier"). |

**Where to find it.** In the shop's shipping email or on your order page, or in GLS's own emails or texts if the shop signed you up. Each box has its own label, so several boxes can mean several numbers. GLS's search without a number needs the sender's account number and reference: ask the shop. See [how to find a tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number). An order number won't work.

**Not the European GLS.** GLS US belongs to GLS Group, and Peek follows its parcels on GLS US's own tracker. A gls-group.com link with `/EU/` or `/DE/` in it leads Peek to [GLS Germany](carrier:gls-de), not to GLS US. If the shop's email names a GLS company in Europe, or another carrier altogether, track it there. [Tracking number formats](guide:tracking-number-formats) shows what other carriers' numbers look like.

## What GLS US tracking statuses mean

Each scan on GLS US's tracker has a date and a time, and most name a place: the town, the state and the country. The scans Peek's code knows are in English, in capitals. In the order a parcel meets them:

| GLS US says | What it means for you | Peek shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `LABEL CREATED` | The shipper has made a label. GLS US may not have the parcel yet. This scan names no place. | "Label created" |
| `ARRIVAL SCAN - DELIVERY SCHED FOR 07/15/2026` | GLS US has scanned the parcel on arrival. The date at the end, month first, is the day GLS US has scheduled the delivery. | "In transit" |
| `SCANNED AT DELIVERY DEPOT` | It has been scanned at the depot that delivers it. | "In transit" |
| `ON ROUTE FOR DELIVERY` | It's on a delivery route today. | "Out for delivery" |
| `SHIPMENT DELIVERED` | Delivered. | "Delivered" |

**The shipment's status.** Above the scans, GLS US's tracker sums the parcel up in a few words: `label created`, `in transit`, `out for delivery`, `delivered` or `returned`. When that status is further along than the scans, Peek goes by it: a parcel summed up as `delivered` shows "Delivered" even if the newest scan doesn't say so, and one summed up as `returned` shows "Returned to sender".

**Other lines.** Peek's code knows no GLS US wording for a missed delivery, a problem, a hold or a pickup point. A line it doesn't know still shows as GLS US writes it, and Peek sorts it by the rules it uses for every carrier. More in [tracking statuses explained](guide:tracking-statuses).

If nothing follows `LABEL CREATED` for a day or more, Peek says "The sender hasn’t handed it to GLS US yet. Tracking starts when they do." Ask the shop.

**GLS's emails and texts.** If the shop signs you up when it makes the label, GLS writes when its depot scans the parcel in (with the expected delivery date), when the parcel is delayed or an attempt fails, when it's out for delivery (with an estimated time) and when it's delivered.

## GLS US delivery times, signatures and missed deliveries

**Address.** GLS doesn't deliver to P.O. boxes, and it sorts parcels by ZIP code, so a wrong one can delay a parcel. It reroutes a parcel only with the sender's authorization: ask the shop. See [changing a delivery address](guide:change-delivery-address).

**Days.** Monday to Friday; Saturday only in some areas, when the sender asks for it; never Sunday. GLS is closed on six holidays; the next are Thanksgiving (November 26), Christmas Day and New Year's Day. A parcel due on a holiday comes the next business day.

**Times.** These depend on the sender's service. Priority comes by 10:30 a.m. the next business day in most areas, Ground by 5 p.m. (6 p.m. in rural areas). On longer trips, Priority comes by 10:30 a.m. on the scheduled day and Ground by the end of it. Saturday deliveries come by 10:30 a.m. in most areas.

**Signature.** The sender picks the option. By default, the driver gets a signature at a business. At a house, the parcel is left if nobody can sign and the spot is safe and dry; in an apartment building, at your door, or at the management or leasing office if the driver can't reach it. With Signature Required, GLS won't leave it without a signature; with Adult Signature Required, needed for alcohol and tobacco, the signer must be 21 or over.

**Missed delivery.** Missed an attempt or found a door tag? Reschedule with the link in GLS's emails or texts, or call GLS's recipient line (below). GLS's pages give no number of attempts or holding time, and present GLS Points only as drop-off places for senders. See [missed delivery](guide:missed-delivery) and [uncollected parcels](guide:uncollected-parcel).

## GLS US parcel not found, not updating, lost or returned

**Not found.** GLS US's tracker says when it doesn't know a number. Peek then shows "Waiting for GLS US’s first scan" for an SH number you left to it, or "Waiting for the carrier" when you picked GLS US yourself, and keeps asking. A new label often takes a while to show up. If there's still nothing two days after you added the parcel, Peek flags it: "Still waiting for the carrier’s first update". Check the number against the shop's email, then ask the shop whether the parcel has really been sent.

**No news for days.** After four days without a new scan, a parcel still showing "Label created", "Received by carrier" or "In transit" gets a flag: "No tracking update for four days". Peek adds that parcels often go quiet over a weekend, and keeps asking GLS US. See [why tracking stops updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

**Delivered, but nothing came.** `SHIPMENT DELIVERED` with no parcel at your door? Look on GLS's tracker for who signed, if anyone did. Check around the door, the mailroom, the leasing office and with neighbors, then call GLS's recipient line and tell the shop. See [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).

**Lost or damaged.** Tell the shop first: it booked the delivery and can take it up with GLS US or send you a new parcel. Only the sender can claim, within 90 days of shipping; GLS works to resolve claims within 45 days. For damage, GLS asks the sender for photos of the item, the inside packaging and the box, so take them before throwing anything away. See [lost parcels](guide:lost-parcel) and [damaged parcels](guide:damaged-parcel).

**Returned.** "Returned to sender" means the parcel went back to the shop. Ask it for a refund or a new delivery. See [returned to sender](guide:returned-to-sender).

## GLS US customer service and contact

As listed on GLS US's [customer support page](https://gls-group.com/US/en/support/customer-support/) on November 13, 2026:

- **Phone:** 1-800-322-5555, option 2 (recipient support: tracking, delivery problems, delivery help).
- **Hours:** Monday to Friday, 7 a.m. to 7 p.m., and Saturday, 7 a.m. to 4 p.m., Pacific time.
- **Live chat**, offered in GLS's FAQ. GLS gives no email address for recipients.

> **Scams.** GLS never asks recipients by email to pay for goods by card, online payment or bank transfer, though for a parcel from abroad it or its agent may ask for duties and taxes. Genuine GLS sites are gls-group.com and those it links to. Report fakes to security@gls-us.com, and see [delivery scam texts](guide:delivery-scam-texts).

## Track a GLS US parcel with Peek

**An SH number.** Paste it in the box above. While Peek asks GLS US, the carrier line reads "Checking tracking services…". When GLS US knows the number, the line names GLS US and says "has this parcel", and Peek files the parcel under it. When GLS US doesn't know it yet, the line says "not found yet · Peek keeps checking". Add the parcel anyway: it shows "Probably GLS US" as its carrier until GLS US scans it.

**Another number.** Paste it all the same: Peek follows more than 3,500 carriers. Then pick GLS US yourself:

1. Press "Change" beside the carrier line and search for GLS US ("GSO", "Golden State Overnight" or "United States" finds it too).
2. Pick GLS US: the line then reads "Chosen by you". Track the parcel.

On a parcel you've already added, use "Change carrier". Peek asks GLS US only about numbers of 5 to 20 letters and digits.

**What you see.** Peek asks GLS US's tracker by the number alone: no account and no ZIP code. It shows the status, each scan with its town and state on a map, and, when GLS US's tracker gives one, the expected delivery day until the parcel is delivered or returned. It leaves out the recipient's name and address, who signed, the signature and proof of delivery, and the shipper's reference. Peek's code hands GLS US parcels over to no other carrier. If GLS US files several shipments under one number, Peek follows the one with the newest scan. "Open the GLS US website", on the parcel's page, opens the number on GLS's US tracker.

**Times.** GLS US gives each scan's time without a time zone. Peek reads it on the clock of the scan's town or, when it doesn't know the town, on that of the scan's state (the main one where a state has more than one). For a scan that names no town or state, it uses the clock of the closest earlier scan it can place, else of the closest later one; failing both, Pacific time. Peek shows every time in your device's time zone.

**When GLS US doesn't answer.** If GLS US's tracker can't be reached, Peek asks [tracking services](guide:universal-tracker) and shows their history, in their own words.

**How often.** While someone waits for the parcel (notifications on, or you looked in the last hour) and GLS US's tracker answers, Peek checks every 10 minutes, or every 2 minutes from 6 a.m. local time on the day GLS US puts the parcel on a delivery route; through tracking services, every 15 minutes. After two days without news (12 hours while it still shows "Label created" or "Received by carrier"), it checks hourly. Notifications can cover "Every scan", "Important steps" or "Delivery only".

Peek can't contact GLS US or the shop for you, change the delivery or file a claim.

## Questions about GLS US tracking

### Is GSO the same as GLS US?

GLS bought Golden State Overnight (GSO) in 2016, and gso.com now leads to GLS US's site. GSO was founded in 1995 to deliver parcels in California, Arizona, Nevada and New Mexico. Peek treats them as one: its catalog lists GSO, Golden State Overnight and GLS USA as other names for GLS US, so searching for any of them finds GLS US, and it reads tracking links from gso.com and gls-us.com as GLS US's.

### What does a GLS US tracking number look like?

The shape Peek knows is SH followed by 18 digits, such as `SH000000000000000000` (invented). GLS's pages show no format, and GLS US may use other shapes: paste them all the same and pick GLS US with "Change".

### What does the GLS US arrival scan mean?

`ARRIVAL SCAN - DELIVERY SCHED FOR 07/15/2026` means GLS US has scanned the parcel on arrival and scheduled its delivery for the date at the end, month first. Peek shows "In transit".

### Why does Peek say "Waiting for GLS US’s first scan"?

Your number has GLS US's shape, and Peek has asked GLS US, but GLS US doesn't list it yet. That's normal for a new label, and Peek keeps asking. If GLS US still doesn't list it after a week, Peek says so and suggests checking the number with the sender or choosing another carrier.

### Is GLS US the same as GLS in Europe?

It belongs to the same group, GLS Group, but Peek treats GLS US as a carrier of its own and asks GLS US's own tracker. GLS's companies in Europe, such as [GLS Germany](carrier:gls-de), have theirs: track a parcel with the GLS company the shop's email names.
