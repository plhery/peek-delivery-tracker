GOFO Express (Gofo Inc.) is a US last-mile carrier that delivers online orders for shops and marketplaces; it says it covers 47 states and more than 12,000 ZIP codes. Track a parcel on [gofo.com/us/track](https://www.gofo.com/us/track) or paste its number in the box above. The number comes from the shop that shipped your order, in its shipping email or on your order page.

## GOFO tracking number: what it looks like

GOFO says its tracking number "typically begins with GFUS". The shapes Peek links to GOFO, with invented digits:

| Shape (invented) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `GFUS12345678901234` | GFUS followed by 14 digits, the usual GOFO number |
| `GF1234567890123` | GF followed by 13 digits |
| `CR123456789012` | CR followed by 12 digits |

Some parcels carry both a GFUS waybill number and a CR tracking number. GOFO's page shows them as `WAYBILL NO` and `TRACKING NUMBER`, and either one finds the same parcel. GOFO's tracker also takes several numbers in one search.

**Where to find it.** GOFO's support page sends you to the sender: the order confirmation email (shipping details), your account on the shop's site under Orders or Purchase History, or the shipping notification sent after dispatch. Still nothing? See [how to find a tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number).

**Numbers that look alike.** GOFO also runs networks in other countries. `GFFR` and `CIFR` numbers belong to [GOFO France](carrier:gofo-fr), `GFIT` and `CIIT` numbers to [GOFO Italy](carrier:gofo-it). Type one into GOFO's US tracker and it offers a link to the right country's site. A `YT` number is [YunExpress](carrier:yunexpress)'s, even when GOFO does the final delivery.

## GOFO tracking statuses explained

GOFO's tracking page shows a summary under `CURRENT STATUS`: `Processing`, `In Transit`, `Delivered`, `Alert` or `Returned`. GOFO doesn't explain these labels. Below it come the scans, in English (GOFO's Spanish page writes them in Spanish). These are the ones a parcel usually meets, in order:

| GOFO's status | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `Shipping Label Created` | The sender made the label. GOFO doesn't have the parcel yet. |
| `Pick Up Success` | GOFO collected the parcel from the sender. |
| `Arrived at GOFO Regional Origin Hub` | It reached GOFO's first hub. |
| `Arrived at GOFO Regional Hub` | It reached a hub on the way to you. |
| `Departed GOFO Regional Hub` | It left that hub. |
| `In Transit to Next Facility` | Moving between GOFO buildings. |
| `Arrived at GOFO Delivery Station` | At the local station that delivers your area. |
| `Departed GOFO Delivery Station` | It left the station. |
| `Out for Delivery` | With the driver today. |
| `Delivered, Door/Yard` | Delivered, and GOFO names where it was left. |
| `Delivery Exception, Business Closed` | The driver couldn't deliver because the business was closed. |

The delivered line names the drop-off spot: `Delivered, Mail Room/Parcel Locker`, `Delivered, Mail Room` or `Delivered, Left with Individual` are the other ones you may see. Once a parcel shows `Arrived at GOFO Delivery Station`, GOFO says delivery is "typically completed within 1–2 calendar days". GOFO's page also shows an `Estimated Delivery` field.

## GOFO delivery: drop-off, photos and missed deliveries

GOFO delivers through partner delivery companies and their drivers. Its driver guide sets where a parcel goes: the front door of a house, never the street; the unit door, lobby desk, reception, parcel room or mailbox area of an apartment building where that's allowed; the reception or collection point of a business or school. Drivers never use USPS mailboxes, and they don't leave parcels at a closed business.

**Delivery photos.** Drivers take 3 to 5 photos at each drop-off (the label, the surroundings and where the parcel was placed). On GOFO's tracking page, "view POD" opens them after you enter a detail of the delivery; the page currently asks for the destination ZIP code.

**Missed delivery.** When a driver can't deliver (a locked gate, a dangerous animal, nobody available), the parcel goes back to the warehouse. GOFO's FAQ says a new attempt is typically made within 24 hours. An address problem, such as a missing apartment number, can send the parcel back to the local warehouse to be checked. GOFO tells shippers parcels are returned after three failed attempts.

GOFO doesn't list pickup points, redirection or rescheduling for recipients.

## GOFO package delayed, not received or returned

**Not moving.** If the parcel is late or seems lost, GOFO recommends contacting the sender, the shop or person who shipped it, who can open an inquiry with GOFO for you. If only `Shipping Label Created` shows, GOFO doesn't have the parcel yet. More on [tracking that isn't updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

**Delivered but not received.** GOFO's steps: open the delivery photo on its tracking page, then look around your mailbox, any shared mailbox area and other spots where parcels get left, and ask neighbours, people you live with, the concierge or security. Still missing? Contact GOFO; a photo of the outside of your home or building helps, though it isn't required. See also [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).

**Wrong contents or a parcel you didn't order.** Wrong items go to the sender. For a parcel that isn't yours, or a complaint, email GOFO support.

**Returned.** GOFO lists damage in transit, a wrong or incomplete address, and addresses it can't serve (some postal facilities, military addresses) among the reasons. "Once a package has been returned, it cannot be intercepted": ask the sender for a replacement or a new shipment. GOFO publishes no claim deadline or compensation for recipients; claims go through the sender.

## Tracking GOFO Express parcels with Peek

[Peek](/) names GOFO Express at once for a `GFUS` number, typed with or without spaces. For `GF` and `CR` numbers it can't ask GOFO, so it names GOFO only if a tracking service does; otherwise pick GOFO Express with "Change", or paste a GOFO US tracking link (gofo.com/us or gofoexpress.com) with `searchID=` and the number in it.

Peek reads GOFO's US tracking every 10 minutes from 8 am to 10 pm Swiss time (about 2 am to 4 pm Eastern, 11 pm to 1 pm Pacific), every 2 minutes in those hours once the parcel is out for delivery, and every hour or two the rest of the time. It shows the status, each scan with its town and state on a map, the exact time of each scan, the delivery time, the service name and the destination. Delivered scans appear as "Delivered" in Peek's own words, without GOFO's support line; other scans show as GOFO writes them. Peek shows no expected delivery day and leaves out the delivery photos, coordinates and weight, so open GOFO's page for the photos. Peek can notify you of every scan, important steps only or delivery day only. It can't contact GOFO or the shop for you.

## Questions about GOFO tracking

### Is GOFO Express legit?

Yes. GOFO Express is a US last-mile delivery company run by Gofo Inc.; GOFO also delivers in Canada, France, the Netherlands, Italy and Spain. Its own tracking and support are on gofo.com/us.

### Are GOFO packages delivered by USPS?

No GOFO page says so. GOFO delivers with drivers from its partner delivery companies, and its driver rules forbid using USPS mailboxes.

### How long does GOFO take to deliver?

GOFO says delivery is typically completed within 1–2 calendar days once the parcel reaches its local delivery station.

### Why is my GOFO tracking number not working?

Check that it's a US GOFO number, which typically begins with GFUS; a `GFFR` or `GFIT` number belongs to GOFO's French or Italian site. If it still finds nothing, ask the shop that shipped it, which can open an inquiry with GOFO.
