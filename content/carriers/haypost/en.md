HayPost (*"Haypost" Closed Joint-Stock Company*, Armenian *Հայփոստ*) is the national postal operator of the Republic of Armenia. Founded in 1994 and reorganized as a state-owned CJSC in 1998, it manages roughly 777 post offices across Armenia, 23 regional hubs and an international mail exchange centre, operating under the Universal Postal Union (UPU) and PostEurop. You can look up an item on [HayPost's Track & Trace page](https://www.haypost.am/en/track-trace), or paste the tracking number into the box above.

## HayPost tracking numbers: S10 format and where to find them

Your tracking number is printed on the post-office receipt or sent in the online store's dispatch email. HayPost tracks standard 13-character UPU S10 barcodes with a check digit:

| Format (invented) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `RR482917367AM` | Registered international letter or small packet sent from Armenia (`R` + second letter + 9 digits + `AM`). |
| `CP482917367AM` | Postal parcel sent from Armenia (`CP` + 9 digits + `AM`). |

HayPost's tracker also looks up inbound international UPU S10 codes originating in other countries (ending in the sending country's two-letter ISO code, such as `US`, `DE` or `CN`) once the sending post relays electronic dispatch data or the item enters Armenia. Ordinary untracked mail does not carry an S10 barcode and cannot be followed online. See [tracking number formats](guide:tracking-number-formats) if your shipment uses a private courier format instead.

## What HayPost tracking statuses mean

HayPost returns English Universal Postal Union (IPS) scan lines tagged with the leg of the journey: `(Otb)` outward from the sending country, `(Inb)` inward in the destination country, or `(EDI-received)` electronic dispatch data relayed from the sending post.

| HayPost says | What it means for you | Peek shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Receive item from customer (Otb)` | Accepted at a post office from the sender. | "Received by carrier" |
| `Insert item into bag (Otb)`, `Send item abroad (EDI-received)`, `Receive item at office of exchange (Otb)` / `(Inb)` | Bagged for international dispatch, reported abroad by electronic data, or arrived at the international office of exchange. | "In transit" |
| `Send item to customs (Otb)` | Export customs clearance at the outward office of exchange before leaving the origin country. | "In transit" |
| `Send item to customs (Inb)`, `Record item customs information (Inb)` | Held or inspected by destination import customs. | "At customs" |
| `Return item from customs (Inb)` | Released by customs back into the postal network (not a return to the sender). | "In transit" |
| `Send item to domestic location (Inb)`, `Receive item at location (Inb)`, `Receive item at sorting centre (Inb)`, `Item was delivered to the postal office (Inb)` | Moving through domestic sorting hubs and arriving at the local postal office that handles handover. | "In transit" |
| `Send item out for physical delivery (Inb)` | Out with a postal carrier for delivery today. | "Out for delivery" |
| `Unsuccessful item delivery attempt (Inb)`, `The notification was sent to the customer: ...` | Delivery was not completed; HayPost often appends the reason and next step (for example `Addressee not available; Scheduled for further action/processing today`). | "Delivery attempted" |
| `Receive item at collection point for pick-up (Inb)` | Ready for collection at the post office. | "Ready for pickup" |
| `Deliver item (Inb)` | Handed over and recorded as delivered. | "Delivered" |

Watch two easy-to-misread wordings. First, `Return item from customs (Inb)` is good news: customs finished checking the parcel and returned it to the post for onward delivery. Second, `Item was delivered to the postal office (Inb)` (often relayed from destination posts abroad) means the local postal office received the item into its custody, not that it has been handed to you.

## HayPost domestic, international and express services

Across its network of approximately 777 post offices ([About HayPost](https://www.haypost.am/en/about-us)), HayPost operates:

- **Domestic postal services ([haypost.am/en/postal-services/domestic-postal-services](https://www.haypost.am/en/postal-services/domestic-postal-services)):** postcards, letters, small packets up to 2 kg, parcels up to 20 kg, **Yerevan Express Service (YES)** for tracked post-to-post or home delivery inside Yerevan (orders placed 09:00–16:00 processed same day; after 16:00 next working day; Saturday orders on Monday; booking line `+374 10 514 513`) and **HayPost Sprint** for tracked domestic e-commerce packets up to 2 kg.
- **International postal services ([haypost.am/en/postal-services/international-delivery](https://www.haypost.am/en/postal-services/international-delivery)):** international letters, small packets up to 2 kg (length + width + thickness ≤ 0.9 m, longest side ≤ 0.6 m), parcels up to 20 kg (any side ≤ 1.05 m), M-bags up to 30 kg and EMS accelerated mail.
- **HayPost Express to the United States ([haypost.am/en/postal-services/Haypost-Express](https://www.haypost.am/en/postal-services/Haypost-Express)):** air parcel delivery from any Armenian post office to Los Angeles (pick-up offices in Glendale, Van Nuys and Sylmar), up to 30 kg, with an average transit time of 7 to 10 working days.

**HayPost's FAQ.** [HayPost's FAQ](https://www.haypost.am/en/faq) has questions on the control terms for domestic delivery, customs fees, compensation and how long EMS items are kept; for the figures that apply to your item, ask your post office or HayPost.

## Delayed, customs-held or missing HayPost parcels

For online purchases shipped to or from Armenia through postal channels, contact the seller first regarding refunds or reshipments.

**Quiet gaps between countries.** After `Send item abroad (EDI-received)`, international mail waits for air transport, arrival sorting and import customs at the destination country's office of exchange, which can leave gaps of several days. See [why tracking stops updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

**Customs clearance.** Inbound parcels to Armenia and outbound `AM` parcels entering destinations such as the EU or US pass through destination import customs (`Send item to customs (Inb)`). Keep your purchase invoice and tracking number ready if customs requests valuation proof. See [parcels held at customs](guide:customs).

**Delivered, damaged or returned.** If an item shows `Deliver item (Inb)` when you do not have it, check with household members and your local branch (using [HayPost's network directory](https://www.haypost.am/en/our-network)), then contact the seller and HayPost support. See [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received), [damaged parcel](guide:damaged-parcel), [lost parcel](guide:lost-parcel) and [returned to sender](guide:returned-to-sender).

## Track a HayPost parcel with Peek

For UPU S10 codes ending in `AM` (such as `RR482917367AM` or `CP482917367AM`, digits invented), [Peek](/) follows HayPost automatically as the preferred origin post, and can also track inbound S10 codes sent to Armenia once you pick HayPost. Because HayPost does not have dedicated tracking URL rules in Peek, paste the 13-character S10 number itself; if you need to select HayPost manually, tap "Change" while adding or "Change carrier" on the parcel view and choose HayPost (searching "Armenia Post" or "Հայփոստ" finds it too).

Peek reads HayPost's public JSON tracking API directly in English and shows the mapped stage ("Received by carrier", "In transit", "At customs", "Out for delivery", "Delivery attempted", "Ready for pickup", "Delivered"), IPS scan wording (including failure reasons and next-step notes such as `Addressee not available`), office/country location (dropping numeric postcodes), destination country and delivery time. Armenian scans and `Send item abroad (EDI-received)` rows are dated on Yerevan time, and a foreign post's scans on their place's time zone (France's for Paris, for example); Peek shows them on your device's clock. When HayPost pre-publishes a future-dated `Send item abroad (EDI-received)` plan (often scheduled hours ahead), Peek omits that planned row until its actual time arrives. HayPost's API does not return an estimated delivery date, weight, service name, sender/recipient details or proof-of-delivery photo.

While you have the parcel open in Peek, or on its delivery day with notifications on, Peek checks up to every 10 minutes; on the day HayPost shows `Send item out for physical delivery (Inb)`, every 2 minutes from 6 a.m. on the clock of HayPost's newest scan, while HayPost's own tracker answers. Otherwise Peek checks hourly. After 12 hours with only "Label created" or "Received by carrier", or two days without news, it checks hourly however you follow it, and every 6 hours after seven days at "Label created". When HayPost's tracker doesn't answer and Peek follows the parcel through tracking services alone, it checks hourly, every 3 hours after a quiet day, and up to every 15 minutes on the out-for-delivery day.

When the newest scan is four days old and the parcel is still at "Label created", "Received by carrier" or "In transit", Peek flags it with that scan's day, such as "No tracking update since Mon 12 Oct". The parcel's page then says "Parcels often go quiet on long flights, at customs or over a weekend. Peek keeps asking HayPost.", with a "Get help from HayPost" link to HayPost's contact page. After three weeks with no new scan, it says "Nothing new for over three weeks. If it still hasn’t arrived, ask the seller. Peek keeps asking HayPost."

You can choose alert presets for "Every scan", "Important steps" or "Delivery only". Peek monitors tracking updates only and cannot clear customs or file an inquiry with HayPost on your behalf.

## Questions about HayPost tracking

### What does a HayPost tracking number look like?

HayPost uses standard 13-character UPU S10 postal codes: two letters, nine digits and `AM` at the end for items posted in Armenia (for example `RR482917367AM` or `CP482917367AM`, digits invented). Inbound international mail keeps the sending country's two-letter ending.

### Why did my HayPost parcel say `Return item from customs (Inb)`?

That IPS status means customs cleared the item and released it back to the postal service for onward transport — it does not mean the parcel is being returned to the sender.

### Can HayPost track parcels sent to Armenia from abroad?

Yes. Any tracked international UPU S10 code destined for Armenia can be checked on [haypost.am/en/track-trace](https://www.haypost.am/en/track-trace) or in Peek once electronic dispatch data arrives or HayPost scans it in Armenia.

### How do I contact HayPost customer support?

Call `+374 10 514-514` (or `+374 10 544-444` for corporate services), email `info@haypost.am` or use the contact form on [haypost.am/en/contact-us](https://www.haypost.am/en/contact-us) or the live chat on HayPost's site.
