Hermes Germany is the Otto Group's Hamburg-based parcel carrier, focused on deliveries within Germany. Track a parcel with its Sendungsnummer (tracking number) on [myhermes.de](https://www.myhermes.de/empfangen/sendungsverfolgung/), in German only, or paste the number in the box above. The sender has the number; Hermes's own emails reach you if the sender passed on your email address.

## Hermes tracking number: where to find it

Hermes's form asks for the "Sendungs- oder Quittungsnummer" (shipment or receipt number), 14 to 20 letters or digits. Hermes doesn't say where recipients find it, and sends no SMS. No number? Ask the sender, or see [how to find a tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number).

| Shape (invented) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `H1234567890123456` | H and 16 to 19 digits: Hermes's alone. |
| `12345678901231` | 14 digits: Hermes, [DPD](carrier:dpd-de), BRT, SEUR and others use them. |
| `H123456789012349` | H and 15 digits: Hermes's, or an [Evri](carrier:evri) barcode from the UK. |

The 4-digit TAN Hermes emails for some parcels is a handover code, not a tracking number.

## Hermes tracking statuses in English

The German lines and labels you'll meet, in order:

| Hermes says | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `Die Sendung wurde Hermes elektronisch angekündigt.` | Label created: the sender booked it; Hermes may not have it yet. |
| `Die Sendung wurde von Hermes in … übernommen und wird für den weiteren Versand vorbereitet.` | Hermes has it, at the place named. |
| `In 1 - 2 Tagen`, `In 2 - 4 Tagen`, `Offen` | Hermes's forecast: 1 to 2 or 2 to 4 days, or none yet. |
| `Die Sendung wurde ins Zustellfahrzeug geladen und wird voraussichtlich heute zugestellt.` | Out for delivery, expected today. |
| `Du bist der nächste Stopp` | Live tracking: you're the courier's next stop. |
| `Die Zustelladresse wurde nicht gefunden. Mögliche Gründe: Fehler in der Adressübermittlung. Bitte PaketShop buchen oder Kundenservice kontaktieren.` | The courier couldn't find your address (see below). |
| `Abholbereit seit`, `Abzuholen` | Ready for pickup since, and until, the dates shown. |
| `Abgeholt am` | Picked up on that date. |
| `Die Sendung wurde an der Empfangsadresse zugestellt.` | Delivered to your address. |
| `Die Sendung wurde erfolgreich zugestellt.` | Delivered (seen on parcels abroad). |

If the first line doesn't change within 2 to 3 days, Hermes says to ask the sender. `Für die Sendung wurde ein WunschPaketShop gebucht.` is a booked preference, not a movement. Left with a neighbour? Hermes's card or email says which one, as does its tracking once you enter your postcode.

## Hermes delivery, ParcelShops and missed deliveries

**When it comes.** Monday to Saturday, 8:00 to 20:00. Within Germany, the tracking, app or an email usually shows a non-binding window of up to 4 hours the day before.

**Changing it.** Hermes PreferredDelivery (WunschZustellung) offers four free options: a safe place, a ParcelShop, a neighbour or another day (up to 6 working days later). Book under "Zustellung ändern" in Hermes's tracking or app with your postcode, no account needed, until shortly before delivery. Greyed out means no date yet, the sender doesn't allow it or logistics rule it out. Safe place and neighbour are at your own risk.

**If you're out.** The courier tries a nearby neighbour or your safe place, then leaves a card or sends an email. Within Germany it tries on three consecutive working days, though Hermes's terms allow a ParcelShop after the first. After three misses Hermes keeps the parcel for ten days; you can ask for a fourth attempt, and if that fails it goes back to the sender.

**ParcelShops** (PaketShop; over 17,000 in Germany) keep a domestic parcel for 7 working days from arrival. Bring an ID card from the EU, EEA or Switzerland, a German residence permit or a passport in English or French; a driving licence won't do. Someone else needs your signed authorisation (Vollmacht) and their own ID, though not every sending shop accepts one.

**Address not found.** Hermes writes by post and holds the parcel at its depot (7 working days, or ten days: its pages differ). Send the right address through the contact form; redelivery is usually the next working day.

## Hermes parcel late, lost or damaged

**No update.** Hermes doesn't list every step, so a quiet day can be normal. See [why tracking stops updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

**Missing.** After 5 working days, ask customer service to search through the contact form. If the search fails for 20 days (30 internationally), Hermes emails a loss form. Its terms make the sender the claimant, so tell the shop too.

**Damaged.** For an online shop's parcel, Hermes sends you to the shop. For a private sender's parcel, report visible damage at delivery and hidden damage within 7 days, per Hermes's terms.

**Delivered but not there.** Hermes's Declaration of non-receipt opens only from a link in a Hermes email, and asks you to confirm you've asked your household and neighbours. See [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).

Hermes lists no customer-service phone line, hours or chat; the head-office number is marked as not customer service, and couriers can't be contacted.

> Hermes Germany sends no SMS: any text in its name is a scam, except from [Hermes Einrichtungs-Service](carrier:hermes-einrichtungs-service). Its emails never ask for payment details or customs fees, and recipients pay nothing extra.

## Tracking a Hermes parcel with Peek

[Peek](/) recognises `H` and 16 to 19 digits at once. For 14 digits it asks Hermes among the likely carriers when the last digit fits Hermes's check digit; otherwise press "Change" and pick Hermes Germany. For `H` and 15 digits it asks Hermes Germany, and Evri too when the number also fits Evri's check digit. With an extra leading zero, a 14-digit number isn't recognised as Hermes's: drop the zero. Other numbers Hermes accepts (14 to 20 letters or digits) need "Change" too. myhermes.de links can be pasted; no postcode is needed.

It shows the status, history, expected day (not Hermes's time window), sending shop and delivery time. At a ParcelShop it shows "Ready for pickup" with the shop's name and address. It drops your name, address and booked preferences. Each scan keeps Hermes's own sentence, which Peek asks for in English, though some lines stay German; delivery to a neighbour, or pickup at a ParcelShop, counts as delivered. A parcel in a Hermes Box shows "Ready for pickup" until you take it out with its pickup TAN. Hermes gives no scan places, so the map stays empty.

Peek checks every 10 minutes by day, every 2 once out for delivery, and every hour or two overnight, and can notify you of every scan, important steps only or delivery day only. It doesn't follow parcels to other carriers, and old numbers show as not found once Hermes deletes them after 30 days. Hermes Einrichtungs-Service (furniture) is a separate carrier, picked by hand.

## Questions about Hermes tracking

### How long does Hermes take to deliver?

Within Germany, up to 3 working days from dispatch, says Hermes, though its terms promise no fixed date.

### How long does a Hermes ParcelShop keep a parcel?

7 working days from arrival for parcels within Germany, with no extension; then it goes back to the sender. A Hermes Box keeps it 72 hours: request its pickup TAN in Hermes's tracking with the box's postcode and your email.

### Is Hermes now Evri?

Only in the UK: Hermes UK became Evri in March 2022. Hermes Germany is a different carrier; for a UK parcel, see the [Evri page](carrier:evri).

### Can I email or WhatsApp Hermes customer service?

No. Hermes has ended WhatsApp support and lists no customer-service email address: use the contact form; replies come by email.
