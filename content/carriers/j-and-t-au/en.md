J&T Express Australia (J&T Express Australia Pty Ltd, Villawood NSW) is the Australian arm of J&T Express, the parcel group behind J&T in Southeast Asia and China. It has its own network, website and tracker, and delivers for online marketplaces, brands and retailers, including orders from overseas once they've cleared customs. Track a parcel on [jtexpress.au/tracking](https://www.jtexpress.au/tracking) or paste its number in the box above. The number comes from the shop or marketplace you ordered from.

## J&T Express Australia tracking number: what it looks like

J&T calls it the tracking number or waybill number. An Australian J&T waybill is `JTAU` followed by 12 digits, 16 characters in all. Other J&T networks use other shapes:

| Number (invented) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `JTAU123456789012` | J&T Express Australia: JTAU and 12 digits. |
| `JTDE123456789012` | Not Australia: [J&T Express Germany](carrier:j-and-t-de), a separate network with its own tracker on jtexpress.de. |
| `JTE123456789012` | Not Australia: J&T Express in Saudi Arabia, the UAE and Kuwait. |
| `JT1234567890123` | Not Australia: JT and 13 digits is a J&T number from China or Southeast Asia. |

J&T's sites in other countries run separate trackers, so a `JTAU` number belongs on jtexpress.au, not on J&T's Philippine, Malaysian or Chinese sites.

**Where to find it.** Look in the shop's shipping email or on your order page. J&T gets your name, address and contact details from the sender or the marketplace you ordered on, so the shop is the one to ask if you can't find the number. More places to look in [how to find a tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number).

**Searching.** J&T's tracker takes up to 10 numbers at once, separated by spaces or commas. For a number it doesn't know, it says `No tracking records yet`. Check all 16 characters against the shop's email, then ask the shop. J&T publishes no advice on how long a new waybill takes to show.

## J&T Express Australia tracking statuses explained

J&T's scans are full sentences in English, each with a date and time but no depot or town. Some end with J&T's customer service number. These are the ones a parcel usually meets, in order:

| J&T's status | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `The parcel has been picked up from customs clearance.` | A parcel from overseas has cleared customs, and J&T has collected it from the customs broker. Often the first J&T scan. |
| `The parcel has been successfully picked up and is being prepared for transportation.` | J&T has the parcel and is getting it ready to move. |
| `The parcel arrives at the distribution center and is prepared for transport to the next destination.` | Sorted at a J&T centre for the next leg. |
| `The parcel is dispatched from the transit center to the next destination` | It has left a J&T centre. |
| `Your parcel is out for delivery.` | With a driver, on its way to you. The line goes on with "If you have any questions, please call" and J&T's number. |
| `Your parcel has been delivered.` | Delivered. The same sentence invites you to call J&T with questions. |

`The parcel has been picked up from customs clearance.` sounds like a customs hold, but it means the opposite: customs is done and J&T has the parcel. More on parcels from abroad in [customs](guide:customs).

Because the scans name no place, the tracker can't tell you which state or depot the parcel is in. J&T publishes no glossary of its statuses.

## J&T Express Australia delivery and proof of delivery

J&T recruits delivery partners across Australia, local companies that do its last-mile delivery and collection. Its privacy notice describes what happens at your door:

- The driver records each delivery or attempted delivery in J&T's app: photos of the parcel and where it was left, the date and time, the location of the driver's device, a signature where one is required, and delivery notes.
- Drivers are told to photograph the parcel and the spot and, where they can, to leave people, number plates and the inside of homes out of the picture.
- You get the proof of delivery as a link by SMS or another electronic notification. Anyone with the link can open it. It doesn't name the driver.
- If you don't want a photo taken, J&T says to give delivery instructions through its privacy contact, dataprotection@jtexpress.au.

J&T Express Australia publishes no delivery times for recipients, and nothing on safe drop, calling cards, the number of delivery attempts, pickup points or redelivery. Its quote form asks businesses to choose between same-day, next-day and two-day delivery.

## J&T parcel not moving, missing or delivered but not received

**No new scans.** J&T doesn't say how long a parcel can go between scans. If it seems stuck, contact J&T's customer service with your waybill number, or the shop. More on [tracking that isn't updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

**Delivered but not there.** Open the proof-of-delivery link from J&T's text and check the spot the photo shows. Still missing? Contact J&T and the shop: J&T's privacy notice says it uses these records to investigate missing, damaged and misdelivered parcels. See also [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).

**Lost or damaged.** J&T publishes no claims process or compensation for recipients. Its customers are the shops and platforms that book it, and it gives them the proof of delivery too, so ask the shop or marketplace for a refund or replacement. More in [lost parcel](guide:lost-parcel).

## J&T Express Australia customer service number

As listed on [J&T's contact page](https://www.jtexpress.au/contact) on 21 October 2026:

- **Phone:** 13 18 18, working hours 08:00 to 20:00. The page names no days.
- **Email:** service@jtexpress.au.

The page's other addresses are for businesses (business@jtexpress.au) and would-be delivery partners (partner@jtexpress.au). J&T's privacy notice says its call centre and customer support providers are in Malaysia and the Philippines.

J&T Express Australia publishes no scam warning. It does send a proof-of-delivery link by SMS after a delivery, so a text about a J&T parcel isn't necessarily fake. It says nothing about texts asking for payment: check those against [delivery scam texts](guide:delivery-scam-texts) before tapping anything.

## Tracking J&T Express Australia parcels with Peek

[Peek](/) names J&T Express Australia at once for a `JTAU` number, in upper or lower case, with or without spaces. A jtexpress.au/tracking link with `?q=` and the number works too. No account or phone number needed.

Peek reads J&T Express Australia's own tracker and shows the status, each scan at the exact time J&T recorded it, and the delivery time. J&T's scans name no place, so there's no map, and Peek shows no expected delivery date, weight or proof of delivery: open J&T's text for the photo. Scans show in J&T's English wording, without the customer service number or any name or phone number a scan holds. For a long history, Peek keeps the newest 100 scans.

Peek checks every 10 minutes from 08:00 to 22:00 Swiss time, about 18:00 to 08:00 in Sydney (16:00 to 06:00 in winter; Perth's clocks are 2 to 3 hours behind Sydney's), every 2 minutes in those hours once the parcel is out for delivery, and every hour or two the rest of the time, so most of a delivery day is checked every hour or two. It can notify you of every scan, important steps only or delivery day only. It can't contact J&T or the shop for you.

## Questions about J&T Express Australia tracking

### Is J&T Express Australia legit?

Yes. J&T Express Australia Pty Ltd is an Australian company with an active ABN (18 698 743 609) and its registered office in Villawood NSW. It's part of J&T Express, a parcel group listed in Hong Kong (01519.HK) that started in Indonesia in 2015.

### What is the J&T Express Australia phone number?

13 18 18, with working hours from 08:00 to 20:00, as listed on its contact page on 21 October 2026. You can also email service@jtexpress.au.

### What does "picked up from customs clearance" mean on J&T tracking?

Customs is done. The parcel came from overseas, cleared customs, and J&T collected it from the customs broker to deliver it. It's often J&T's first scan of an imported parcel.

### Does J&T Express Australia take a photo on delivery?

Yes. Its privacy notice says drivers photograph the parcel and where they left it, and you get the proof of delivery as a link by SMS or another electronic notification.
