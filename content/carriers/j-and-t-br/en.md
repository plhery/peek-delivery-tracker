J&T Express Brazil is the Brazilian operation of J&T Express, an express logistics network founded in 2015 that operates across 13 countries. In Brazil it delivers e-commerce orders, personal parcels and cross-border shipments across all 5,571 municipalities through more than 50 transfer centres, over 1,600 regional routes and more than 6,000 partner drop-off and collection points (Pontos J&T). You can check a parcel on [J&T Express Brazil's tracking page](https://www.jtexpress.com.br/trajectoryQuery), or paste the waybill number into the box above.

## J&T Express Brazil tracking number: format and where to find it

Look for the waybill number (*código de rastreio* or *número de pedido*) in the online store's dispatch email, inside your marketplace order page or on the receipt issued at a Ponto J&T. J&T Express Brazil uses a 15-digit waybill number beginning with `8880`.

| Format (invented) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `888048291736405` | J&T Express Brazil waybill: `8880` followed by 11 digits. |

J&T's tracking page accepts up to 30 numbers at once, separated by commas or line breaks. If your dispatch message carries a `BR` code with 13 characters, that belongs to [SPX Express Brazil](carrier:spx-br) rather than J&T; a 13-character code ending in `BR` with two leading letters and nine digits belongs to [Correios](carrier:correios-br); a 17-character code starting with `AJ` belongs to [Anjun Logistics](carrier:anjun); and a 14-digit number without the `8880` prefix often belongs to [Jadlog](carrier:jadlog). See [tracking number formats](guide:tracking-number-formats) if you are unsure which carrier issued your code.

## What J&T Express Brazil tracking statuses mean

J&T Express Brazil publishes its scan descriptions in Portuguese. Some stages share the same headline (`Em transferencia`) while the detailed line explains whether the parcel is still waiting to be picked up or moving between hubs.

| J&T Express Brazil says | What it means for you | Peek shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Em transferencia` (before collection) | The sender registered the waybill electronically, or the parcel is queued before physical collection. | "Label created" |
| `Coletado`, `... retirou a encomenda` | A J&T driver or unit collected the parcel from the sender or drop-off point. | "Received by carrier" |
| `Em transferencia`, `Chegada da encomenda expressa`, `Saída da encomenda expressa` | The parcel arrived at or left a transfer centre or sorting unit on its way toward your state. | "In transit" |
| `Em Rota de Entrega`, `... enviando a encomenda` | A local courier has loaded the parcel for delivery to your address. | "Out for delivery" |
| `Pedido Entregue` | J&T recorded the parcel as delivered. | "Delivered" |

Between collection and `Em Rota de Entrega`, a parcel crossing several Brazilian states can travel by road for days between transfer centres without an intermediate scan. The next event usually appears once the truck is unloaded at a regional sorting hub or your destination delivery unit.

## J&T Express delivery, Pontos J&T and personal parcels

J&T Express Brazil handles [B2C e-commerce logistics](https://www.jtexpress.com.br/service/b2c) for online stores and marketplaces, [C2C personal shipments](https://www.jtexpress.com.br/clients/c2c) and international cross-border deliveries.

**Weight and size limits.** On its [homepage](https://www.jtexpress.com.br/) freight calculator, J&T states that a parcel can weigh up to 30 kg, with no single side longer than 120 cm and the sum of length, width and height no greater than 240 cm.

**Pontos J&T.** J&T operates more than 6,000 partner shops across Brazil ([Pontos J&T](https://www.jtexpress.com.br/joinUs/pudo)), which act as drop-off and collection points integrated into the national network.

**Delivery attempts and scheduling.** J&T Express Brazil's public pages do not publish a single fixed delivery-attempt count, guaranteed transit window or pickup-point holding period for all retail contracts, because marketplace and store agreements set those terms. Check the estimated date and any delivery instructions inside the store or marketplace app where you bought the item.

## Cross-border parcels and Remessa Conforme customs

J&T Express Brazil also offers [cross-border logistics and customs clearance](https://www.jtexpress.com.br/service/cbt) integrated with Brazil's *Programa Remessa Conforme* (PRC). Under [Receita Federal's Remessa Conforme rules](https://www.gov.br/receitafederal/pt-br/assuntos/aduana-e-comercio-exterior/manuais/remessas-postal-e-expressa/programa-remessa-conforme-o-que-e-como-funciona), how you pay import taxes depends on whether the international store is certified in the programme:

- **Certified store (PRC):** taxes are charged upfront at checkout. For personal consignments up to US$50 (goods, freight and insurance), federal Import Duty (*Imposto de Importação*) is 0%, plus state ICMS of 17% to 20% depending on the destination state. Above US$50, federal Import Duty is 60% with a US$30 discount applied to the tax amount, plus 17% to 20% ICMS.
- **Non-certified store:** federal Import Duty is 60% with no US$30 discount — even on orders up to US$50 — plus 17% to 20% ICMS, billed when the consignment enters Brazil rather than at checkout.

Because cross-border parcels undergo customs review on entry, tracking can pause for several days before handing off to J&T's domestic transfer network. See [parcels held at customs](guide:customs) for general guidance.

## J&T Express Brazil parcel stuck, delayed or missing

When you bought from an online store or marketplace, the seller holds the transport contract with J&T Express Brazil and is responsible for refunds or reshipments.

**No updates for several days.** A newly created waybill stays at `Em transferencia` before collection until the seller hands the parcel over. Once collected, long road legs between Brazil's transfer centres and weekends often leave gaps between scans. See [why tracking stops updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

**Marked `Pedido Entregue` but not received.** Check first with household members, neighbours or your building's *portaria* (doorman or reception). If nobody has the parcel, contact both the seller and J&T Express Brazil promptly so they can verify delivery details. See [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).

**Damaged or lost parcel.** Photograph the outer packaging and label before discarding anything and report the issue to the store right away. See [damaged parcel](guide:damaged-parcel) and [lost parcel](guide:lost-parcel). If a parcel is returned after failed deliveries, contact the seller about a refund or new shipment; see [returned to sender](guide:returned-to-sender).

## J&T Express Brazil customer service and security warning

Contact the seller first for order refunds or address changes. To reach J&T Express Brazil directly about a parcel in transit:

- **Toll-free phone (SAC):** `0800-055-0050` (listed across [jtexpress.com.br](https://www.jtexpress.com.br/)).
- **Customer service email:** `meajuda@jtexpress.com.br`.
- **Order feedback form:** [J&T Express Brazil order feedback](https://www.jtexpress.com.br/contactus/orderFeedback).
- **Escalations email:** `escalations@jtexpress.com.br`.
- **Ombudsman (*Ouvidoria*):** J&T's [Ouvidoria page](https://www.jtexpress.com.br/contactus/supervise) notes that ethics and internal conduct reports belong there, while delivery and order questions should go through the customer service channels above.
- **Consumer mediation:** if a dispute with a participating store or carrier goes unresolved, Brazilian consumers with a silver or gold gov.br account can file a free complaint on [Consumidor.gov.br](https://www.consumidor.gov.br/pages/conteudo/publico/1), where registered companies commit to reply within 10 days.

> **Security alert on J&T's tracking page:** J&T Express Brazil states that it does **not** ask recipients to pay customs fees via WhatsApp, SMS or third-party links to release or deliver a parcel. For any charge or delivery update, use only J&T Express's official channels. See [delivery scam texts](guide:delivery-scam-texts).

## Track a J&T Express Brazil parcel with Peek

[Peek](/) recognises a 15-digit `8880` waybill number as J&T Express Brazil automatically, with high confidence, and also recognises pasted `jtexpress.com.br/trajectoryQuery` or `/mobile/expressTracking` links containing `waybillNo=`. If Peek ever guesses a different carrier for another code, tap "Change" while adding or "Change carrier" on the parcel screen and choose J&T Express Brazil (searching for "J&T Express Brasil" or "J&T Brazil" finds it too).

Peek queries J&T Express Brazil's guest tracking directly and displays the current stage ("Label created", "Received by carrier", "In transit", "Out for delivery", "Delivered"), J&T's Portuguese scan descriptions and timestamps read in Brazilian local time (`America/Sao_Paulo`, displayed in your device's time zone). J&T's public tracker does not expose an estimated delivery date, pickup point details, recipient or courier names, or proof-of-delivery photos, so those do not appear in Peek.

Peek checks J&T Express Brazil at most every 30 minutes, including while you have the parcel open and on delivery day — refreshing by hand will not check sooner — and waits 60 minutes after a failed check. Between active bursts it checks hourly, stays hourly while open once a parcel has sat 12 hours at "Label created" or "Received by carrier" or 2 days without a new scan, and drops to every 6 hours after 7 days still at "Label created". When tracking services fill in as a fallback, they run hourly, slow to every 3 hours after a day with no change, and step up to every 15 minutes on delivery day. If tracking goes quiet for 4 days in transit, Peek flags it (for example "No tracking update since Mon, Oct 12"), shows "Parcels often go quiet on long flights, at customs or over a weekend. Peek keeps asking J&T Express Brazil." and offers "Get help from J&T Express Brazil", which opens J&T's order feedback form. After more than three weeks without a new scan, Peek shows "Nothing new for over three weeks. If it still hasn’t arrived, ask the seller. Peek keeps asking J&T Express Brazil."

You can choose notification alerts for "Every scan", "Important steps" or "Delivery only". Peek only monitors tracking updates and cannot redirect a parcel or contact J&T Express Brazil on your behalf.

## Questions about J&T Express Brazil tracking

### What does a J&T Express Brazil tracking number look like?

A J&T Express Brazil waybill is 15 digits starting with `8880` (for example `888048291736405`, digits invented). You can track up to 30 numbers at once on J&T's site or paste one into Peek.

### Why does my J&T Express parcel say `Em transferencia` for days?

J&T uses `Em transferencia` both before collection and while a parcel moves between regional transfer centres. Long road journeys across Brazil and weekends commonly leave multi-day gaps before the next hub scan.

### Does J&T Express Brazil charge customs fees over WhatsApp or SMS?

No. J&T's tracking page explicitly warns that it never requests customs fee payments through WhatsApp, SMS or third-party links. Treat any message demanding a quick payment link as a scam.

### How do I contact J&T Express Brazil customer service?

Call `0800-055-0050`, email `meajuda@jtexpress.com.br` (or `escalations@jtexpress.com.br` for complaints) or submit the [order feedback form](https://www.jtexpress.com.br/contactus/orderFeedback). For refunds on online orders, contact the seller as well.
