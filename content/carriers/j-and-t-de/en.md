J&T Express Germany (J&T Express Germany GmbH, Frankfurt am Main) delivers parcels across Germany for J&T Express, the parcel group founded in Indonesia in 2015 and listed in Hong Kong. Track a parcel on [jtexpress.de/tracking](https://www.jtexpress.de/tracking) or paste its number in the box above. The number starts with `JTDE`, and the shop that sent your order has it: look in your order details or its shipping email.

## J&T Express Germany tracking number: what it looks like

J&T says its numbers usually start with JTDE and mix letters and digits. In practice that's JTDE followed by 12 digits:

| Shape (invented) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `JTDE123456789012` | JTDE and 12 digits, the waybill J&T Express Germany prints |

**Where to find it.** J&T's FAQ lists the sender, the shop's order page, the order confirmation email, a shipping email or text message, an email before delivery or confirming it, and the label on the parcel. If you can't find it, J&T sends you to the sender or the shopping platform's customer service. For a Temu order, see [Temu order tracking](guide:temu-orders); otherwise [how to find a tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number).

J&T's tracker takes up to 10 numbers in one search, separated by spaces or commas.

**Numbers that look alike.** J&T's networks in other countries have their own sites and numbers: `JTAU` and 12 digits is [J&T Express Australia](carrier:j-and-t-au), `JTE` and 12 digits J&T in the Middle East, and `JT` with 13 digits is used by J&T in China, among others. Only `JTDE` belongs to the German network. If the German tracker answers `No tracking records yet`, compare the number with the shop's email, or ask the shop.

## J&T Express Germany tracking statuses explained

J&T's tracker opens in German; switch the site to English or Chinese and the scans reload in that language. Each scan is a time and one sentence, with no place. The English sentences, in the order a parcel meets them:

| J&T's status | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `The shipment has been successfully picked up and is being prepared for transportation` | J&T has collected the parcel from the sender. |
| `The shipment arrives at the distribution center and is prepared for transport to the next destination.` | It reached a J&T sorting centre. |
| `The shipment is dispatched from the transit center to the next destination` | It left that centre for the next stop. |
| `Your package has been loaded and is on its way to you` | It's in the delivery van: out for delivery. |
| `Delivery failed because the address could not be found` | The driver couldn't find your address. |
| `Your package has been successfully delivered.` | Delivered. J&T adds how (see below). |
| `Returns have begun` | After a failed delivery, the parcel is on its way back to the sender. |
| `The shipment has been returned` | The return is complete. |

The loading line can appear several times: J&T writes it once for each delivery run. The delivered line goes on with the delivery type in 【】 brackets (`Left with neighbor`, `Front Door Porch`, `Front Desk Reception`, `Garden Yard`, `Mailbox` or `Garage`) and ends with J&T's hotline number. The brackets can also be empty.

**Which neighbour?** When a parcel was left with a neighbour, the tracker shows `Click to view delivery details` under that scan. Enter the postcode of the delivery address to see the neighbour's name and house number. After five wrong postcodes you have to wait before trying again.

## J&T Express Germany delivery: neighbours, safe places and photos

**Delivery preferences.** J&T's delivery notification email carries a link to its [delivery preferences page](https://www.jtexpress.de/delivery-preference). Confirm the delivery postcode, then choose: signed for by you, left with a neighbour (you give their name and whether they live next door or on your floor), or left without a signature at the front door, at reception, in the garden, in the mailbox or in the garage. J&T says the driver follows your choice, and if the parcel is left at the place you chose, the risk of loss is yours. Once the parcel's status no longer allows a choice, the page says so.

**Delivery photo.** J&T's proof-of-delivery notice says the driver photographs the parcel when handing it over or leaving it at an agreed place, framing only the parcel and the spot; faces caught by accident are blurred. J&T keeps the photo for six months, longer while a complaint is open, and may share it with the sender to settle one.

**Missed delivery.** J&T publishes no number of delivery attempts, no storage period and no delivery hours, and its German site lists no parcel shops, lockers or depots where you could collect a parcel. When the driver can't find an address, tracking shows `Delivery failed because the address could not be found`, and `Returns have begun` can follow. More on [missed deliveries](guide:missed-delivery).

**Wrong address.** J&T's FAQ sends you to the sender or the shopping platform, quickly: some parcels can't be redirected once shipped, depending on where they are and on the sender's rules.

## J&T Express parcel late, missing or returned

The shop chose J&T, so for an online order the shop is your first contact. Under German law, when you buy as a consumer, the seller carries the risk of loss or damage in transit unless you hired the carrier yourself.

**Not moving.** J&T publishes no delivery times for recipients. If the last scan is days old, ask the shop, or call J&T with your number. More on [tracking that isn't updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

**Delivered but not received.** J&T's FAQ asks you to check safe places first (doorway, mailbox, garage, garden, reception) and whether family, a neighbour or building staff took the parcel in. Still missing? Call J&T's hotline to report it. See also [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).

**Lost, damaged or returned.** J&T publishes no claim form, deadline or compensation for recipients. Ask the shop for a refund or replacement, with your tracking number; see [lost parcels](guide:lost-parcel), [damaged parcels](guide:damaged-parcel) and [returned to sender](guide:returned-to-sender).

## Tracking J&T Express Germany parcels with Peek

[Peek](/) names J&T Express Germany at once from a `JTDE` number, typed with or without spaces, in capitals or not. You can also paste a jtexpress.de/tracking link with `?q=` and the number.

Peek reads J&T's own tracker and shows the status, each scan at its exact time and the delivery time. Scans show in J&T's English, even if you read them in German on jtexpress.de. Peek counts the loading line as out for delivery and `Returns have begun` as a return, so the parcel's status reads "Out for delivery" or "Returned to sender". On the delivered line it keeps the delivery type, such as `Left with neighbor`, without brackets, and drops the hotline sentence. J&T gives no places, expected delivery day or weight, so the map stays empty. Peek doesn't open the neighbour details behind J&T's postcode check, and on a very long history it keeps the newest 100 scans.

Peek checks every 10 minutes by day, every 2 minutes once the parcel is out for delivery, and every hour or two overnight, and can notify you of every scan, important steps only or delivery day only. It can't contact J&T or the shop for you.

## Questions about J&T Express Germany tracking

### Is J&T Express Germany legit?

Yes. J&T Express Germany GmbH, registered in Frankfurt am Main, is the German company of J&T Express, a parcel group founded in Indonesia in 2015 and listed in Hong Kong. It looks for franchise partners across Germany to deliver its parcels, and reviewers on Trustpilot mostly write about Temu orders.

### Which neighbour has my J&T parcel?

J&T's tracker tells you: click `Click to view delivery details` under the delivered scan and enter the delivery postcode to see the neighbour's name and house number.

### Does J&T Express have parcel shops in Germany?

No J&T page lists any. Its delivery choices are you, a neighbour or a safe place at your address.

### Why is my J&T tracking number not found?

Check that it starts with `JTDE` and has 12 digits after it: numbers from J&T's other networks, such as `JTAU` in Australia, aren't German. If the tracker still shows no records, ask the shop to confirm the number.

### How do I contact J&T Express in Germany?

Call +49 6931090650 (Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 20:00), use the live chat on jtexpress.de or email de-jtservice@jtexpress.de.
