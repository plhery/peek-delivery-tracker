J&T Express delivers parcels in Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates and Kuwait. In Saudi Arabia it offers online shops same-day, basic and economy delivery, and brings parcels door to door from China. The three countries share one J&T website, jtexpress.me, and one kind of tracking number, which Peek files under J&T Express Middle East. Track a parcel on [J&T's tracking page](https://www.jtexpress.me/KSA/trackOrder) or paste its number in the box above. The number starts with `JTE`, and the shop that sent your order has it: look in your order details or the shop's shipping message.

## J&T Express Middle East tracking number: what it looks like

A J&T Express Middle East waybill number is `JTE` followed by 12 digits. With invented digits:

| Number (invented) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `JTE000000000001` | `JTE` and 12 digits: a J&T Express Middle East waybill. Peek names J&T Express Middle East at once. |
| `JTE00000000001` | `JTE` with 11 digits (or 13): not a J&T Middle East number. Peek recognises no carrier, so check for a missing or extra digit. |
| `JTDE000000000001` | `JTDE` and 12 digits: [J&T Express Germany](carrier:j-and-t-de). `JTAU` is [J&T Express Australia](carrier:j-and-t-au) and `JTUK` is J&T Express UK. |

Spaces and lower case don't matter when you paste a number into Peek. Can't find yours? See [how to find a tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number). For an order shipped from China, [tracking parcels from China](guide:tracking-from-china) explains the first days, before J&T has the parcel.

## J&T Express Middle East tracking statuses explained

J&T's tracker lists a parcel's scans newest first. Each scan has a short heading with a sentence under it, a time, and the town and province of the J&T branch that scanned it. The tracker can also name the courier, give contact details and show proof of delivery.

`Order creation` means the sender, often the shop, has created the order, and J&T hasn't collected the parcel yet. Peek's code knows that heading by name. It reads J&T's other headings by their words to place the parcel in one of its own steps:

| Peek shows | What it means for a J&T parcel |
| --- | --- |
| "Label created" | J&T's `Order creation`: the order exists, but J&T doesn't have the parcel yet. |
| "In transit" | J&T has the parcel and is moving it between its branches. Scans name the branch's town and province. |
| "Out for delivery" | A J&T driver has it for delivery. Keep your phone on: J&T tries to reach recipients by phone, email or SMS. |
| "Delivery attempted" | The driver couldn't deliver. J&T makes up to three attempts. |
| "Delivered" | Delivered to the address, not necessarily to you in person. |
| "Returned to sender" | On its way back to the sender, or back there. |
| "Waiting for the carrier" | J&T doesn't list the number yet, or lists it with no scans. |

A heading Peek can't place reads as "In transit", unless the parcel was already out for delivery or at a later step, such as an attempt, a delivery or a return: then it stays at that step. Either way, you see J&T's words in the history.

## J&T delivery in Saudi Arabia, the UAE and Kuwait

**How long.** J&T's shipping information pages give its standard for the first delivery attempt:

- **Saudi Arabia:** within 24 hours in Tier 1 areas, 48 hours in Tier 2, and 72 to 96 hours in Tier 3, Tier 4 and remote areas. J&T doesn't say which places fall in which tier.
- **UAE:** within 24 hours in normal areas, 3 to 7 business days in remote areas.
- **Kuwait:** J&T's Kuwait page repeats the Saudi table, prices in Saudi riyals, so go by the shop's estimate.

**Hours and attempts.** J&T's published terms, written for parcels sent between individuals and shown on its Saudi, UAE and Kuwait pages alike, give delivery hours of 9:00 to 22:00, Sunday to Saturday, and up to three delivery attempts. If J&T can't reach you by phone, email or SMS after three failed attempts, it holds the parcel for up to 7 working days, then sends it back to the sender.

**Cash on delivery.** The same terms ask you to have the exact amount ready: J&T's drivers don't give change. They take cash or a card on their payment terminal, and nothing else.

**Who can take it.** Under those terms, J&T delivers to the address the sender gave, not necessarily to the person named on the label. In a building with a central mail department, the parcel goes there.

**Changing the address or day.** J&T's public pages don't explain how to do it. Contact J&T with your number (below) or ask the shop, quickly. See [changing a delivery address](guide:change-delivery-address).

**Customs.** J&T's pages say nothing to recipients about duties on parcels from abroad. Ask the shop whether your price included them, and see [parcels held at customs](guide:customs).

## J&T Express parcel late, missing or returned

The shop chose J&T, so for an online order the shop is your first contact.

**Stuck on `Order creation`.** J&T doesn't have the parcel yet. If nothing changes for days, ask the shop whether it has handed the parcel over. More on [tracking that isn't updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

**Not found.** J&T's tracker has nothing for a number it doesn't know yet, or one without scans. Check that the number is `JTE` and 12 digits, then give it a day.

**No movement.** If the last scan is days old, send J&T your number on WhatsApp or call its hotline (below). In Saudi Arabia, J&T's compensation policy lets the sender, or whoever paid for the shipping, ask J&T to check a parcel with no update for 7 working days.

**Delivered but not received.** Ask your household and your building's mail room or reception: J&T can hand the parcel to someone else at the address. Then contact J&T with your number, and the shop. In Saudi Arabia, a parcel J&T handed to the wrong person by mistake is one of the cases its compensation policy covers. See [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).

**Lost or damaged.** For an online order, ask the shop for a refund or a replacement: see [lost parcels](guide:lost-parcel) and [damaged parcels](guide:damaged-parcel). J&T's own claim rules differ from page to page. In Saudi Arabia, its compensation policy lets the private person who paid for the shipping, sender or recipient, claim within 30 calendar days of delivery, unless J&T's contract with a business sender says otherwise. The claim goes by email to claim@jtexpress.sa.com, with an invoice showing the tracking number, photos or video of the parcel as received and opened, and an IBAN certificate. J&T decides within 7 days; if it accepts, it pays within 16 working days, up to 200 SAR for an uninsured parcel, or its value if lower. Its terms and shipping information pages, shown in all three countries, give the sender 7 days from proof of delivery to claim for damage, in writing to cs@jtexpress.sa.com, and cap compensation at 200 SAR for a damaged parcel and 300 SAR for a lost one.

**Returned.** After three failed attempts and up to 7 working days at J&T, the parcel goes back to the sender. Ask the shop to send it again or refund you. See [returned to sender](guide:returned-to-sender).

## Track a J&T Express Middle East parcel with Peek

Paste the `JTE` number in [Peek](/): it names J&T Express Middle East at once ("Detected carrier"), whether you type it with spaces or in lower case. A tracking link from J&T's site works too, from jtexpress.me or jtexpress.ae, with the number after `waybillNo=`. If you meant another carrier, press "Change".

Peek asks J&T's own tracker, through the same lookup its tracking page makes, in English. It shows each scan's heading, its exact time and the branch's town and province, and on a very long history it keeps the newest 100 scans. It leaves out the sentence under each heading, the courier's name and contact details and the proof of delivery. J&T gives no expected delivery day or weight, so Peek shows none. Until J&T lists a scan, the parcel reads "Waiting for the carrier" and Peek keeps asking. When J&T's tracker asks for a captcha, Peek doesn't solve it: it tries J&T again later and asks other tracking services in the meantime.

Peek checks J&T about every 10 minutes while you follow the parcel, or every 2 minutes once it's out for delivery. After two days without a new scan (12 hours while it shows "Label created"), it checks hourly. It can notify you of "Every scan", "Important steps" or "Delivery only". It can't contact J&T or the shop for you.

## Questions about J&T Express tracking in the Middle East

### Is J&T Express legit in Saudi Arabia and the UAE?

Yes. J&T Express is a parcel group founded in Indonesia in 2015. Its Saudi company is JNT Express KSA LLC in Riyadh (commercial registration 1010728810), and its UAE pages name J&T Express Couriers Services LLC, in Dubai. Their tracking numbers start with `JTE`.

### What does Order creation mean on J&T tracking?

The sender has created the order with J&T, but J&T hasn't collected the parcel yet. Peek shows it as "Label created". If it doesn't change for days, ask the shop.

### How long does J&T take to deliver in Saudi Arabia?

J&T's standard is a first delivery attempt within 24 hours in Tier 1 areas, 48 hours in Tier 2 and 72 to 96 hours in Tier 3, Tier 4 and remote areas. In the UAE it's 24 hours in normal areas and 3 to 7 business days in remote ones.

### What happens if I miss the J&T driver?

Under J&T's published terms, it tries up to three times, between 9:00 and 22:00. After three failed attempts it holds the parcel for up to 7 working days, then returns it to the sender.

### How do I contact J&T Express in the UAE?

Call 600566122, write to cs@jtexpress.ae or send a WhatsApp message to +971 526477476. In Saudi Arabia, call 800-1010-111 or write to cs@jtexpress.sa.com; in Kuwait, call +965 65850830 or write to cs.kw@jtexpress.ae.
