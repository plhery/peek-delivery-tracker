Jet Express (Jet Express OÜ) is a courier company in Tallinn with its website at kuller.ee. It delivers across Estonia every day, runs same-day couriers in Tallinn and sends parcels and freight abroad by road and air. It is also the official representative of Jetpak in Estonia. Track a shipment with its order number on Jet Express's tracking page at portal.kuller.ee, or paste the number in the box above.

## Jet Express tracking number: what it looks like

Jet Express publishes no number format, and no public page shows a whole Jet Express number. Its pages name the number three ways:

| Where | What it asks for |
| --- | --- |
| FAQ on kuller.ee | Your order number (Estonian: *tellimuse number*) to track a shipment. |
| Track & Trace page at portal.kuller.ee | A "Package number". |
| Claims form, "A case with a shipment" | The "Order/shipment number" (*tellimuse/saadetise number*). |

**Where to find it.** Jet Express says that to get an order number, you go to its self-service portal or ask customer service for it. Regular customers can also follow their shipments in real time in the self-service portal. If a company or a shop sent you the parcel, ask the sender for the number. If you can't find any number, see [how to find your tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number).

## Who runs portal.kuller.ee

portal.kuller.ee is Jet Express's own tracking and self-service portal, on its kuller.ee domain. On kuller.ee, the menu item "Jälgi saadetist" ("Tracking" in English) and the "Jälgimine" button open the Track & Trace page there, and "Iseteenindus" (self-service) opens the portal's login for customers. The Track & Trace page has sections for the tracking log, the receiver, deviations and proof of delivery (POD).

## How Jet Express delivers

As read on kuller.ee:

- **Across Estonia**: 1 working day to the big cities, 1 to 2 working days to rural areas and 1 to 3 to the islands.
- **Same day in Tallinn and nearby**: a direct VIP courier and 1-, 2-, 3-, 4- and 6-hour services, with bike couriers in the city centre.
- **Express courier** in Tallinn, Tartu, Narva, Pärnu, Viljandi and Kuressaare.
- **Abroad**: Jet Express quotes 3 to 5 hours to Finland, 4 to 24 hours to Scandinavia and the rest of Europe, and 48 hours worldwide. Its global courier service covers countries inside and outside the EU, and it is an IATA-accredited cargo agent.

A courier can be ordered 24 hours a day, public holidays included. In Tallinn and Tartu, the latest pickup is at 16:00. Each parcel gets a Jet Express label, even when a shipment has several parcels, and a shipment over 35 kg must be on a pallet.

Jet Express's terms say that:

- If a parcel can't be delivered as ordered, Jet Express phones the customer who ordered the transport to agree where it goes, and adds a surcharge from its price list.
- If the recipient refuses the parcel, the driver takes it back to the sender.
- The recipient confirms delivery with their name and signature in Jet Express's system. A signature means the parcel arrived complete and undamaged, so check it before you sign.
- Jet Express may use subcontractors.

Its site mentions no parcel lockers or pickup points.

## Customs: shipments from outside the EU

Jet Express carries imports and exports worldwide, and its customs service helps with import, export and transit clearance in Estonia and abroad. Its terms say that the customer who ordered the transport pays all customs duties, taxes and fees unless agreed otherwise, and that Jet Express isn't liable for delays at customs.

Since 1 July 2026, goods from outside the EU worth up to €150 pay an EU customs duty of €3 per item. Since 1 November 2026, a Union handling fee of €2 per item comes on top, whatever the value. The Estonian Tax and Customs Board (EMTA) says that a gift from one private person to another doesn't pay the handling fee. If you didn't order the transport yourself, ask the sender who pays. More in [parcels held at customs](guide:customs).

## Jet Express shipment late, stuck or lost

**No update.** Check the Track & Trace page with your order number first: tracking services may know less, or nothing, about a Jet Express shipment. If it shows no progress, call customer service on a weekday. More in [why tracking stops updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

**Change or cancel.** To change or cancel an order, contact customer service by email or phone.

**Lost.** Jet Express's terms count a shipment as lost if it hasn't reached the recipient within 30 working days of the planned delivery time.

**Late, lost or damaged: the claim.** Claims for delay, loss or damage go in within 7 days, through the "A case with a shipment" form on kuller.ee (with the order or shipment number, a description and files) or in writing to info@kuller.ee. The terms say the customer who ordered the transport makes the claim. All shipments are covered by the carrier's liability insurance, and Jet Express pays compensation under the CMR Convention and Estonia's Law of Obligations Act. If a shop sent you the parcel, ask the shop for a refund or a new parcel: see [lost parcels](guide:lost-parcel) and [damaged parcels](guide:damaged-parcel).

**Delivered but not there.** The delivery is signed for in Jet Express's system with a name. Ask Jet Express or the sender who signed, then see [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).

## Track a Jet Express shipment with Peek

[Peek](/) can't tell a Jet Express shipment by its number alone: Jet Express publishes no number shape, and Peek's catalog links none to it, so Jet Express never shows under "Fits this number". Paste the number all the same: Peek follows more than 3,500 carriers. Then pick Jet Express with "Change" (on a parcel you've already added, "Change carrier"). Typing "Jet Express OÜ" or "Estonia" finds it too; "kuller" doesn't.

A tracking link is quicker: paste a portal.kuller.ee Track & Trace link that starts `https://portal.kuller.ee/opter/TT?keyword=` and ends with the number (with or without `https://`), and Peek takes the number from it and names Jet Express straight away. A link without `keyword=` has no number in it for Peek. A number of six digits alone, typed or in a link, first shows "That looks like a pickup code": once you pick Jet Express with "Change", Peek takes it as the tracking number.

If you leave the carrier open, Peek looks for it through tracking services. When the number looks like other carriers' numbers, Peek asks the services while you add it ("Checking tracking services…"), and if none knows it yet, the carrier list says "Not found by tracking services yet. Peek will keep checking after you add it." A number that looks like no other carrier's isn't checked while you add it: the carrier list says "Peek will look it up after you add it." When the services name Jet Express as the carrier and show the parcel moving, Peek files it under Jet Express.

Peek doesn't read Jet Express's tracking page itself. For a parcel filed under Jet Express, it asks ParcelsApp, Ship24 and 17TRACK in turn (Ship24 first for a ten-digit number) and keeps the first one that has the parcel. It shows the status and history that service holds, in its wording. That history can lag behind Jet Express's own, and for some shipments there's nothing at all. Until a service has the parcel, its tracking link opens Jet Express's Track & Trace page for the number; after that, it opens the service's page. Peek doesn't pass your IP address or country on to the services.

Peek checks every 15 minutes, out for delivery too; a service that didn't know the number is asked again a day later. It can notify you of every scan, important steps only or "Delivery only". It can't contact Jet Express or the sender for you.

## Questions about Jet Express tracking

### What is Jet Express?

Jet Express OÜ is a Tallinn courier company, at kuller.ee, that delivers across Estonia and sends parcels and freight abroad by road and air. It began in 1995 as a bike courier in Tallinn, represented the Jetpak brand in Estonia from 2010 to 2015 and has been Jet Express since 2015. It is still Jetpak's official representative in Estonia.

### Is portal.kuller.ee Jet Express's site?

Yes. kuller.ee links its tracking menu and its self-service login to portal.kuller.ee, so it is Jet Express's own tracking and self-service portal.

### Which number do I need to track Jet Express?

Your order number. The tracking page calls it a "Package number". Get it from the self-service portal, from customer service or from whoever sent the shipment.

### How long does Jet Express take in Estonia?

Jet Express gives 1 working day to the big cities, 1 to 2 working days to rural areas and 1 to 3 to the islands. In Tallinn, it also delivers the same day.

### How do I contact Jet Express?

Call +372 646 3222 on weekdays, 8:00–18:00, or write to info@kuller.ee. For a late, lost or damaged shipment, use the "A case with a shipment" form within 7 days.
