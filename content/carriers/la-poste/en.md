Colissimo is the parcel service of La Poste, France's national post. Paste a Colissimo or La Poste tracking number, or a laposte.fr tracking link, in the box above to follow the parcel. La Poste's own tracking is on laposte.fr. The number comes from the sender, usually in the shop's shipping email.

## Colissimo tracking number: what it looks like

| Shape (invented) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `6A12345678905` | 1 digit, 1 letter and 11 digits: a Colissimo parcel, or a tracked or registered letter. The last digit is a check digit. |
| `LA000000005FR` | 2 letters, 9 digits and FR: an international postal number from France, used for Colissimo parcels and for tracked and registered letters. The digit before FR is a check digit. |
| `87000000000000P` | 15 characters starting 863 to 866, 870, 875, 880 or 885: a Smart Data number, which La Poste uses for tracked and registered letters. The last character is a check character; La Poste's tracking also finds the number without it. |

Postal numbers starting XU, XR, XT, XW, XY or PZ and ending FR belong to [Chronopost](carrier:chronopost). Twelve characters suggest [Colis Privé](carrier:colis-prive). Plain 13- or 14-digit numbers also fit DPD and others, so check the shop's email. Lost the number? See [where to find a tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number).

## What Colissimo tracking statuses mean

Peek shows La Poste's scans in French, word for word, and gives the parcel a stage of its own, in your language, from the latest scan. These are wordings La Poste uses, roughly in the order a parcel meets them, with the stage Peek gives each:

| La Poste's scan | Peek shows | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Votre Colissimo va bientôt nous être confié ! Il est en cours de préparation chez votre expéditeur.` | Label created | The sender is preparing the parcel; La Poste doesn't have it yet. |
| `Votre colis a été déposé dans un point postal.` | Received by carrier | The sender has handed it in at a postal point. |
| `Votre colis est en transit sur nos plateformes logistiques.` | In transit | Moving between sorting centres. |
| `Les formalités import/export sont en cours sur votre colis.` | At customs | It's going through customs. |
| `Votre Colissimo est en cours de traitement sur le site de tri local.` | In transit | At the local sorting site. |
| `Votre colis est sur son site de distribution. Nous le préparons pour le mettre en livraison.` | Out for delivery | Being sorted into today's round. |
| `Votre colis n'a pas pu vous être remis. Il sera mis en livraison demain (hors dimanche et jours fériés).` | Delivery attempted | Not delivered; it goes out again the next day, except Sundays and public holidays. |
| `Nous sommes passés mais nous n'avons pu vous remettre votre colis. Il va être acheminé vers votre point de retrait.` | Delivery attempted | Missed; it's going to a pickup point. |
| `Votre colis est disponible dans votre point de retrait pendant un délai de 15 jours calendaires.` | Ready for pickup | Waiting for you at a pickup point, until the deadline the message gives. |
| `Votre colis est livré dans votre boite aux lettres.` | Delivered | In your letterbox. |
| `Votre colis vous a attendu dans votre point de retrait jusqu'à la date limite. Nous sommes contraints de le renvoyer à l'expéditeur.` | Needs attention | Not collected in time; it's going back to the sender. |
| `Votre colis est livré à son expéditeur à la suite d'un retour.` | Returned to sender | It's back with the sender. |

The confusing one is `Votre colis est sur son site de distribution…`. It reads like "still at the depot", but it's the sort into the morning's delivery round: delivery, or a failed attempt, follows the same day.

## Missed delivery and pickup points

After a missed delivery, the tracking can say what comes next: another delivery the next day, or a pickup point (see the scans above). The scan that says it's waiting there can give the deadline, such as `Le délai de retrait est de 5 jours.` (5 days) or 15 calendar days. Peek shows the post office, shop or locker holding it, with its address. If nobody collects it in time, it goes back to the sender. Changes to a delivery are made with La Poste, on laposte.fr; Peek can't make them. More in [missed deliveries](guide:missed-delivery) and [uncollected parcels](guide:uncollected-parcel).

## Colissimo parcel late, lost, damaged or at customs

**No movement.** See [tracking that isn't updating](guide:tracking-not-updating), then contact the sender.

**Lost or damaged.** If you bought from a shop, start there. In the EU, Your Europe says the shop is responsible for damage until you receive the goods. Without an agreed date, it must deliver within 30 days; if it doesn't, remind it and give it a reasonable extra time, after which you can cancel and get your money back. See [lost parcels](guide:lost-parcel) and [damaged parcels](guide:damaged-parcel).

**Complaining to La Poste.** ARCEP, France's postal regulator, says a complaint is free and that the sender or the recipient can make one, though their rights differ. Use the channels below. If the answer doesn't satisfy you, ask La Poste's appeals service (service recours) through the same channels. After that, or with no answer two months later, the Médiateur du Groupe La Poste can take the case, for free, within a year of your first written complaint.

**Customs.** A parcel coming into France from outside the EU may owe VAT and, on orders up to €150, the EU's flat €3 customs duty per item, in force since 1 July 2026; the seller or the carrier collects it. French customs says the carrier, La Poste for example, may ask you for these taxes on delivery and may add its own clearance fees, which have nothing to do with what customs collects. More in [parcels held at customs](guide:customs).

## Colissimo contact number and customer service

La Poste's channels, as listed by ARCEP and France's official public-service directory:

- **From abroad:** +33 810 821 821, the international customer service, Monday to Friday, 8am to 7pm French time.
- **From France:** 3631, Monday to Friday 8:30am to 7pm and Saturday 8:30am to 1pm. The service is free; you pay the cost of a call to a landline.
- **Online:** through La Poste's website, laposte.fr.
- **By post:** Service consommateurs, 99 999 La Poste. Or ask at a post office.

> Cybermalveillance.gouv.fr, the French government's cybercrime help service, quotes La Poste: it "will never ask you to pay to collect a parcel by SMS or email". In doubt, don't tap the link: check the real tracking on laposte.fr or in Peek. Report La Poste or Colissimo phishing to phishing@laposte.fr and, in France, scam texts to 33700 for free. More in [delivery scam texts](guide:delivery-scam-texts).

## Questions about Colissimo tracking

### What is Colissimo?

La Poste's parcel service. Its tracking, on laposte.fr, also covers tracked and registered letters. A Colissimo can come to your letterbox or wait for you at a post office, a shop or a locker.

### How long does Colissimo take?

La Poste Groupe says 41% of Colissimo parcels were delivered the day after posting in 2025, as it speeds up sorting and delivery towards next-day delivery as the standard. It gives no figure for the rest. Peek shows the expected delivery when La Poste gives one.

### Does La Poste deliver on Saturday?

Yes. La Poste Groupe says it delivers mail, newspapers and parcels six days a week, and after a missed delivery the tracking can say the parcel goes out again the next day "hors dimanche et jours fériés" (except Sundays and public holidays).

### Why is La Poste tracking not working?

Check the number against the shapes above: one ending FR that starts XU, XR, XT, XW, XY or PZ is Chronopost's. Once a parcel leaves France, La Poste's tracking can name the company delivering it abroad and give that company's number; Peek can follow it there once it shows progress. If the tracking has simply stopped, see [tracking that isn't updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).
