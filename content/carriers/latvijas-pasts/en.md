Latvijas Pasts (Latvian Post) is Latvia's state-owned national post, with the country's largest network of parcel lockers. Track an item on [mans.pasts.lv/track](https://mans.pasts.lv/track), which opens in Latvian and has an English switch, or paste the number in the box above. The number comes from the shop's or sender's shipping message, or from the courier's SMS before delivery.

## Latvijas Pasts tracking number

An item posted with Latvijas Pasts has a 13-character [international postal number](guide:tracking-number-formats): two letters, nine digits, then `LV`. The first letters tell you the service:

| First letters (invented examples) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `RR000000005LV` | Registered item: you sign when you receive it. |
| `LA000000005LV` | Traceable item: tracked at each stage, put in your mailbox without a signature. |
| `CA000000005LV` | Parcel: you get an invitation to collect it at the post office. |
| `EE000000005LV` | Express Mail Service (EMS). |

Simple or Economy items are not registered at any stage, so they can't be tracked, and no compensation is paid if they're damaged. If you have no number, the item may have been sent this way; see [how to find a tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number).

Where to look: the courier's SMS includes the shipment number, the time it should arrive and a way to redirect the parcel. The parcel locker SMS gives the locker's address, the collection deadline and the code to open it.

Other country letters mean another post: `…LT` is [Lietuvos paštas](carrier:lietuvos-pastas), `…EE` is [Omniva](carrier:omniva). An item from abroad keeps its foreign number, and mans.pasts.lv/track takes those too once Latvijas Pasts has the item; foreign scans then show the country instead of a Latvian office.

## What Latvijas Pasts tracking statuses mean

The tracker shows each step with its date, time and the Latvian post office. Above the list, a five-step bar runs from `Shipment created` to `Delivered to recipient`. Here are the English wordings, in the usual order:

| Latvijas Pasts's status | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `Electronic data about shipment received` | [The sender's data](guide:tracking-statuses) has reached Latvijas Pasts. It doesn't have the item yet. |
| `Item information received` | The sender has registered the item; Latvijas Pasts doesn't have it yet either. |
| `Item received from sender` | Latvijas Pasts has the item. |
| `Item posted / received from customer` | Posted at a post office; the foreign post's posting scan of an item from abroad reads the same. |
| `Item sent to the processing facility` | It has left the post office where it was posted, on its way to sorting. |
| `Item arrived at office of exchange` | At the international exchange office, in Riga or abroad. |
| `Item sent to the Customs` | Presented to customs at the exchange office, as the item leaves the sending country. |
| `Item returned from Customs` | Customs has released it. Not a return to the sender. |
| `Item sent abroad` | It has left the exchange office for the country it's going to. |
| `Item sent abroad (EDI-received)` | The post abroad has sent the item on its way to Latvia; Latvijas Pasts knows it from that post's electronic data. |
| `Item accepted for temporary storage` | An item from abroad, held in temporary storage under customs supervision until it's cleared. Not an acceptance from the sender. |
| `Electonic notification (SMS) of customs clearance sent to recipient` | You've been sent an SMS about the item's customs clearance (the spelling is Latvijas Pasts's). It's still in temporary storage. |
| `Customs clearance completed, item to be delivered to recipient` | Cleared by customs; it goes on to the office that delivers it. |
| `Item arrived at processing facility`, `Item arrived at sorting centre` | At a sorting centre. |
| `Item sent to next processing facility` | On its way to the next sorting centre. |
| `Item sent to delivery facility` | On its way to the office that will deliver it or hand it out. |
| `Item received at delivery office` | At the office that will deliver it or hand it out. It can still go out for delivery from there. |
| `Item redirected` | Sent elsewhere, for example to another pickup point (see Redirecting below). |
| `Item address re-labelled` | Relabelled for its new address, where it now goes. |
| `Item handed over for delivery` | Out for delivery. |
| `Unsuccessful delivery attempt` | Delivery failed: see missed deliveries below. |
| `Notification - pick-up invitation printed` | Ready for pickup: the post office has printed an invitation for you to collect it there. |
| `Item arrived at pick-up point` | A foreign post's scan: the item waits at a pickup point abroad. It names the country, not the point. |
| `Item delivered` | Delivered, also when the destination country's post delivers an item sent abroad. |

`Item returned from Customs` is the one that worries people: it means the item is back from the customs check and moving again, not that it's going back. `Item awaiting next planned cycle` means it's waiting for the next dispatch at the exchange office. For duties and EU fees on parcels from outside the EU, see [parcels held at customs](guide:customs).

If the tracker says `No information received on the tracking of the postal item. Please, try again!`, check the number, or wait: the item may not be scanned yet, and an item from abroad shows up once the post abroad has sent its data. `Postal item number entered incorrectly. Please check it and enter it again.` means the number is mistyped. If a correct number stays unknown for several days, ask the sender whether the item was sent with tracking: Simple and Economy items never show up.

## Latvijas Pasts parcel lockers, couriers and missed deliveries

**Parcel lockers.** When your item reaches a locker you get an SMS with the address, the deadline and a six-digit code (and an e-mail, if the sender gave your address). It waits up to seven days; if it's still there on the third day the code is sent again, and you get a reminder before the time runs out. After that the courier takes it to the sorting centre and a paper invitation comes to your address asking you to arrange redelivery; it's kept for up to 30 days, then goes back to the sender. If the code never came, ask the sender to contact Latvijas Pasts: it corrects the phone number only at the sender's request. Lockers take parcels up to 30 kg and 38 × 38 × 58 cm.

**Unisend lockers.** A parcel sent from Latvia to a Unisend locker in Lithuania or Estonia also waits seven days, with the code sent by SMS and e-mail (to a Latvian, Lithuanian or Estonian number) and again after 48 hours. If nobody collects it, it goes straight back to the sender.

**Courier to your door.** Latvijas Pasts says it attempts delivery up to three times. If an attempt fails and you haven't redirected the parcel, the courier leaves a notice and comes back; if that fails too, the parcel is put into storage: you can collect it at the Main Post Office or call customer support to arrange another option. The sender sets how long it's stored. An Express Mail (EMS) item that misses you leaves a "You have received a shipment" notice in your letterbox: call the number on it to arrange redelivery, with the shipment number at hand.

**Post office or Customer Centre.** You get an SMS or a written notice naming the office. Bring an identity document; a driver's licence works for items worth up to €150. You can collect without the notice if you know the shipment number, or call the number on the notice to have it delivered to your address, paying the delivery fee on hand-over. Someone else can collect with your authorisation and their passport or ID card. For registered or insured items, a second notice comes after 5 days, and the item goes back to the sender if not collected within 30 days of the first. If the label has only part of your name and the item comes from an online shop abroad, bring proof that the order is yours as well as your ID.

**Redirecting.** If the sender gave your phone number, or you signed up for SMS notifications, the SMS has a link to send the item to a parcel locker, a Customer Service Location or another address, or to the free "Postman at home" service where your address has it. The redirection page lists a parcel locker as free, a Customer Service Location at €1.89 and a courier to an address at €4.99, VAT included. You can't redirect an item already in a locker or one under customs control, nor send to a locker a registered or insured item with proof of delivery, or a cash-on-delivery item larger than 38 × 38 × 58 cm. When you sign up for SMS notifications you can also name a favourite parcel locker for your items.

## Latvijas Pasts parcel lost, damaged or not arriving

A claim for a lost or damaged locker parcel must come from the sender, so if you bought online, write to the shop. The claim lists the contents and their value, with a receipt, invoice or payment confirmation, and photos of any damage. Latvijas Pasts reviews claims within one month.

When a courier brings a registered or insured item, check the contents in front of the courier so a report can be signed. If you open it alone and find damage, take photos and contact the sender for a replacement or refund; Latvijas Pasts says compensation then isn't its responsibility.

For an item sent abroad that hasn't arrived, the sender can ask for an international search: it's free once 30 days have passed since posting.

Tracking stuck, or held at customs on the way in? See [tracking that isn't updating](guide:tracking-not-updating) and [parcels held at customs](guide:customs).

## Tracking Latvijas Pasts with Peek

[Peek](/) names a postal number starting C, E, L or R and ending `LV` as Latvijas Pasts's straight away when its check digit is valid. For other valid numbers ending `LV`, such as `VV…LV`, it asks Latvijas Pasts and [Chronopost](carrier:chronopost) at once and shows "Latvijas Pasts has this parcel." when it knows the number, or "Not found at … yet. Peek will keep checking after you add it." With a wrong check digit Latvijas Pasts isn't asked, and picking it by hand doesn't help (Peek says it "doesn’t issue numbers like this one"): check the number with the sender. A mans.pasts.lv/track link with the number in it always names Latvijas Pasts. Searching the picker for "Latvian Post" finds it too. Until Latvijas Pasts knows the number, a parcel filed under it shows "Waiting for the carrier" and Peek keeps checking.

An item from abroad is followed at its foreign post first. When that post says the item is going to Latvia (or names no destination and you're in Latvia), Peek also asks Latvijas Pasts with the same number, and moves to it once it has a fresh scan; the earlier history stays. To follow it at Latvijas Pasts straight away, pick "Latvijas Pasts" with "Change".

Latvijas Pasts's times carry no time zone: Peek reads them on the clock of the scan's place, Latvian time for Latvian post offices, and lists Latvijas Pasts's own history. Statuses keep Latvijas Pasts's English wording, `Item delivered` included. Peek can show the post office where an item waits and can notify you when the status changes. It gives no expected arrival day and no weight. Peek checks every 10 minutes, and up to every 2 minutes on the day Latvijas Pasts hands the parcel over for delivery, from 6 a.m. Latvian time, while Latvijas Pasts's own tracker answers; through tracking services, every 15 minutes. After two days without news (12 hours while the parcel still shows "Label created" or "Received by carrier"), it checks hourly. When another carrier takes over the delivery, Peek follows the pace of the carrier delivering the parcel.

## Questions about Latvian Post tracking

### What is Latvijas Pasts?

It's Latvia's state-owned national post, offering postal, logistics and financial services in Latvia and abroad. Its website is pasts.lv.

### Is Unisend Latvijas Pasts?

No. Unisend is the parcel brand of Lietuvos paštas, the Lithuanian post. The two run a joint parcel locker network, so Latvijas Pasts customers can send to Unisend lockers in Lithuania and Estonia.

### Can I track an international Latvijas Pasts item?

Yes, if it was sent with tracking. mans.pasts.lv/track also shows the scans it gets from the post abroad, with the country instead of an office. Once the item is abroad the destination country's post delivers it; the tracker's "International Tracking" button leads to a third-party site.

### Does ordinary post from Latvia have a tracking number?

Not always. Simple and Economy items aren't registered, so they can't be tracked; registered, traceable, parcel and EMS items can.
