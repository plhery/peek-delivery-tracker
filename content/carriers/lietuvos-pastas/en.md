Lietuvos paštas (Lithuania Post) is Lithuania's national post, and LP Express is its brand for parcel lockers, couriers and self-service sending. Track a parcel on [lpexpress.lt/track](https://lpexpress.lt/track), which has an English switch, or paste the number in the box above. The number is in the SMS or email Lietuvos paštas sends you, or on the receipt if you posted the item yourself.

## Lithuania Post tracking number

A Lithuania Post number has 13 characters in the [international postal format](guide:tracking-number-formats): two letters, nine digits, then `LT`. Lietuvos paštas's rules name two LP Express prefixes, `CC` and `CH`.

| Shape (invented) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `RR000000005LT` | A postal item posted in Lithuania. |
| `CC000000005LT` | An LP Express parcel going to a parcel locker. |
| `CH000000005LT` | An LP Express parcel a courier brings to an address in Lithuania or abroad. |

When a parcel reaches a locker, Lietuvos paštas sends an SMS and/or email with the number, the locker's address and a PIN.

Genuine messages come from the sender name `LPASTAS` or `LP_EXPRESS`, never from a phone number.

Other country letters mean another post: `…LV` is [Latvijas Pasts](carrier:latvijas-pasts), `…EE` is [Omniva](carrier:omniva). An item from abroad keeps the number its own post gave it. [Venipak](carrier:venipak), another courier in Lithuania, uses a different shape (`V00000E0000000`). Lost it? See [how to find a tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number).

## What Lithuania Post tracking statuses mean

lpexpress.lt/track shows every step in English, Lithuanian, Latvian or Estonian; post.lt, the main site, is in Lithuanian only. Each step has a short headline and a longer event line. Here are the English event lines, in order:

| Lietuvos paštas's status | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `The label for the parcel has been created` | The sender made the label. Lietuvos paštas doesn't have the parcel yet. |
| `Parcel received` | Lietuvos paštas has accepted it from the sender. |
| `The parcel is at the distribution center` | At a sorting centre; the step names the town. |
| `The parcel is transported to another distribution center` | Moving between sorting centres. |
| `The parcel has been sent abroad` | It has left Lithuania. |
| `The parcel was received at the distribution center of the recipient's (or transit) country` | It has reached a sorting centre abroad. |
| `The parcel has been forwarded for delivery` | Out for delivery: on its way to you or to your locker. |
| `The parcel was delivered to the UNISEND parcel locker` | Ready for pickup in the locker the step names. |
| `The parcel has been delivered to the recipient` | Delivered, or picked up from the locker. |
| `Sent to receiver` | A headline, not a step: on its way, or waiting in a locker. |

Two of these confuse people. The headline `Sent to receiver` stays from the first sorting scan until you collect the parcel, including the whole time it waits in a locker; only the event line tells you it's ready. And the locker line says UNISEND even for lockers in Lithuania, which carry the LP EXPRESS brand: Unisend is their name in Latvia and Estonia.

If the page says `Your parcel could not be found`, check the number; `Without tracking` means the item was sent untracked. Inside Lithuania only registered items show a route. More in [what tracking statuses mean](guide:tracking-statuses).

## LP Express parcel lockers, post offices and missed deliveries

**Parcel lockers.** There are about 1,500 LP EXPRESS and Unisend lockers across Lithuania, Latvia and Estonia, open 24/7. At the locker choose "Atsiimti siuntą" (collect parcel) and enter the PIN from your SMS or email. You have 72 hours from the moment the parcel arrives. After that a courier takes it out and you get an SMS with a link: within 4 days you can send it back to the same locker or to another one, once and free. Otherwise it goes back to the sender. A parcel too big for the locker waits 15 days at a Lietuvos paštas service point instead.

**Redirecting.** To send a parcel that's still on its way to a different locker, open the tracking page, enter the number and choose a new one under "To parcel locker" (there is also "To home address"). If it's too late, you'll see `Redirection is not possible` or a message that the redirection term has expired.

**Courier to your door.** The courier needn't wait more than 10 minutes; you sign on the courier's device. If nobody is found, you're told by SMS or email and the parcel is kept free at the logistics centre for 7 calendar days from the day it first went out for delivery.

**Post office items.** Registered items are brought to your address once; if you're out, a notice comes by letterbox, email or SMS. Bring it with ID. Parcels and letters wait 30 days from the day after the notice under the TAI-16 rules; the post.lt FAQ says one calendar month, unless the sender's contract says otherwise.

**From outside the EU.** Lietuvos paštas asks you to pay import charges in LP EXPRESS self-service within 7 calendar days of the payment notice, or the parcel goes back to the sender. Once paid, it is sent on to a post office or locker, with nothing left to pay there. To declare a parcel for you, Lietuvos paštas charges €6.99 up to €150 and €30 above that or for a standard declaration, plus 21% import VAT on the goods and shipping. If you declare it yourself, or the shop collected the VAT at checkout (IOSS), its fee is €2. Since 1 July 2026, goods bought online from outside the EU carry a €3 EU customs duty per item in parcels worth up to €150; from 1 November 2026, they also carry a €2 Union handling fee per item, whatever their value. An item is the goods in a parcel that share a tariff classification, description and, where required, origin. See [parcels held at customs](guide:customs).

## Lithuania Post parcel late, lost or returned

Lietuvos paštas asks you to report a lost or late registered or insured item on 1842. A claim is the sender's, within 6 months of posting, so for a webshop order write to the shop first. Nothing is paid once you have signed for the item.

For an item posted abroad, Lietuvos paštas says to contact the sender. If you spot damage when a courier delivers, have it noted on the courier's device or the accompanying document while the courier is there; Lietuvos paštas does not accept liability otherwise.

If tracking hasn't moved for days, see [tracking that isn't updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

## Lietuvos paštas contact and customer service

The channels as post.lt listed them on 13 October 2026 (the contact pages are in Lithuanian):

- **Phone from Lithuania:** 1842 or +370 700 554 00, at your operator's rate plus a connection fee.
- **Phone from abroad:** +370 5 233 3060.
- **Hours:** working days 8:00 to 19:00, Saturdays 9:00 to 16:00, Lithuanian time.
- **Email:** info@post.lt.
- **Web form:** "Rašykite mums" (write to us) on post.lt, with tabs for claims and international searches. The site also has a chat consultant.

> Lietuvos paštas warns of fake SMS about wrong addresses, held parcels and returns. Its real messages come from `LPASTAS` or `LP_EXPRESS`, never ask for card or personal details and never link to a direct payment. Report fakes to prevencija@post.lt; if you entered card details, call your bank.

## Tracking Lietuvos paštas with Peek

[Peek](/) recognises a number ending `LT` with a valid check digit by its shape if it starts with `C`, `E` or `HC`, and shows Lietuvos paštas with "Detected carrier". Most `CC`, `CH` and `HC` numbers fail the check digit, though: Peek then says "This number’s check digit doesn’t add up.", and "Track it as typed" asks Lietuvos paštas only. For any other postal number ending `LT` with a valid check digit, Peek asks Lietuvos paštas and [Chronopost](carrier:chronopost) at once and shows "Lietuvos paštas has this parcel." when it knows the number. If not yet, you're told it hasn't been found yet and that Peek will keep checking after you add it. Links from lpexpress.lt and post.lt tracking work too.

An item from abroad with an international-format number is first followed at the sending country's post. When that post says the item is going to Lithuania, or names no country and you're in Lithuania, Peek can also ask Lietuvos paštas; once Lietuvos paštas shows new steps, Peek follows the parcel there and keeps the earlier ones. Otherwise pick "Lietuvos paštas" with "Change".

Peek reads the service behind lpexpress.lt/track in English, checks every 10 minutes by day (every 2 once the parcel is out for delivery) and every hour or two overnight, and can notify you when the status changes. It shows each step on a map with its town, sorting centre or country, customs steps, the time it was delivered and the locker or post office where the parcel waits, with its town (not the street or postcode). Lithuanian steps are read on Vilnius time and shown in your device's time zone. There's no expected arrival day and no weight. Statuses keep Lietuvos paštas's English wording; a return shows "Needs attention", then "Returned to sender" once it's back.

## Questions about Lithuania Post tracking

### What is LP Express?

LP Express is the name Lietuvos paštas uses in Lithuania for its parcel lockers, couriers and self-service sending. In Latvia and Estonia the same service is called Unisend.

### Is there Lithuania Post tracking in English?

Yes, on lpexpress.lt/track: switch the language to English. post.lt itself has no English version.

### Can I track an international parcel with Lithuania Post?

Yes, if it was sent with tracking: from Lithuania that means a service "Su sekimu" (with tracking). For an item posted abroad, ask the sender.

### How long does an LP Express parcel take?

Lietuvos paštas says a locker parcel usually reaches a locker in Lithuania the next working day or on Saturday, if the sender drops it off before the collection time.
