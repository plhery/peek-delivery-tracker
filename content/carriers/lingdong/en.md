Lingdong International (领东国际, Shenzhen Lingdong International Freight Forwarding Co., Ltd.) is a freight forwarder in Shenzhen, China, that ships parcels abroad for online sellers. Its numbers start with SZLD: type yours in the 快件查询 (parcel lookup) box on Lingdong's site, lingdong56.com, which opens its tracker, or in the box above. The shop that sent your order gives the number with its shipping details.

## Lingdong tracking number: what it looks like

Lingdong publishes no number format. The waybills its tracker answers are SZLD followed by ten digits. With invented digits:

| Number | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `SZLD1234567890` | SZLD and ten digits: a Lingdong International waybill. |
| `SZLD123456789` | Only nine digits: not a Lingdong shape. Check for a missing digit. |
| `LX000000005CN` | Two letters, nine digits and CN: a China Post number, not Lingdong's. |

Lower case and spaces don't matter when you paste the number into Peek.

**Where to find it.** Lingdong's customers are sellers in China. Its site offers them an account login, a price calculator and booking instructions; apart from the tracking box, nothing on it is aimed at recipients. The seller books the parcel and passes the number on, usually with the order's shipping details. If you can't find it, see [how to find your tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number).

**Other numbers.** Lingdong's tracker also finds a parcel by the reference the seller gave it, which it shows as 参考号 (reference number). Lingdong also sells China Post, PostNL, UPS, DHL and FedEx services, so a number in another company's format belongs to that company: see [tracking number formats](guide:tracking-number-formats).

## Lingdong tracking statuses explained

Lingdong publishes no list of its statuses. It writes its scans in English, or in Chinese with the English after. The wordings seen on its tracker, in the order a parcel meets them:

| Lingdong says | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `货物电子信息已经收到` | Chinese for electronic shipment information received: the seller has created the order. Lingdong doesn't have the parcel yet. |
| `Shipment arrived at facility` | The parcel reached Lingdong's warehouse in Shenzhen. It's the first scan in Lingdong's hands. |
| `Shipment departed from facility` | It left that warehouse on its way abroad. |
| `预计…航班/Flight scheduled for …`, `预计…号航班【Expect flight ….】` | Booked on a flight: the flight's day stands where the dots are. |
| `送往航司途中【On the way to the airline company】` | On its way to the airline. |
| `航班已起飞【The flight has taken off.】`, `航班已启运/Package in transit to destination country` | The flight has left. |
| `航班抵达【Flight arrival】`, `航班已落地，拆板中/Package arrived at destination country` | The flight has landed; with the second wording, its pallets are being broken down. |
| `拆板作业中，等待取货通知【Under destuffing, waiting for pickup instructions.】` | After the landing, the flight's pallets are being broken down while the cargo waits to be collected for customs clearance: you don't have to collect anything. |
| `货物在清关【Customs clearance in progress】` | In customs in the destination country, after the landing. |
| `已完成国内报关手续【Domestic customs clearance procedures have been completed.】`, `货物已清关完成/Import customs clearance complete`, `货物已完成清关【The goods have completed customs clearance】` | The destination country's customs have cleared it, after the landing and before the delivery. "Domestic" here means the destination country's, not China's. |
| `货物已交服务商【The shipment has been transferred to the logistics partner.】` | After customs clearance, Lingdong has handed the parcel to the company that delivers it. |

The Chinese line isn't a scan of the parcel. Lingdong's tracking system writes it by itself for an order that has no scans yet, dated when the order was created.

None of the Lingdong lines Peek knows is a delivery line: the last is the hand-over after customs clearance in the destination country. Another company delivers the parcel there, and its tracking has the rest.

## Who delivers Lingdong parcels

Lingdong's site lists what it sells to sellers: small-parcel lines (专线小包) to the US, the UK, the EU, Australia, Mexico, Canada and Japan; China Post's ePacket (E邮宝) and large-parcel services; PostNL (荷兰邮政); lines for light, bulky goods; and UPS, DHL and FedEx express, for which it is an agent. It also describes itself as an international freight forwarder handling air freight, and offers sellers single-order dispatch and warehousing.

It doesn't say which company delivers on each line. For China Post's parcel service, Lingdong's page says it reaches more than 200 countries and territories, by air, by surface or by a mix of the two, with tracking all the way.

Lingdong publishes no delivery times and no rules for delivery attempts, notice cards, pickup points or how long a parcel is held. Once a local carrier has the parcel, its rules apply. In the EU, since 1 July 2026, an online order from outside the EU worth up to €150 is charged a temporary customs duty of €3 per item; since 1 November 2026, goods sold at a distance from outside the EU are also charged a €2 Union handling fee per item, whatever their value. The €3 duty doesn't change how VAT is charged on parcels up to €150: if the shop uses the EU's IOSS scheme, the VAT is collected when you pay for the order, and none is due at import. For what you pay in the UK, Ireland, the US, Canada or Australia, see [parcels held at customs](guide:customs).

## Lingdong parcel not arriving or tracking stuck

**Stuck on `Shipment departed from facility`.** That's the parcel leaving Lingdong's warehouse in Shenzhen. Flight and customs lines can follow, but none of the lines Peek knows is a delivery line, so Lingdong's history can stay still while the parcel moves on. Ask the seller which carrier delivers it in your country and for that carrier's number; its tracking has the rest. More in [why tracking stops updating](guide:tracking-not-updating) and [tracking a parcel from China](guide:tracking-from-china).

**Only the Chinese line.** `货物电子信息已经收到` on its own means the seller has booked the parcel but it hasn't reached Lingdong's warehouse. If nothing follows for several days, ask the seller whether it has been sent.

**Nothing found.** For a number it doesn't know, Lingdong's tracker shows the number with every other field empty. Check the number with the seller.

**Lost, damaged or returned.** Lingdong publishes no claim process for recipients: its terms of carriage page is empty. Its site has a chat button for problem parcels, claims and complaints, but the seller holds the contract with Lingdong, so ask the seller or the marketplace for a refund or a new parcel.

As listed on lingdong56.com on 27 October 2026, in Chinese only:

- **Phone**: two Chinese mobile numbers, in the site's header and on its contact page (联系我们). No hours or languages are given.
- **QQ**: the site's chat buttons for quotes, bulk business and problem parcels, claims and complaints all open a chat on QQ, a Chinese messaging app.
- **Address**: 深圳市宝安区福海街道展城社区福园二路10号濠和智创B栋625, in Bao'an District, Shenzhen.

Lingdong lists no email and no office outside China. For anything about your order, the seller is the one to ask.

## Track a Lingdong parcel with Peek

[Peek](/) names Lingdong International at once for an SZLD number, a shape no other carrier in its catalog uses. A link to Lingdong's tracker with `documentCode=` and the number in it can be pasted too.

Peek reads Lingdong's own tracker and shows the status, each scan with its place, and the destination country when Lingdong's record gives it. Every wording shows as Lingdong writes it, Chinese included, since none matches Peek's own words. Scan times are read on China's clock, as the tracker gives no time zone; a line dated later than the moment Peek reads it waits until then. Peek never shows a line written more than 12 hours before the time it gives, or one from the tracking system's custom-track feature (轨迹定制), which can place a parcel abroad before it reaches the airport: both are forecasts, not scans. Peek leaves out the recipient's name the tracker shows, and shows no expected delivery date: Lingdong gives none.

Unless Lingdong's record names a delivery carrier Peek can hand the parcel over to, the parcel's history in Peek stops where Lingdong's does. The only such carrier Peek recognises in it is [Swiss Post](carrier:swiss-post), when the record names Swiss Post as its line, holds a Swiss Post number and gives no destination outside Switzerland and Liechtenstein: the parcel then shows "Delivery with Swiss Post", and Peek follows it there once Swiss Post has a scan at least as recent as Lingdong's newest, keeping Lingdong's scans. Lingdong doesn't say it ships with Swiss Post. If the seller gives you the local carrier's number, add it as a parcel of its own.

Peek checks every 10 minutes, and every 2 minutes once the parcel shows "Out for delivery": no Lingdong line Peek knows gives that status, but Swiss Post's scans can after a hand-over. After 12 hours without a new scan while the parcel shows "Label created" or "Received by carrier", or two days otherwise, it checks hourly. It can notify you of "Every scan", "Important steps" or "Delivery only". It can't contact Lingdong or the seller for you.

## Questions about Lingdong tracking

### What is Lingdong International?

It is Shenzhen Lingdong International Freight Forwarding Co., Ltd. (深圳市领东国际货运代理有限公司), a Shenzhen company that ships parcels abroad for Chinese online sellers. Its site is lingdong56.com.

### Why is my Lingdong tracking stuck on "Shipment departed from facility"?

That scan marks the parcel leaving Lingdong's warehouse in Shenzhen, and Lingdong's history can stay on it while the parcel travels on. Flight and customs lines can follow, but none of the Lingdong lines Peek knows is a delivery line: another company delivers the parcel in your country. Ask the seller which company it is and for your tracking number with it.

### How long does Lingdong take to deliver?

Lingdong publishes no delivery times. Go by the estimate the shop gave for your order.

### Where can I track an SZLD number?

On Lingdong's tracker, through the 快件查询 box on lingdong56.com, or in Peek, which recognises SZLD numbers at once.

### Who do I contact about a missing Lingdong parcel?

The seller you ordered from: it booked the parcel with Lingdong and holds the contract. Lingdong lists two Chinese mobile numbers and a QQ chat, but nothing for recipients.
