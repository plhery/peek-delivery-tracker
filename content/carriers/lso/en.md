LSO (Lone Star Overnight) is a regional parcel carrier based in Texas, delivering across Texas and Oklahoma plus major metropolitan areas of Louisiana, Arkansas, Kansas and Missouri. Track a shipment by entering its airbill number (those seen so far are 8 characters starting `ZY0`) on [LSO's tracking page](https://www.lso.com/tracking) or pasting it in the box above. The airbill number comes from the shop or sender that booked LSO.

## LSO tracking number: what it looks like and where to find it

LSO's tracker asks for the "Airbill No", and each label gets its own unique number. LSO doesn't say where recipients receive it: look in the shop's shipping message or on your order page, and if it isn't there, ask the sender. [How to find your tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number) lists more places to look.

LSO publishes no format. The LSO airbill numbers seen so far are 8 characters: `ZY0` followed by five letters or digits.

| Number (invented) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `ZY048291` | An LSO airbill number: ZY, the digit zero, then five characters. |
| `ZY0K7M4P` | Also LSO: the last five can be letters. |
| `ZYO48291` | A typo: the letter O instead of the digit zero. This is no longer an LSO shape. |

The third character is the digit zero. Typed as the letter O, the number looks like nothing LSO uses, and Peek may suggest GLS instead. Spaces, hyphens and lower case don't matter when you paste it into Peek. LSO's own tracker takes up to 10 numbers at once, one per line.

## LSO tracking statuses explained

LSO's site is in English only. Its tracker shows a progress bar with five steps, and under it the airbill number, a tracking status line, the delivery date, who signed and the delivery address. The steps, in the order a parcel meets them:

| LSO's step | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `Label created` | The shipper has made a label. LSO may not have the parcel yet. |
| `Picked Up` | LSO has collected the parcel from the sender. |
| `Inbound scan at destination` | Scanned at the LSO facility that serves your area. |
| `Out for Delivery` | On a delivery vehicle today. |
| `Delivered` | Delivered. The tracker can show who signed for it. |

`Label created` confuses people most. It means the sender has paperwork ready, not that the parcel is moving. If it stays there for days, the shop probably hasn't handed it to LSO yet, so ask the shop. `Inbound scan at destination` sounds final, but it only means the parcel has reached the facility near you; delivery usually follows.

The free-text status line can say more than the five steps. LSO publishes no list of those wordings, so read it as written and contact LSO if it isn't clear.

## LSO delivery days, times and signatures

**Delivery days.** LSO picks up Monday to Friday in most areas. Saturday delivery isn't available for LSO Ground, Early Overnight or Economy Next Day. LSO posts pickup and delivery information for upcoming observed holidays on its holidays page.

**How long it takes.** It depends on the service the sender paid for. LSO Ground takes 1 to 3 business days. Its next-day services arrive by 8:30 am (Early Overnight), by 10:30 am (Priority Next Day; noon or end of day in some rural areas) or by 3:00 pm (Economy Next Day). There is also a 2nd Day service. LSO's Ground delivery guarantee doesn't apply to parcels handed over during its stated peak season.

**Signatures.** LSO doesn't get a delivery signature for its 8:30 am Early Overnight deliveries. On LSO Ground, a sender can pay extra for an adult signature.

**Pickup points.** LSO doesn't mention any pickup points or lockers for recipients. Its locator lists service centres and drop boxes by ZIP code, but those are for shipping. LSO offers no self-service way to reschedule or redirect: use the contact form below.

## LSO package not delivered, delayed or damaged

**Delayed.** LSO's tracking page says that if your parcel is more than 4 days late, an LSO agent can text you an update during business hours: use "Text with a live agent!" on that page. For stalled tracking in general, see [tracking not updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

**Outside LSO's area.** LSO works with other carriers for the rest of the country.

**Damaged.** LSO must receive a claim for a damaged package within 15 days. Keep all the packaging available for inspection until the claim is settled.

**Late or lost.** Claims for missing or late packages must be filed within 30 days.

**Who acts.** Only an LSO account holder can file a claim. If you are expecting a package, LSO tells you to reach out to the vendor, so for an online order, contact the shop. If tracking says delivered and you have nothing, read [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).

## LSO customer service number and contact

As listed on lso.com:

- **Phone:** (888) 688-6909, in the footer of every page. LSO publishes no opening hours.
- **Email:** customerservice@lso.com.
- **Recipient form:** on the [contact page](https://www.lso.com/contact-us), choose the recipient form. LSO says it gets back to you within 24 hours.
- **Text with an agent:** for parcels more than 4 days late, from the [tracking page](https://www.lso.com/tracking), during regular business hours.

Have the airbill number ready. LSO's holidays page gives another number, 866.215.8009, but that one is for supplies and pickups.

## Tracking LSO parcels with Peek

[Peek](/) names LSO straight away for a number of the `ZY0` shape. A tracking link from lso.com/tracking (with `airbillnos=`) or app.lso.com/tracking (with `barcodes=`) works too. No account, postcode or phone number needed.

Peek can't read LSO's own tracking. It follows LSO parcels through tracking services instead and shows the status and history those services hold, in their wording. That history is often hours behind LSO's page, and for some parcels there's nothing yet. Peek shows no proof of delivery or pickup point for LSO, and an expected delivery only when 17TRACK, one of those services, passes on LSO's own estimate.

Peek checks hourly, every 3 hours once a day passes without a new scan, and every 6 hours after seven days without news at "Label created". On the day the parcel is out for delivery, it checks up to every 15 minutes while you have it open or with notifications on.

When the newest scan is four days old and the parcel is still at "Label created", "Received by carrier" or "In transit", Peek flags it with that scan's day, such as "No tracking update since Mon 12 Oct". The parcel's page then says "Parcels often go quiet on long flights, at customs or over a weekend. Peek keeps asking LSO.", with a "Get help from LSO" link to LSO's contact page. After three weeks with no new scan, it says "Nothing new for over three weeks. If it still hasn’t arrived, ask the seller. Peek keeps asking LSO."

Peek can notify you of "Every scan", "Important steps" or "Delivery only". For the signature, a claim or a delivery problem, go to LSO or the shop.

## Questions about LSO tracking

### Who delivers LSO packages, and how long does LSO take?

LSO delivers directly across Texas, Oklahoma and major metro areas of Louisiana, Arkansas, Kansas and Missouri, and works with partner carriers outside that footprint. LSO Ground takes 1 to 3 business days, while next-day services deliver by 8:30 am, 10:30 am (later in some rural areas) or 3:00 pm depending on the service.

### Does LSO deliver on Saturdays or require a signature?

Saturday delivery isn't available for LSO Ground, Early Overnight or Economy Next Day. LSO doesn't collect a signature on 8:30 am Early Overnight deliveries, while senders can add an adult signature option on LSO Ground.

### What should I do if my LSO package is delayed or stuck on Label created?

`Label created` means the shipper created paperwork before LSO collected the parcel—ask the shop if nothing changes after a few days. If your parcel is more than 4 days late, use "Text with a live agent!" on lso.com/tracking during regular business hours.

### How do I file a claim for a lost or damaged LSO package?

Only an LSO account holder can file a claim, so online shoppers must reach out to the vendor or shop. LSO requires damaged-package claims within 15 days (keep all packaging for inspection) and lost or late claims within 30 days.

### What is the LSO customer service phone number and email?

Call (888) 688-6909, email customerservice@lso.com, or submit the recipient form on lso.com/contact-us (LSO replies within 24 hours). Have your airbill number ready.
