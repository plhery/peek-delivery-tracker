Matkahuolto is a Finnish parcel carrier with parcel points and lockers across Finland, including some K-markets and R-kiosks. Track your parcel in English on matkahuolto.fi/tracking or in the Paketit app using the shipment ID (lähetystunnus) from the shop's shipping email or Matkahuolto's SMS, or paste it in the box above. Below: what Matkahuolto's English statuses mean, how pickup codes and the 5-day storage limit work, and who to contact if tracking stops.

## Matkahuolto tracking number and where to find it

Matkahuolto calls it the shipment ID or consignment ID (lähetystunnus in Finnish). It's in the shop's shipping email and in every SMS or email from Matkahuolto. The pick-up code in the arrival notice is something else: it opens the locker, or you show it at the parcel point.

Matkahuolto doesn't publish its number formats. These are the shapes Peek's catalog knows (digits invented):

| Number | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `MH482910372FI` | `MH`, 9 digits and `FI`. |
| `MA7394028156342019482` | `MA` followed by 19 digits. |
| `00373000000000000005` | A 20-digit parcel barcode (SSCC). Matkahuolto's tracking also finds it as 18 digits, without the `00`. |

Numbers starting `JJFI`, and [postal numbers](guide:tracking-number-formats) ending `FI` with letters other than `MH` in front (`RR482910372FI`), belong to [Posti](carrier:posti), the Finnish post. If Matkahuolto can't find it, check which carrier the shop's email names.

Moving a parcel to another pickup point creates a new consignment ID. A GLS parcel handed to Matkahuolto in Finland shows its Matkahuolto number on GLS's tracking as "Finland parcel number", starting with 00.

No number? If you're registered with Matkahuolto or use the Paketit app, parcels sent to your phone number or email appear there; if one doesn't, the sender can add your details. See [how to find your tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number).

## What Matkahuolto tracking statuses mean

Matkahuolto's tracking comes in Finnish (the default), Swedish and English, each scan with a date, time and town. The English wordings, in order:

| Matkahuolto's status | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `Matkahuolto is waiting for the consignment from the sender` | The sender has [created the shipment](guide:tracking-statuses); Matkahuolto doesn't have the parcel yet. |
| `Consignment is being processed` | Scanned at a terminal. |
| `The consignment is on its way` | Moving between terminals. |
| `Your parcel has been sorted and will be taken next to the pickup point.` | Sorted at the last terminal; the pickup point is next. |
| `We will deliver the consignment to the pickup point during the day` | On its way to your pickup point today. |
| `Your pick up point has changed` | It's going to another point, named in the line. |
| `The shipment is available for pickup with the pickup code at the pick up point` | Ready for pickup, with the point's name and last pickup day. |
| `We have sent the recipient a message about the arrival of the consignment (sms).` | Your arrival notice went out. Not a movement. |
| `The consignment has been delivered. Thank you for choosing Matkahuolto!` | Picked up from the point or locker. |

**During the day.** The first point on the route may not be yours, Matkahuolto says, but the parcel should be ready for pickup the same day. You get a notice when it is.

## Matkahuolto pickup points, lockers and home parcels

**Picking up.** You need only the pick-up code from the arrival notice. No ID unless the notice marks a hand-delivered shipment, usually alcohol or documents. Anyone with the code can pick it up, except at app-based outdoor lockers, which open only with the recipient's own phone in the Paketit app. If the notice names no locker, ask at the parcel point's checkout.

**Arrival notice.** A Paketit app notification first, then SMS or WhatsApp, and email if the sender gave one.

**How long it waits.** 5 days, then it goes back to the sender; 14 days when the notice comes on paper because the sender gave no phone or email. You can buy 1 to 16 more days in the app or, if registered, on the website, unless the sender has blocked it. GLS's own page gives its parcels 10 days, so go by the last pickup day in your notice.

**Changing the point.** Once, free, in the app: before Matkahuolto has the parcel, says its Q&A, or when the first arrival notice comes, says its Swedish page on receiving parcels. Not while it's in transit. After the arrival notice you can buy a transfer.

**Home Parcels** come on weekdays between 8:00 and 18:00 Finnish time; Business Parcels and [FedEx](carrier:fedex) parcels come on weekdays between 8:00 and 16:00 Finnish time, without notice. A notice gives the planned day, which you can move to one of the next five business days on the day the notice arrives. The parcel is left in a safe, sheltered spot by the door; if that isn't possible and you can't be reached, it goes to a nearby pickup point the next business day.

**Åland.** Since 1 October 2026, Åland Post has delivered Matkahuolto's parcels on Åland, and its post offices, postal agents, lockers and home deliveries are part of Matkahuolto's network.

**Returns.** Get an activation code with the Palauta button in the app or on the website, write it on the parcel with your name and phone number, and take it to a locker or parcel point.

## Matkahuolto parcel delayed, not updating or damaged

Most parcels arrive the day after dispatch, says Matkahuolto, and all within three days of it receiving them (three working days in its Finnish Q&A). Parcels from abroad can pass through other carriers first and take a little over a week to reach Matkahuolto.

**Not updating.** Tracking can pause, for example while the parcel waits to go out or its packaging is checked. If it still doesn't move, Matkahuolto asks you to contact the sender. See [tracking that isn't updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

**No arrival notice.** Usually a typo in your contact details: check spam folders and ask the sender to correct them.

**Damaged or lost.** Tell the shop or sender without delay, with photos. The company that sent the parcel files any claim with Matkahuolto; between consumers, the sender does. Note visible damage when you pick it up; otherwise report it in writing within 7 days, or within 30 days as a consumer. Matkahuolto's liability is €20 per kg, or the goods' value if lower.

- **Feedback form** in Finnish at matkahuolto.fi/palautelomake. No email address for recipients is published.

> Matkahuolto warns of fake messages, faked sender names and lookalike sites such as matkahuoltoo.fi. It says it never relays payments or acts as a payment gateway; the payment link it does send goes to senders, for return costs. Check a message by typing the shipment ID into Matkahuolto's tracking or app; if you paid, call your bank.

## Tracking a Matkahuolto parcel with Peek

[Peek](/) asks Matkahuolto, Posti and [Chronopost](carrier:chronopost) about an `MH…FI` number and says "Matkahuolto has this parcel." when only Matkahuolto knows it; if Posti knows it too, you choose. An `MA` number goes to Matkahuolto alone. For an 18 or 20-digit barcode with Finland's `64` or Sweden's `73` after its first digit (after `00` and one digit in the 20-digit form), Peek asks Matkahuolto too, along with the other carriers it fits, and names Matkahuolto when only Matkahuolto knows it. If [Posti](carrier:posti) knows a `64` code too, or [Bring](carrier:bring) a `73` code, Peek names that carrier: pick Matkahuolto with "Change". If another carrier, such as [PostNord](carrier:postnord), knows it too, you choose. Other barcodes aren't offered to Matkahuolto, so pick it with "Change". A matkahuolto.fi tracking link with `parcelNumber=` files any number under Matkahuolto, but Peek can ask Matkahuolto only about its own `MH…FI` and `MA` codes and about postal numbers and SSCC barcodes with a valid check digit: any other number fails.

Matkahuolto's scans carry no time zone: Peek reads each on the clock of its town (Finnish time in Finland; a row with no place on a neighbouring row's, else Finnish time) and lists Matkahuolto's own history. Statuses keep Matkahuolto's English wording. Peek can show the pickup point or locker with its town and street, and can notify you of "Every scan", "Important steps" or "Delivery only".

Peek gets no expected arrival day or weight from Matkahuolto. It leaves out additional services and postcodes and doesn't show the last pickup day separately: it's in the status line and your arrival notice. For an `MH…FI` number tracked from a country other than Finland, Peek also asks that country's post where it knows one (not for the Netherlands or Åland) and follows the parcel there once it shows a dated scan no older than Matkahuolto's newest, at that post's pace, keeping Matkahuolto's steps; for `MA` numbers and barcodes, Peek doesn't follow the parcel to another carrier.

While the parcel is open in Peek, or on its delivery day with notifications on, Peek checks up to every 10 minutes, and every 2 minutes from 6 a.m. on the latest scan's clock (Finnish time in Finland) on the day it goes out for delivery, while Matkahuolto's own tracker answers. After two days without news (12 hours while the parcel still shows "Label created" or "Received by carrier"), Peek checks hourly, and after seven days without news at "Label created", every 6 hours. After four days without a scan on a parcel at "Label created", "Received by carrier" or "In transit", Peek shows "No tracking update since …" and the note "Parcels often go quiet on long flights, at customs or over a weekend. Peek keeps asking Matkahuolto." and a "Get help from Matkahuolto" link.

## Questions about Matkahuolto tracking

### Is Matkahuolto legit?

Yes. Matkahuolto is an established Finnish parcel carrier with parcel points and lockers across Finland. Treat any message asking you to pay online in its name as a scam: Matkahuolto says it never relays payments, and its only payment link goes to senders for return costs.

### Can I track a Matkahuolto parcel in English?

Yes. Matkahuolto's tracker has an English version at matkahuolto.fi/tracking, and you can switch the Paketit app's language in its settings.

### How long does Matkahuolto hold a parcel at a pickup point?

Five days before returning it to the sender, or 14 days when the arrival notice goes out on paper because the sender gave no phone number or email. You can buy 1 to 16 extra days in the Paketit app or, if registered, on matkahuolto.fi unless the sender blocked extensions; GLS's page gives its parcels 10 days, so go by the last pickup day in your notice.

### Can I track a Matkahuolto parcel without the shipment ID?

Yes, if you're registered with Matkahuolto or use the Paketit app and the sender included your phone number or email: the parcel appears automatically. Otherwise ask the shop for the shipment ID.

### How long does a Matkahuolto parcel take to arrive?

Usually 1 to 2 working days to lockers and parcel points, and within three days once Matkahuolto has the parcel (three working days in its Finnish Q&A). Parcels sent from abroad can take a little over a week to reach Matkahuolto.
