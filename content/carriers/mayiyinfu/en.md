Mayi Yinfu (蚂蚁银服) is the short name of 广东蚂蚁银服国际物流有限公司, a forwarder in Guangdong, China, that ships express and small parcels abroad. Its tracking numbers are MY, ten digits and US: paste yours in the box above, or type it in the 快递查询 (parcel lookup) box on mayiyinfu.com.cn, which opens Mayi Yinfu's tracker. Mayi Yinfu flies parcels to your region and hands them to a company that delivers there: into Europe, Deutsche Post DHL.

## Mayi Yinfu tracking number: what it looks like

MY stands for 蚂蚁 (mayi, "ant"). With invented digits:

| Number | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `MY0000000017US` | MY, ten digits and US: a Mayi Yinfu number. |
| `MY000000017US` | Only nine digits: not Mayi Yinfu's layout. Check for a missing digit. |
| `RR000000005CN` | Two letters, nine digits and CN: a [China Post](carrier:china-post) number. |

Lower case and spaces don't matter in Peek.

**Where to find it.** Mayi Yinfu works for businesses (its site offers them a login, a sign-up and a price lookup), so the number comes from the shop you bought from. If you can't find it, see [how to find your tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number). Mayi Yinfu's tracker also finds an order by the seller's reference, typed exactly as the seller wrote it, dashes included.

## Mayi Yinfu tracking statuses explained

Mayi Yinfu publishes no status list. Its own lines are in Chinese, most followed by an English translation; after the hand-over it copies the delivering company's lines, in English. Its main lines, in order:

| Mayi Yinfu says | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `货物电子信息已经收到` | "The goods' electronic information has been received": the seller has created the order. Not a scan: Hualei (华磊), the platform behind this tracker and [BYHD](carrier:byhd)'s, writes it. |
| `The instruction data for this shipment have been provided by the sender to DHL electronically` | Copied from DHL: DHL has the label's data. Not a scan. |
| `已入库,到达收货点` | Booked into the warehouse at the receiving point: Mayi Yinfu has your parcel. |
| `已出库,货物离开操作中心` | It has left the operations centre. |
| `货物已交机场` | Handed over at the airport for a flight, not delivered to you; then 等待安排航班中 (awaiting a flight) or the flight's day. |
| `航班延误` | The flight is delayed; the line gives the day of the next one. |
| `头程起飞`, `头程落地` | The first flight has left, then landed. |
| `二程起飞` | The connecting flight has left. Its English, "Flight 202 is taking off", reads 二程 (second leg) as a flight number. |
| `航班落地，拆板清关The flight landed, and the board was dismantled and cleared` | Landed in your region; the pallets are broken down for import customs clearance. |
| `清关完成，待转运Clearance has been completed and it is awaiting transfer.` | Through customs, waiting for the delivering company. |
| `已转运transferred` | Handed to the delivering company, whose number the tracker now shows. |
| `The international shipment has been processed in the parcel center of origin`, `The international shipment has been processed in the export parcel center` | Copied from DHL: processed by DHL in Germany. |
| `The shipment has arrived in the destination country/destination area.` | In your country. It can end with a link to the delivering company's tracking site. |
| `The shipment has been loaded onto the delivery vehicle` | Out for delivery. |
| `The shipment has been successfully delivered` | Delivered. |

**Lines that run ahead.** `国内清关中` (export clearance), `已排航班` (flight booked), `起运地航班起飞` (flight has left), `已到达目的国` (arrived in the destination country) and `清关完成` (clearance done), on their own, come from a script: they can have your parcel abroad before it reaches the airport, so go by the lines around them.

Mayi Yinfu's times carry no time zone. Its lines in China are Beijing time, and so, usually, are the DHL lines it copies: a DHL scan at 10:00 in Germany shows on Mayi Yinfu's tracker as 17:00 in winter.

## Who delivers Mayi Yinfu parcels

Mayi Yinfu's site doesn't say who delivers where. Its tracker shows the hand-over: after `已转运transferred`, the delivering company's number, then that company's lines.

**Into Europe**, that's a Deutsche Post DHL number, and DHL's tracker follows the parcel to its delivery in your country (see [DHL](carrier:dhl)). A link at the end of DHL's arrival line leads to the company that brings it to your door.

**Elsewhere**, Mayi Yinfu names no partner. The number's shape often tells you whose it is (see [tracking number formats](guide:tracking-number-formats)), or ask the seller. Delivery attempts, pickup points and holding times are the delivering company's rules. Asked to pay import charges? See [parcels held at customs](guide:customs).

## Mayi Yinfu parcel not arriving or tracking stuck

**Only the first lines.** `货物电子信息已经收到`, perhaps with DHL's label line, means the seller has booked the parcel but nobody has scanned it. If nothing follows after a few days, ask the seller whether it has really been sent.

**Stuck after `已转运transferred`.** Mayi Yinfu's part is done: follow the delivering company's number from its tracker. More in [tracking a parcel from China](guide:tracking-from-china) and [why tracking stops updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

**Nothing found.** For a number it doesn't know, the tracker shows one result with the number under 参考号 (reference) and every other field empty. Compare it with the seller's message, character by character.

**Lost, damaged or returned.** Mayi Yinfu publishes no claim process, so ask the seller or the marketplace for a refund or a new parcel. See [lost parcels](guide:lost-parcel), [returned to sender](guide:returned-to-sender) and [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).

## Mayi Yinfu contact number and customer service

As of 28 October 2026, Mayi Yinfu's site, in Chinese only, gives one phone number, +86 13705996996, a Chinese mobile number: in its header as its 全国服务热线 (national service hotline), and in its 在线咨询 (online enquiries) box. ## Track a Mayi Yinfu parcel with Peek

Paste a Mayi Yinfu number into [Peek](/) and it goes straight to Mayi Yinfu: no other carrier in Peek's catalog uses that layout. So does a link to Mayi Yinfu's tracker with `documentCode=` and the number or the seller's reference in it. A reference you type has no shape to go by, but Peek follows more than 3,500 carriers: add it anyway, press "Change" and pick Mayi Yinfu (typing 蚂蚁银服 or MYYF finds it too), or use "Change carrier" on a parcel you already added. Peek then shows the MY number under "Mayi Yinfu tracks this as". Peek drops dashes, though, so look up a reference with dashes in Mayi Yinfu's own box.

Peek shows each line in Mayi Yinfu's words, with its place, service name and destination country when given; a flight line with the day it expects shows as its step alone, in English, such as "The first leg has departed". The first lines show as "Label created", `已入库,到达收货点` as "Received by carrier", `航班落地，拆板清关…` as "At customs", the delivery-vehicle and delivered lines as "Out for delivery" and "Delivered", and the other known lines as "In transit". The lines that run ahead stay out, and so does a line Mayi Yinfu's record shows was written more than twelve hours before the time it carries: a forecast, not a scan. A line dated after Peek's check waits until then. Lines in China, and DHL's copied lines placed in a country once DHL's number is in the record, are read on Beijing time; the others on the clock of where the parcel was, as best Peek can tell. Names, address, postcodes, weight, fees, signature and proof of delivery stay out, and there's no expected delivery date.

**DHL takes over.** DHL is the only delivering company Peek takes from Mayi Yinfu's record, and only when the delivering number there has a shape Peek's catalog gives to DHL alone. The parcel then shows "Delivery with DHL" and DHL's number after yours, and Peek asks DHL whether it has the parcel. Once DHL shows a scan at least as recent as Mayi Yinfu's latest line, Peek follows the parcel at DHL and keeps Mayi Yinfu's lines in the history. Any other delivering number, a DHL one of a shape other carriers share included, doesn't show in Peek, and Peek doesn't follow the parcel under it: copy it from Mayi Yinfu's tracker and add it as a parcel of its own.

Peek checks Mayi Yinfu every 10 minutes by day, every hour or two overnight, and hourly once it goes quiet. If the record still holds only its first lines two days on, Peek also asks tracking services. Of Mayi Yinfu's known lines, "Important steps" (delivery, pickup, customs, missed attempts and returns) alerts you at `航班落地，拆板清关…`, out for delivery and delivery; "Delivery only" at the last two.

## Questions about Mayi Yinfu tracking

### Why does my Mayi Yinfu number end in US?

Every Mayi Yinfu number does. The ending doesn't say where the parcel is going, and it isn't a US postal number.

### Does "delivered to the airport" mean my parcel has arrived?

No. `货物已交机场` ("The goods have been delivered to the airport") means Mayi Yinfu handed the parcel over for its flight. Delivery to you comes later, from the delivering company.

### How long does Mayi Yinfu take to deliver?

Mayi Yinfu publishes no delivery times. Go by the estimate the shop gave for your order.

### Who do I contact about a missing Mayi Yinfu parcel?

The seller you ordered from: it booked the parcel with Mayi Yinfu, whose site gives only a mobile hotline.
