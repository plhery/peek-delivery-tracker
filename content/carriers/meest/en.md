Meest is an international parcel group with Ukrainian roots, operating Meest America (US), Meest Corporation (Canada), Meest UK, Meest Post (Poland) and Meest International for cross-border online orders. Track your parcel on t.meest-group.com, on your country's meest.com tracking page, or by pasting the tracking number in the box above. You'll find the number in your shipment confirmation email, on the shipping label or in the shop's dispatch notice.

## Meest tracking number: where to find it and what it looks like

Meest's US and Canadian sites say the number is in your shipment confirmation (sent by email, says the US site) and on the shipping label. If you bought online and have nothing, see [how to find your tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number).

Meest publishes no exact format. Its US page says only that a number "usually starts with a specific letter code followed by a series of digits". The shapes you may meet, with invented digits:

| Number | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `CV4829173CA64058G` | Two letters, 7 digits, two letters, 5 digits and G: Meest's consolidation number, seen on parcels from China to Europe. |
| `ANS4829173640582UA` | ANS, 13 digits and UA: another shape Peek knows as Meest's. |
| `RR482917367UA` | An international postal number. Meest's tracker answers for some, but the post that issued it tracks it too. |
| `CV482917367UA` | Postal in shape too: Meest Canada's FAQ gives a number starting CV to a parcel from Ukraine booked on its form. |
| `IHB4829173640` | IHB and 10 digits: a shape Meest's tracker answers for, which the Italian carrier HR Parcel uses too. |
| `20482917364058` | 14 digits: a Ukrainian waybill from [Nova Poshta](carrier:nova-poshta), not Meest. |

One trap: the tracking box on us.meest.com, ca.meest.com and uk.meest.com accepts 6 to 16 characters without spaces, so a 17-character consolidation number ending in G is refused there. The same number opens on t.meest-group.com.

## Meest tracking statuses and what they mean

t.meest-group.com shows the steps in Ukrainian only; the tracking box on Meest's US, Canadian and UK sites opens in English. Here are the English wordings Meest's tracker also sends, as Peek shows them, in the order a parcel meets them:

| Meest says | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `Parcel/shipment registered` | The parcel is made up for dispatch at Meest's warehouse in the sending country. |
| `Departure from outward office of exchange` | Leaving the export sorting centre for abroad. |
| `Pending customs clearance` | Waiting for import customs, which for a parcel into the EU can be in another EU country than yours ([how customs works](guide:customs)). |
| `Customs clearance completed` | Customs has released it. |
| `To be delivered to Box Now` | A partner in your country has registered it and will deliver it. Not delivered yet. |
| `Arrival to local HUB. Delivery by BOXNOW with barcode 0000000000` | It has reached the local carrier; the barcode is that carrier's own number. |
| `Arrived at international sorting center` | A parcel to Ukraine has reached an international sorting centre. |
| `Shipment handed over to the courier` | A courier has it: for your door, or for the branch where you pick it up. |
| `Shipment ready for pickup` | Waiting for you at a branch or parcel shop. |
| `Your Parcel is located at the locker` | Waiting in a locker. |
| `Parcel is DELIVERED` or `Shipment received` | Delivered or picked up. |
| `Delivery suspended by the sender` | The sender has stopped the delivery: ask them. |

`To be delivered to Box Now` sounds final but comes early, sometimes while the parcel is still in China: it only names [BOX NOW](carrier:box-now) as the company that will deliver it. Lines with "office of exchange" are postal sorting centres for export and import, not exchange offices. `Shipment handed over to the courier` can appear before `Shipment ready for pickup`, so it doesn't always mean a courier is coming to your door.

The tracking box words some steps differently from this table: where one says `Shipment received`, the other may say `Delivered`. Meest Canada notes that "Delivered" "does not always mean that you received it in person. Check your porch or mailbox."

## Meest delivery, pickup points and lockers

**With local partners abroad.** Meest Canada says an international shipment may be handed to a local partner for final delivery, and Meest's tracking then brings that partner's scans together. The history names the partner when it takes over (such as BOX NOW).

**In Ukraine.** Meest's pages disagree on branch storage: 3 to 5 days in Meest Canada's FAQ, 5 working days free in ua.meest.com's rules, then a daily fee (partner branches limited to 10 kg send the parcel back instead). You get a push notification in the Meest Post app, otherwise an SMS or Viber message with the branch number, address and hours.

## Meest parcel not updating, lost or damaged

**Tracking stuck.** Meest USA calls a day or two without updates normal; after more than a week, check the number and contact the delivery service. If it stays quiet, see [tracking not updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

**Who claims.** If a shop sent your order, start with the shop. Deadlines differ by Meest company:

- **Meest USA:** its pages disagree. The complaints FAQ takes claims for damaged, lost, misdelivered or badly delayed parcels "within 30 days from the scheduled delivery date" and decides within 10 business days. The File a Claim page says "only a sender may file a claim", within 5 days of delivery or 60 days of drop-off if undelivered, and takes 14 to 60 business days.
- **Meest Post (Poland):** claims within 6 months of posting; damage within 7 days of delivery. The right to claim is the sender's, or the recipient's once the parcel is delivered or the sender passes that right on.

## Tracking a Meest parcel with Peek

[Peek](/) knows Meest's consolidation number (`CV4829173CA64058G`) and the `ANS…UA` shape (`ANS4829173640582UA`), and asks Meest alone, which settles it. For IHB and 10 digits, it asks both Meest and HR Parcel whether they know the number and picks the one that does. Postal numbers, CV ones included, and senders' references reach Meest only after you pick "Meest" with "Change", or from a Meest link. Typed alone, a postal number goes to the post that issued it, often with Chronopost (CV…PL to Poczta Polska, CV…CA to Canada Post), or to the destination's post ([Ukrposhta](carrier:ukrposhta) for Ukraine); a CV…US number goes only to Chronopost and tracking services. Peek reads links from t.meest-group.com, meest.com country sites (`parcel_number=`) and tracking-iframe.meest.com, but not from Meest Post's tracker on meestpost.com, where pl.meest.com's tracking box leads.

Meest gives no time zone on its scans: Peek reads them on the clock of the scan's place and lists Meest's own history. Peek keeps the status, the destination country and, when a partner abroad delivers, that carrier and its number: the parcel's page says "Delivery with BOX NOW", for example (or "Announced to BOX NOW, not received yet" while that carrier has only the announcement of the parcel, with no scan of its own), and Peek follows that carrier when it can track it directly.

There is no expected delivery, weight or pickup point, and the branch, its hours and the pickup deadline are left out: check those with Meest. A number Meest knows only as a partner's reference isn't tracked, and old histories drop out and read as not found.

While you have the parcel open in Peek, or on its delivery day with notifications on, Peek checks up to every 10 minutes; on the day it is out for delivery, from 6:00 local time at the newest scan's place, every 2 minutes while Meest's tracker answers. Otherwise it checks hourly. After 12 hours with only "Label created" or "Received by carrier", or two days without news, it checks hourly however you follow it, and every 6 hours after seven days at "Label created".

When the newest scan is four days old and the parcel is still at "Label created", "Received by carrier" or "In transit", Peek flags it with that scan's day, such as "No tracking update since Mon 12 Oct". The parcel's page then says "Parcels often go quiet on long flights, at customs or over a weekend. Peek keeps asking Meest." After three weeks with no new scan, it says "Nothing new for over three weeks. If it still hasn’t arrived, ask the seller. Peek keeps asking Meest." Meest has no help page in Peek: a "Get help from …" link appears only once Peek follows a delivering carrier that has one.

Peek can notify you of "Every scan", "Important steps" or "Delivery only".

## Questions about Meest tracking

### Is Meest a legitimate parcel carrier?

Yes. Meest is an established parcel group whose roots go back to 1989 (Meest Post), running regional operations in the US, Canada, the UK, Poland and Ukraine plus Meest International for cross-border online orders.

### How long does Meest take to deliver to Ukraine or within the EU?

Meest Post expects 2 to 5 working days within the EU, at least 4 working days from Poland to Ukraine and at least 9 working days from Ukraine to Poland.

### What does a Meest tracking number look like, and why won't us.meest.com accept it?

Meest America says tracking numbers usually start with a letter code followed by digits (such as CV or ANS…UA formats). The tracking box on us.meest.com, ca.meest.com and uk.meest.com only accepts 6 to 16 characters, so 17-character consolidation numbers ending in G must be tracked on t.meest-group.com or in Peek.

### How long does Meest keep a parcel at a branch in Ukraine?

Meest's Ukrainian rules allow 5 working days of free branch storage before a daily fee applies (partner branches limited to 10 kg return uncollected parcels), while Meest Canada's FAQ cites 3 to 5 days.

### How do I contact Meest customer service?

Call +1 862-682-4224 (US), +1-800-361-7345 (Canada), +44 330 533 0230 (UK) or +48 22 113 40 54 (Meest Post Poland), or use the contact form on international.meest.com for cross-border online orders.
