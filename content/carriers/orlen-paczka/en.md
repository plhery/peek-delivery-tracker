ORLEN Paczka is a Polish parcel network, formerly Paczka w RUCHu, that delivers to parcel lockers and pickup points. Track a parcel on ORLEN Paczka's own tracking page at orlenpaczka.pl, or paste the number in the box above. The number is the 13-digit parcel number from the shop's or seller's message.

## ORLEN Paczka tracking number and where to find it

The parcel number has 13 digits: it starts 210 and ends in a check digit. Type it without spaces.

| Shape (invented) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `2109876543210` | 13 digits starting 210: the parcel number. |
| `1234567890` | A 10-digit sending code the sender uses at drop-off, not the parcel number. |

Where it turns up:

- **The shop's or seller's order page.**
- **The tracking page's** copy-link button, which copies a link with the number in it.

A pickup code isn't a tracking number, and neither is your phone number. No message at all? See [how to find your tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number).

Allegro Delivery parcels carry a number starting `AD`: track those in the Allegro app or on allegro.pl. An international number ending in PL, such as `RR000000005PL`, belongs to [Poczta Polska](carrier:poczta-polska).

## What ORLEN Paczka's statuses mean

ORLEN Paczka's tracking gives each step a date, a time and a Polish wording.

| ORLEN Paczka's status | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `Twoja paczka jest w trakcie przygotowania przez nadawcę` | The sender has created the parcel. ORLEN Paczka doesn't have it yet. |
| `Paczka nadana w punkcie` | The sender dropped it off at a point. |
| `Twoja paczka została odebrana przez kuriera i ruszyła w drogę` | A courier collected it from the sender. |
| `Twoja paczka została przyjęta w sortowni` | At a regional sorting centre, named after the wording. |
| `W sortowni centralnej` | At the central sorting centre. |
| `Twoja paczka znajduje się w sortowni` | At the destination's sorting centre. |
| `Twoja paczka jest już w drodze do punktu odbioru` | On its way to your pickup point. |
| `Twoja paczka czeka na odbiór w punkcie` | Ready for pickup at your point. |
| `Paczka przekazana do przewoźnika w innym kraju. Dalszą drogę możesz śledzić w aplikacji Vinted` | A Vinted parcel handed to a carrier in another country. Follow it in the Vinted app. |
| `Cieszymy się, że Twoja paczka została już odebrana z punktu odbioru` | Picked up from the point. |

Some parcels show older wordings for the same steps: `Paczka przygotowywana przez nadawcę`, `Paczka przyjęta w sortowni regionalnej` (no town) and `Odebrana przez Klienta`, where the "Klient" is you. The hand-over abroad is sometimes worded more briefly: `Paczka w innym kraju`. The first line is a ["label created" status](guide:tracking-statuses): the parcel isn't moving yet.

## ORLEN Paczka parcel lockers and pickup points

ORLEN Paczka delivers to a parcel locker (automat paczkowy), many of them at fuel stations, or to a pickup point such as a Ruch kiosk or a partner outlet. When the parcel arrives, you get the pickup code by SMS, email or a notification in the app. On Sundays you can collect from the lockers and from selected ORLEN stations. ORLEN Paczka doesn't deliver cash-on-delivery parcels.

**Picking up.** At a locker, type the five- or six-digit pickup code, scan the QR code from the message or the app, or open the compartment remotely in ORLEN Paczka's mobile app, without touching the locker. Someone else can collect the parcel if you pass them the code; without the code or the app, the parcel can't be collected. If the SMS hasn't come, check spam and blocked messages, and look for the code in the email too; if you still have no code, write to customer service through the contact form. If your phone number was given wrong, contact the sender. If a locker doesn't work, call the helpline (below).

**How long it waits.** You have 3 working days, counted from the first SMS or email with the pickup code. After that, a parcel from an online shop goes back to the sender, and one from a private person, such as a Vinted parcel, to the point where it was posted. Allegro gives the same 3 working days for Allegro Delivery parcels at ORLEN Paczka points and lockers; one nobody collects goes back to the seller.

**Allegro Delivery parcels** are handled by Allegro, which takes charge of their delivery and customer service: the pickup notice comes by email, SMS or in the Allegro app, and you track and pick them up with the Allegro app, under "Moje przesyłki".

## ORLEN Paczka delivery time, delays, damage and complaints

ORLEN names next-day delivery among the service's strengths, and ORLEN Paczka's FAQ puts figures on it: a parcel from an online shop reaches the point in 1 working day (98% of parcels), one from a private sender or from Vinted in 1 to 3 working days. If tracking stops moving, ask the shop, which holds the contract with ORLEN Paczka, and see [tracking not updating](guide:tracking-not-updating). If the status says the parcel is lost, ORLEN Paczka asks you to contact its customer service centre through the contact form; see also [lost parcel](guide:lost-parcel).

**Late.** If the parcel reached you after the delivery deadline, that is later than four working days after the day it was posted, ORLEN Paczka says you can, as the recipient, claim back the amount for the service not performed.

**Damaged.** ORLEN Paczka is on the register of postal operators kept by Poland's postal regulator UKE, which describes the general rules for postal operators: damage you see at handover is recorded in a report written with the operator's staff, and damage not visible from outside is reported no later than 7 days after you receive the parcel.

**Who complains.** Under the same rules, the sender, or you once the parcel has been delivered to you (or if the sender passes the claim to you). A complaint can be made in writing, electronically, for example through [ORLEN Paczka's complaint form](https://www.orlenpaczka.pl/reklamacje/), or orally, within 12 months of posting. The operator answers within 30 days; no answer in time means the complaint is accepted. Compensation follows postal law and the service's own terms; ORLEN Paczka says it is liable for a parcel up to 500 zł when a private customer sent it, and up to 5,000 zł when a business did.

## ORLEN Paczka customer service and contact

For an Allegro Delivery parcel, contact Allegro, which handles customer service for those parcels. For a shop order, start with the shop. ORLEN Paczka's own contact page is on its Polish site, orlenpaczka.pl, under "Kontakt". Its channels as of 6 November 2026, all in Polish:

- **Helpline:** 22 33 33 999, charged at your operator's rate. Monday to Friday 7:00–22:00, Saturday 9:00–19:00 (its FAQ says until 16:00) and Sunday 10:00–16:00.
- **[Contact form](https://www.orlenpaczka.pl/formularz-kontaktowy/)**, for example when you have no pickup code or the parcel is lost.
- **[Complaint form](https://www.orlenpaczka.pl/reklamacje/)** for loss, damage or delay, open to senders, recipients and payers.

ORLEN Paczka doesn't send links by SMS and doesn't ask for extra payment for a parcel through a link: such a message is a scam attempt. More in [delivery scam texts](guide:delivery-scam-texts).

## Tracking ORLEN Paczka parcels with Peek

[Peek](/) recognises 13-digit numbers starting 210 with a valid check digit. Other carriers share that shape, so it asks ORLEN Paczka and several of them, such as Delhivery and [FAN Courier](carrier:fan-courier), at once and says "ORLEN Paczka has this parcel." when only ORLEN Paczka knows it. For other 13-digit numbers starting 2 or 3, pick ORLEN Paczka with "Change" or paste the orlenpaczka.pl tracking link that carries the 13-digit number. The 7- and 10-digit sending codes can't be tracked in Peek, and `AD` numbers aren't ORLEN Paczka's.

It shows the Polish wordings as written, the sorting-centre town on a map where named, and the delivery time, with times read on Warsaw time and shown in your device's time zone; on peektracker.com, the history ends with "Times are in your time zone:" and its name. There is no pickup point, expected delivery, weight or sender, and the return parcel's number is dropped. A Vinted parcel handed to another country's carrier isn't followed and stays in transit. Parcels already picked up drop out of ORLEN Paczka's tracking after some months.

It checks every 10 minutes and can notify you when the status changes. After two days without news (12 hours while the parcel still shows "Label created" or "Received by carrier"), Peek checks hourly.

## Questions about ORLEN Paczka tracking

### What is ORLEN Paczka?

A Polish parcel service started in 2021, formerly Paczka w RUCHu, with RUCH as its logistics partner. Online shops, Allegro and Vinted use it to deliver to lockers and pickup points in Poland.

### Can I track ORLEN Paczka in English?

ORLEN Paczka's tracking words every step in Polish; the status table above translates its wordings.

### Does ORLEN Paczka deliver abroad?

No: ORLEN describes a network of lockers and pickup points across Poland, and the FAQ on orlenpaczka.pl says ORLEN Paczka delivers only within Poland. A Vinted parcel can be handed to a carrier in another country, and the Vinted app shows the rest of its way.
