Packeta is the group brand behind Zásilkovna, its Czech name, and ships to 33 countries. Track a parcel on [Packeta's tracking page](https://tracking.packeta.com/en/), in the Packeta app or in the box above. The number is a `Z` and ten digits, found in Packeta's email, its SMS or the app.

## Packeta tracking number: the Z code

Packeta's tracking page accepts one shape: `Z` followed by 10 digits.

| Shape (invented) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `Z1234567890` | A Packeta tracking number. |
| `1234567890` | The ten digits without the `Z`: the shape also fits [DHL](carrier:dhl-express), Relais Colis and others, so add the `Z`. |
| `Z123456789` | Nine digits: not Packeta ([Australia Post](carrier:australia-post)'s shape). |
| `Z12345678901` | Eleven digits: not Packeta (Colis Privé's shape). |

The **pick-up code** is different: Packeta sends it by SMS and email once the parcel reaches your point, and you need it to pick the parcel up, not to track it. No `Z` number in the shop's email? Ask the shop. More in [how to find a tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number).

## Packeta tracking statuses explained

These are Packeta's English step sentences, in the order a parcel meets them; "…" marks a fragment of a longer sentence.

| Packeta's status | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `We are aware of your parcel and are waiting for the sender…` | The shop has registered the parcel but hasn't handed it over yet. |
| `…assigned a tracking number…` | The label exists. Nothing has moved. |
| `…successfully received the parcel for transport…` | Packeta has the parcel. |
| `The parcel is currently on its way to the depot.` | On the road to a sorting depot. |
| `…arrived at the depot…` | Being sorted at a depot. |
| `The parcel has been handed over to the carrier…` | Passed to the company that delivers it, often a partner carrier abroad. |
| `…on its way to you…` | Out for delivery. |
| `The parcel is ready for pickup…` | Waiting at the pickup point or Z-BOX. Use your pick-up code. |
| `Last day to pick up your parcel.` | Today is the last day to collect it: extend storage if the page offers it, or it goes back to the sender. |
| `The package has been delivered` | Picked up or delivered. |
| `…investigating the status of the parcel…` | Something went wrong and Packeta is looking into it. |
| `Return (on the way back)` | On its way back to the sender. |

`The parcel has been handed over to the carrier…` means another company now has it: Packeta's own drivers deliver only in Czechia and Slovakia, partner carriers elsewhere. On the last day the tracking page shows `Last day to pick up your parcel.` with a button to extend storage, or `Your parcel will wait for you only today!` once storage can't be extended any more. A finished return reads `We have returned the parcel back to the sender.` or `Returned to sender`.

`Parcel not found` means Packeta doesn't know the number, or no longer shows an old one. Check the `Z` and ten digits.

## Packeta pickup points, Z-BOX and home delivery

Packeta says you have 7 days to pick a parcel up, with the code from the SMS or email, and can extend storage twice for free. In Czechia that makes 7 + 7 + 7, so 21 days at most, and an ID with your name works instead of the code. In Poland, an SMS to Packeta extends it to 21 days for free. Extend on the tracking page or in the app; storage may be shorter at Christmas. In Hungary, Packeta's terms give 5 days, at most 7, from 1 October to 20 January, and 7, at most 21, the rest of the year. Someone else can pick it up with your code.

**Z-BOX lockers** (Czechia, Slovakia, Romania) open 24/7 with the app over Bluetooth; Hungary's became FOXPOST Z-BOXes in 2026, and the FOXPOST app has replaced Packeta's there; in Czechia a six-digit code typed on the keypad opens them too. In Czechia a parcel waits 2 calendar days, plus 1 if extended.

**Home delivery.** In Czechia and Slovakia Packeta's drivers send a delivery window (Slovakia's site gives an estimated time to the minute and live tracking for the last hour); if they miss you, the parcel can go to the nearest pickup point. In Czechia the driver calls you twice on arrival and tries the address once: a missed parcel goes to the nearest pickup point with room, where you can collect it the same day. Elsewhere partner carriers deliver.

**Not picked up?** In Czechia it goes back to the sender automatically.

**Weekends.** Zásilkovna delivers to addresses on working days only. In Prague, Brno and Ostrava it also fills Z-BOXes and some pickup points on Saturdays.

## Packeta parcel not moving, damaged or returned

**No update for days.** In Czechia, once a parcel hasn't moved for more than 24 hours, Zásilkovna asks you to write through its contact form (topic "Kde je moje zásilka?") and say what the parcel holds. The shop booked the shipment, so it can chase it too. See [tracking that isn't updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

**Not received.** The sender claims for a lost parcel, so contact the shop; in Slovakia the recipient may claim too, and in Hungary with the sender's written assignment or an order confirmation showing the full price paid. Under Zásilkovna's terms for shops, it must report a parcel not delivered within 20 days of posting it and claim within 3 months. Delivered but missing? Read [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).

**Damaged.** Claim yourself on claims.packeta.com/en ("I am the recipient") within five working days of receipt (in Slovakia, hidden damage by the next working day after delivery; in Hungary, hidden damage within 3 working days, and visible damage must be written up at delivery); Packeta answers within 30 days. Pickup points don't take claims. In Poland, Packeta sends recipients to the shop.

**Returned.** Ask the shop to resend or refund.

## Tracking Packeta parcels with Peek

[Peek](/) recognises a `Z` and ten digits as Packeta at once. Ten digits without the `Z` fit several carriers, so Peek asks them, Packeta among them unless the first digit is 0, and names the one that knows the number; you can also pick Packeta with "Change". Links from tracking.packeta.com work, and packeta.com links with `id=`; zasilkovna.cz links only with the `Z` number inside. No postcode needed.

Peek reads Packeta in English and shows its sentences as written (`Returned to sender` in Peek's words): the status, the newest 20 scans, the shop that sent it, the delivery time and the pickup point or Z-BOX address when the parcel heads there (none for home delivery or returns). It drops your name, address, phone and signature.

Scans that name a depot or pickup point show it as the place, and those naming a town go on the map. Packeta gives no expected delivery. Times are Prague time (Romanian depot scans may be an hour off). When a scan links a partner carrier's tracker Peek knows, Peek names that partner as the one delivering and, when Peek can track the partner itself, follows the parcel there once it shows fresh progress. It checks Packeta every 10 minutes by day and hourly overnight, and can notify you of changes.

## Questions about Packeta tracking

### Where is my Packeta parcel?

The latest tracking line tells you: `…on its way to you…` is out for delivery, `The parcel is ready for pickup…` means it waits at your point.

### Is Packeta legit?

Yes, it's a real carrier: the group brand behind Zásilkovna, shipping to 33 countries. Real Zásilkovna texts show "Zásilkovna" as sender.

### Is Packeta the same as Foxpost?

In Hungary they are merging: [FOXPOST](carrier:foxpost)'s company runs packeta.hu, and Packeta's Hungarian Z-BOXes now carry the FOXPOST name. Elsewhere Packeta's tracking page asks for its own `Z` number.

### Is Packeta in Poland?

Yes, as Packeta Poland, with its own phone line and pickup points; Z-BOX lockers aren't listed there.
