Parcelforce Worldwide is part of [Royal Mail](carrier:royal-mail) Group, so track your 13-character `EA…GB` or 14-character `PB` parcel number on Royal Mail's Track your item page, or paste it in the box above. Below are status meanings, delivery options in the Royal Mail app, UK customs bills (held for about 3 weeks) and Royal Mail Customer Services (03457 740 740).

## Parcelforce tracking number: where to find it

Look in the shop's order confirmation email, or ask the shop. Lost it? See [how to find your tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number). Three shapes you may see:

| Number (invented) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `EA482917367GB` | Two letters (`E` and a letter, or `CP` or `GI`), nine digits and `GB`, with a check digit before `GB`. |
| `PBTR4829173654` | A 14-character parcel number: `PB`, the consignment number and the parcel's place in it. |
| `TR4829173` | A consignment number on its own: Royal Mail's tracker asks for each parcel's own number instead. |

The 13-character shape is also Royal Mail's (`RR482917367GB` is Royal Mail, not Parcelforce), and some express48 parcels carry a Royal Mail number. Both are tracked on the same page, so that changes little for you.

## Parcelforce tracking statuses explained

Royal Mail's tracker shows these lines in English. In the order a parcel usually meets them:

| Tracking says | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `Sender has despatched item` | The sender has booked the parcel. Parcelforce doesn't have it yet. |
| `Item Collected` | Picked up from the sender. |
| `Accepted at Parcelshop` | The sender dropped it off at a Post Office or parcel shop. |
| `Item received` | It's in the network. |
| `Customs charges raised` | An import from outside the UK owes VAT, duty or a fee before it's delivered. |
| `Despatched to delivery office` | On its way to your local depot or delivery office. |
| `Received at Delivery Depot` | At your local depot. It isn't ready to pick up yet. |
| `Out for delivery` | With the driver today. |
| `Delivery attempted` | The driver couldn't deliver it. |
| `Delivered by` | Delivered. The line can name the office that delivered it. |
| `Delivered to Neighbour/Reception by` | Left with a neighbour or a reception desk, named in the line. |
| `Returned to sender` | Going back to the sender. |

On parcels sent abroad, the history goes on with lines such as `Item Leaving the UK` and the delivering partner's `Due to be delivered today`.

## Parcelforce delivery options and missed deliveries

**Before delivery.** In the Royal Mail app or through Royal Mail Customer Services, you can choose another delivery date for a Parcelforce parcel or ask for it to go to a neighbour, and ask the driver to knock louder or give you more time to reach the door. A safe place is set with Royal Mail too, even for Parcelforce parcels.

**Missed it.** Tracking shows `Delivery attempted`, or `Delivered to Neighbour/Reception by` with the name of whoever took it in. If you still can't tell where the parcel is, call Royal Mail Customer Services (below).

## Parcelforce tracking not updating, delayed or lost

**No scans yet.** The sender may have created the number before handing the parcel over: `Sender has despatched item` means Parcelforce doesn't have it yet. Check the number and try later. More in [why tracking stops updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

**Late, lost or damaged.** If you bought online, contact the seller first: Citizens Advice says you'll usually get a better result, and you can ask for a refund or for the item to be sent again. For compensation, check how to claim on Parcelforce's own website.

**Customs charges.** Parcelforce takes parcels from outside the UK through customs and contacts you if VAT, duty or other charges are due, with a bill that says exactly what to pay ([more on customs](guide:customs)). It normally holds the parcel for about 3 weeks; unpaid by then, it goes back to the sender. Questions about the goods go to Parcelforce too.

> Not sure an email or text asking you to pay for a parcel is genuine? Don't click its links or give bank details. Forward scam emails to report@phishing.gov.uk and scam texts to 7726, free.

## Parcelforce contact number and customer service

Citizens Advice sends Parcelforce customers to Royal Mail for delivery needs and safe places:

- **Royal Mail Customer Services:** 03457 740 740, Monday to Friday 8am to 6.30pm, Saturday 8am to 3pm and Sunday 9am to 2pm (UK time). **Relay UK:** 18001 then 0345 266 0075.
- **Royal Mail app:** delivery date, neighbour, safe place and delivery needs for Parcelforce parcels too.

For a claim or a complaint, go through Parcelforce's own website.

## Tracking Parcelforce parcels with Peek

[Peek](/) names Parcelforce at once for `EA482917367GB`-style numbers with a valid check digit and for 14-character `PB` numbers. A consignment number on its own isn't tracked. A Parcelforce tracking link with `trackNumber=` works, and so does a Royal Mail link ending `#/tracking-results/` and a Parcelforce number.

Peek reads Parcelforce on Royal Mail's tracking site by opening it in a real browser on its server, so a check takes longer and can fail when the site blocks automated visits. Peek then tries universal tracking services, which hold little for Parcelforce.

Peek shows the status, each scan with its place (no postcodes), the expected delivery day, the delivery time and the destination country. While you have the parcel open, or on its delivery day with notifications on, it checks up to every 10 minutes (up to every 5 minutes from 6 a.m. UK time or, when Parcelforce gives a time window, from half an hour before it opens, on the day Parcelforce scans the parcel out for delivery, while Parcelforce's own tracker answers). Otherwise, and after two days without news (12 hours while the parcel still shows "Label created" or "Received by carrier"), Peek checks hourly, and every 6 hours after seven days without news at "Label created". It puts each scan on a map and can notify you when the status changes. Statuses keep Royal Mail's English wording. A scan from abroad that comes without a time zone isn't dated. For an export with an `EA482917367GB`-style number, Peek can also ask the national post of the destination Parcelforce reports (if it reports none, that of the country Peek places you in, other than the UK) and follow it once it shows a scan no older than Parcelforce's newest.

## Questions about Parcelforce tracking

### Is Parcelforce part of Royal Mail?

Yes. GOV.UK counts Parcelforce in Royal Mail Group, and its parcels are tracked on Royal Mail's Track your item page.

### Why is my Parcelforce tracking number not recognised?

The parcel may not have reached Parcelforce yet (`Sender has despatched item`), or the number may be mistyped. A consignment number on its own isn't tracked: ask the sender for the 14-character `PB` number of each parcel, and try later.

### How long does Parcelforce hold a parcel with customs charges?

About 3 weeks. Parcelforce contacts you with the bill for any VAT, duty or fee; if it isn't paid by then, the parcel goes back to the sender.

### Can I reschedule a Parcelforce delivery or choose a safe place?

Yes, in the Royal Mail app or through Royal Mail Customer Services. You can choose another delivery date, a neighbour, a safe place or extra delivery needs.

### What is the customer service phone number for Parcelforce?

For delivery options and safe places, call Royal Mail Customer Services on 03457 740 740 (Mon–Fri 8am–6.30pm, Sat 8am–3pm, Sun 9am–2pm UK time; Relay UK: 18001 then 0345 266 0075). Forward scam texts asking for payment to 7726.
