Pošta Srbije (the Post of Serbia) is Serbia's national post, and Post Express is its courier service. Track an item for free on posta.rs's [Track and Trace](https://www.posta.rs/eng/alati/pracenje-posiljke.aspx), in the Post of Serbia app, or by pasting the number in the box above. The number comes from the sender.

## Serbian Post tracking number

Pošta Srbije uses the [international postal format](guide:tracking-number-formats) for every trackable item: two letters, nine digits, two letters. Posta.rs calls it the reception (identification) number, and its search takes only that number, not a name or address. The first letters tell you the kind of item.

| Shape (invented) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `PE000000005RS` | Post Express, the courier service: `PE`. |
| `CP000000005RS` | A parcel: `C` and a second letter. |
| `RE000000005RS` | A domestic registered item: `RE`. |
| `RR000000005RS` | A registered item to or from abroad: `R` and a second letter. |
| `EE000000005RS` | EMS: `E` and a second letter. |
| `RR000000005CN` | An item from abroad keeps the foreign post's number, ending in its country. |

Insured letters start with `V`. If you're the recipient, ask the shop or sender for the number: Pošta Srbije's own SMS and Viber messages only announce an item's arrival. Can't find it? See [how to find a tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number).

The same shape with other letters at the end belongs to another post: `…SI` is [Pošta Slovenije](carrier:posta-slovenije), `…HR` is [Hrvatska pošta](carrier:hrvatska-posta).

## What Pošta Srbije tracking statuses mean

Posta.rs has Cyrillic, Latin and English versions. The table mixes the short English words of Pošta Srbije's app with the site's published status list, in order.

| Pošta Srbije's status | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `Receipt` | Accepted and weighed at the counter. The site's list calls it `Reception`. |
| `Dispatch` | Sent on to the next office, also by a post abroad. |
| `Transport` | Handed to transport abroad at the office of exchange. |
| `Arrived` | Arrived at an office, in Serbia or abroad. |
| `Notified` | You've been notified of its arrival; the step names the post office holding it. |
| `In charge of delivery at post office` | Ready to collect at the counter of the post office that notified you. |
| `Neuspešan pokušaj uručenja` | A post abroad tried and failed to hand it over. |
| `Delivered` | Delivered. |
| `Refuses reception/Not requested` | Refused by the recipient, or not collected. |
| `Returned to sender` | On its way back to the sender. |
| `Delivered (to the sender)` | Back with the sender: returned, not delivered to you. |

`Delivered (to the sender)` is the confusing one: it says delivered, but to the sender. `Neuspešan pokušaj uručenja` stays in Serbian even in English answers. A delivered item also shows who signed, with date, time and place.

## Notices, post office pickup and parcel lockers

If nobody is in, the postman leaves a notice of arrival, and Peek shows `Notified`. Storage counts from the day after the notice, whether it came on paper, by SMS, Viber or email. Pošta Srbije keeps items, unless the sender agreed otherwise:

- 5 working days at the post office for most items;
- 20 days for items arriving from abroad;
- 30 days for poste restante items.

You can ask in writing at any post office to keep it up to 30 days, or to forward it to another address in Serbia, with ID and postage paid. You, an authorised person or an adult family member can collect it with the notice and an ID showing the address (ID card, passport, refugee ID or foreign travel document; not the new-form driving licence).

**Post Express** brings items to the address by 19:00 the next working day, with a free SMS on arrival. If the sender chose it, you collect at one of 1,100 post offices with ID and the SMS.

**Parcel lockers** (*Poštin paketomat*): the notice comes by Viber or SMS. Open the locker 24/7 with the link in the message, the Direct4.me app, or by calling the number given. The locker site says 48 hours in the locker, the posta.rs FAQ 2 working days: count on the shorter. Then it waits out the storage period at the locker's post office. To have a parcel go to a locker instead, answer "yes" to Pošta Srbije's Viber or SMS question by 17:00.

## Serbian Post parcel delayed or lost

Pošta Srbije guarantees no deadlines abroad and expects international parcels to take 7 to 15 days; domestic parcels take up to 5 working days. Tracking of an item sent abroad ends at the office of exchange, when it leaves Serbia. If nothing moves for days, see [tracking that isn't updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

**Lost or late.** The sender starts the claim, with the confirmation of receipt, usually at the posting office, but not within 10 days of posting (express items and parcels with a set deadline can be claimed once it has passed). Deadlines: 60 days from posting within Serbia, 6 months for international items. Pošta Srbije answers within 8 days, or 2 months for international claims. Unregistered items have no claim procedure.

**Damaged or missing contents.** Claim when you receive it, at the latest the next working day, at the post office, which inspects it with you and writes a record. Not by email; later claims bring no compensation. If the item says delivered but you don't have it, see [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).

## Pošta Srbije contact and call centre

Pošta Srbije's channels, as posta.rs listed them in October 2026:

- **Contact Centre:** 0700 100 300 from Telekom Srbija landlines; 011 3607 788 from other landlines and mobiles (+381 11 3607 788 from abroad). Weekdays 08:00 to 20:00, Saturdays and Sundays 08:00 to 15:00.
- **Post Express Contact Centre:** 0800 100 808 or +381 11 3607 607, weekdays 8:00 to 18:00, Saturdays 8:00 to 15:00.
- **Email:** contact@ptt.rs, for complaints or compliments.
- **Online forms:** "Ask a Question" and "Claims" on posta.rs, handled weekdays 7:30 to 15:30.
- **By post:** Pošta Srbije, Takovska 2, 11120 Beograd, or at any post office.

> Pošta Srbije warns of fake SMS about unpaid customs or an address to update, with links to lookalike sites such as posta-rs.com. It never asks you to pay online or by email, and sends SMS or Viber only to announce arrivals, from its verified account. The only real site is posta.rs.

## Tracking Pošta Srbije with Peek

[Peek](/) names Pošta Srbije straight away for a number starting `C`, `E`, `L` or `R`, ending `RS` and with a valid check digit. For other letters, such as `PE` or `V`, the shape names no carrier, so Peek asks Pošta Srbije, M-ONE and [Chronopost](carrier:chronopost), and shows "Pošta Srbije has this parcel." when Pošta Srbije knows the number. If not yet, you see "Not found at … yet. Peek will keep checking after you add it." With a wrong check digit no carrier is asked: compare the number with your shipping message, as Pošta Srbije won't track it even if you pick it with "Change". Posta.rs tracking links can be pasted too and always name Pošta Srbije; from a postexpress.rs link, Peek reads the number as if you'd typed it.

An item from abroad keeps its foreign post, even when it's going to Serbia. Pošta Srbije's lookup takes any postal number, though: pick "Pošta Srbije" with "Change", or paste a posta.rs link. Peek can also follow it there when the carrier abroad names "Serbian Post" or "Post Express".

Peek reads the same service as Pošta Srbije's app. It checks every 10 minutes by day and every hour or two overnight, and can notify you when the status changes. Each step goes on a map with its Serbian post office (only the country for a step abroad), with the weight and the time it was delivered. Times in Serbia are read on Belgrade time and a step abroad on that country's clock, and shown in your device's time zone; a step in a country with several time zones doesn't appear. Pošta Srbije gives no expected arrival day and names no pickup point. Who signed, postcodes and payment details are left out. Statuses keep Pošta Srbije's wording, except `Delivered`, shown in Peek's own words.

## Questions about Serbian Post tracking

### How do I track a parcel from abroad to Serbia?

Enter the foreign post's number, ending in the sending country's code, in posta.rs's Track and Trace: it covers registered items, parcels and EMS from abroad.

### Can you track Post Express?

Yes. A Post Express number starts with `PE` and ends in `RS`, and you track it on posta.rs or in the Post of Serbia app, which shows Post Express items at the main transfer points.

### What is Pošta Srbije's email address?

contact@ptt.rs, for complaints or compliments. Damage claims can't be made by email: report damage at the post office when you receive the item.

### How long does Pošta Srbije keep a parcel at the post office?

5 working days for most items and 20 days for items from abroad, counted from the day after the notice. You can ask in writing for up to 30 days.
