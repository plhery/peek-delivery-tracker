Quick Rabbit is a Spanish delivery company for online shops and retailers, based in San Fernando de Henares near Madrid. It delivers across Spain to your door, to pick-up points and to Citibox lockers. Track a parcel with its `PKG` code on [Quick Rabbit's tracking page](https://web.quickrabbit.es/es/tracking) or paste it in the box above. Quick Rabbit says you'll find the code in your confirmation email or shipping receipt.

## Quick Rabbit tracking number: the PKG code

Quick Rabbit calls it the tracking code (`código de seguimiento`). It comes in two layouts, and the tracker also takes the shipper's reference (digits invented):

| Number | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `PKG00020260101123456789` | `PKG`, three digits, the date the parcel was registered (year, month, day) and nine more digits: the usual Quick Rabbit code. |
| `PKG000260101ES1234567890123A` | `PKG`, nine digits, `ES`, 13 digits and a letter: a longer, rarer layout. |
| `AB1234567890123456` | Two letters and 16 digits: the shipper's reference, shown as `Referencia` on Quick Rabbit's page. |

A code the tracker doesn't know gets `Paquete no encontrado` ("Package not found"): check it against the shop's email. No code at all? Ask the shop, or see [how to find a tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number).

## What Quick Rabbit's tracking statuses mean

Quick Rabbit's tracking page opens in Spanish (English and Chinese too). Here are its Spanish lines, with the English line and the status Peek shows for each, roughly in the order a parcel meets them:

| Quick Rabbit says | In Peek: line, status | What it means for you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Documentado`, `Creado` | `Documented`, `Created`: Label created | The shop has registered the parcel. Quick Rabbit doesn't have it yet. |
| `Ingresó a Centro de Clasificación`, `Salió de Centro de Clasificación` | `Entered Sorting Center`, `Left Sorting Center`: In transit | In and out of Quick Rabbit's sorting centre. |
| `Tránsito a almacén local`, `Recibido en Almacén`, `En Almacén Local` | `Transit to local warehouse`, `Received at Warehouse`, `Local Warehouse`: In transit | On its way to, then at, the local warehouse that delivers your area. |
| `Listo para Asignación de Ruta` | `Ready for Route Assignment`: In transit | In a delivery box, waiting to go on a courier's round. |
| `Repartidor Tiene el Paquete`, `En Reparto` | `Driver Has Package`, `In Delivery`: Out for delivery | A courier has it and is on the round. |
| `Entregado al Destinatario`, `Entregado a Vecino`, `Entregado al Portero`, `Entregado en Buzón`, `Entregado en Recepción`, `Entregado a Compañero de Piso` | `Delivered to Recipient`, `Delivered to Neighbor` and so on: Delivered | Delivered to you, a neighbour, the doorman, your mailbox, a reception desk or a flatmate. |
| `Entrega Citibox Iniciada` | `Citibox Delivery Initiated`: Out for delivery | The courier is taking it to a Citibox locker. |
| `Depositado en Citibox` | `Deposited in Citibox`: Ready for pickup | It's in the locker, waiting for you. |
| `Retirado de Citibox` | `Retrieved from Citibox`: Delivered | You've taken it out. |
| `Almacenamiento Citibox Expirado` | `Citibox Storage Expired`: Needs attention | Its storage time in the locker has run out. |
| `En Camino a Punto de Recogida`, then `En Punto de Recogida` or `Listo para Recoger` | `En Route to Pickup Store` (In transit), then `At Pickup Store` or `Ready for Pickup`: Ready for pickup | On its way to, then waiting at, a pick-up point. |
| `Destinatario Ausente`, `Primera Ausencia`, `Segunda Ausencia`, `Tiempo Insuficiente`, `Fuera de Horario` | `Recipient Absent`, `First Absence`, `Second Absence`, `Insufficient Time`, `Outside Delivery Hours`: Delivery attempted | Nobody was in, or the courier ran out of time or came outside service hours. |
| `Dirección Incorrecta`, `Dirección Incompleta`, `Destinatario Desconocido` | `Incorrect Address`, `Incomplete Address`, `Unknown Recipient`: Delivery attempted | The courier couldn't deliver at the address: correct it (see below). |
| `Entrega Rechazada` | `Delivery Rejected`: Delivery attempted | The parcel was refused at the door. |
| `Potencialmente perdido` | `Under investigation`: Needs attention | Quick Rabbit is checking where the parcel is. It may still be on its way. |
| `Paquete perdido`, `No localizado` | `Lost Package`, `Not located`: Needs attention | Lost, or not where it should be. |
| `Incidencia logística` | `Logistics incident`: Needs attention | A delivery problem. Quick Rabbit's note says "Puede solicitar el reembolso" (you may ask for a refund) while its team reviews the case. |
| `Preparado para Devolución`, `Devolución tras intentos estándar`, `Devuelto al Remitente` | `Prepared for Return`, `Return after standard attempts`, `Returned to Sender`: Returned to sender | Going back to the shop, for instance after the planned attempts, then back there. |

Some lines only note something: `Cliente Solicitó Cambio de Dirección` (you asked for an address change), `No Se Pudo Contactar al Cliente` (Quick Rabbit couldn't reach you), `Almacén - Esperando Gestión de Cliente` (held at the warehouse until customer service sorts out an incident). `Primer Intento de Entrega desde Almacén` says an attempt was scheduled, not how it went. Peek lists these without changing the parcel's status.

## Quick Rabbit delivery: couriers, Citibox and pick-up points

Quick Rabbit says it covers all 50 provinces of Spain, and its services page offers shops SMS and email notifications and a retry after a failed delivery. Missed a delivery? See [missed deliveries](guide:missed-delivery).

**Citibox lockers and pick-up points.** Instead of your door, the courier can leave the parcel in a Citibox locker, or it can go to a pick-up point. Quick Rabbit's pages don't give the code to open the locker, name the pick-up points or say how long either keeps a parcel. `Retirado de Citibox por Repartidor` ("Retrieved from Citibox by Courier") means the courier took it back out of the locker. More in [parcel lockers](guide:parcel-lockers) and [uncollected parcels](guide:uncollected-parcel).

## Change a Quick Rabbit delivery: address, phone or date

While the parcel is on its way, Quick Rabbit's tracking page shows a box, `Actualizar datos de entrega` ("Update delivery details"). Enter your email and Quick Rabbit sends you a secure link. On it you can change the phone number or the address (only within the same city), or choose a preferred delivery date. Changes apply "in the next few hours". Each link allows a limited number of changes, and it expires.

Don't wait too long. Once "more than 5 days have passed since it was received", in Quick Rabbit's words, the link answers "Ya no es posible realizar cambios en este paquete" (no more changes can be made) and the parcel "comenzará el proceso de devolución a origen": it starts going back to the sender. More in [changing a delivery address](guide:change-delivery-address).

## Quick Rabbit parcel late, lost or delivered but not received

For any problem with a parcel, Quick Rabbit's contact page sends you to the claim process on its tracking page.

**Delivered but not received.** Read the delivery line first: a neighbour or the doorman may have it. Still nothing? On the tracking page of a delivered parcel, press `Click aquí` under "¿Quieres informar sobre un problema?" ("Want to report a problem?"). Confirm the email address the order was placed with, and Quick Rabbit emails you instructions. The claim form asks what happened, may ask for photos, and lets you choose a reply by email, phone or SMS. More in [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).

**Stuck, lost or damaged.** If nothing moves for days, see [tracking that isn't updating](guide:tracking-not-updating). For a parcel shown as lost, damaged or stolen, the tracking page's help box becomes `Soporte Urgente` (urgent support), linking to Quick Rabbit's contact page. Under EU rules the shop answers for your order until you receive it, so ask it for a refund or a new parcel too: see [lost parcels](guide:lost-parcel), [damaged parcels](guide:damaged-parcel) and [returned to sender](guide:returned-to-sender).

## Track a Quick Rabbit parcel with Peek

[Peek](/) recognises a Quick Rabbit number by its shape: `PKG`, three digits, the eight-digit date and nine digits, in capitals or not, spaces allowed. The longer `PKG…ES…` code isn't enough on its own: Peek asks Quick Rabbit and shows "Quick Rabbit has this parcel." when it knows the code; if it doesn't, Peek asks tracking services too and keeps checking after you add the parcel. The shipper's reference has no shape: paste it all the same (Peek follows more than 3,500 carriers), press "Change" and pick Quick Rabbit. Peek then shows the `PKG` code under "Quick Rabbit tracks this as". A Quick Rabbit tracking link with `ref=` works too.

Peek reads Quick Rabbit's own tracker: the status, the history in the English lines above and the delivery time, read on Madrid time and shown in your device's time zone. Quick Rabbit names no places, so there's no map. Peek leaves out the estimated delivery date (Quick Rabbit keeps it a few days ahead, even for parcels returned long ago), the destination city, the shipper's reference and the courier's coordinates, which can be your door. There's no proof of delivery and no locker code, and Peek doesn't hand the parcel on to Citibox: the locker steps are Quick Rabbit's own lines.

Peek checks every 10 minutes by day, every 2 minutes once the parcel is out for delivery, and every hour or two overnight. It can notify you of every scan, important steps only or "Delivery only". It can't change the delivery, open a claim, or contact Quick Rabbit or the shop for you.

## Questions about Quick Rabbit tracking

### Is Quick Rabbit legit?

Yes. It's a Spanish logistics company that says it has more than 5 years of experience delivering for online shops and retailers across Spain, and it names AliExpress, Amazon, [Cainiao](carrier:cainiao), DHL, [Paack](carrier:paack), [CTT Express](carrier:ctt-express), [MRW](carrier:mrw) and Celeritas among its partners.

### How do I change my Quick Rabbit delivery address?

Press `Actualizar datos de entrega` on the tracking page and enter your email. The link changes the address within the same city, the phone or the date. Once more than 5 days have passed since the parcel was received, as Quick Rabbit puts it, no more changes can be made and it goes back to the sender.

### What does "Potencialmente perdido" mean?

"Potentially lost": Quick Rabbit is investigating where the parcel is, and says it may still be in transit or out for delivery. Peek shows `Under investigation`. If `Paquete perdido` ("Lost Package") follows, ask the shop for a refund or a new parcel.

### How do I contact Quick Rabbit?

Email customer@quickrabbit.es or WhatsApp +34 628 437 468. For a problem with a parcel, use the claim on the tracking page, as the contact page asks.
