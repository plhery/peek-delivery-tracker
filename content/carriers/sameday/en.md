Sameday is a Romanian courier also operating in Bulgaria, with its own easybox parcel lockers and SAMEDAY points. Track a parcel with your AWB number (from Sameday's SMS, email or the SAMEDAY App) on sameday.ro, under "Check AWB" on sameday.bg, or in the box above. Below: what Sameday's English statuses mean, easybox pickup times and PIN rules, missed-delivery rescheduling, and customer service numbers.

## Sameday AWB: your tracking number

Sameday calls the tracking number the AWB: 5 to 50 letters and digits, in the SAMEDAY App, Sameday's email or SMS, and usually the shop's shipping email. These examples are invented:

| Example (invented) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `1AWB482917364` | An AWB: `1`, three to five letters and 9 to 12 digits. |
| `1AWB482917364001` | A parcel number: the AWB plus 3 digits. Track the AWB without them. |

The SAMEDAY App lists every AWB linked to the phone number you registered with; orders placed with another number don't appear. Some 17-character AWBs, such as `1ABCD482917364058`, look like [Colis Privé](carrier:colis-prive) numbers, and some 16-character ones like [Royal Mail](carrier:royal-mail)'s, so check which carrier the shop's email names.

## What Sameday's tracking statuses mean

Sameday's trackers open in Romanian, Hungarian or Bulgarian, with a switch to English. The headline is a short title such as `The parcel is in transit`; each history line is a sentence. The English sentences, in order:

| Sameday's status | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `We will pick up your parcel from the sender soon.` | The shop has [booked the delivery](guide:tracking-statuses). Sameday doesn't have the parcel yet. |
| `The sender has handed the parcel to Sameday and it will be processed in the Sameday warehouse.` | Sameday has it. |
| `The parcel is in the central warehouse waiting to be processed.` | At the sorting hub. |
| `The parcel is on the way to the local warehouse.` | Travelling between depots. |
| `The parcel has arrived at the local agency and will be prepared for delivery.` | At the depot that delivers your area. |
| `The courier will deliver the parcel soon. Additional details are available in the SAMEDAY App, email or SMS.` | Out for delivery, to your door or to an easybox. |
| `The parcel has arrived at the delivery point. Pickup details are available in the SAMEDAY App, email or SMS.` | Ready for pickup in an easybox or SAMEDAY point. |
| `The parcel was redirected. As soon as it is in the courier's possession, it will be processed as requested.` | Someone asked to send it to a pickup point instead. |
| `The parcel has been successfully delivered.` | Delivered, including when you took it out of an easybox. |

"The courier will deliver the parcel soon" also appears when the courier is heading to an easybox, not your door. The pickup PIN or QR code is never on the tracking page, only in the app, email or SMS. A missed delivery and a return to the sender have lines of their own, which Peek files under "Delivery attempted" and "Returned to sender". A redirection line can appear before Sameday has the parcel. And after a return line, "successfully delivered" means the parcel reached the sender.

## Easybox, SAMEDAY points and missed deliveries

**Door delivery in Romania.** Sameday's 2025 commercial offer includes two free attempts on different days. After a failed delivery, Sameday notifies you by email, WhatsApp, SMS or the app, and its terms say it keeps the parcel 5 working days from that notice. While the parcel is out with the courier, the SAMEDAY App lets you leave it with a neighbour, choose another address, send it to an easybox if it fits, postpone it (up to 5 working days in Bucharest, 2 elsewhere) or cancel.

**Door delivery in Bulgaria.** The delivery-day SMS gives the courier's number, and the courier calls first. Call them to change the time that day, or use the email's link for the next business day.

**easybox.** Sameday counts more than 4,700 easyboxes in Romania and, depending on the page, 800 to nearly 1,000 in Bulgaria. In Bulgaria the PIN comes only in the SAMEDAY App or the email sent when the parcel is loaded, never by SMS. Storage times are in the questions below; in the app you can extend storage, for a fee, by up to 7 days. When time runs out, the reminder email has a link valid for 12 hours to collect the parcel from a local agency; otherwise it goes back to the sender. PIN refused? Check you're at the easybox named in the email and haven't mixed up 0 and O or 1 and I.

**SAMEDAY points** (partner shops, petrol stations and other businesses) keep a parcel 36 working hours in Romania and 48 in Bulgaria.

**Hungary.** Sameday's Hungarian site announces that it stopped operating there from 23 July 2026, while still delivering parcels registered up to that day.

## Sameday parcel delayed, lost or damaged

Sameday's Romanian terms promise domestic delivery within 5 working days of collection, 12 for international items. For Shein or Temu orders, its FAQ warns the sender may take 10 to 15 days to hand the parcel over, so the first status stays put; see [tracking a parcel from China](guide:tracking-from-china) or [tracking that isn't updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

To cancel or return an order, Sameday points you to the shop. For a delay, damage or missing item, use the form on [sameday.ro/contact](https://sameday.ro/contact/) with your AWB.

In Romania the sender or recipient can claim in writing within 6 calendar months of posting, by registered post, at Sameday's office or through that form. Sameday has 3 months to resolve it, and compensation goes to the sender unless they give it up to you. If the answer doesn't satisfy you or none comes within 3 months, complain to ANCOM, the regulator, within a year of posting. In Bulgaria, the sender or recipient can send a written claim within 6 months of Sameday accepting the parcel, to claims@sameday.bg, by post or in person. Sameday answers within 1 month (3 for international parcels); compensation goes to the sender unless they give it up to you, €7.67 for a lost or damaged parcel with no declared value. If the claim is rejected or unanswered, ask the CRC, the regulator, for its opinion.

## Sameday contact number and customer service

Sameday's channels:

- **Romania:** 021 637 06 60, Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 13:00 and 13:30 to 17:00 Romanian time. SAMY, the virtual assistant on sameday.ro, answers around the clock, including easybox PIN problems.
- **Bulgaria:** 0700 16 226, Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00 Bulgarian time, charged at your plan's rate for 0700 numbers, plus a contact form.
- **Hungary**, where Sameday no longer operates: a contact form only. Sameday's banner sends eMAG customers to eMAG.

> Sameday says it never asks for payment by SMS or on social media. You pay only the shop in advance, in the SAMEDAY App, or the courier or easybox on delivery.

## Tracking a Sameday parcel with Peek

[Peek](/) recognises Sameday's AWB shape and asks Sameday's Romanian network, then its Bulgarian one, whether they know it: you'll see "Sameday has this parcel." or "not found yet · Peek keeps checking". Some 17-character AWBs are also asked of Colis Privé; some 16-character ones are tried at Royal Mail only if Sameday doesn't confirm them. A parcel number is offered to Sameday when its AWB has 9 digits; otherwise pick "Sameday" with "Change". Either way Sameday answers with its AWB, shown as "Sameday tracks this as". A sameday.ro or sameday.bg tracking link with the AWB in it works too.

Peek shows the status, the history with each scan's county and country on a map, and the delivery time, in Sameday's English wordings. There's no expected delivery day and no easybox or point name.

While you have the parcel open in Peek, or on its delivery day with notifications on, Peek checks up to every 10 minutes; on the day Sameday scans it out for delivery, from 6 a.m. Romanian and Bulgarian time, every 2 minutes while Sameday's own tracker answers. Otherwise it checks hourly. After 12 hours with only "Label created" or "Received by carrier", or two days without news, it checks hourly however you follow it, and every 6 hours after seven days at "Label created". If Sameday's tracker doesn't answer and Peek has to go through tracking services, it checks hourly, every 3 hours once a day passes without a new scan, and, on the out-for-delivery day, up to every 15 minutes while the parcel is open in Peek or has notifications on.

When the newest scan is four days old and the parcel is still at "Label created", "Received by carrier" or "In transit", Peek flags it with that scan's day, such as "No tracking update since Mon 12 Oct". The parcel's page then says "Parcels often go quiet on long flights, at customs or over a weekend. Peek keeps asking Sameday.", with a "Get help from Sameday" link to sameday.ro's contact page. After three weeks with no new scan, it says "Nothing new for over three weeks. If it still hasn’t arrived, ask the seller. Peek keeps asking Sameday."

Peek can notify you of "Every scan", "Important steps" or "Delivery only". It can't extend easybox storage or send you a PIN.

## Questions about Sameday tracking

### How long does a parcel stay in a Sameday easybox?

In Romania, 36 hours if loaded Sunday to Thursday, 72 hours on Friday and 48 hours on Saturday. Bulgaria's easybox FAQ says 48 hours Monday to Thursday and 72 hours Friday to Sunday, while its easybox terms state 36 hours from the notice (24 at peak periods such as Black Friday or Christmas). You can check the remaining time and extend storage by up to 7 days for a fee in the SAMEDAY App.

### Why is my Sameday tracking not updating?

If tracking stays on `We will pick up your parcel from the sender soon.`, the shop has booked the AWB but hasn't handed the parcel over yet — Sameday's FAQ notes Shein and Temu orders can take 10 to 15 days before handover. Once collected, Sameday's Romanian terms allow up to 5 working days domestically and 12 working days internationally.

### Is easybox the same as Sameday?

Yes. easybox is Sameday's own parcel-locker network (more than 4,700 lockers in Romania and 800 to nearly 1,000 in Bulgaria), and SAMEDAY points are its partner pickup shops, holding parcels for 36 working hours in Romania and 48 in Bulgaria.

### Can I track a Sameday parcel without the AWB number?

Yes, if the order used the phone number registered in your SAMEDAY App: every AWB linked to that number appears automatically. Otherwise ask the seller or see [how to find a tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number).

### How do I contact Sameday customer service in Romania or Bulgaria?

In Romania, call 021 637 06 60 (Monday to Friday 09:00–13:00 and 13:30–17:00), use the 24/7 SAMY assistant or submit the form at [sameday.ro/contact](https://sameday.ro/contact/). In Bulgaria, call 0700 16 226 (Monday to Friday 09:00–17:00) or email written claims to claims@sameday.bg. Sameday closed operations in Hungary on 23 July 2026 and offers a contact form only there.
