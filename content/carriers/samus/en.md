SAMUS (UAB „SAMUS“) is a Lithuanian courier service for letters and parcels, working since 2011. It delivers for companies that have a contract with it: registered and express letters, letters handed to the recipient in person against a signature, and express parcels in Lithuania and abroad. You can track an item on [samus.lt, under "Parcel tracking"](https://www.samus.lt/en/parcel-tracking/), or paste the number into the box above.

## SAMUS tracking number: what it looks like

SAMUS's FAQ says the parcel number is shown on the label its clients make in its self-service system. So ask the company that sent the item for the number.

SAMUS's documentation for business clients shows the number and the barcode on the label in one form: two letters, nine digits and LT, 13 characters in all. That's the same shape as a postal number from Lithuania.

| Number (made up) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `RD000000005LT` | Two letters, nine digits, LT: the form in SAMUS's documentation. Peek treats the ninth digit as a check digit, as on postal numbers. |
| `RD000000000LT` | The same shape with a wrong check digit: Peek names no carrier. Check the number, or pick SAMUS yourself (see below). |

Spaces, dashes and lower case don't matter when you paste the number into Peek. On samus.lt, the result shows a "Parcel number" and a "Parcel code". If the site says `Your letter with number "…" is not found`, check the number with the sender.

If your number has another shape, a different company may deliver your parcel, such as [Lietuvos paštas](carrier:lietuvos-pastas), [Omniva](carrier:omniva) or [Venipak](carrier:venipak). [Tracking number formats](guide:tracking-number-formats) helps you tell which.

## SAMUS tracking statuses

SAMUS lists its statuses on its tracking page, in Lithuanian only. Its English page shows the same Lithuanian lines. The main ones:

| Status on samus.lt | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `Siunta įvesta savitarnoje` | The sender entered the item in SAMUS's self-service. SAMUS doesn't have it yet. |
| `Siunta paruošta išsiuntimui` | The item is ready to send, still with the sender. |
| `Siunta neperduota kurjeriui surinkimo metu` | The sender didn't give the item to the courier at pickup. |
| `Siunta surinkta iš siuntėjo` | The courier collected it from the sender. |
| `Siunta rūšiavimo centre` | It's at SAMUS's sorting centre. |
| `Siunta pas kurjerį` | A courier has it for delivery. |
| `Gavėjui paliktas pranešimas` | The courier didn't find you and left a notice. |
| `Gavėjas atvyks atsiimti į terminalą` | You will collect it from the terminal. |
| `Siunta palikta UAB Samus Žalgirio g. 92 (vidinis kiemas), Vilnius` | It waits at SAMUS's pickup point in Vilnius. A similar line names Minijos g. 49 in Klaipėda. |
| `Siunta sėkmingai įteikta` | Delivered. |
| `Siunta sėkmingai įteikta, įteikimo pranešimas grąžinamas siuntėjui` | Delivered, and the signed delivery notice goes back to the sender. |
| `Siunta neįteikta, neatsiliepė į pranešimą` | Not delivered: no one answered the notice. |
| `Siunta neįteikta (…)` | Not delivered, with the reason in brackets: refused, wrong address, recipient not found, cancelled by the sender, recipient moved, a registration-only address, a prison, a broken letterbox or another reason. |
| `Siunta išsiųsta iš Lietuvos` | It left Lithuania. Later lines follow it to the sorting centre, customs and post office in the destination country. |
| `Siunta perduota Lietuvos paštui` | SAMUS handed it to Lietuvos paštas. |
| `Siunta perduota į tranzitinį paštą` | SAMUS handed it to a transit post, which carries it on. |
| `Siunta peradresuota` | It was sent on to another address. |
| `Siunta neįteikta. Grąžinama siuntėjui` | Not delivered, and on its way back to the sender. `Siunta grąžinta siuntėjui` means it's back. |
| `Siunta pamesta` | The item is lost. |

Peek can't read SAMUS's tracker, so it doesn't show these lines: see "Track a SAMUS parcel with Peek" below. For common status lines, see [tracking statuses explained](guide:tracking-statuses).

## SAMUS delivery and collection

**Delivery times.** SAMUS's FAQ says standard courier delivery in Lithuania is made the next working day, and the same day or delivery by 10:00 or 12:00 can be ordered as extras. Its rules give letters and S, M and L items 2 to 6 working days in Lithuania, up to 10 in the EU and up to 20 outside it. An express S item reaches the recipient in Lithuania the next working day by 17:00.

**Letterbox or signature.** Letters and S items without tracking go into your letterbox. Tracked items are handed over against a signature, to you or to someone you have authorised. For an item addressed to a company, any employee can sign, and if the courier can't reach the recipient in person, the item is left with the receptionist, the person on duty or a security guard. M and L items are always handed over against a signature.

**When you're out.** The courier leaves a notice after each attempt. If your phone number is on the item, the courier may call you to agree a time and place. The rules say the courier doesn't have to wait more than 10 minutes. The notice says where you can collect the item; agree the time with the courier. The courier keeps letters for 14 working days after the notice, then returns them to the sender. Express parcels are kept at the terminal for 3 days free of charge.

**SAMUS pickup point in Vilnius.** Žalgirio g. 92, LT-09303 Vilnius, ground floor in the inner courtyard. Look for the building with an orange front: a passage under it leads to the courtyard. Then look for the sign "Siuntų išdavimo vieta" (parcel pickup point) and the door with the SAMUS sign. From the city centre or the station, take bus 1G or 53 to the stop "Žalgirio st." on Kalvarijų g., or bus 10 or 34, then walk a few minutes.

**Check the parcel.** Check its condition with the courier. Write any damage on the waybill and fill in a damage report. If you sign without remarks, SAMUS treats the item as delivered intact. Keep the original packaging, or you may lose the right to compensation.

**Changing the address.** After handing in the item, the sender can have SAMUS change the recipient's address as a paid extra, as early as possible. Ask the sender.

**Parcels sent abroad.** SAMUS delivers to most European countries and to other countries worldwide. Express parcels usually take 1 to 5 working days. The tracking can show the destination country's customs. The rules say the recipient pays duties and taxes, unless the waybill says the sender or someone else pays them. The heaviest standard parcel is 30 kg.

## SAMUS parcel late, not delivered or lost

**No update.** First check the tracking on samus.lt. If the status is unclear or there's no information, SAMUS asks you to email info@samus.lt or call +370 602 33354, and it will look for the parcel. If a delivery in Lithuania is late, the rules say SAMUS refunds the delivery fee to its client, the sender. More in [why tracking stops updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

**Lost or damaged.** Only the sender, or someone the sender authorises, can claim from SAMUS. A claim must reach SAMUS within 6 months of sending. SAMUS pays within 30 days for items in Lithuania and within 90 days for items sent abroad. For a lost or damaged express parcel, it pays up to €200. For items without tracking, it takes no claims. So if your order is lost or damaged, ask the company that sent it. See also [lost parcels](guide:lost-parcel).

**Returned.** An item SAMUS can't deliver goes back to the sender when there are no other instructions. See [returned to sender](guide:returned-to-sender).

**Delivered but not there.** For an item addressed to a company, a colleague, the receptionist or a security guard may have signed for it. Otherwise, ask the sender. See [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).

**Disagreements.** SAMUS's rules say disputes are settled under Lithuanian law.

## Track a SAMUS parcel with Peek

[Peek](/) can't tell a SAMUS item by its number alone. A number in SAMUS's form, two letters, nine digits and LT, is a postal number to Peek. While you add it ("Checking tracking services…"), Peek asks Lietuvos paštas and Chronopost, which know numbers of that shape, then tracking services. If one of them has the item, the carrier list says so; if a service names SAMUS with scans that show the item moving, Peek picks SAMUS. If none has it yet, the list says "Not found by tracking services yet. Peek will keep checking after you add it."

To follow the item as SAMUS's, press "Change" and type "SAMUS" in "Search carriers". "UAB SAMUS" finds it too. It isn't listed under "Fits this number". On an item you've already added, press "Change carrier".

You can also paste a link from SAMUS's Lithuanian tracker, which ends in `/siuntu-sekimas/?id=` and the number. Peek then reads the number and picks SAMUS ("Detected carrier"). A link from the English tracker, `/en/parcel-tracking/?id=`, doesn't name SAMUS to Peek: Peek reads the number only if it has the postal shape, and then treats it as a postal number. Paste the number or the Lithuanian link instead.

For an item filed under SAMUS, Peek asks ParcelsApp, Ship24 and 17TRACK in turn and keeps the first that has it; for a number with a correct check digit, it asks the Universal Postal Union's tracking (UPU) last. While the item is on its way, in its first 30 days in Peek, Peek asks Lietuvos paštas and Chronopost first; if one shows real progress, Peek moves the item there ("Swapped automatically from SAMUS"). Peek shows the status and history the service holds, in its wording. It can lag behind SAMUS's own, and for some items there's nothing at all. If you left the carrier to Peek and the services name SAMUS, with scans that show the item moving, Peek names SAMUS for you. Until a service has the item, Peek's link opens SAMUS's tracker with your number; after that, the service's page. Peek doesn't pass your IP address or country on to the services.

Until a service knows the number, an item filed under SAMUS shows "Waiting for the carrier". While a service has the item, Peek checks every 15 minutes, out for delivery too. A service that didn't know the number is asked again a day later. After two days without a new scan, Peek checks hourly. It can notify you of "Every scan", "Important steps" or "Delivery only". It can't contact SAMUS or the sender for you.

## Questions about SAMUS tracking

### Why does Peek ask Lietuvos paštas about my SAMUS number?

Because SAMUS numbers have the shape of Lithuanian postal numbers, and Peek can't tell them apart. Press "Change" and search "SAMUS", or paste the link from SAMUS's Lithuanian tracker.

### Where is the SAMUS pickup point in Vilnius?

At Žalgirio g. 92, LT-09303 Vilnius, in the inner courtyard, under the sign "Siuntų išdavimo vieta". Buses 1G, 53, 10 and 34 stop a few minutes' walk away.

### How long does SAMUS keep a letter I missed?

Letters stay with the courier for 14 working days after the notice, then go back to the sender. Express parcels wait at the terminal 3 days free of charge.

### Can I claim from SAMUS as the recipient?

No. Only the sender, or someone it authorises, can claim, within 6 months of sending. Ask the company that sent the item.

### How do I contact SAMUS?

Call +370 602 33354 or email info@samus.lt (paieska@samus.lt for parcels), Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 17:00.
