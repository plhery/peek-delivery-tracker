Siuntos autobusais ("parcels by bus") sends parcels on intercity buses across Lithuania, often on the same day, and on to other countries in Europe. You hand in and collect parcels at terminals in bus stations. The site's copyright line names UAB Kautra, and its prices apply at the terminals UAB „Kautra“ runs. Track a parcel on [siuntosautobusais.lt, under "Siuntos sekimas"](https://www.siuntosautobusais.lt/siuntos-lietuvoje/siuntos-sekimas/), or paste the number into the box above. The site is in Lithuanian only.

## Siuntos autobusais tracking number

The site says the number is on the parcel's label, under the words "Kvito numeris" (receipt number). As the recipient, you can track the parcel too: ask the sender for the number.

Siuntos autobusais doesn't publish a number format, and Peek knows no number shape for it.

| What you have | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The number under "Kvito numeris" | Type it on siuntosautobusais.lt, or paste it into Peek. |
| A parcel inside Lithuania | The site tracks it, except from some towns: see below. |
| A parcel to another country | The site's tracker covers parcels inside Lithuania only. Ask the sender or the terminal. |

Some towns have a parcel branch but send no data to the tracker: Molėtai, Šilalė, Zarasai and Anykščiai. For those, call the terminal. If the site says `Siuntos tokiu numeriu nėra. Pasitikslinkite siuntos numerį.`, it found no parcel with that number: check the number with the sender.

> **Check before you pay.** Siuntos autobusais warns people who buy online: if a seller sends you a parcel number or a photo of a parcel label, check on siuntosautobusais.lt that the parcel was really sent.

## Siuntos autobusais tracking statuses

Siuntos autobusais publishes no list of statuses. Its tracker shows where the parcel is now and, the site says, the exact arrival time. The departure and arrival times in its price calculator are only a guide.

Peek can't read the tracker on siuntosautobusais.lt. It shows the history a tracking service holds, if any, in that service's words: see "Track a Siuntos autobusais parcel with Peek" below. For what common status lines mean, see [tracking statuses explained](guide:tracking-statuses).

## Where to collect a Siuntos autobusais parcel

**At a terminal.** Parcels wait at the parcel terminal in the bus station of the town they were sent to. Check the terminal's hours before you go. UAB „Kautra“ runs these terminals, as read on 14 November 2026:

| Vilnius | Vilnius bus station, Sodų g. 22 | Mon–Fri 05:25–21:00, Sat–Sun 07:00–19:00 |
| Kaunas | Kaunas bus station, Vytauto pr. 24 | Mon–Sat 05:45–19:30, Sun 07:15–18:30 |
| Alytus | Alytus bus station, Naujoji g. 17L | Every day 6:30–18:30, with two short breaks |
| Druskininkai | Temporary bus station, Aušros g. 41 | Every day 6:30–18:30, with two breaks |

| Prienai | Prienai bus station, Vytauto g. 11 | Every day 06:30–18:30, break 12:00–12:30 |
| Vilkaviškis | Vilkaviškis bus station, Vytauto g. 103 | Mon–Fri 07:00–20:00, Sat 08:00–18:00, Sun 09:00–18:00, break 12:30–13:00 |
| Nida | Nida bus station, Naglių g. 18E | Every day 07:45–20:00 |

In other towns, such as Klaipėda, Panevėžys and Šiauliai, the terminal in the bus station is run by a partner company. The site lists them all with their hours on its [terminals page](https://www.siuntosautobusais.lt/kontaktai/siuntos-autobusais-terminalai-lietuvoje/). Partner terminals set their own prices.

**From the driver.** In a town without a terminal, you collect the parcel from the bus driver at the time the bus arrives. In the price calculator, these towns have an asterisk (`*`). An asterisk can also mean the bus arrives after the terminal closes. If nobody collects the parcel from the bus, it goes back to the terminal in the sender's town. A parcel can go only to a town the bus serves directly: parcels handed in on the bus aren't moved to other buses or sent on to other towns.

**ID.** Bring an identity document with a photo. The terminal gives the parcel only to the recipient, or to someone with the recipient's signed written authorisation. A parcel for a company goes only to someone with a company employee card or the company's authorisation.

**SMS.** The site says the SMS to the recipient isn't sent in every town and is for information only: you don't need it to collect the parcel. The sender must tell you where, when and on what terms to collect it.

**How long it waits.** A parcel waits at the terminal free of charge for two days; weekends and public holidays aren't counted. The FAQ then charges €0.30 for each further working day, €2 for a bicycle. An uncollected parcel stays 20 days, then goes back to the sender's station.

**Check the parcel.** Look at the packaging while the terminal staff are there. Write any damage on the parcel receipt. If you don't, Siuntos autobusais takes no responsibility for the damage.

**Courier.** Siuntos autobusais couriers deliver to homes in Vilnius, Kaunas, Klaipėda and Alytus. They also collect and deliver for companies with a contract in Vilnius, Kaunas, Klaipėda, Alytus, Vilkaviškis, Jonava and Mažeikiai. The courier costs €1.80 per parcel, VAT included, on top of the standard price.

**Parcels abroad.** Within the EU, Siuntos autobusais delivers to the address given, in every city, and it also has terminals in Riga and Tallinn. Apart from parcels for those two terminals, it hands parcels in Europe straight to the recipient, so it gives no exact delivery time: they take 1 to 6 working days, depending on the country. For parcels to countries outside the EU, its rules say the recipient pays the duties in that country. If the recipient doesn't pay, the parcel goes back at the sender's cost, or is destroyed if the sender won't pay for that.

## Siuntos autobusais parcel late, damaged or lost

**No update.** If the tracker shows nothing, check the number with the sender, or call the terminal in your town. More in [why tracking stops updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

**Lost or damaged.** Both the sender and the recipient can claim. For damage, claim within 10 working days of delivery. For a lost parcel, claim within 10 working days of sending. A parcel inside Lithuania counts as lost if it hasn't arrived within 4 days; an international one, within 30 days. Siuntos autobusais pays up to ten times the carriage fee. If the sender declared a value, for a fee of 10% of it, it pays the full justified declared value. It pays nothing for delays. For international parcels without the declared-value service (€15, for up to €1,000), it pays proven direct losses up to €82, plus the carriage price. See also [lost parcels](guide:lost-parcel).

**Returned.** A parcel that can't reach its destination goes back to the branch it was sent from. An uncollected parcel goes back after 20 days. See [returned to sender](guide:returned-to-sender).

## Track a Siuntos autobusais parcel with Peek

[Peek](/) can't tell a Siuntos autobusais parcel by its number, because Peek knows no number shape for it. Paste the number all the same: Peek follows more than 3,500 carriers. While you add it, Peek may check tracking services or carriers whose numbers have the same shape ("Checking tracking services…"). That doesn't mean they carry your parcel.

To follow the parcel as a Siuntos autobusais one, press "Change" and type "Siuntos autobusais" in "Search carriers". "Kautra" finds it too. It isn't listed under "Fits this number". On a parcel you've already added, press "Change carrier". A link from siuntosautobusais.lt doesn't help: the tracker there takes the number in a form, so its address holds no number, and Peek reads none from it.

Siuntos autobusais has no tracker Peek can read. So Peek asks ParcelsApp, Ship24 and 17TRACK in turn and keeps the first one that has the parcel. It shows the status and history that service holds, in its wording. That history can lag behind the tracker on siuntosautobusais.lt, and for many parcels there may be nothing at all. If you left the carrier to Peek and the services name Siuntos autobusais or Kautra, with scans that show the parcel moving, Peek names Siuntos autobusais for you. Peek has no link that opens the Siuntos autobusais tracker with your number: type it on siuntosautobusais.lt. Once a service has the parcel, Peek's link opens that service's page. Peek doesn't pass your IP address or country on to the services.

Until a service knows the number, a parcel filed under Siuntos autobusais shows "Waiting for the carrier". While a service has the parcel, Peek checks every 15 minutes, out for delivery too. A service that didn't know the number is asked again a day later. After two days without a new scan, Peek checks hourly. It can notify you of "Every scan", "Important steps" or "Delivery only". It can't contact Siuntos autobusais or the sender for you.

## Questions about Siuntos autobusais tracking

### Where do I find the Siuntos autobusais tracking number?

On the parcel's label, under "Kvito numeris". The sender has it: ask them for it, then type it on siuntosautobusais.lt or paste it into Peek.

### Can I track a Siuntos autobusais parcel sent abroad?

Not on siuntosautobusais.lt: its tracker covers parcels inside Lithuania only. Ask the sender or the terminal that took the parcel.

### Do I need the SMS to collect my parcel?

No. The SMS isn't sent in every town and is for information only. Bring an identity document with a photo, or the recipient's signed written authorisation if you collect for someone else.

### How long does a parcel wait at the terminal?

Two days free of charge, not counting weekends and public holidays, then €0.30 for each further working day. After 20 days, an uncollected parcel goes back to the sender's station.

### Who is behind Siuntos autobusais?

The site's copyright line names UAB Kautra. Its prices apply at the terminals UAB „Kautra“ runs in Vilnius, Kaunas, Alytus, Kėdainiai, Vilkaviškis, Prienai, Druskininkai and Nida. Partner companies run the terminals in other towns.
