Slovak Parcel Service (SPS) is a Slovak courier, owned by [Austrian Post](carrier:austrian-post) since 2002, that delivers parcels from shops across Slovakia, to your door or to a Balíkovo box or partner shop. Your tracking number is in the shop's shipping email or in SPS's SMS or email: type it on sps-sro.sk, or paste it in the box above.

## Slovak Parcel Service tracking number

SPS's parcel number is 14 digits starting `703`. On the label it's printed in groups with two extra digits, like `703-010-00000000 00` (digits invented). Those 16 digits are the label barcode; the 14-digit parcel number is the same without the last two.

| Number | Whose it is |
| --- | --- |
| `70300000000000` | SPS: 14 digits starting 703. |
| `703-010-00000000 00` | The same parcel as printed on the label, with 2 extra digits. |
| `00000000000000` | 14 digits not starting 703: DPD, [SEUR](carrier:seur), [BRT](carrier:brt) and others, not SPS. |
| `RR000000005SK` | A postal number ending SK: [Slovenská pošta](carrier:slovenska-posta), not SPS. |

If sps-sro.sk answers "Please check that you entered the shipment number correctly (no spaces)", type it again without spaces or dashes. SPS says the correct format is in your confirmation email or SMS. Its tracking form can also ask for the destination postcode or the customer number to show more detail. No email at all? Ask the shop, and see [how to find your tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number).

## SPS tracking statuses explained

SPS's tracking page (sps-sro.sk, in Slovak and English) lists every scan with date, time, status and centre. The scan wordings stay in Slovak even on the English site:

| SPS status | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `Definícia dát` | [Label created](guide:tracking-statuses): the shop has sent SPS the parcel's data, SPS doesn't have it yet. |
| `Odnáška` | A courier collected the parcel from the sender. |
| `Prvá registrácia` | First scan in an SPS depot. |
| `Vstup` | Arrived at a depot, in transit. |
| `Doručovací hárok` | On today's delivery sheet: out for delivery. |
| `Uložený v balíkomate` | Placed in a Balíkovo box, ready for pickup. |
| `Informácia o doručení` | Delivery details recorded, minutes before delivery. Not a movement. |
| `Doručenie` | Delivered. |
| `Balík odmietnutý` | The parcel was refused. |
| `Uskladnené` | Held in storage at the depot. |
| `Návrat odosielateľovi` | Going back to the sender. |

Two cash-on-delivery steps, `Editor hotovosti` and `Dobierka odoslaná zákaznikoví`, are bookkeeping: nothing changes for the parcel.

From there the site forwards you to mySPS (mysps.sk), which shows a progress bar in English or Slovak: `Received Info`, `Taken by carrier`, `In transit`, `Delivering`, `In the collection point` (only for a pickup point), then `Delivery` or `Undelivered`. Careful: `Delivering` means on its way to you today, while `Delivery` means delivered.

## Delivery, Balíkovo boxes and missed deliveries

**Delivery to your address.** For senders, SPS's standard domestic delivery is the next working day after pickup. The courier brings the parcel to the first lockable door of the building, upstairs only by agreement. If the sender paid for notices, SPS tells you by SMS, email or phone at the latest on the delivery day, with the day, any cash-on-delivery amount and a way to redirect. Email notices go out the day before delivery.

> SMS and calls go only to Slovak mobile numbers. With a foreign number you get no SMS, so give the shop your email too.

**One attempt.** SPS's terms give the courier one delivery attempt. After a failed one, SPS may redirect the parcel to the nearest free Balíkovo box or partner shop and tells you where by SMS or email.

**Balíkovo.** SPS's pickup network, formerly SPS Parcel Shops: partner shops and self-service boxes, more than 2,400 points according to SPS's Balíkovo page. When your parcel arrives you get an SMS or email with a PIN; whoever collects needs that PIN. A box keeps the parcel 48 hours, with a reminder 24 hours before the end; a partner shop keeps it up to 7 of its working days. SPS can change these periods in busy seasons, and mySPS offers "Extend storage period", which may cost a fee. A parcel left uncollected in a box goes back to the sender.

**Changing the delivery.** mySPS has "Flexible Delivery" to change the planned date or delivery type, one change per day. Redirecting to a pickup point needs your email or mobile number on the parcel.

**Cash on delivery.** Pay the courier in cash (up to €500 unless the courier agrees) or by card. For a box, you pay online through a link sent by SMS or email, then the PIN arrives. Once you've opened a parcel at the door you can't hand it back to the courier: deal with the shop.

## SPS parcel late, lost or damaged

**No update for days.** SPS publishes no rule for this. The shop holds the contract with SPS, so ask it first; see [why tracking stops updating](guide:tracking-not-updating). SPS's "Where is my parcel" section opens a contact form once you enter the parcel's details.

**Damaged.** Check the parcel when it's handed over and report visible damage. Keep the original box and filling and don't use the goods: SPS's Rules for claims void a claim without the packaging. You or the sender can claim through the "Report parcel damage" form on sps-sro.sk, with the parcel number, the postcode or customer number, photos, a description, the amount and proof of content and value.

**Deadlines** (Rules for claims valid from 1 May 2026): damage or partial loss within 2 working days of delivery; complete loss, disputed delivery or a late guaranteed service within 30 calendar days of SPS taking the parcel. SPS's FAQ says 7 days from delivery, but the Rules for claims are binding, so act within 2 working days. SPS decides within 30 calendar days. Compensation goes to the sender, or to you with the sender's written authorisation.

**Delivered but not received.** A disputed delivery has the 30-day deadline above; tell the shop too, and see [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).

**Refused or returned.** A refused parcel goes back to the sender without delay; `Návrat odosielateľovi` confirms it.

## Slovak Parcel Service contact and customer service

As listed on sps-sro.sk on 12 October 2026:

- **Phone:** your local area code followed by 16877, for example 02/16877 (Bratislava and most regions), 055/16877 (Košice), 037/16877 (Nitra), 032/16877 (Trenčín). From abroad: +421 2 168 77.
- **Hours:** every working day, 8:00 to 16:30.
- **mySPS:** SPS recommends mysps.sk for the fastest handling: tracking around the clock and customer-service requests.
- **Email or chat:** none listed for recipients.

> On 27 May 2026 SPS warned that fake SMS and emails are circulating again. Be careful with links asking for card or personal details, and delete them if you ordered nothing.

## Tracking an SPS parcel with Peek

[Peek](/) knows SPS's shape, 14 digits starting 703, but it's shared with DPD, SEUR, BRT, [Hermes Germany](carrier:hermes-de) and others. In Slovakia Peek asks SPS first. Elsewhere it asks SPS just after you save the number, not while you add it. If another carrier claims it, or none does yet, use "Change" and pick "Slovak Parcel Service" (searching "SPS" finds it). Type the 14 digits: the 16-digit label code doesn't name SPS. An sps-sro.sk tracking link with `shipment_id=` names SPS whatever the number; a mysps.sk link doesn't.

SPS's scan times carry no time zone: Peek reads them on Slovak time and lists SPS's own history. From SPS it can show the status, the depot of each scan and the weight, with wordings in Slovak as SPS writes them; `Doručenie` counts as delivered. There's no expected delivery date, and a Balíkovo box deposit shows only as a status, without the box's name. Peek can notify you when the status changes. It can't extend storage or resend your PIN.

## Questions about Slovak Parcel Service tracking

### What is the Slovak Parcel Service phone number?

Dial your local area code followed by 16877, such as 02/16877 in Bratislava, or +421 2 168 77 from abroad. Lines are open working days from 8:00 to 16:30.

### How long does SPS keep a parcel in a Balíkovo box?

48 hours from when it's placed in the box, with a reminder 24 hours before the end. A Balíkovo partner shop keeps it up to 7 of its working days.

### Does Slovak Parcel Service have an app?

SPS's pages point you to mySPS at mysps.sk, a web portal where you can track a parcel, change the delivery, get your PIN again and pay cash on delivery.
