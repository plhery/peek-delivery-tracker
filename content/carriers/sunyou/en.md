SunYou, also written SYPost, is a Shenzhen logistics company that carries small parcels for online sellers in China and hands them to a local carrier in your country for the last stretch. Its numbers start with SY: type yours on SunYou's tracking site, [sypost.net](https://www.sypost.net), or in the box above. The shop gives the number with your order's shipping details.

## SunYou tracking number: what it looks like

SunYou publishes no number format, but the numbers seen on its tracking take two shapes. With invented digits:

| Number | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `SY12345678901` | SY and 11 digits: SunYou. |
| `SYGB000000000` | SY, two letters and 9 digits: SunYou too. |
| `LP00000000000000` | LP and 14 digits: an AliExpress and Cainiao logistics number. |
| `RR000000005CN` | Two letters, nine digits, CN: a [China Post](carrier:china-post) number. |

The two letters after SY don't always match the destination. On SunYou's tracking, an `SYRM` number went to Japan and another to Bulgaria, and an `SYAE` number went to Greece.

**Where to find it.** No SunYou page says how the number reaches you. The seller books the parcel and gives the number with the order's shipping details. If you can't find it, see [how to find your tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number).

**Numbers that look alike.** Some shops say an `LP` number travels with SunYou, but that shape belongs to AliExpress's logistics; track it as a [Cainiao parcel](carrier:cainiao). An SY number with only 10 digits, or with more than 11, is not a SunYou shape.

**A second number later on.** Once the parcel leaves SunYou's centre in China, the tracking often adds "Carrier Tracking Number:" with the local carrier's own number, its name after "Distributor:" and its website. That number works on the local carrier's tracking.

## SunYou tracking statuses: what they mean

sypost.net shows the scans in the language you pick there (13 languages, including English, Spanish, French, German and Portuguese). In English, in the order a parcel usually meets them:

| SunYou says | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `Pre-Shipment Info Sent To United Kingdom` | The seller registered the parcel. SunYou doesn't have it yet. |
| `Acceptance, Sent To United Kingdom` | SunYou received it in China. |
| `Departed Sunyou Facility` | It left SunYou's centre, often with the local carrier's number. |
| `Export Customs Clearance Successed` | Cleared to leave China. |
| `Departed From Port Of Origin` | The flight or ship has left. |
| `Arrived At GMTC` | At SunYou's global mail transit centre, a hub on the way. |
| `Arrived At Destination Country Airport` | Landed in your country. |
| `Customs Clearance In Process` | Going through import customs. |
| `Customs Clearance Successed` | Through customs. |
| `Hand Over To Last Mile` | The local carrier has it. Follow its number from here. |
| `Delivery In Progress` | Out for delivery. |
| `Delivered` | Delivered. |

Some lines are written differently from parcel to parcel: `GMTC, Arrived` is the same as `Arrived At GMTC`, and `Customs Clearance Was Completed` the same as `Customs Clearance Successed`. `Delivered, At Mailbox` and `Delivered, Left At The Door` say where the parcel was left.

Two things confuse people. First, `Delivered` can come straight after `Hand Over To Last Mile`, or even after the airport line, with nothing in between: the local carrier's own tracking has the steps SunYou skips. Second, the summary at the top of the page has its own labels. `Alert` means a possible problem and `Expired` means the parcel has been in transit too long; neither means a failed delivery. `Undelivered` does mean a delivery failed; the history may give a reason such as `Delivery Failed, Reason: Incorrect Address`.

For customs stops, `Customs Clearance Hold` and `Delivery failed, Reason: Refusal to pay VAT / customs duties` are the lines to watch; see [parcels held at customs](guide:customs).

## Who delivers SunYou parcels

SunYou delivers nothing itself in Europe: its commercial line hands parcels to local carriers in the destination country, and its tracking names that carrier. In histories read on its tracking, [Royal Mail](carrier:royal-mail) delivered in the UK, [DHL Paket](carrier:dhl) in Germany, [USPS](carrier:usps) or [GOFO](carrier:gofo) in the US and [Canada Post](carrier:canada-post) in Canada.

SunYou publishes no rules for delivery attempts, cards, pickup points or how long a parcel is held. Those are the local carrier's rules, so once you have its name and number, check its site to rearrange a delivery or find where the parcel waits.

Some SunYou parcels go as ordinary mail, which SunYou says can be tracked only until the destination's post receives them. On those, the history may stop once that post has the parcel.

SunYou's tracking also has a "Proof of Delivery" link, which asks for the recipient's phone number.

## SunYou parcel not arriving or tracking stuck

**Who acts.** The seller is SunYou's customer: they open an account, book the parcel and hold the contract. SunYou's pages give recipients no claim route, so for a late, lost, damaged or returned parcel, contact the seller or the marketplace you ordered from. SunYou's compensation rules are for sellers.

**How long it takes.** SunYou's figures for sellers give "4-5 days from acceptance to some main destinations" on its general line, "fastest delivery within 5 days" to main European and American countries on its commercial line, and 20 to 30 calendar days on its US economy line. These are sales figures, not a date for your order.

**No update.** If the history shows a carrier number, check that carrier's tracking first. More in [why tracking stops updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

**Lost or returned.** SunYou's wordings for these are `Unfortunately, Your Parcel Is Lost`, `Returned` and `Package Return, Reason: Incomplete Address Information`. Any of them is a reason to contact the seller.

## SunYou contact number and customer service

As read on 12 October 2026, SunYou lists the same channels for every country, with no local office for recipients:

- **Hotline**: +86 4006075388, a Chinese number. No hours or call costs are published.
- **Email**: mto@sypost.com, its track-and-trace mailbox.
- **Complaints**: tousu@sypost.com, on a contact page in Chinese.

For anything that needs action, the seller and the local carrier are the ones to ask. SunYou publishes no warning about fake delivery messages.

## Track a SunYou parcel with Peek

[Peek](/) names SunYou at once for both SY shapes, which no other carrier in its catalog shares. A sypost.net link with the number in it, such as the one "Copy Link" gives, can be pasted too.

SunYou's lines show in English whatever your language, except exact matches such as `Delivered`, which appear in Peek's own words. Peek shows the status, the history (up to 20 scans, with no place for each), the destination country, the delivery time and the local carrier with its number. It shows no expected delivery date and no pickup point for SunYou parcels. Scan times keep the time zone SunYou gives them, China's and then your country's.

When SunYou gives the local carrier's number, Peek can ask that carrier (Royal Mail, DHL in Germany, Canada Post, USPS or the national post, for example) and switch to it once it shows newer progress. The parcel keeps its SunYou number, its page says "Delivery with" that carrier and lists the carrier's own number with a link. This doesn't happen for every parcel: [Meest](carrier:meest) in Greece, for one, isn't followed.

It checks every 10 minutes by day, every 2 minutes once the parcel is out for delivery, and hourly overnight, and can notify you of every scan, important steps only or "Delivery day only". It can't contact SunYou or the seller for you.

## Questions about SunYou tracking

### Is SunYou tracking real or fake?

SunYou is a real logistics company, founded in Shenzhen in 2008, and its tracking is at sypost.net. If your number shows `Not Found` there, ask the seller to check it.

### What is SunYou Post?

It is SunYou Logistics, a Shenzhen company that ships small parcels abroad for online sellers in China and hands them to a local carrier for delivery.

### Where is the SunYou facility?

SunYou is based in Shenzhen, China, with branches in Guangzhou, Dongguan, Yiwu, Kunshan and Ningbo. `Departed Sunyou Facility` means the parcel has left SunYou's centre in China.

### How long does SunYou take to deliver?

SunYou tells sellers 4 to 5 days to some main destinations on its general line and 20 to 30 calendar days on its US economy line. They are not a promise for your order.

### Who delivers SunYou parcels in the UK?

In the UK histories read on SunYou's tracking, Royal Mail delivered. Your parcel's own history names the carrier after `Departed Sunyou Facility`, with that carrier's number.
