Swiss Post Cargo is Swiss Post's freight brand: pallets and other goods carried by truck, from single items to whole truckloads, not ordinary parcels. Track a shipment on its Track & Trace page (tt.swisspost-cargo.com) with the barcode or the reference the sender gave you, or paste it in the box above. If what you have is a Swiss Post parcel number, it belongs on [Swiss Post's page](carrier:swiss-post) instead.

## Swiss Post Cargo tracking number

Swiss Post Cargo's tracker takes a barcode for one item or a reference from the sender. A reference brings up every barcode in its consignment. The shapes Peek links to Swiss Post Cargo (digits invented):

| Number | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `00012345600000000005` | 20 digits starting `00`: a pallet or item barcode (an SSCC). The last digit is a check digit. |
| `PL-12345678` | `PL-` and 8 digits: a customer reference. |

Check the shop's emails, then ask the shop. [How to find your tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number) has more places to look. If the tracker finds nothing, check the digits, then ask the sender.

Two kinds of number that aren't tracked as Swiss Post Cargo:

- **Swiss Post parcels:** 18 digits starting `99` (printed `99.00.123456.12345678`) or two letters, nine digits and `CH` (`RR000000005CH`).
- **PostLogistics references** like `12345678-123`: PostLogistics now trades as Swiss Post Cargo, and its old tracker, tracking.postlogistics.ch, no longer opens. Peek tracks these references as PostLogistics.

## Swiss Post Cargo track and trace statuses

Swiss Post Cargo words its scans in English, German, French or Italian. In English, a delivered shipment reads like this:

| Swiss Post Cargo says | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `Data entry POST` | The sender's data has arrived. The goods are not with Swiss Post Cargo yet. |
| `Received warehouse POST` | The goods have reached a Swiss Post Cargo hub or depot, named next to the scan. |
| `Loaded for Delivery` | It's on the delivery truck: out for delivery. |
| `IMAGE` | A delivery photo, listed just before the delivery scan. |
| `DELIVERED SCANNED` | Delivered, in the town shown. |
| `SIGNATURE` | The signature taken at delivery, at the same time as the delivery scan. |

`Data entry POST` is only data: "POST" is part of the wording, and nothing has moved yet. `IMAGE` and `SIGNATURE` are proof that belongs to the delivery, not new events.

## Swiss Post Cargo delivery: when, where and missed deliveries

**When.** Swiss Post's freight brochure says freight is generally delivered on the next working day. In Switzerland and Liechtenstein, places a heavy goods vehicle can reach get it by 17:00 on the next working day. Hard-to-reach Alpine valleys and mountain areas (Valais, Ticino and Graubünden) get it by 17:00 on the second working day.

**Where.** As standard, freight goes to the first door on the ground floor, the ramp or the kerb, during business hours, not upstairs. The driver expects access with a pallet truck, and unloading is the recipient's job: a driver who unloads does it on your behalf. The driver asks you to sign to confirm the delivery was correct.

**Extras the sender books.** A notice by phone, email or SMS, a planned delivery, delivery to a floor and a second delivery are extra services, so you get them only if the sender ordered them.

**Missed delivery and pickup.** Swiss Post's freight brochure mentions no pickup points or lockers, and a second delivery is one of the extras above. It doesn't say how long goods wait at a depot or how you can reschedule. Ask the shop, which booked the transport.

## Swiss Post Cargo shipment late, lost or damaged

The shop is Swiss Post Cargo's customer: businesses order its services, so a delay, damage or a missing item goes to the shop first. Report damage to the shop straight away, with photos.

**No update for days.** `Data entry POST` alone means the goods may still be with the sender: ask the shop when it hands them over. [Tracking that isn't updating](guide:tracking-not-updating) explains the usual reasons.

**Delivered but not there.** Look for the `SIGNATURE` and `IMAGE` rows on the tracker, then contact the shop. [Delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received) walks through the next steps.

**Suspicious messages.** Swiss Post's phishing page says fake messages in Swiss Post's name arrive by SMS, email, WhatsApp, websites, calls and social media, often about parcels, fees, tracking or personal data.

## Swiss Post Cargo contact number

Swiss Post Cargo works for the shop, so start there: the shop booked the transport. Swiss Post's freight brochure, dated April 2022, gives one contact, for business customers and without a phone number: Customer Service Transport CH, Lagerstrasse 12, 5606 Dintikon, sales.ls4@post.ch.

For Swiss Post letters and parcels (not Cargo), Swiss Post's line is +41 848 888 888 (CHF 0.08 a minute from a Swiss landline), Monday to Friday 07:30 to 18:00 and Saturday 08:00 to 12:00.

## Tracking a Swiss Post Cargo shipment with Peek

A 20-digit barcode starting `00` belongs to whoever shipped it, not to one carrier, so [Peek](/) shows "asking Swiss Post Cargo, NZ Post…" and names Swiss Post Cargo only if Swiss Post Cargo knows the number. A `PL-12345678` reference is asked of Swiss Post Cargo and TNT Italy, whose numbers can take the same shape. For any other reference of 6 to 40 letters and digits, pick Swiss Post Cargo with "Change", or paste a tt.swisspost-cargo.com or apv.swisspost-cargo.com Track & Trace link. A Swiss Post parcel number in such a link is tracked as Swiss Post.

Peek shows the status and the history with the town of each scan on a map, with the delivery time. Times are read on Zurich time and shown in your device's time zone. Wordings stay in English; `Loaded for Delivery` counts as out for delivery. The signature and photo rows fold into the delivery scan, without the picture. Peek leaves out your name and address and has no expected delivery, weight or pickup point.

A reference shared by several shipments is read only while one of them has scans from the last 60 days; otherwise it reads as not found, so use the barcode. Peek checks every 10 minutes by day, every 2 once out for delivery, and every hour or two overnight, and can notify you of every scan, important steps or delivery day only.

## Questions about Swiss Post Cargo tracking

### What is Swiss Post Cargo?

It's the name Swiss Post's goods logistics has traded under since 1 March 2025: logistics for businesses, in Switzerland and abroad, freight included. Swiss Post's group companies in Switzerland include Swiss Post Cargo CH AG, wholly owned by Swiss Post. PostLogistics was one of the first group companies to take the name.

### Is PostLogistics the same as Swiss Post Cargo?

Yes, PostLogistics now trades as Swiss Post Cargo. Its old tracker, tracking.postlogistics.ch, no longer opens; Swiss Post Cargo's tracker is tt.swisspost-cargo.com. Peek still tracks PostLogistics references like `12345678-123`.

### Is there a Swiss Post Cargo email address?

Swiss Post's freight brochure, dated April 2022, gives sales.ls4@post.ch, for business customers. As a recipient, write to the shop first: it booked the transport.

### Why does Swiss Post Cargo send me an SMS?

Often because the sender booked a delivery notice: Swiss Post's freight brochure lists notice by phone, email or SMS as an extra. Fake texts in Swiss Post's name exist too, so check the number on the tracker yourself.

### Can I track Swiss Post Cargo live?

Not on a live map. The tracker lists each scan with its town, and Swiss Post's freight brochure speaks of regular updates on where the consignment is.
