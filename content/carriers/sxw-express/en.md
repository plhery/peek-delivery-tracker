SXW Express is the name Peek uses for Shenzhen Shengxingwang Supply Chain Management Co., Ltd. (深圳胜兴旺供应链管理有限公司), a logistics company in Shenzhen, China, that ships goods abroad for businesses and online sellers. If a Chinese shop sent your order with SXW, look for a number of SXW and twelve digits, the shape Peek knows as SXW's, and paste it in the box above. You can also check it yourself on SXW's own tracker, which opens from the "Express Tracking" box on sxwexpress.cn. Peek reads that same tracker for you.

## SXW Express tracking number: what it looks like

SXW's site gives no number format. The shape Peek knows as SXW Express's is SXW and twelve digits, 15 characters in all. With invented digits:

| Number | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `SXW000000000017` | SXW and twelve digits: SXW Express's layout. Peek names SXW Express straight away. |
| `SXW00000000017` | Only eleven digits: not SXW's layout. Check for a missing digit. |
| `GFUS00000000000017` | GFUS and fourteen digits: a [GOFO Express](carrier:gofo) number. SXW's record can give one for a parcel to the US (see below). |

Spaces, dashes and lower case don't matter when you paste the number into Peek.

**Where to find it.** The seller books the parcel with SXW and passes the number on. If you can't find it, see [how to find your tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number).

**Checking it on SXW's site.** On the home page of sxwexpress.cn, the "Express Tracking" tab has a box that says "Enter your tracking number here!" and a "Track" button. The result opens in a new tab, on SXW's tracker, in Chinese. That tracker also has an English page. Next to the box, a 17TRACK logo links to 17TRACK, a separate tracking service: it isn't SXW's tracker.

## SXW Express tracking statuses explained

Of the steps Peek knows from SXW's tracker, seven are in English and three in Chinese. Here they are, each in its own words:

| SXW's tracker says | What it means for you | Peek shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Parcel Data Received` | The seller has sent the parcel's details. Nobody has the parcel yet. | "Label created" |
| `货物电子信息已经收到` | Means that the goods' electronic information has been received: the same thing, written by SXW's tracking system. | "Label created" |
| `到达收货点` | Means that the parcel has arrived at the receiving point: SXW has it. This is the first scan in its hands. | "Received by carrier" |
| `Parcel picked up` | The parcel has been collected: the first scan on the shipping line that carries it. | "Received by carrier" |
| `货物离开操作中心` | Means that the goods have left the operation centre: SXW's own centre. | "In transit" |
| `Parcel received at Shenzhen Sorting Centre.` | A sorting centre in Shenzhen has the parcel. | "In transit" |
| `Parcel processed at Shenzhen Sorting Centre.` | Sorted there. | "In transit" |
| `Parcel departure in Shenzhen Sorting Centre.` | On its way out of that sorting centre. | "In transit" |
| `Parcel departure in Transit Facility, Parcel Departure` | The parcel has left a transit facility and is on its way. | "In transit" |
| `Customs declaration completed` | The export declaration in China is done. This is customs in China, not in your country. | "At customs" |

**Chinese and English lines.** The Chinese lines come from SXW's own system, and most English ones from the shipping line that carries the parcel. So two lines can say nearly the same thing: `货物电子信息已经收到` and `Parcel Data Received` both mean the details are in, and `到达收货点` and `Parcel picked up` both mean someone has the parcel now.

**Other lines.** SXW's tracker can show lines that aren't in this table. Peek reads such a line by its words where it can, and shows it as SXW's tracker wrote it.

**Times.** Peek reads every time on SXW's tracker as Beijing time and shows it in your device's time zone. In winter, a step at 20:00 in Beijing is 13:00 in Paris or Berlin, 12:00 in London and 7:00 in New York. A step dated after the moment Peek checks, on Beijing time, waits until its time has come.

## Who delivers SXW Express parcels

SXW's site says it offers "DDU/DDP door-to-door delivery" and "local last-mile delivery", and that its network covers Europe, America, Southeast Asia, the Middle East and Africa. Its product list names UPS lines from Hong Kong, an "ARAMEX Special Line", a "Middle East and Europe small package" and a "US Express Mail Small Packet". It doesn't say which company delivers the parcel in your country.

**What Peek's code shows.** For a parcel to the US, SXW's record can give a [GOFO Express](carrier:gofo) number (GFUS and fourteen digits) beside SXW's own. When it does, and the record names no shipping channel, Peek names GOFO Express as the company that delivers ("Delivery with GOFO Express") and shows its number and a link to GOFO's tracking. If the record names a channel, Peek names no delivering company. While GOFO lists the parcel but hasn't scanned it, the line reads "Announced to GOFO Express, not received yet". Once GOFO's own tracking shows a scan as recent as SXW's latest, or newer, Peek follows the parcel there. GOFO Express is the only delivering company Peek's code names for SXW.

**Delivery attempts and pickup.** These follow the delivering company's rules, and SXW's site gives none. Ask the seller which company delivers and for its tracking number, and see [missed delivery](guide:missed-delivery).

**Customs fees in the EU.** Since 1 July 2026, goods bought online from outside the EU, in parcels worth up to €150, pay a €3 customs duty per item. Since 1 November 2026, a €2 Union handling fee per item is due as well, whatever the value. An item here means the goods in a parcel that share the same tariff classification and description, and origin where it must be declared, whatever their number. For what you pay, and how, in the UK, Ireland, the US, Canada and Australia, see [parcels held at customs](guide:customs).

## SXW Express parcel not arriving or tracking stuck

**Only `Parcel Data Received` or `货物电子信息已经收到`.** The seller has created the shipment, but nobody has scanned the parcel. If nothing follows after a few days, ask the seller whether it has really been sent.

**"Waiting for the carrier" in Peek.** Peek has no step for your number yet: SXW's tracker doesn't know it, or has no steps for it, and the tracking services Peek asks have nothing either. Peek keeps checking. Compare the number with the seller's message too: SXW and twelve digits.

**"At customs" after `Customs declaration completed`.** That's the export declaration in China, not customs in your country. If you're later asked to pay import charges, see [parcels held at customs](guide:customs).

**No new step for days.** If Peek shows a GOFO Express number, check GOFO's tracking. Otherwise go by the shop's delivery estimate, and ask the seller once it has passed. More in [why tracking stops updating](guide:tracking-not-updating) and [tracking a parcel from China](guide:tracking-from-china).

**Delivered, but nothing came.** Ask the delivering company and the seller. More in [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).

**Lost or returned.** The seller booked the parcel with SXW, so ask the seller, or the marketplace, for a refund or a new parcel. See [lost parcels](guide:lost-parcel) and [returned to sender](guide:returned-to-sender).

## SXW Express contact number and customer service

As read on 14 November 2026, SXW's website, sxwexpress.cn, lists no phone number, email or street address. Its "Contact US" page has a title and nothing else, and its "Online Support" panel is empty. The site's member sign-in is for accounts with SXW: you don't need one to track a parcel.

SXW's customer is the seller, so ask the seller about your order first, and the marketplace if the seller doesn't answer. For a parcel GOFO Express delivers in the US, ask GOFO about the delivery itself.

## Track an SXW Express parcel with Peek

[Peek](/) names SXW Express straight away for a number of SXW and twelve digits, with "Detected carrier". No other carrier in Peek's catalog uses that shape. If the seller gave you a number of another shape, paste it all the same: Peek follows more than 3,500 carriers. To put a number under SXW by hand, press "Change" and search for SXW Express (typing Shengxingwang finds it too), or use "Change carrier" on a parcel you've already added.

Peek asks SXW's own tracker, the one the box on sxwexpress.cn opens, with no account, postcode or phone number. It shows the status, each step with its place when the tracker gives one, and the destination country. It leaves out the names, address, postcodes, weight, number of pieces, customer reference, payment details, signature and proof of delivery the record can hold. SXW's tracker gives no delivery date, so Peek shows none.

Peek holds back any step dated after the moment it checks, and any step written more than 12 hours before the time it shows, or more than 6 hours before it in the same second as such a step: steps written ahead are plans, not scans. When SXW's tracker doesn't know your number or can't be reached, Peek asks tracking services instead and shows their copy of the steps, in their own words.

Apart from the GOFO hand-over above, Peek stays with SXW's tracker. If the seller gives you the delivering company's number, add it to Peek as a parcel of its own.

Peek checks every 10 minutes, and hourly once the parcel has gone quiet. It can notify you of every scan, important steps only or the delivery only. Of SXW's own steps, "Important steps" covers only `Customs declaration completed`, as Peek shows it "At customs": that alert is about the export declaration in China, not your country's customs. "Delivery only" covers the delivery round and the delivery. Peek can't contact SXW Express or the seller for you.

## Questions about SXW Express tracking

### What is SXW Express?

The name Peek uses for Shenzhen Shengxingwang Supply Chain Management Co., Ltd. (深圳胜兴旺供应链管理有限公司), at sxwexpress.cn. Its site describes it as a one-stop cross-border logistics provider in Shenzhen: sea and air freight, rail to Europe, international express and door-to-door delivery for exporters and online sellers.

### Where can I track an SXW Express parcel myself?

In the "Express Tracking" box on the home page of sxwexpress.cn: type the number and press "Track". SXW's tracker opens in Chinese, with an English page too. The 17TRACK logo next to the box leads to a separate tracking service.

### What does "Customs declaration completed" mean on SXW tracking?

That the export declaration in China is done. Peek shows it as "At customs", but this is customs in China, not in your country.

### Who delivers SXW Express parcels in my country?

SXW's site doesn't say. For a parcel to the US, SXW's record can give a GOFO Express number: Peek then names GOFO and follows the parcel on to it once GOFO has scanned it. Otherwise, ask the seller which company delivers, and for its tracking number.

### Who do I contact about a missing SXW Express parcel?

The seller you ordered from: it booked the parcel with SXW. SXW's website lists no phone number or email. If the seller doesn't answer, open a dispute on the marketplace.
