TNT delivers parcels, documents and freight for people and businesses across the world, with its own road network across Europe. tnt.com now presents FedEx Express and TNT as "coming together", and its US and Canadian pages say TNT is operating as FedEx there. A TNT consignment has a 9-digit number, also called the shipment or waybill number. Track it on [tnt.com's Track & Trace](https://www.tnt.com/express/en_gb/site/shipping-tools/tracking.html), by that number or by the sender's reference. A parcel sent within Italy belongs to [TNT Italy](carrier:tnt-it) instead.

## TNT tracking number: what it looks like and where to find it

| What you have | What it is | Invented example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 9 digits | TNT's consignment number, the one tnt.com tracks | None: TNT reuses these numbers |
| Two letters and 8 digits | A domestic waybill from Italy: see [TNT Italy](carrier:tnt-it) | `NA00000000` |
| Letters and digits chosen by the sender | The customer reference, an optional reference the sender gave when booking | – |

The number is in the shipping confirmation email, or on the collection manifest under the barcode. Senders also find it on TNT's invoice, on the left, about 3 cm below their customer number. If you're receiving the parcel, ask the shop or the sender for it. tnt.com tracks several numbers at once, separated by commas. See also [how to find a tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number).

TNT handles over two million shipments a month, so its 8- and 9-digit numbers come back every three or four weeks. If tnt.com lists several shipments under your number, pick yours by date, origin and destination.

## TNT tracking statuses: what they mean

tnt.com's English wordings, in the order a parcel usually meets them:

| TNT's words | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `Shipment collected from collection address` | TNT has picked the parcel up from the sender. |
| `Shipment now at the depot nearest to collection address` | It's at the TNT depot near the sender. |
| `Shipment in transit` | Moving through TNT's network. |
| `Shipment arrived at connection point` | At a hub between two legs of the journey. |
| `Shipment arrived at TNT location` | At a TNT site on the way. |
| `Shipment being processed at TNT location.` | Being sorted there. |
| `Customs has released the goods` | Customs has cleared it. |
| `Shipment now at depot nearest to delivery address` | At the depot that delivers to you. |
| `Shipment is out for delivery.` | With the driver today. |
| `Receiver not home. We've left them a message and we're waiting for them to get in contact` | The driver missed you and TNT is waiting for you to rearrange. |
| `Shipment delivered in good condition` | Delivered. |
| `Shipment partially delivered. Recovery actions underway` | Only part of the consignment arrived; TNT is tracing the rest. |
| `Delay due to routing error. We're working hard to deliver on time.` | It went the wrong way and TNT is putting it back on route. |
| `Delay due to routing error. Recovery actions underway` | The same. |
| `Returned to sender as agreed` | It's going back to the sender, as arranged. |

Track & Trace also shows when TNT expects the parcel to arrive and, once delivered, who signed for it.

## TNT delivery: missed deliveries, redelivery and the depot

TNT delivers to the recipient's address, to you or to someone who appears to be allowed to accept it for you. Its FAQ says it can't deliver to PO boxes and needs a full street address; its conditions allow PO boxes for certain international destinations, if the sender gives TNT your phone number. Those conditions are TNT's for shipments sent from Europe: one sent from outside Europe is governed by the terms of the TNT company that accepted it.

**Missed delivery.** The driver leaves a "We Missed You" card. TNT's FAQ says to use it with the Re-delivery Service to book another delivery or a pickup at the depot. No card, or no attempt yet? Contact TNT with the shipment number, the delivery address and the day you want. Redeliveries happen Monday to Friday only.

**What TNT's conditions allow.** When a signature was required at a home address and the first attempt fails, TNT may try again, hold the parcel until you or the sender give instructions, or deliver it to a place it chooses. A parcel for a business address still not delivered after three attempts, or five business days after the first, counts as undeliverable. TNT then tries to reach the sender to arrange a return; if it can't within five business days, it may return the parcel, store it or dispose of it.

**Changing the delivery.** In some places, TNT follows the recipient's instructions for parcels from a business to a consumer: a later delivery, a neighbour, another address in the same country, leaving it without a signature, or a pickup point. Another change of address counts as a new shipment, and new charges may apply.

## TNT customs: duties and taxes on international parcels

Where local law allows, TNT clears international shipments through customs itself. It may pay duties and taxes for whoever owes them and charge a clearance fee for that, which varies by country. It can also ask for payment before it releases the parcel to you. The sender is ultimately responsible: if you refuse to pay, TNT may ask the sender, and if the sender doesn't pay either, the parcel may go back.

For parcels bought from outside the EU and sent into the EU, a flat €3 customs duty per item type has applied since 1 July 2026 to consignments worth up to €150. From 1 November 2026, a €2 EU handling fee applies too. The details, and the rules in other countries, are in [the customs guide](guide:customs).

## TNT parcel not showing, late, lost or damaged

**Not showing.** tnt.com says a new shipment can take up to an hour to appear in its system, and stays there for about 90 days. More in [tracking that isn't updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

**Delivered but not there.** Check on Track & Trace who signed, then contact TNT. TNT's shipping tools, such as myTNT, give the signature image as proof of delivery. See [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).

**Lost or damaged.** TNT's conditions give 21 days to notify a claim: from delivery for damage or delay, from the expected delivery date for loss or non-delivery. Write any damage on the delivery receipt: taking the parcel without a note is evidence it arrived in good condition. For a damaged item, TNT's FAQ says the claimant must be the person who booked the shipment: if a shop sent it, the shop claims. Keep the contents and all the packaging for TNT to inspect. TNT aims to settle a claim within three weeks of the completed claim form. See [damaged parcel](guide:damaged-parcel) and [lost parcel](guide:lost-parcel).

**Scam messages.** TNT says it offers no escrow services, and a consignment number alone doesn't prove TNT has the goods. Don't pay in advance through a money transfer service; forward fake TNT emails to e-crime@fedex.com. More in [delivery scam texts](guide:delivery-scam-texts).

## Tracking a TNT parcel with Peek

Paste the 9-digit number. When its check digit is right, [Peek](/) asks TNT, and XPO too when the number also fits XPO's check, then shows "TNT has this parcel." when TNT knows it. If the check digit doesn't fit, paste it all the same: Peek follows more than 3,500 carriers. If TNT doesn't come up, pick it with "Change", or "Change carrier" on the parcel. A number of GE, 9 digits and WW is named as TNT straight away when its check digit is right. You can also paste a tnt.com tracking link (one ending in `tracking.html?cons=` and the number): Peek reads the number in it.

Peek reads tnt.com's tracking in English, so the wordings above stay English in every language. It shows the status, the history with the city and country of each scan, the destination country and the expected delivery date while TNT gives one. The sender's reference and the signature stay on tnt.com. Since numbers get reused, Peek follows the shipment with the newest scan: check that its route is yours. Peek also follows TNT France's 16-digit national numbers, on TNT France's own tracking and in French; many carriers share that length, so you may have to pick TNT. Peek checks every 10 minutes by day, every 2 once the parcel is out for delivery, and every hour or two overnight, and can notify you of "Every scan", "Important steps" or "Delivery only". It can't contact TNT or the sender for you.

## Questions about TNT tracking

### Is TNT part of FedEx?

tnt.com presents FedEx Express and TNT as coming together, and its US and Canadian pages say TNT is operating as [FedEx](carrier:fedex) there. Elsewhere, TNT consignments are still tracked on tnt.com.

### Why does my TNT number show someone else's shipment?

TNT reuses its 8- and 9-digit numbers every three or four weeks. Pick your shipment by date, origin and destination.

### Can I pick up my TNT parcel from the depot?

Yes, after a missed delivery: TNT's FAQ says to use the "We Missed You" card with its Re-delivery Service to book a new delivery or a pickup at the depot.

### Does TNT deliver on Saturday?

Redeliveries happen Monday to Friday only. Saturday delivery, where TNT offers it, costs a special handling fee in countries where Saturday isn't a business day.
