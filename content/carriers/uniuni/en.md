UniUni (Uni Express Inc., based in Richmond, B.C.) is a last-mile delivery company that brings online orders to the door in Canada and the United States, for shops such as Shein and Temu. Track a parcel on [uniuni.com/tracking](https://www.uniuni.com/tracking/) or paste its number in the box above. The number comes from the shop or marketplace you ordered from, which hands the labelled parcel to UniUni for the final stretch.

## UniUni tracking number: format and where to find it

Parcels reach UniUni already labelled by the seller, so the number is in your order's shipping details. Can't find it? See [where else to look for a tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number). The shapes, with invented digits:

| Shape (invented) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `UUS0000000000000000` | UUS followed by 16 letters and digits |
| `UUSC000000000000` | UUSC followed by 12 digits |
| `4C000000000US` | 4C, 9 digits, then US |
| `U000000000000000` | U followed by 15 digits |
| `AA00CAA0U000000000` | 2 letters, 2 digits, CAA0, a letter and 9 digits |
| `JY00CA00A000000000` | JY, 2 digits, CA, 2 digits, A and 9 digits |
| `UR00000000000000000` | UR and 17 digits, from UniUni's seller platform |

A reference starting with `UNI` is an order number, and `URB` with 16 digits a batch ID: neither is the parcel's tracking number. UniUni's tracker takes up to 25 numbers at once, separated by commas.

These shapes don't overlap with USPS, [GOFO](carrier:gofo) or [SpeedX](carrier:speedx) numbers.

## What UniUni's tracking statuses mean

These are the lines a parcel usually meets on UniUni's English tracking page, in order.

| UniUni's status | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `Order received` | The seller has sent UniUni the shipping details. UniUni doesn't have the parcel yet. |
| `Gateway transit in` | The parcel has reached a UniUni gateway warehouse. |
| `Gateway transit out` | It has left the gateway for the warehouse that will deliver it. |
| `Parcel in transit to local UniUni warehouse for delivery` | On its way to your local UniUni warehouse. |
| `Arrival scan` | Scanned in at the local warehouse. |
| `Your parcel has been assigned to a delivery driver` | Sorted onto a driver's route, not on the road yet. |
| `Out for delivery` | With the driver today. |
| `Delivered` | Delivered. UniUni has photos of the drop-off. |
| `Failed delivery attempt, returning to the warehouse` | The driver couldn't deliver. The parcel goes back to the warehouse for another try. |
| `The parcel will be transferred to a third-party courier for delivery.` | UniUni is giving the parcel to another courier to finish the job. |
| `Parcel has been handed over to a third-party courier` | The other courier has it now. |

Wordings vary: `Label Created` means the same as `Order received`, `Parcel received by UniUni` means UniUni physically has it, and `Parcel has been delivered` can stand for `Delivered`. UniUni's page can add where the parcel was left, as in `Delivered. (Recipient's front door)`.

A failed attempt on UniUni's page carries the reason in brackets, such as no safe place to leave the parcel, a business closed at delivery time or a failed contact attempt. After a second failed attempt the status becomes `storage`. Once a parcel goes to a third-party courier, that courier's own scans follow in its own wording, such as `Item out for delivery` or `Parcel is ready for pickup`.

## UniUni delivery, pickup and missed deliveries

UniUni tells shippers it delivers from next day up to 7 days. An estimated delivery date appears only once UniUni physically has the parcel, and the tracking page can show a time window for the delivery.

**Missed delivery.** After a first failed attempt, UniUni says it tries again within 48 hours in most cases, with nothing for you to do unless your delivery details need changing. After a second failed attempt, the parcel goes back to UniUni's warehouse. Once tracking shows `storage`, contact UniUni support to arrange a new delivery. The parcel is kept for 30 days.

**Address and instructions.** To add a unit number, a gate or buzz code or a phone number, use the UniUni chatbot; once the parcel is out for delivery, the driver may not get the update in time. UniUni doesn't deliver to post offices or PO boxes.

**Picking it up.** UniUni offers pickup only in Canada, in a few cities such as Montreal, Toronto and North York. Elsewhere, ask for a new delivery instead.

**Delivery PIN.** In Canada, the sender can require a PIN, sent to you by email or text. Without it, the attempt fails and the parcel goes on a later route.

**Proof of delivery.** Drivers take photos at drop-off. To see them, track the parcel on uniuni.com, choose `View Delivery Confirmation` and enter the postal or ZIP code of the delivery address.

## UniUni package not moving, not delivered or damaged

No updates at all usually means the seller hasn't handed the parcel over yet. UniUni asks you to contact the seller for the latest order status. If scans stop after UniUni has the parcel, see [tracking that isn't updating](guide:tracking-not-updating). UniUni's French FAQ also describes misrouted parcels: sent by mistake to a warehouse in another city, then passed automatically to Canada Post or USPS. These arrive late, and you don't need to do anything.

**Delivered but not there.** Look at the delivery photos first. UniUni then suggests asking people in your household, neighbours, building management and mailroom or lobby staff. If it's still missing, contact UniUni through its chatbot. More ideas in [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).

**Damaged.** Take clear photos of the parcel and its contents and share them, with your contact details and tracking number, through the chatbot. UniUni's support reviews the case, and you can also contact the seller.

**Wrong or missing items, returns.** These go to the seller. UniUni's claims process is for shippers who bought parcel protection, so as a recipient you go through the chatbot and the seller.

## Tracking UniUni parcels with Peek

[Peek](/) names UniUni at once for numbers starting `UUS`, `UUSC`, `4C` (ending `US`) and `U9999`, for `UR` IDs, and for the CAA0 shape with a U after `CAA0`. For other numbers that are U and 15 digits, other CAA0 numbers and the JY shape, it asks UniUni and names it when UniUni knows the number. A uniuni.com tracking link with the number in it works too. A master number for a multi-piece shipment can't be tracked as one: add each parcel's own number.

Peek reads UniUni's tracking and shows the status, each scan with its town and province or state on a map, the expected day or time window, the delivery time and the destination country. It leaves out addresses, driver names and the delivery photos, so look at the photos on UniUni's page. `Delivered` and `Out for delivery` appear in Peek's own words; other lines show as UniUni writes them, without the reason in brackets.

When UniUni hands a parcel to another courier, that courier's scans show up inside UniUni's history, but Peek doesn't follow the other courier's number. Parcels sent with [SpeedPAK](carrier:speedpak) or [DHL eCommerce](carrier:dhl-ecommerce) can name UniUni for the last mile, and Peek can then follow them on UniUni. Peek checks every 10 minutes from 8am to 10pm Swiss time (about 2am to 4pm ET, 11pm to 1pm PT), every 2 minutes in those hours once the parcel is out for delivery, and hourly the rest of the time, and can notify you when the status changes.

## Questions about UniUni tracking

### Are UniUni packages delivered by USPS?

UniUni delivers with its own drivers. Some parcels are handed to a third-party courier to finish the delivery, and UniUni says misrouted parcels can be passed to [USPS](carrier:usps) or [Canada Post](carrier:canada-post).

### How long does UniUni hold packages?

After a second failed attempt, UniUni keeps the parcel in its warehouse for 30 days. Contact support once tracking shows `storage` to arrange a new delivery.

### Is UniUni legit?

Yes. UniUni is run by Uni Express Inc., a delivery company based in Richmond, British Columbia, that delivers for Shein and Temu among others.

### Why does my UniUni tracking show no updates?

Usually the seller hasn't given UniUni the parcel yet. UniUni's estimated date appears only once it physically has the parcel, so ask the seller first.
