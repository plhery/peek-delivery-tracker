Venipak is a courier that delivers parcels for shops in Lithuania, Latvia and Estonia, to your door, to one of its parcel lockers or to a pickup point. Enter the number on Venipak's tracking page for your country, or paste it in the box above. It comes from the shop, or, in Lithuania, from Venipak by SMS.

## Where to find your Venipak tracking number

Venipak's Lithuanian FAQ says it texts the parcel number the evening before delivery and again on the day; its Latvian pages mention only a morning SMS with the delivery window. You may also get the number from the sender, for example in the shop's shipping email.

Venipak's texts and calls only reach Lithuanian, Latvian or Estonian phone numbers: with a foreign one, ask the shop for the number. See [how to find your tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number) if nothing arrived.

Venipak's help pages give no format, but its labels follow one shape (digits invented):

| Number | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `V12345E1234567` | A Venipak parcel number: `V`, 5 digits, `E`, 7 digits. The 5 digits identify the sender's account, the last 7 the parcel. |
| `1234567` | A shipment number of 7 to 10 digits, which can cover several parcels. |

Venipak's form takes a "Shipment/parcel code", or a "Customer ID" with a "Document number", which only the sender has. [Baltic numbers of another shape](guide:tracking-number-formats) belong to someone else: `JJEE` codes and postal numbers ending `EE` are [Omniva's](carrier:omniva), postal numbers ending `LT` are [Lietuvos paštas's](carrier:lietuvos-pastas), and 14 digits are usually DPD's.

## Venipak parcel tracking statuses explained

Peek asks Venipak's tracking in English, so these are Venipak's English statuses; where a row has two, the second is the Lithuanian site's English wording. Each step reads as the status, then the place: a terminal in capitals such as `VILNIUS TM2`, or a Venipak locker with its name and town.

| Status | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| `At sender` | The shop has [registered the parcel](guide:tracking-statuses) and still has it. |
| `Is placed for dispatch` | The sender dropped it in a Venipak locker, named after the status. |
| `Picked up`, `Picked up from sender` | A courier collected it from the sender or the drop-off locker. |
| `At terminal` | It's at a Venipak sorting terminal, such as KAUNAS or VILNIUS TM2. |
| `Out for delivery`, `The parcel is out for delivery` | On its way to your door, locker or pickup point. |
| `Delivered to pickup point` | Ready for pickup: it's in the locker or at the pickup point named. The PIN comes by SMS, or a QR code by email. |
| `Delivered` | Handed over at your door, or picked up from the locker or pickup point named after it. |

Two wordings catch people out: `Delivered to pickup point` means the parcel is waiting and its storage time has started, and `Delivered` also covers a parcel picked up from a locker. `Wrong shipment ID` isn't a status: it's how Venipak's tracker refuses a number, so check it for typos.

Venipak publishes no list of statuses, and no status for a failed attempt or a return has turned up in its tracking. If the parcel sits `At terminal`, Venipak's advice is to wait for the SMS saying when and how it will arrive.

## Venipak lockers, pickup points and missed deliveries

**When it comes.** Venipak says about 98% of parcels arrive the next working day. Homes are served Monday to Friday, 8 am to 5 pm, and on the morning it's due an SMS gives a time window and the courier's number. Saturday deliveries happen only in Vilnius, Kaunas, Klaipėda, Šiauliai, Panevėžys and Alytus, if the sender ordered them.

**At the door.** The courier can hand the parcel to someone else at the address, such as a colleague or neighbour. Check the packaging with the courier there and note visible damage on the manifest: Venipak isn't responsible for damage not noted then.

**Not at home.** Venipak can leave the parcel at the nearest locker or pickup point: arrange it with the courier, or call or email customer service. With no choice made, it comes the next business day. Venipak's rules allow two more attempts after the first time nobody is found; after three, or if you refuse the parcel, it goes back to the sender.

**Changing the address.** Possible from the business day after the driver collected the parcel: email the new address to customer service or call, and it arrives a business day later. Venipak's Lithuanian FAQ allows a change on the delivery day itself too, with delivery moved to the next working day.

**Lockers.** An SMS comes the moment the parcel is placed inside. Pick it up within 3 to 5 calendar days (the date is in the SMS or email) with the PIN from the SMS or the QR code from the email. If your locker has a fault or no free compartment of the right size, the parcel goes to the nearest available one: check the message.

**Pickup points** are shops or service centres, open on weekdays and weekends, for parcels too big for lockers. Venipak gives 5 working days on one page and 5 calendar days on another: go by your SMS. Storage can't be extended, and an uncollected parcel goes back to the sender.

**Returns.** The sender sets the conditions: hand the parcel to a courier or drop it at a Venipak locker or pickup point with the return sticker.

## Venipak parcel late, lost or damaged

The shop holds the contract with Venipak, so a lost, damaged or wrong item goes through the sender: Venipak's claims FAQ says the recipient "must contact the Sender".

**No update for days.** Wait for Venipak's SMS; if none comes, ask the shop. [Tracking that isn't updating](guide:tracking-not-updating) explains the usual reasons.

**Delivered but not there.** Look at the place after `Delivered`: it may name a locker or pickup point. Then contact the shop and Venipak, and see [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).

**Damaged or lost.** The sender claims, with photos of the goods and outer packaging and the invoice for damage, or the invoice and any photos for loss. Venipak's rules set a six-month deadline from dispatch for a written claim, and its English FAQ says local claims are handled within 14 business days (14 calendar days in the Lithuanian one), international ones within up to 90 calendar days.

## Tracking a Venipak parcel with Peek

[Peek](/) names a number like `V12345E1234567` as Venipak's straight away; no other carrier shares the shape, and lowercase or a space before the `E` is fine. Tracking links on venipak.com or venipak.lt with the number in them work too; from a venipak.lv link Peek reads only a `V…E…` parcel number.

A 7 to 10-digit shipment number is never named Venipak: pick Venipak with "Change", or paste a venipak.com or venipak.lt link. A one-parcel shipment is then followed by its parcel number; for several parcels, add each parcel number.

Venipak's scans carry no time zone: Peek reads them on the clock of the scan's place, the same in all three Baltic countries, and lists Venipak's own history. It checks every 10 minutes by day, every 2 minutes once the parcel is out for delivery, and hourly overnight. It can show the locker or pickup point where the parcel waits, with its town. Statuses keep Venipak's English wording, except `Delivered` and `Out for delivery`, shown in Peek's own words. You can be notified when the status changes.

Venipak gives no expected arrival day and no weight. Peek leaves out map coordinates, the parcel count and the recipient check, and can't contact Venipak for you.

## Questions about Venipak tracking

### Can I track a Venipak parcel without the tracking number?

Not on your own: Venipak's form also takes a customer ID with a document number, but only the sender has those. Ask the shop, or, in Lithuania, wait for Venipak's SMS the evening before.

### Can I change the address for a Venipak parcel?

Yes, from the business day after the driver collected it, or even on the delivery day according to Venipak's Lithuanian FAQ. Email the new address to customer service or call, and it arrives one business day later.

### Does Venipak deliver the next day?

Usually: Venipak says about 98% of parcels arrive the next working day. Homes are served Monday to Friday; Saturdays only in six Lithuanian cities, when the sender ordered it.
