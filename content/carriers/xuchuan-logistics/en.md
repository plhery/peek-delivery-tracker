Xuchuan International Logistics (旭川国际物流) is a freight forwarder in Xiamen, in China's Fujian province. Its website, xcwl.ltd, offers logistics for online sellers who ship abroad, as well as air and sea freight. Xuchuan has no tracker of its own: the tracking box on its site shows results from 17TRACK, a tracking service. Paste your number in the box above, or ask the shop that sent your order.

## Xuchuan tracking number: what it looks like

Xuchuan publishes no number format. Peek's catalog links one shape to Xuchuan: XC, ten digits and CN, 14 characters in all, like the invented `XC0000000000CN`. It's only a weak match: the shape alone doesn't prove the number is Xuchuan's. No other carrier in Peek's list uses it.

| Number | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `XC0000000000CN` | XC, ten digits, CN: the shape Peek links to Xuchuan. |
| `XC000000005CN` | Two letters, nine digits, CN: the shape of a [postal number](guide:tracking-number-formats) from China. Peek reads it as [China Post](carrier:china-post) if its check digit is right. Count the digits again. |
| `XC00000000000CN` | Eleven digits: no shape Peek knows. Check for an extra digit. |

Spaces, dashes and lower case don't matter when you paste the number into Peek.

**Where to find it.** Xuchuan's site doesn't say how the number reaches you. If a shop sent your order with Xuchuan, look in the shop's shipping email or on your order page. If you can't find it, see [how to find your tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number).

**A number of another shape.** If the shop gave you a number that doesn't start with XC, it may belong to another carrier, such as a post or courier in your country. [Tracking number formats](guide:tracking-number-formats) helps you tell which.

## Xuchuan tracking statuses

Xuchuan publishes no list of statuses, and Peek's catalog holds no Xuchuan wording. With no tracker of its own, the status lines you see come from the tracking service that answered, in that service's words. They are not Xuchuan's words, and they can lag behind the carrier that has the parcel in its hands.

Peek adds a few lines of its own while it waits for a first scan: see "Track a Xuchuan parcel with Peek" below. For the quiet spells and handovers that are usual on parcels from China, see [tracking a parcel from China](guide:tracking-from-china), and for what common status lines mean, [tracking statuses explained](guide:tracking-statuses).

## Who delivers Xuchuan parcels

Xuchuan's site doesn't name the companies that deliver for it, or the countries it ships to. It lists:

- air, sea and multimodal freight;
- supply-chain services, from buying to distribution;
- logistics for online sellers shipping abroad: warehousing, order handling, last-mile delivery and returns;
- project and heavy cargo, and warehousing and distribution.

It says it handles the last mile for online sellers, but not who does it in your country. If the tracking history names a post or courier in your country, with a number of its own, that company delivers. Use its own tracking to rearrange a delivery or find the pickup point where the parcel waits. Its rules for delivery attempts, notice cards and held parcels apply, not Xuchuan's.

**Customs.** Xuchuan's site says nothing about duties or taxes, or about who pays them. In the EU, online orders from outside the EU pay a €3 customs duty per item on parcels worth up to €150 (since 1 July 2026), and a €2 Union handling fee per item, whatever the value (since 1 November 2026). Xuchuan doesn't say whether the shop paid them for your order. If a carrier asks you to pay before delivery, or your parcel is held, see [parcels held at customs](guide:customs). Outside the EU, your own country's customs rules apply.

## Xuchuan parcel late, stuck or lost

**No update.** Xuchuan's site gives no delivery times, so it promises no date. If the history names a carrier in your country with its own number, check that carrier's tracking: it can be ahead of the tracking services. More in [why tracking stops updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

**Lost, damaged or returned.** Xuchuan publishes no rules for claims, delays or returns. If a shop sent your order, ask the shop for a refund or a new parcel: it booked the shipment, not you. If you bought on a marketplace, its order guide explains how to ask: [AliExpress](guide:aliexpress-orders), [Temu](guide:temu-orders), [Shein](guide:shein-orders). See also [lost parcels](guide:lost-parcel) and [returned to sender](guide:returned-to-sender).

**Delivered but not there.** Start with the carrier that delivered it, named in the history. See [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).

## Xuchuan contact and customer service

As read on 14 November 2026, xcwl.ltd, in Chinese only, lists:

- **Phone**: one Chinese mobile number, given as the customer service hotline. One part of the page says it answers 24/7; the footer says Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00. No time zone is given.
- **Chat**: a WeChat ID and a QQ number for customer service. The boxes that say to scan a QR code show only an icon, no code. QQ is online from 9:00 to 21:00.
- **Email**: one address at qq.com.
- **Address**: Jimei District, Xiamen, Fujian, China.

The site's language menu lists English, but the page stays in Chinese. Xuchuan lists no office or contact for recipients abroad. If a shop sent your parcel, ask the shop first; for the delivery itself, ask the carrier that delivers it.

## Track a Xuchuan parcel with Peek

[Peek](/) can't tell a Xuchuan parcel by its number alone. The XC shape above is only a weak match, so Peek doesn't name Xuchuan when you add one. Paste it all the same: Peek follows more than 3,500 carriers. While you add it, Peek asks tracking services ("Checking tracking services…"). If none of them knows the number yet, the carrier list says "Not found by tracking services yet. Peek will keep checking after you add it."

To follow the parcel as Xuchuan's, press "Change": Xuchuan International Logistics is listed under "Fits this number", and typing "Xuchuan", "旭川" or "xcwl" finds it too. If you leave the carrier to Peek and the services name Xuchuan as the carrier, with scans showing the parcel moving, Peek names Xuchuan for you.

Xuchuan has no tracker for Peek to read. So Peek asks ParcelsApp, Ship24 and 17TRACK in turn and keeps the first one that has the parcel. It shows the status and history that service holds, in its wording. That history can lag behind the carrier's own, and for some parcels there's nothing at all. The parcel's tracking link opens that service's page for the number. Peek doesn't pass your IP address or country on to the services.

Until a service knows the number, the parcel shows "No tracking history yet", or "Waiting for the carrier" once you've picked Xuchuan. Once the parcel is filed under Xuchuan, a service may name the carrier that took it over, with that carrier's own number. If Peek can read that carrier's own tracking, it asks there too. Once that carrier has a scan at least as new as the newest in the history, Peek follows the parcel there and keeps the earlier scans. The parcel shows "Delivery with" and that carrier's name; while that carrier has only been told the parcel is coming, it shows "Announced to …, not received yet".

While a service has the parcel, Peek checks every 15 minutes, out for delivery too. A service that didn't know the number is asked again a day later. After two days without a new scan, Peek checks hourly. It can notify you of "Every scan", "Important steps" or "Delivery only". It can't contact Xuchuan or the shop for you.

## Questions about Xuchuan tracking

### What is Xuchuan International Logistics?

A freight forwarder in Xiamen, Fujian, called 旭川国际物流 in Chinese, with the website xcwl.ltd. Its site, in Chinese, offers air, sea and multimodal freight, warehousing, and logistics for online sellers shipping abroad. In Peek's carrier list, it's also found as Xuchuan Logistics, XC and xcwl.ltd Logistics.

### Does Xuchuan have a tracking page?

Not one of its own. The tracking box on xcwl.ltd runs 17TRACK's tracking widget and shows 17TRACK's result. You can paste the number into Peek instead: it asks ParcelsApp, Ship24 and 17TRACK.

### What does a Xuchuan tracking number look like?

The shape Peek links to Xuchuan is XC, ten digits and CN, like the invented `XC0000000000CN`. Xuchuan publishes no format itself, so the shape is only a weak match, not proof. With nine digits, it's the shape of a China Post number instead.

### Who delivers Xuchuan parcels?

Xuchuan doesn't say. Its site mentions last-mile delivery for online sellers but names no carrier and no country. If the tracking history names a post or courier in your country with its own number, that company delivers.

### How do I contact Xuchuan?

Through xcwl.ltd, in Chinese: a customer service mobile number, an email address at qq.com, and WeChat and QQ chat. If a shop sent your parcel, ask the shop first.
