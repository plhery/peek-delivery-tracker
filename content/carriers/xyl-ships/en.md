XYL-Ships (XYL-ships International Logistics, also seen as FXYL) is a Chinese cross-border logistics provider holding air transport sales agency qualifications recognised by the Civil Aviation Administration of China (CAAC) and IATA. Alongside commercial air, sea, rail and road freight, it moves cross-border single-item e-commerce small parcels from China to international destinations and hands them over to local postal or courier partners for final delivery. You can look up a parcel via the "Track Order" option on [xylships.com](https://xylships.com/), or paste your tracking number into the box above.

## XYL-Ships tracking numbers: NUDE and XLWL formats

Check your online store's dispatch email or order-history page for the tracking number. XYL-Ships small parcels commonly carry a 16- or 17-character alphanumeric code starting with `NUDE` or `XLWL` and ending in `EX` or `EXP`:

| Format (invented) | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `NUDE4829173640EX` | `NUDE`, 10 digits and `EX`. |
| `XLWL4829173640EX` | `XLWL`, 10 digits and `EX`. |
| `XLWL4829173640EXP` | `XLWL`, 10 digits and `EXP`. |

Because these codes are distinctive to XYL-Ships, they do not collide with national postal barcodes. If your shop instead gave you an S10 international postal code (two letters, nine digits and two country letters such as `CN`) or a courier-specific code, check [tracking number formats](guide:tracking-number-formats) to see which carrier owns it.

## What XYL-Ships tracking statuses mean

XYL-Ships publishes its tracking milestones in English, following a parcel from origin sorting in China through linehaul transport and into the destination country's local distribution partner:

| XYL-Ships says | What it means for you | Peek shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Shipment inbound in sorting center` | XYL-Ships received and scanned the parcel at its origin sorting centre. | "In transit" |
| `Departed from sorting center`, `Arrived at sorting warehouse`, `Leaving warehouse` | Moving through origin sorting warehouses before linehaul handover. | "In transit" |
| `Hand over to transportation company`, `Departed from origin transportation center`, `Arrived at destination transportation center` | Handed to the international linehaul carrier, departed China and reached the destination country's intake centre. | "In transit" |
| `Released and the package is being transferred`, `Package is being transferred locally` | Cleared into the destination country and moving into local domestic transfer. | "In transit" |
| `Transfer again, send to the transportation partner department`, `Arrival at transportation partner department, waiting for processing`, `Leaving transportation partner department, sent to the transit hub` | Transferred to the local delivery partner's processing department and dispatched toward its transit hub. | "In transit" |
| `Arriving at the transit hub, waiting for distribution`, `Leave the transit hub, transport to the destination city`, `Arriving at the destination city, about to distribute to the post office` | Sorted at the regional transit hub and transported to your destination city for local handoff. | "In transit" |
| `The courier has received this parcel for delivery and will set a delivery time shortly` | The local delivery courier has the parcel, though the delivery round has not started yet. | "In transit" |
| `Parcel currently being delivered` | Out on the local courier's delivery round today. | "Out for delivery" |
| `Delivered` | Recorded as delivered at your destination. | "Delivered" |

Notice two easy-to-misread lines near the end of the journey. `Arriving at the destination city, about to distribute to the post office` means the parcel is being handed into the local postal or partner network, not that it is waiting for you to walk into a post office counter. Similarly, `The courier has received this parcel for delivery and will set a delivery time shortly` still sits in "In transit" because no delivery round has begun yet — wait for `Parcel currently being delivered` to expect arrival that day.

## How XYL-Ships cross-border delivery works

On [xylships.com](https://xylships.com/), XYL-Ships presents commercial air freight (including domestic 6-hour and 12-hour premium air freight and FBA door-to-door consolidation), ocean container consolidation, rail and road haulage, plus cross-border single-item e-commerce parcel handling — citing a single-item small-parcel customer case with 99% completion within 20 days.

**Local final-mile delivery.** As XYL-Ships' own tracking lines show, once a parcel reaches the destination country it is transferred to a local transportation partner or post office for final delivery.

**Delivery rules and missing details.** `xylships.com` does not publish recipient delivery-attempt counts, parcel-locker rules, holding deadlines, transit guarantees or claims windows. Once the parcel enters your country, actual doorstep procedures (leaving with a neighbour, safe-place drop-off or pickup-card holding days) depend on whichever local postal or courier partner completes delivery.

**Import duties and customs.** Because XYL-Ships shipments originate in China, parcels entering the EU, UK, US or other regions are subject to your country's import VAT and customs rules. For shipments entering the EU, remember that a €3 customs duty per kind of product on parcels up to €150 applies, and a €2 EU handling fee per kind of product whatever the value. See [parcels held at customs](guide:customs) for how destination customs checks affect delivery.

## XYL-Ships parcel stuck, quiet or not received

When you bought an item from an online store shipped via XYL-Ships, the seller is your contract partner and primary contact for delays, refunds or replacement orders.

**Long silence during transit.** Multi-day gaps are common between `Departed from origin transportation center` and `Arrived at destination transportation center` while parcels wait for flights, customs clearance and destination intake scans. See [why tracking stops updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

**No local tracking number in Peek.** Peek follows the parcel through the local partner under your `NUDE` or `XLWL` number and shows no separate local reference. If a delivery fails and you need the local partner's name or number, ask the seller.

**Marked `Delivered` but nowhere to be found.** Check your letterbox, porch, building reception and neighbours first. If it still does not turn up, report it to the seller immediately and ask for proof of delivery. See [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).

**Damaged, lost or returned.** Keep photos of damaged packaging and contact the store straight away, or open a payment/marketplace buyer-protection dispute before your order window closes. See [damaged parcel](guide:damaged-parcel) and [lost parcel](guide:lost-parcel) and [returned to sender](guide:returned-to-sender).

## Contacting XYL-Ships (and why to ask the seller first)

`xylships.com` is an enterprise freight-forwarding site with minimal consumer support details:

- **Email:** `fzxyl9527@163.com` (the sole contact listed in the footer of [xylships.com](https://xylships.com/)).
- **Phone, street address and claims portal:** none published on `xylships.com`.

Because XYL-Ships works with business shippers rather than individual retail recipients, contact the online store or marketplace first for missing parcels, address mistakes or refunds — the merchant has direct access to their freight account and buyer-protection processes.

> Treat unexpected SMS or email demands asking you to click a payment link to "unlock" or "reschedule" an XYL-Ships parcel with suspicion. Confirm any genuine customs payment directly with your country's postal service or customs authority, never through an unverified text message. See [delivery scam texts](guide:delivery-scam-texts).

## Track an XYL-Ships parcel with Peek

[Peek](/) recognises `NUDE` (`NUDE` + 10 digits + `EX`) and `XLWL` (`XLWL` + 10 digits + `EX` or `EXP`) tracking numbers as XYL-Ships automatically, with high confidence. Because XYL-Ships does not use carrier-specific tracking URLs in Peek, paste the tracking number itself rather than a link; if you ever need to pick the carrier manually, tap "Change" while adding or "Change carrier" on the parcel screen and choose XYL-Ships (searching "FXYL" finds it too).

Peek reads XYL-Ships' JSON tracking feed directly and shows the stage ("In transit", "Out for delivery" or "Delivered"), XYL-Ships' English scan descriptions, the scan location when the feed includes one, and timestamps carrying explicit time-zone offsets (displayed in your device's time zone). Peek drops sender and recipient address fields and internal customer references. It shows no expected date, weight, local carrier's number or delivery photo.

While you have the parcel open in Peek, or on its delivery day with notifications on, Peek checks up to every 10 minutes; on the day XYL-Ships shows `Parcel currently being delivered`, every 2 minutes from 6 a.m. on the clock of XYL-Ships' newest scan, while XYL-Ships' own tracker answers. Otherwise Peek checks hourly. After 12 hours with only "Label created" or "Received by carrier", or two days without news, it checks hourly however you follow it. When XYL-Ships' tracker doesn't answer and Peek follows the parcel through tracking services alone, it checks hourly, every 3 hours after a quiet day, and up to every 15 minutes on the out-for-delivery day.

When the newest scan is four days old and the parcel is still at "Label created", "Received by carrier" or "In transit", Peek flags it with that scan's day, such as "No tracking update since Mon 12 Oct". The parcel's page then says "Parcels often go quiet on long flights, at customs or over a weekend. Peek keeps asking XYL-Ships." After three weeks with no new scan, it says "Nothing new for over three weeks. If it still hasn’t arrived, ask the seller. Peek keeps asking XYL-Ships."

You can choose alert presets for "Every scan", "Important steps" or "Delivery only". Peek monitors tracking only and can't name the local carrier or contact XYL-Ships for you.

## Questions about XYL-Ships tracking

### What is XYL-Ships (FXYL)?

XYL-Ships International Logistics is a Chinese freight forwarder and cross-border e-commerce shipper with CAAC and IATA air-agency qualifications. Merchants use it to move small parcels out of China before handing them to local carriers in your country.

### What do `NUDE...EX` and `XLWL...EX` tracking numbers mean?

Both prefixes are XYL-Ships tracking formats: `NUDE` or `XLWL` followed by 10 digits and `EX` (or `EXP` on some `XLWL` parcels). Paste the full code into Peek or into the Track Order box on [xylships.com](https://xylships.com/).

### Why does my XYL-Ships tracking say `about to distribute to the post office`?

It means the parcel has arrived in your destination city and is being handed over to the local postal or delivery partner for final-mile delivery. Wait for `Parcel currently being delivered` before expecting delivery that day.

### Who delivers XYL-Ships parcels in my country?

XYL-Ships transfers parcels to a local transportation partner or postal service after destination intake, and its tracking lines don't name that local carrier; Peek shows no second tracking number. If delivery fails, ask the seller for the local carrier's details.
