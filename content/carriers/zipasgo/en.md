ZipasGo is a Lithuanian parcel service that ships to every country in the world. Senders book on its platform, compare offers from several couriers and pick one; ZipasGo's own courier or a partner collects the parcel. Its earlier name was Parcel Stars, which is how eBay lists it. ZipasGo carries the whole journey, but the last part, delivery to your door, may be done by a partner courier or post, such as UPS, DHL, DPD, Royal Mail or Lietuvos paštas. Track the parcel on [zipasgo.com, under "Where is my shipment?"](https://zipasgo.com/en), or paste the number into the box above.

## ZipasGo tracking number

ZipasGo doesn't publish a number format, and Peek knows no number shape for it. You may get one of two numbers from the sender:

| What you have | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| ZipasGo's internal number | ZipasGo's own number. It tracks the parcel from its first scan, when ZipasGo takes it over. |
| A number from UPS, DHL, DPD, Royal Mail or another partner | The partner's number, for the part of the journey it carries. Track it with that carrier too. |
| "Parcel Stars" as the carrier on eBay | Parcel Stars is ZipasGo's earlier name, and the number is ZipasGo's. |

ZipasGo's FAQ says its internal number passes the pickup and departure steps on to eBay. So if you bought on eBay and the order names Parcel Stars, it's a ZipasGo parcel. If you can't find a number, ask the seller; see also [how to find your tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number).

If the number has the shape of a big carrier's, such as UPS's `1Z` numbers, that carrier delivers the parcel. [Tracking number formats](guide:tracking-number-formats) helps you tell which.

## ZipasGo tracking statuses

ZipasGo publishes no list of statuses. Its FAQ says senders see "In transit" (sent) and "Delivered" in their ZipasGo account. The tracker on zipasgo.com shows the parcel's steps once you enter the number.

Peek can't read ZipasGo's tracker. It shows the history a tracking service holds, in that service's words: see "Track a ZipasGo parcel with Peek" below. For what common status lines mean, see [tracking statuses explained](guide:tracking-statuses).

## Who delivers ZipasGo parcels

ZipasGo's terms say it acts as the carrier, not a go-between. It collects the parcel, sorts it, carries it abroad and makes sure it's delivered. Depending on the destination and the service, final delivery can be done by a partner: another courier or postal service working for ZipasGo. Its site lists Post NL, Poczta Polska, Royal Mail, Evri, Parcelforce, UPS, DHL, DPD, GLS, BRT, Lietuvos paštas, Deutsche Post, InPost, CTT Express, Colissimo, USPS, Venipak, Asendia, Fastway, Omniva, Smartposti and Spring.

If the tracking names one of them with a number of its own, that company delivers your parcel. Use its own tracking to rearrange a delivery or find the pickup point where the parcel waits. Its rules for delivery attempts, notice cards and held parcels apply.

ZipasGo lists parcel consolidation and returns centres in Vilnius, Kaunas, Riga, Tallinn, Warsaw, Berlin, Bonn, London, New York and Hong Kong. Senders can hand in parcels at its warehouses in Vilnius and Kaunas.

**Cash on delivery.** In Poland, Lithuania, Latvia and Estonia, the courier can collect a sum in cash or by card on delivery, if the sender ordered it.

**Delivery times.** The terms say the times shown on the site are a guide, not a promise. Weather, customs, security checks, public holidays and strikes can delay a parcel. If the courier can't reach you, or the address is wrong, ZipasGo may charge its client, the sender, for another attempt, storage or return.

**Customs.** For parcels to countries outside the EU, ZipasGo asks the sender for a description, an HS code, the value, the quantity and the weight. Its terms say import duties and VAT aren't part of the carriage price, and that the sender takes full responsibility for paying import duties, taxes and charges. If a carrier asks you to pay before delivery, ask the seller first. See [parcels held at customs](guide:customs).

## ZipasGo parcel late, damaged or lost

**No update.** If the history names a partner carrier with its own number, check that carrier's tracking: it can be ahead. Otherwise ask the seller, who booked the shipment. More in [why tracking stops updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

**Damaged.** Check the parcel when you receive it. If you see damage, a shortage or anything else wrong, mark it at delivery and, if you can, have it written in a report. ZipasGo's terms ask for claims in writing within 3 calendar days of delivery, with photos of the item and packaging, documents and proof of value. Its FAQ says the sender claims after the recipient has told them about the problem. So tell the seller at once, with your photos.

**Lost.** The sender claims for a lost parcel too. ZipasGo pays the cost price of the goods, up to the limit of the service the sender chose. See [lost parcels](guide:lost-parcel) and [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).

**Disagreements.** ZipasGo's terms follow Lithuanian law: disputes are settled by talks first, then in Lithuanian courts.

## ZipasGo contact and customer service

As read on 14 November 2026, zipasgo.com lists:

- **Email**: help@zipasgo.com.
- **Company**: the service is provided by Zipas, UAB or MB ZipasGo, MB, depending on which one the client uses.

Its pages show no phone number or office address for recipients. Give the tracking number when you write. If a seller sent your parcel, ask the seller first: the seller is ZipasGo's client and the one who can claim. For the delivery itself, ask the partner carrier named in the history.

## Track a ZipasGo parcel with Peek

[Peek](/) can't tell a ZipasGo parcel by its number alone, because Peek knows no number shape for it. Paste it all the same: Peek follows more than 3,500 carriers. If the number has a partner's shape, such as UPS's, Peek may name that carrier.

To follow the parcel as ZipasGo's, press "Change" and type "ZipasGo" in "Search carriers". "Parcel Stars" and "Zipas" find it too. It isn't listed under "Fits this number". On a parcel you've already added, press "Change carrier".

You can also paste a link from ZipasGo's Lithuanian tracker, which ends in `/lt/siuntimas?tid=` and the number. Peek then reads the number and picks ZipasGo ("Detected carrier"). A link from the English tracker, `/en/tracking`, gives Peek nothing: copy the number itself.

ZipasGo has no tracker Peek can read. So Peek asks ParcelsApp, Ship24 and 17TRACK in turn and keeps the first one that has the parcel. It shows the status and history that service holds, in its wording. That history can lag behind ZipasGo's own, and for some parcels there's nothing at all. If you left the carrier to Peek and the services name ZipasGo or Parcel Stars, with scans that show the parcel moving, Peek names ZipasGo for you. Until a service has the parcel, Peek's link opens ZipasGo's tracker with your number; after that, it opens that service's page. Peek doesn't pass your IP address or country on to the services.

Until a service knows the number, a parcel filed under ZipasGo shows "Waiting for the carrier". Once the parcel is filed under ZipasGo, a service may name the carrier that took it over, with that carrier's own number. If Peek can read that carrier's own tracking, it asks there too. Once that carrier has a scan at least as new as the newest in the history, Peek follows the parcel there and keeps the earlier scans. The parcel shows "Delivery with" and that carrier's name; while that carrier has only been told the parcel is coming, it shows "Announced to …, not received yet".

While a service has the parcel, Peek checks every 15 minutes, out for delivery too. A service that didn't know the number is asked again a day later. After two days without a new scan, Peek checks hourly. It can notify you of "Every scan", "Important steps" or "Delivery only". It can't contact ZipasGo or the seller for you.

## Questions about ZipasGo tracking

### Is Parcel Stars the same as ZipasGo?

Yes. ZipasGo's FAQ calls Parcel Stars its earlier name, and eBay lists it under that name. Peek finds ZipasGo when you search "Parcel Stars".

### Who delivers my ZipasGo parcel?

ZipasGo carries it, but the final delivery may be done by a partner courier or post, such as UPS, DHL, DPD, Royal Mail or Lietuvos paštas. If the tracking names one with its own number, that company delivers.

### Can I track a ZipasGo parcel in English?

Yes: zipasgo.com/en has a "Where is my shipment?" box. Peek can't read a link from that tracker, so paste the number into Peek, not the link.

### How long do I have to report a damaged ZipasGo parcel?

ZipasGo's terms ask for a written claim within 3 calendar days of delivery, with photos and documents. Mark the damage at delivery and tell the seller at once: the seller makes the claim.

### How do I contact ZipasGo?

By email at help@zipasgo.com. Its pages show no phone number. If a seller sent the parcel, ask the seller first.
