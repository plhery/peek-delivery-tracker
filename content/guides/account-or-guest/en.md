You don't need an account to track a parcel on [Peek](/): look it up, follow it, turn on its notifications and share it, with no email address and no password. Sign in when you want your parcels kept together on the website, iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android, or the extras an account adds, such as delivery emails and adding parcels by email.

Here is everything that changes when you sign in:

| | Without an account | With an account |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where your parcels are | On the device that looked them up ("Kept in this browser only." on the website) | On the website, iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android, wherever you sign in |
| How long a parcel stays | 30 days after delivery or return, or 90 days without news | Until you delete it or the account; archiving only hides it |
| How many parcels | 100 in a browser's list | 50 active and 500 in all |
| New parcels a day | On the website, a few lookups a day for each network, then "No lookups left for today" | A daily allowance for adding parcels |
| [Notifications](guide:parcel-notifications) | On the website, "Notify me" on a parcel's page, then every parcel in that browser if you like. In the apps, Settings › "Notifications" sets which updates every parcel on the device gets, and a parcel's bell turns its own off | Settings › "Notifications" covers every parcel, and can email you when one arrives |
| [Add parcels by email](guide:add-parcels-by-email), [AI assistants](guide:ai-assistants) | Not offered | Settings › "Add parcels by email" (Beta); "Assistants" (Beta), on the website only |
| [Passport and Friends](guide:delivery-passport) | Website: no tabs, but a delivered parcel from abroad offers "Start a passport". Apps: both tabs; Passport counts the parcels on that device, Friends asks you to sign in | Both tabs, on the website and in every app |
| Settings | Website: the gear button "Settings" holds "Preferences", with "Language" on a parcel's page; on the front page, the language is in the menu at its foot. Apps: "Deliveries" (with "Notifications"), "Preferences" and "On this device" | "Deliveries", "Preferences" (with "Language") and "Account & data". On the website, only "Dot on updated parcels" and "Peek AI" follow you to your other devices |
| Your data | "Forget it now" on a parcel's page or "Forget all"; the apps also have "Download my data" | "Download my data", "Sign out", "Delete account" |

Signing in takes a minute, with a code Peek emails you:

:::steps
- Choose "Sign in" | On the website's front page or at the top of a parcel's page; in the apps, in Settings or on the Friends tab.
- Ask for a code | Choose "Sign in with email", type your "Email address" and choose "Email me a code".
- Type the code | Under "Enter the six-digit code sent to" your address, type the code, then choose "View my parcels".
:::

## Do I need an account to track a parcel?

No. Without one, Peek stores no account and no email address: only the tracking number, the carrier, any postcode, tracking link or phone digits the carrier needs, the tracking history and a link to the parcel.

A name you give the parcel stays on your device and never reaches Peek, unless you share the parcel as a gift (see [sharing a parcel](guide:share-parcel)). Only the device that looked the parcel up sees its full number, can forget it and can keep it in an account later. On the website, your parcels are listed under "On this device", whose download button saves the list as a file.

An account costs nothing: Peek shows no ads and sells no data. To see the signed-in screens first, Passport and Friends included, open the [demo](https://peektracker.com/demo) with its sample parcels.

## How long does Peek keep a parcel I track without an account?

Peek forgets it 30 days after it's delivered or returned, 90 days after its last news (a scan, or someone opening its link), or at once when you ask from the device that looked it up. The foot of the parcel's page says when: "Peek forgets this parcel 30 days after delivery." or "Peek forgets this parcel on" a date. Your browser then keeps its last copy, marked as no longer updated, until you remove it.

To forget a parcel now on the website, choose "Forget it now" at the foot of its page: "The link stops working and the number is deleted. Nothing to sign out of." In the apps, Settings › "On this device" › "Forget all" forgets every parcel on that device.

## How do I open my parcels on another phone or computer?

On the website, choose "Open them on another device" under your "On this device" list, then scan its code or send yourself its link:

:::steps
- Scan or send | "Scan this code with your other phone or computer, or send yourself the link."
- Open it there | The other device adds the parcels it has room for and says how many arrived. Open the link again for the rest.
:::

> "Anyone with this link can follow these parcels and forget them: keep it to yourself."

The apps don't open these links: on a phone, the link opens in the browser. To have the same parcels in the apps and on the website, sign in on each.

## What happens to my parcels when I sign in?

They come with you. On the website, a parcel's page offers "Sign in to keep it": the parcel joins your account with its name and history, and the copy on the device is forgotten.

Once you're signed in, the website asks "Bring these parcels too?" with a button such as "Add 4 parcels", for the parcels that browser looked up. If your account is full, the rest stay on the device: "Archive or delete a few, and Peek will offer them again."

The apps move their parcels into the account by themselves when you sign in; any a full account refuses stay on the device, and the list says how many. Signing out later clears the account's copy from that device.

## How do I sign in to Peek without a password?

Peek has no passwords: it emails you a six-digit code, which lasts 30 minutes. Not arrived? [Peek's help](https://peektracker.com/support.html) says to check your spam and the address, wait a minute and choose "Email me a code" again.

The website also offers "Continue with Google", which opens the same account as the email code for a Google address, and "Continue with Apple". The Android app never offers Apple.

## How do I delete my Peek account or my data?

Open "Settings", then "Account & data", and choose "Delete account" at the foot. Without an account, there is nothing to delete: forget your parcels, as above.

:::steps
- Open "Account & data" | The round account button with your initial opens "Settings" (on a Mac, the Settings window's "Account" tab). To keep a copy first, choose "Download my data".
- Choose "Delete account" | Peek asks "Delete this account permanently?": "All parcels, tracking history and notification settings will be erased."
- Confirm | Type your email address, then choose "Permanently delete".
:::

On the website, if you last signed in more than ten minutes ago, Peek first asks you to "Sign in again to delete your account".

Deleting also removes your shared links, notification sign-ups, delivery emails, forwarding address and its emails, connected assistants, and your Friends profile, connections and invitations. It can't remove what a carrier already received, and Peek's own logs keep a tracking number for up to 30 days and error reports for up to 90.

## Settings and preferences: theme, language and units

Everyone has "Preferences", with or without an account, on the website and in the apps; the table above shows where.

- **"Appearance"**: "System" (follows your device), "Light" or "Dark".
- **"Language"**: English, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, Brazilian Portuguese, Polish, Russian or Simplified Chinese.
- **"Time format"**: "24-hour", "12-hour" or Automatic.
- **"Distance"** and **"Weight"**: kilometres or miles, kilograms or pounds. Left on Automatic, they follow your device; pounds also give parcel sizes in inches.
- **"Dot on updated parcels"**: "A dot appears on a parcel with a new update since you last opened it."
- **"Peek AI"**: "When Peek has a strong hint, it says when it thinks a parcel will come." See [Peek AI's delivery estimate](guide:peek-ai).

To add Peek to an iPhone's Home Screen for notifications, see [parcel delivery notifications](guide:parcel-notifications).
