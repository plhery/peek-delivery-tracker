The delivery passport is the "Passport" tab in [Peek](/): it counts the parcels delivered to you and gives you a stamp for some of them, such as your first delivery or a parcel that crossed a border. It also shows your average and fastest delivery times, and with an account you can compare passports with friends while your parcels stay private.

Who gets what depends on where you use Peek:

| | Website | iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android apps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Passport" | Once you sign in | With or without an account; without one, it counts the parcels on that device |
| "Friends" | Once you sign in | With an account only: without one, the tab says "Share stamps and delivery milestones with friends." and offers "Sign in" |

On the website without an account, a delivered parcel that crossed a border shows "Across borders" and "Start a passport", which leads to signing in. How signing in works is in [Peek without an account](guide:account-or-guest).

:::steps
- Open "Passport" | It's the tab beside your deliveries.
- Tap what you want explained | The count, a stamp, a time or "First scanned in" each say how they're worked out.
- See every stamp | The grid shows the stamps you have and the next three you could earn; "All stamps" shows the rest.
:::

## What is the delivery passport in Peek?

It's a page that adds up your deliveries: a "Delivered" count, your "Stamps", your "Average delivery time" and "Fastest delivery", and your top three countries under "First scanned in".

"Every delivered parcel counts, archived ones included." The passport is worked out from the parcels in your list each time it opens, so a parcel you delete for good stops counting. "First scanned in" is where the carrier first scanned each parcel: "This may differ from the sender’s country."

## How do I earn each stamp?

Each of the 12 stamps has its own rule. Tap one to read it and see how far you are, such as 3 / 10; "Express arrival" shows "Within 48 hours".

| Stamp | How you earn it |
| --- | --- |
| "First arrival" | Your first delivered parcel |
| "Double digits" | Ten delivered parcels, archived ones included |
| "Well connected" | Parcels from three different carriers, those still on their way included |
| "Express arrival" | A parcel delivered within 48 hours of its first scan |
| "Across borders" | A delivered parcel scanned in two countries, at two different times |
| "Around the world" | Parcels first scanned in five different countries |
| "The regular" | 25 delivered parcels, archived ones included |
| "Right next door" | A parcel first scanned and delivered in the same country |
| "Worth the wait" | A parcel that took more than 30 days from first scan to delivery |
| "A busy doorstep" | Three parcels delivered on the same day |
| "Picked up" | A parcel you collected yourself from a pickup point or [locker](guide:parcel-lockers) |
| "Home for the holidays" | A parcel delivered in December |

A few rules keep the count fair:

- A carrier that repeats its delivery scan still counts the parcel once.
- The country stamps need a scan placed in a country: registration scans, two scans at the same instant, a bare state code and returns don't count.
- "A busy doorstep" and "Home for the holidays" follow your device's time zone in your own passport but UTC in what friends see, so the two can differ near midnight.

What counts as delivered is explained in [what tracking statuses mean](guide:tracking-statuses).

## How is my average delivery time worked out?

From the carrier's first scan to the delivery, never from when you added the parcel. A label-created (registration) step doesn't start the clock.

Tap a time to see how many journeys it comes from: "Counted from the first scan to delivery." The fastest one also names the parcel and its delivery date. Until one delivered parcel has both a first scan and a delivery time, the times say "Waiting for a complete journey". For the time of day each carrier delivers, see [what time parcels get delivered](guide:delivery-times).

## How do I add a friend on Peek?

Create a Friends profile, then send your friend an invitation link.

:::steps
- Create your profile | In "Friends", choose "Create profile", pick a nickname of up to 24 characters, check the preview and choose "Create profile".
- Choose what you share | "Stats & stamps" starts on and "Arrivals this week" off; the next section says what each shows.
- Invite | Choose "Invite a friend" (under "Add your first friend" at first). The link is "One use · expires in 7 days".
- Send it | Choose "Share invitation", where your device can share, or "Copy link".
:::

Whoever opens the link sees only your nickname; it gives no access to your account or parcels. Without an account, your friend is asked to "Sign in to accept the invitation", and the invitation waits through sign-in. Someone new to Friends chooses "Turn on Friends to accept", sets a nickname and switches, then "Turn on Friends & accept"; someone already in Friends chooses "Become friends". The page then says "Friendship delivered", and you're told they accepted. On an iPhone with Peek installed, the link opens in the app.

You can have 20 unused links at a time; past that, the oldest stops working. Under "Manage links", "Cancel this invitation" stops the current link, and another button cancels your older unused ones without touching your friends. A used, cancelled or expired link says "This invitation is no longer available. Ask your friend for a new link." A circle holds up to 50 friends.

## What can my friends see about my parcels?

Only the numbers you choose to share, never a parcel. Two switches decide:

- "Stats & stamps": your delivered count, your average delivery time in whole days (rounded up) and the stamps you've earned. Turned off, your card says "Stats kept private".
- "Arrivals this week": only "A parcel arrived this week", with no parcel and no exact time.

Friends never see parcel names or contents, tracking numbers, carriers, places, exact delivery times or parcels still on their way, nor your email, Google name or photo. Peek's server works the numbers out from your parcels, so no app can send made-up ones. There are no public profiles, no search, no contact upload, and no follower counts, leaderboards or feed.

To change what you share, choose "What you share" at the top of Friends, change the switches under "Sharing preferences", then "Save". Friends see the change at their next refresh, within about a minute while Friends is open, and their cards are never kept for offline use. Someone who already saw your card could have kept a screenshot, as Peek's [privacy notice](https://peektracker.com/privacy.html) points out.

You see your friends the same way: "Your circle" lists them, each card with a nickname and a stamp count, and tapping one opens that friend's passport.

## How do I remove a friend?

Open the friend's card, then "Manage friendship" › "Remove friend", and confirm with "Remove friend": "You’ll stop seeing each other’s Passports. You can reconnect with a new invitation." Either friend can remove the other, and it ends on both sides at once.

Deleting your account deletes your Friends profile, connections and invitations; [Peek without an account](guide:account-or-guest) explains how. "Download my data" includes your Friends profile and your friends' nicknames, never their stats.
