Douglas, the beauty and perfume retailer, doesn't sell online in the UK, Ireland, the US, Canada or Australia: its online shops are in continental Europe. In Germany, DHL delivers douglas.de orders in 1 to 3 working days, free from €34.95, and Douglas confirms the shipment by email. If a parcel is missing, damaged or leaking, contact Douglas customer service; in Germany, it also sends free return labels.

## Where does Douglas sell online?

Douglas's country list has no shop for the UK, Ireland, the US, Canada or Australia. It lists Germany, Austria, Switzerland, Belgium, the Netherlands, Italy, Spain, Portugal, Poland and ten more European countries, each with its own Douglas site. In France and Monaco, the group sells through Nocibé, which has its own online shop.

Under their terms, douglas.at delivers to Austrian addresses only, and douglas.ch only when both your delivery and billing addresses are in Switzerland.

The rest of this guide uses douglas.de and Douglas's German terms, with their German wording and a translation, and says where Austria and Switzerland differ.

## Who delivers my Douglas order?

Douglas's German terms name DHL for standard delivery:

| Delivery under the German terms | Who delivers | How long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Standardlieferung" (standard delivery) | [DHL](carrier:dhl) | 1–3 working days |
| "Expresslieferung" (express delivery) | Douglas's express carrier, which the terms don't name | The next working day if you order Monday to Friday before 14:00 |
| To a "DHL-Packstation" (a DHL parcel locker) | DHL | A pickup code in the Post & DHL app |

The terms also list delivery to a Douglas store ("Douglas-Filiale") or a post office ("Postfiliale"), when your checkout offers it. For a Packstation or a post office, DHL needs your PostNummer, your DHL customer number. Douglas doesn't deliver to PO boxes.

Some items are delivered by a Douglas partner rather than by Douglas itself.

**Austria.** Douglas's terms name DPD, [Austrian Post](carrier:austrian-post) and Atalogic, with delivery in 2 to 4 working days.

**Switzerland.** Douglas's terms don't name the carrier. Douglas doesn't deliver to PO boxes, post offices or parcel lockers, and its deliveries arrive with duty and tax already paid.

- warehouse | Douglas's warehouse | Partner items can ship from the partner
- truck | DHL in Germany | 1 to 3 working days
- locker | A DHL Packstation | Code in the DHL app
- home | You | Free from €34.95 in Germany

## Why is my Douglas order late, or why isn't tracking moving?

When nothing moves, it's usually one of these:

- **A split order.** Douglas can split an order into several parcels and pays the extra shipping itself, and a partner delivers its own items. One parcel can arrive days before another, so check each parcel's tracking.
- **Express after 14:00.** In Germany, an express order placed Monday to Thursday after 14:00 arrives 2 working days later; one placed on Friday after 14:00 arrives on Tuesday. With PayPal, these times can vary.
- **A public holiday.** In Germany, a holiday can push delivery back by up to 2 working days.
- **Another country's times.** Austria and Switzerland: 2 to 4 working days, and a public holiday there pushes delivery back by 2 working days.

When waiting is still normal: [tracking not updating](guide:tracking-not-updating) and [delivery times](guide:delivery-times).

## Douglas delivery options and costs

**Germany.** The order total is your subtotal after discounts, partner items included.

| Option | What it costs |
| --- | --- |
| Standard delivery (DHL) | Free from €34.95; €4.95 below that |
| Express delivery | €10.95, even when standard delivery would be free; German mainland only |

For a Packstation, give your PostNummer and the Packstation's number when you order. Douglas's terms say your payment options can be limited when the delivery address isn't your billing address.

For delivery to a Douglas store, you can also pay when you collect; the store then holds the order for up to 5 days.

**Austria and Switzerland.** Free delivery starts at a different amount in each shop:

| Shop | Free delivery | Below that |
| --- | --- | --- |
| douglas.at, by DPD | From €39.95 | €4.95 |
| douglas.at, by Austrian Post | From €69 | €4.50 |
| douglas.ch | From CHF 49.95 | CHF 6.50 |

## Douglas parcel damaged, missing or marked delivered: what to do

Look first, then report. In order:

:::steps
- Check the tracking | A split order and partner items can come in separate parcels, so check the tracking of each one before you report something missing.
- Look around and ask | Marked delivered but not there: check the Packstation, post office or store you chose, and ask neighbours and anyone at home whether they took it in.
- Contact Douglas | Germany: service@douglas.de or 0800 690 690 5 (free from German landlines). Austria: service@douglas.at or 0800 50 05 90. Switzerland: service@douglas.ch or 0840 00 33 44.
- Make a complaint | Douglas's terms say claims and complaints go to these contact details. Say what's missing or damaged, and keep the box and photos of a broken or leaking bottle. Switzerland: report wrong or missing items and defects in writing within 8 days of delivery.
- Refund or new item | For a faulty item, Douglas sends a replacement or fixes the fault, and it pays for the return. If that fails, you can cancel the purchase or get a price cut. In Germany and Switzerland, these claims run for 2 years from delivery.
:::

More: [parcel marked delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received), [lost parcel](guide:lost-parcel) and [damaged parcel](guide:damaged-parcel).

## Returning a Douglas order

**Germany.** You have 14 days from receiving the goods to withdraw; for a split order, from the last parcel. Tell Douglas by letter or email, or online with its withdrawal function (Douglas then confirms receipt by email), and send the items back within 14 days of telling it.

For the return, ask customer service at service@douglas.de or on 0800 690 690 5 for a prepaid return label: with it, the return is free; without it, you pay the postage.

**Refunds.** Douglas refunds within 14 days of your withdrawal, to the payment method you used, but can wait until it has the goods or your proof of sending them. If you paid partly with a Douglas gift card, that part comes back first, on a gift card sent to your order's email address, and the rest goes to your other payment method.

**Austria.** 14 days. The return sticker ("Retouren-Aufkleber") makes the return free; keep the post office receipt. Without it, you pay the postage. Any Austrian Douglas store also takes back items ordered on douglas.at, with the invoice. Douglas refunds within 14 days.

**Switzerland.** 14 days from receipt: write to Douglas, or simply send the goods back. The return is at your own cost unless the goods don't match your order, and you must be able to prove you sent it. If the return breaks an offer's conditions, its free gift goes back too. Douglas refunds within 30 days.

To follow a return: [returns tracking](guide:returns-tracking).

## Can I track a Douglas parcel in Peek?

Yes, with the carrier's tracking number: paste it into [Peek](/). Peek recognises these by their shape alone (made-up examples):

| Carrier | The number looks like |
| --- | --- |
| DHL (Germany) | Most 20-digit numbers starting with `00340434`, like `00340434610000000001` |
| Austrian Post | 2 letters, 9 digits and `AT`, like `RR000000005AT` |

DHL's 12-digit numbers, DPD's 14-digit numbers and Austrian Post's 22-digit numbers share their shape with other carriers' numbers. Peek asks the likely carriers and shows the one that knows the number. If several know it, press "Choose"; if it shows the wrong one, press "Change" and pick the right carrier. For Atalogic, a Swiss delivery or a partner's carrier, paste its number all the same: Peek follows more than 3,500 carriers. If your order comes in several parcels, add each number.

If a parcel changes carrier on the way and the first carrier names the one taking over, Peek keeps the history from the start and follows the new carrier. Peek shows what the carrier publishes. Claims, refunds and returns stay with Douglas.
