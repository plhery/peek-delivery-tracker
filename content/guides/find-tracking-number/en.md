You'll usually find your tracking number in the shop's shipping email or text, or in your order on its website or app, one click past the order number. If you sent the parcel yourself, it's on your post office receipt. No carrier will track a package by your name or address, but a [missed-delivery card](guide:missed-delivery), some carriers' free accounts for incoming parcels, or the sender can still get you tracking.

## Where do I find my tracking number?

[USPS](carrier:usps) puts it plainly: the number "is generated and provided to the sender". It usually reaches you through them, so look in this order:

:::steps
- Your inbox and texts | Search for "shipped", "dispatched", "tracking" or the shop's name, and check spam.
- The shop's order page | One click past the order number (table below).
- A missed-delivery card | Many carry a number the carrier's tracking accepts.
- The carrier's account | Some carriers list parcels heading to your address.
- The sender | "Only the sender can provide the tracking number," says [Canada Post](carrier:canada-post).
:::

Found the email but can't tell which number is which? Paste the whole thing into [Peek](/): it picks out the tracking number and the carrier.

### In your order on Amazon, eBay, Etsy and other shops

| Shop | Where the tracking number is |
| --- | --- |
| Amazon (US, UK) | **Returns & Orders** (app: **Your Orders**) → **Track Package**, where Amazon deliveries are tracked |
| eBay | **Purchase history**, as a link next to the item. As a guest: the "Order Confirmed" email → **View order details** → **Show delivery details** (US: **Show shipping details**) |
| Etsy | **Your account** → **Purchases and reviews** (app: **You** → **Purchases**). No tracking button? Look under **View Receipt** |
| Shein | **My orders** → the order → **Track** |
| Vinted | The order's conversation → **Track parcel**, where you can copy the number |

No number there? There may be none yet, or none at all: eBay shows a number only if the seller used a tracked service and uploaded it.

### On a missed-delivery card

- **USPS** PS Form 3849: search its barcode number on USPS.com "as you would use a tracking number".
- **Canada Post** Delivery Notice Card: 15 digits that serve "the same purpose as a tracking number".
- **[Evri](carrier:evri)** calling card: its 8-digit number works on Evri's tracking page.

## How to track a package without a tracking number

Three routes work, none of them by name or address.

### Ask the sender

Every carrier that writes about missing numbers sends you back to the sender. Ask for:

- **The carrier's name.** The wrong carrier's site won't find the number.
- **The number exactly as printed.**
- **Whether it was tracked at all.** USPS First-Class letters and flats without an extra service have no number, nor do some Marketing Mail parcels.

Bought on a marketplace? Ask through it. On Amazon UK, go to **Your Orders** → **View Your Item** → **Contact Seller**; sellers "should respond to your query within two business days". On eBay, ask the seller to upload the tracking number.

### Let the carrier list parcels coming to you

Several carriers list parcels heading your way once you prove the address, email or phone number is yours. Each shows only its own parcels, and only when the sender's details match yours.

| Where | Service | Good to know |
| --- | --- | --- |
| US | USPS Informed Delivery, free | USPS parcels to your address, by daily email and dashboard. Starts "within three business days", rarely up to 7. Some apartment addresses can't join; the email won't always list every parcel |
| UK | DPD app | [DPD](carrier:dpd-uk) and DPD Local parcels, matched to "your mobile phone number or email address and your delivery address" |
| Canada | Canada Post automatic tracking, free | Personal accounts only, no business or PO box addresses, ID checked. No guarantee every parcel is added |
| Australia | MyPost account, free | [Australia Post](carrier:australia-post) adds parcels "if we have the information required" |

We found nothing like this for Evri: there, you need the number. To follow parcels from every carrier in one list, see [universal parcel tracking](guide:universal-tracker).

### Track by the sender's reference

Some senders give a parcel their own reference number, and some carriers can search by it: Canada Post's Track takes the reference with the shipping date. Ask the sender for it, or look in their shipping confirmation email if you ordered online.

## Can you track a parcel with just a name or address?

No, not on a carrier's tracking page, and not by phone either. The carriers say so:

- **Canada Post**: "For privacy reasons, we don't keep records of shipments by address or name."
- **Australia Post**: name or address "can't be used to locate items".
- **USPS**: Post Offices and Care Center agents "do not have a way to find an item's tracking number".

The nearest thing is a carrier account like those above, once you've proved the address is yours. Parcel trackers can't either, Peek included: it needs a number to ask the carrier about.

> A website that offers to find every parcel sent to your name or address isn't using a carrier's tracking: no carrier offers that to the public. Don't give it your details.

## What if I lost my tracking number?

Waiting for a parcel? Search your inbox again or reopen the order; failing that, ask the sender to resend it.

Sent the parcel yourself? The number is on what the counter gave you. Keep that slip until the parcel arrives: USPS says its Post Offices and Care Center "do not have a way to recover lost tracking numbers", and Australia Post says a name or address "can't be used to locate items".

| Carrier | Where to look |
| --- | --- |
| USPS | Your sales or mailing receipt, your online label record (Click-N-Ship, eBay), or under the barcode on the parcel |
| Canada Post | The bottom of your tracking receipt; for US and international items, your copy of the label and receipt |
| Australia Post | Your counter receipt "in many cases"; on a prepaid satchel, the barcode and/or the removable sticker |

### Can I track a parcel I sent without a tracking number?

No: as the carriers above say, a name or address won't find it. If you paid for a tracked service, though, the number is on your receipt or label (table above).

## Is the order number the same as the tracking number?

No. DHL spells it out: an order number is assigned by your retailer, and it isn't a tracking number. The carrier's tracking number only exists once a label is printed.

:::journey
- shop | Order placed | The shop gives you an order number
- label | Label printed | The tracking number exists, but the carrier has no parcel yet
- truck | First scan | Tracking comes alive, often within a day or two
- home | Missed delivery | The card left behind often has a number that works too
:::

To tell them apart: a number labelled "Order" is the shop's own and won't work on the carrier's site, while a tracking number comes with a carrier's name or a link to its tracking page. `1Z999AA10123456784` is a UPS number; many postal items carry the international 13-character format, like `RR000000005CH`. [Tracking number formats](guide:tracking-number-formats) shows which carrier a number belongs to.

## Why does my tracking number show nothing?

Usually because it's new: the label exists, but the carrier hasn't scanned the parcel yet. Vinted allows up to 48 hours, and Evri can take a few days to receive a parcel. Each carrier's wait is in [why tracking stops updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

Past that wait, check the number itself:

- **Is it a tracking number?** An order number won't work on a carrier's site. A sender's reference works only where the carrier accepts one, as Canada Post's Track does with the shipping date.
- **Is it this carrier's?** Australia Post, for one, asks you to check that it's an Australia Post parcel.

Still nothing? Ask the sender or the shop. For each status, see [what tracking statuses mean](guide:tracking-statuses).
