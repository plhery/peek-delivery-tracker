Fnac sells online in France, Belgium, Switzerland, Spain and Portugal. In France, Fnac's app says you can follow your orders and parcels from your customer account at any time. Fnac Darty sends standard parcels to your home or a pickup point with carriers such as Colissimo, Chronopost or FedEx, or to a Fnac store for Click & Collect, and its Darty network delivers and installs bulky items such as large appliances and big TVs. Items from Fnac marketplace sellers come with the seller's carrier, under their own tracking number when the shipping is tracked.

## Where does Fnac sell online?

Fnac Darty lists these Fnac sites: fnac.com (France), fr.fnac.be and nl.fnac.be (Belgium, with a page for Luxembourg), fnac.ch (Switzerland), fnac.es (Spain) and fnac.pt (Portugal). There's no Fnac in the UK, the US, Germany, Austria or Poland; in Italy the group owns Unieuro, a chain under its own name.

The rest of this guide uses France as the worked example.

## Which carrier delivers my Fnac order?

It depends on the item and who sells it. Fnac Darty's universal registration document describes its own deliveries in France; marketplace sellers pick their own carrier:

| Delivery | Who delivers | Where to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A standard parcel | A parcel carrier such as [Colissimo](carrier:la-poste), [Chronopost](carrier:chronopost) or [FedEx](carrier:fedex) | Your home or a pickup point |
| Click & Collect | Fnac | A Fnac store |
| A bulky item (large appliance, big TV) | Darty's delivery network | Your home, delivered and installed |
| An item from a marketplace seller | The seller's carrier | Up to the seller |

Parcels leave Fnac Darty's warehouses, or come straight from a store when the item is in stock there.

On the Fnac Darty marketplace, more than 3,000 sellers are active, and each one ships itself. Fnac sets the shipping method by item price: up to €25 including VAT, you choose; from €25 to €200, it's tracked or signed for; above €200, it's signed for. Carriers Fnac offers its sellers include La Poste, Colissimo, Chronopost, [Colis Privé](carrier:colis-prive), [DPD](carrier:dpd-fr), [GLS](carrier:gls-fr), DHL, [UPS](carrier:ups), [bpost](carrier:bpost), PostNL, Asendia and YunExpress. Order from several sellers and you get several parcels, each with its own carrier and, when it's tracked, its own number.

:::journey
- warehouse | Fnac or a marketplace seller | Each seller sends its own parcel
- truck | Colissimo, Chronopost, FedEx, Darty or the seller's carrier | Tracking is in your Fnac account
- locker | A pickup point or a Fnac store | Click & Collect in store
- home | You | At home, installed for bulky items
:::

## Where is my Fnac tracking number?

Look in your Fnac customer account. Fnac's app says you can follow your orders and parcels there at any time, and the app's home page shows the pickup code for an item you collect in store. For a marketplace item, the seller ships under its own number when the shipping is tracked. Other shops: [find your tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number).

The carriers' numbers look like this:

| Carrier | What the number looks like |
| --- | --- |
| La Poste / Colissimo | 13 characters: a digit, a letter and 11 digits; or 2 letters, 9 digits and `FR` |
| Chronopost | `XR`, `XT`, `XU`, `XW`, `XY` or `PZ`, then 9 digits and 2 letters |
| DPD France | 12 to 15 digits starting with 0 or 1, or `250` and 12 digits |
| UPS | `1Z` and 16 letters or digits |

## Why is my Fnac order late, or why isn't tracking moving?

For Fnac's own delivery times, see the delivery page on fnac.com. French law adds a rule of its own: before you order, the seller must clearly tell you the delivery date or time frame.

When tracking stands still, it's often because of one of these:

- **No number.** With "Normale" (normal) shipping, the seller ships without tracking. If a seller gives no number for a tracked shipment, Fnac waits 21 days before treating the order as received.
- **No date or delivery time given.** Under French law, the seller must then deliver within 30 days of your order.

Once that date or those 30 days have passed, you can act: see the steps below. When waiting is still normal: [tracking not updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

## Fnac delivery options

Fnac's prices, the options each item offers and its Fnac+ membership are on fnac.com, on its delivery page and its Fnac+ page: check them there before you order. Here is what Fnac's app, Fnac Darty and French law say.

**Store pickup and express delivery.** Fnac's app says an item bought in the app can be collected an hour later in the nearest Fnac store, or delivered within a day with express delivery.

**Bulky items.** Large appliances and big TVs come with Fnac Darty's own delivery network: from about 64 delivery hubs in France, the group's teams and subcontractors deliver and install them at your home, and take your old appliance away.

**The option you chose.** Under French law, the seller must deliver the way your order says, to your home or to a pickup point, for example. More on pickup points: [parcel lockers and pickup points](guide:parcel-lockers).

**Damaged parcel.** You can refuse a damaged delivery and ask the seller, by registered letter, for an item in perfect condition. If you accept it, you can still write your remarks on the delivery slip or the invoice; then contact the seller's customer service quickly.

## Fnac parcel not arrived or marked delivered: what to do

Look first, then report. In this order:

:::steps
- Check the tracking | Open the order in your Fnac account or the Fnac app, and look up the carrier's number in Peek.
- Look around and ask | Check your letterbox and, if you chose one, the pickup point, and ask whoever was in.
- Contact the seller | Contact Fnac's customer service (its contact page is on fnac.com) or, for a marketplace item, the seller. Any line the seller gives you to follow your order can't cost more than a local call.
- Put it in writing | Once the date or the 30 days have passed, ask the seller by registered letter or email to deliver within a reasonable extra time. If it says the parcel was delivered and you have nothing, send it a formal notice, by registered letter with acknowledgement of receipt, demanding the parcel.
- Refund or new item | Until you, or someone you named, have the parcel in hand, the seller carries the risk under French law. If it still doesn't deliver, cancel the order the same way: the seller must refund everything you paid within 14 days.
:::

For a marketplace item, deal with the seller. Fnac tells its sellers they are responsible for delivery, that tracking isn't proof of receipt (only your signature is), and that they must answer within 2 working days and solve a complaint within 8. If you say the parcel never came, you'll have to send the seller a signed statement saying so.

More: [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received), [lost parcel](guide:lost-parcel) and [damaged parcel](guide:damaged-parcel).

## Returning a Fnac order

French law gives you at least 14 days to change your mind after buying online, counted from the day after delivery. Before the deadline, send the seller the withdrawal form or another written statement: just sending the item back or refusing the parcel isn't enough, and the seller may let you do it on its website. Then return the item within 14 days of sending your statement.

The return postage is yours unless the seller pays it or didn't tell you that you would. The seller refunds everything you paid, delivery included (except any extra for a costlier option than the standard one it offers), within 14 days of hearing from you, but it may wait until it has the item back or your proof of sending. Fnac's own return terms are on fnac.com. Where the return's tracking number is and when the money comes: [returns and refunds](guide:returns-tracking).

## Can I track a Fnac parcel in Peek?

Yes, with the carrier's tracking number. Copy it from your Fnac account and paste it into [Peek](/). Peek recognises Colissimo numbers of a digit, a letter and 11 digits by their shape alone, as well as Chronopost numbers starting `XR`, `XT`, `XU`, `XW`, `XY` or `PZ` and UPS numbers starting `1Z`. For a shape several carriers share, such as 12 digits at FedEx and Colis Privé, Peek asks the likely carriers and shows the one that knows the number. If several know it, it asks you to choose; if it shows the wrong one, press "Change" and pick the carrier Fnac or the seller names.

Peek doesn't track Darty deliveries: follow those in your Fnac account. If a seller's parcel changes carrier on the way and the first carrier names the one taking over, Peek keeps the history from the start and switches once the new carrier's own tracking moves; the card then shows that carrier and its number.

Peek shows what the carrier publishes. Claims, refunds and returns stay between you and Fnac or the seller.
