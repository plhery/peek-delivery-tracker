Peek for iPhone, iPad and Mac is a free app that tracks parcels from more than 4,500 carriers, with no account needed. Add its "Parcels" widget to your Home Screen, send tracking links to it with "Add to Peek" in the share sheet, or scan the barcode on the label with your iPhone or iPad.

Paste a tracking number, a carrier's link or a whole shipping email, and [Peek](/) finds the carrier and shows the journey on a map. There are no in-app purchases and no ads.

:::steps
- Get the app | On your iPhone or iPad, open [peektracker.com/app](https://peektracker.com/app): it leads to "Peek: Parcel Delivery Tracker" on the App Store.
- Add a parcel | On the first screen, paste a number, link or message into the tracking field. Peek finds the carrier.
- Send it links | Share a tracking number, link or page from another app and choose "Add to Peek".
:::

## Is there a Peek app for iPhone and iPad?

Yes: "Peek: Parcel Delivery Tracker" on the App Store, called "Peek" on your Home Screen. It needs iOS 18 or later on an iPhone and iPadOS 18 or later on an iPad, and it's made for both: on an iPad it turns in all four directions and takes keyboard shortcuts such as ⌘N to add a parcel.

Get it from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6823615370); on a computer, peektracker.com/app shows a code under "Scan with your phone". Once Peek is installed, its parcel links (peektracker.com/p/…) open in the app. For Android, see [Peek for Android](guide:android-app).

### Where are Peek's settings?

"Settings" opens from the Account button at the top right of Deliveries: a person icon, or your initial when you're signed in.

| To find | Go to |

| Add parcels by email | "Settings" › "Deliveries" › "Add parcels by email", marked "Beta", with an account; see [adding parcels by email](guide:add-parcels-by-email) |

## Can I use the Peek app without an account?

Yes. The parcels you add are listed on that device only, and the widget, "Add to Peek", the barcode scanner and Siri all work. Deliveries shows "All your parcels in one place", with "On this iPhone only, for now." and a "Sign in" button; an iPad or Mac names itself instead.

A parcel followed without an account is forgotten 30 days after it's delivered; "Settings" › "On this device" › "Forget all" forgets them all at once.

An account adds the same list on every device and on peektracker.com, Friends, the notification settings and "Add parcels by email". You sign in with Apple, with Google or with a one-time code sent by email. To look around first, tap "Sign in" on the first screen, then "Explore the demo" at the bottom. [Using Peek without an account](guide:account-or-guest) weighs the choice.

## How do I add the Peek widget to my Home Screen?

Add "Parcels", Peek's one widget, from the widget gallery, where it reads "See the parcels on their way at a glance, the next one first." Apple's steps on an iPhone:

:::steps
- Start editing | On the Home Screen page you want, touch and hold the background until the apps jiggle.
- Open the gallery | Tap **Edit**, then **Add Widget**, and find Peek's "Parcels".
- Choose a size | Swipe to small, medium or large, then tap **Add Widget**.
- Place it | Move it where you want it and tap **Done**.
:::

Apple's current guide also lets you touch and hold Peek's icon and pick a size at the bottom of the menu.

The widget lists the parcels on their way, then those delivered in the last day, in Peek's order. In a medium or large widget, tapping a parcel opens it; the small one opens your list. It refreshes at least every 20 minutes and at midnight, so "tomorrow" turns into "today" even with the app closed.

| The widget shows | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| "Nothing on the way right now" | No parcel is on its way: "Add a tracking number or shipping link to get started." |

"Parcels" lives on the Home Screen only: there's no Lock Screen widget, and Peek shows no Live Activities.

## How do I send a tracking link from Mail or Safari to Peek?

Share it and choose "Add to Peek", which appears in Mail, Messages, Safari and any app that shares text or a web link.

:::steps
- Share | Share the tracking number, link or page, then choose "Add to Peek".
- Save | Tap "Save tracking details". The sheet says "Saved. Open Peek within 10 minutes to review and add your parcel."
- Confirm | Open Peek: Add opens with the details filled in. Nothing is added until you confirm.
:::

If the sheet says "Share a tracking number or link, or paste it directly in Peek.", it found nothing to track. On an iPad or Mac, you can also drop text or a link onto your deliveries to open Add with it.

To send a parcel to someone else, see [sharing a parcel's tracking](guide:share-parcel).

## Can Peek scan the barcode on my parcel label?

Yes, on an iPhone or iPad. Tap the barcode icon at the end of the tracking field (read out as "Scan barcode"), on the first screen or in Add; with an empty list, there's also a "Scan barcode" button under "Paste and track". The scanner, "Scan a tracking barcode", reads any barcode type Apple's scanner knows and puts the first one it reads into the tracking field. iOS asks for the camera the first time.

No barcode icon? It shows only where the camera supports Apple's live scanner and you haven't refused Peek the camera; the Mac app has none. To tell which number on a label is the tracking number, see [how to find your tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number) and [tracking number formats](guide:tracking-number-formats).

## Can I ask Siri about my next delivery?

Yes. Ask "When is my next delivery in Peek", and Siri names your next parcel, its carrier, when it's expected (or else its status) and how many more are on the way, without opening Peek. Say "Add a parcel to Peek", and Siri asks "What’s the tracking number?", then opens Add with it filled in.

Both actions, "Next delivery" and "Add a parcel", are in Spotlight and the Shortcuts app too, on iPhone, iPad and Mac, and their phrases follow your device's language, not Peek's. "Next delivery" reads the widget's data, so until you've opened Peek once, Siri says "Open Peek to see your next delivery."

## Does Peek work on a Mac?

Yes. Peek has a Mac app of its own, named "Peek", for macOS 15 or later, with the "Parcels" widget and "Add to Peek" in the Share menu, but no scanner.

Its menu bar icon lists up to five parcels under "On the way", those out for delivery first, and its menu has "Paste tracking number" (⌘V), "Add parcel" (⌘N) and "Open Peek". In any Mac app, select a tracking number or link and choose **Services** › "Track with Peek" to open it in Peek's Add.

In Peek's Settings window, "General" has "Show in menu bar", which keeps Peek running when you close its window, and "Open at login".
