Leroy Merlin, the DIY and home-improvement chain, doesn't sell in the UK, Ireland, the US, Canada or Australia. In France it sells online at leroymerlin.fr, whose own pages give its delivery options, prices and return conditions. French law makes the seller answer for the delivery it arranges until you have the goods in your hands: it must deliver by the date it gave you, or within 30 days if it gave none, and you can change your mind about most items within 14 days of receiving them.

## Where does Leroy Merlin sell online?

Leroy Merlin belongs to ADEO, a home-improvement group. ADEO's list of its countries has no UK, Ireland, US, Canada or Australia: it names France, Spain, Italy, Portugal, Poland, Greece, Cyprus, Romania, Ukraine, Brazil, South Africa, China and Vietnam. ADEO runs a marketplace on its Leroy Merlin and Tecnomat sites in seven of them: Brazil, France, South Africa, Italy, Spain, Portugal and Poland.

Living elsewhere in the EU and want to order from leroymerlin.fr? The EU's Your Europe portal says a shop whose terms limit delivery to one country doesn't have to deliver to yours; Leroy Merlin's terms of sale on leroymerlin.fr are where to check its delivery area. EU geo-blocking rules do stop a shop from refusing you a service such as click and collect just because you live in another EU country. The rest of this guide covers orders delivered in France.

## Who delivers my Leroy Merlin order?

For Leroy Merlin's delivery options, see its delivery page on leroymerlin.fr. Whatever you choose, French law sets the ground rules. The seller, Leroy Merlin or a marketplace seller, must tell you clearly, before and when you order, how and by when it will deliver. It then has to stick to the delivery method in your contract, such as home delivery or a pickup point. If it gave no date, it must deliver within 30 days of your order.

When the seller chose the carrier, the seller alone answers for the delivery. The risk of loss or damage only passes to you once you, or someone you named, physically take the goods. If you picked a different carrier yourself, the risk passes as soon as the goods are handed to that carrier, and any claim for damage goes to that carrier.

Some items on leroymerlin.fr come from marketplace sellers. On a marketplace, the offer must show who sells, whether the seller is a professional or not, the total price with delivery, and how delivery and disputes are handled. The rights in this guide are the ones you have when you buy from a professional.

:::journey
- warehouse | The seller | Leroy Merlin or a marketplace seller
- truck | The carrier the seller chose | The seller answers for the goods until you have them
- shop | A pickup point | Only if that's the delivery method you chose
- home | You | Check the goods while the driver is there
:::

## Why is my Leroy Merlin order late, or why isn't tracking moving?

When waiting is still normal: [tracking not updating](guide:tracking-not-updating) and [delivery times](guide:delivery-times). What counts legally is the delivery date Leroy Merlin or the seller gave you when you ordered, or 30 days after the order if there was none. Once that date has passed:

- **Ask for a new deadline in writing.** Ask the seller to deliver within a reasonable extra period, by registered letter with acknowledgement of receipt or on another durable medium such as an email.
- **Cancel if it still doesn't come.** Cancel the order the same way, by registered letter or in writing.
- **Cancel at once** if the seller refuses to deliver, or if the date was essential, for example because the order was for a set event and you said so when ordering.
- **Get your money back.** After a cancellation, the seller must refund everything you paid within 14 days. If it pays late, the amount goes up by 10% up to 30 days after the cancellation, 20% up to 60 days and 50% after that.

Service-Public.fr, the French government's information site, has a template letter for cancelling a late order.

For Leroy Merlin's delivery prices, see its delivery page on leroymerlin.fr. Under EU rules, a shop must show you the total price, delivery included, before you pay. ## Leroy Merlin parcel missing, damaged or marked delivered: what to do

Look first, then report. In order:

:::steps
- Check the tracking | Open the carrier's tracking for every parcel number in your order, or paste them into Peek, and read the last scan.
- Look around and ask | Check your letterbox, your doorstep and any safe place, and ask your household and neighbours.
- Contact Leroy Merlin | Use the customer service on leroymerlin.fr with your order number; for a marketplace item, contact the seller. If the seller's carrier delivered and the seller can't prove you received the parcel, the loss is the seller's, and you don't have to prove anyone's fault.
- Put it in writing | If the seller says the parcel was delivered and you have nothing, send it a formal notice by registered letter with acknowledgement of receipt, asking it to send the parcel. If the goods arrive damaged, you can refuse them and ask by registered letter for goods in perfect condition.
- Repair, replacement or refund | The seller answers for damage in transport: it must repair or replace the goods, or, if neither is possible, lower the price or refund you.
:::

**Check before the driver leaves.** Your Europe advises checking the goods while the person who delivered them is there, so the seller can't claim the damage happened afterwards. If you accept a damaged delivery, write your remarks on the delivery note or the invoice and contact the seller's customer service quickly. Under French law, a clause that makes you check the goods on the spot is unfair and has no effect.

**If you chose the carrier.** When you picked a carrier other than the one the seller offered, act against that carrier by registered letter within 3 days of delivery, public holidays not counted, or within 10 days if it didn't let you check the parcel.

More: [parcel marked delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received), [lost parcel](guide:lost-parcel) and [damaged parcel](guide:damaged-parcel).

## Returning a Leroy Merlin order

For Leroy Merlin's own return conditions, see the returns page on leroymerlin.fr. For an online order, French law also gives you these rights:

- **14 days to change your mind.** The period starts the day after you receive the goods, or the last item of an order that comes in several parts. A seller can give you longer. If the seller didn't tell you about this right, the period runs up to 12 months longer.
- **Say it, don't just send it back.** Before the deadline, send the withdrawal form or any written statement. Returning the item or refusing delivery isn't enough on its own. You don't need to give a reason.
- **Send the goods back within 14 days** of telling the seller. You pay the direct cost of the return, unless the seller pays it or didn't tell you that you would. If you pay for returns and an item is too big to go back by post, the seller must tell you what returning it costs before you order.
- **Refund within 14 days** of your withdrawal, standard delivery included, by the payment method you used. For goods, the seller can wait until it has them back or you show proof that you sent them.
- **Exceptions.** You can't withdraw from made-to-measure or clearly personalised goods, such as custom-made furniture, goods that deteriorate quickly, items you've unsealed that can't go back for hygiene or health reasons, or goods that can no longer be separated from other items. Picking a colour or finish from a standard range doesn't make an item personalised.

A faulty item is covered by the legal guarantee for at least 2 years, free of charge, whether you bought it online or in a store. To follow a return: [returns tracking](guide:returns-tracking).

## Can I track a Leroy Merlin parcel in Peek?

Yes, with the carrier's tracking number from the shipping email or your order: paste it into [Peek](/). Peek recognises most Colissimo numbers of a digit, a letter and 11 digits by their shape alone, like `6A12345678905` (made up).

Mondial Relay's numbers of 8, 10 or 12 digits share their shape with other carriers' numbers. Peek asks the likely carriers and shows the one that knows the number. If several know it, press "Choose"; if it shows the wrong one, press "Change" and pick the right carrier. For any other carrier, paste its number all the same: Peek follows more than 3,500 carriers. If your order comes in several parcels, add each number.

If a parcel changes carrier on the way and the first carrier names the one taking over, Peek keeps the history from the start and follows the new carrier. Peek shows what the carrier publishes. Claims, refunds and returns stay with Leroy Merlin or the seller.
