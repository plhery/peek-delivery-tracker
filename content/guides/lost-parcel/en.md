If you bought it from a shop, your lost parcel claim goes to the seller, not the carrier: ask it for a replacement or a refund once the carrier's waiting time has passed (7 days after mailing for a USPS search, 11 working days after posting for Royal Mail 1st Class). The seller booked the carrier, so the carrier's compensation goes to the seller. In the UK and the EU, the parcel stays the seller's responsibility until you have it.

## How do I claim for a lost parcel and get my money back?

:::steps
- Wait out the carrier's time | Before then, the parcel is late, not lost.
- Ask the seller | In writing, with the order and tracking numbers, for a replacement or a refund.
- Open a case on the platform | Marketplaces refund you if the seller doesn't, inside their window.
- Claim from the carrier | If you sent it. Some carriers take a recipient's claim too. Bring proof of posting and value.
- Then your card or PayPal | The last resort: a chargeback can end the platform's case.
:::

The seller may ask you to wait while the carrier searches, or to sign a declaration that nothing came. It shouldn't send you off to the carrier.

Tracking says delivered but nothing came? See [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received). Arrived broken? See [damaged parcel](guide:damaged-parcel).

> Don't start with a chargeback. Once you've filed one, Etsy won't let you open a case, and eBay may close yours.

## How long before you can claim for a lost parcel?

Carriers count from posting or the due date, not from the last scan. Most "lost" parcels are only late: [Canada Post](carrier:canada-post) and [Australia Post](carrier:australia-post) say most turn up within 5 business days of the expected date. Tracking just gone quiet? See [tracking not updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

| Carrier | Search or claim from | Claim deadline | Who claims |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [USPS](carrier:usps) | Search: 7 days after mailing. Insured claim: 15 days (7 for Priority Mail Express) | 60 days after mailing | Sender or recipient, with the mailing receipt |
| [Royal Mail](carrier:royal-mail) | Working days after posting: 11 (1st Class), 14 (2nd Class). Special Delivery: 5 after the due date | 80 days after posting; 6 months abroad | Sender or recipient. Tracked 24 and 48: sender only |
| [Evri](carrier:evri) | Over 7 days with Evri: tell the sender | 28 days after the order (45 abroad), recommended | The sender |
| [DPD UK](carrier:dpd-uk) | The sender reports it within 14 days of despatch | 1 year | The sender |
| Canada Post | After the expected delivery date | 90 days after shipping (6 months abroad) | Only the sender is paid |
| Australia Post | 10 business days after the expected date | 6 months after posting | The sender is paid, unless it waives |

## Who is responsible for a lost parcel, the buyer or the seller?

If you bought from a business, the seller, in most places:

- **UK:** goods stay at the trader's risk until you, or someone you named, physically have them (Consumer Rights Act 2015, s. 29).
- **Ireland and the EU:** the trader is "responsible for any damage to your goods from the time they are dispatched until you receive them", says Your Europe. The CCPC says the business stays responsible when it uses a courier: it should replace a parcel "lost in transit", or refund you in full if it can't deliver.
- **US:** no federal rule; the terms of sale decide. The FTC says a seller charging you for "insurance" implies it "will honor any claim of nondelivery".
- **Australia:** Australia Post tells buyers to contact the seller, "best placed to provide you with a replacement item".

The risk is yours if you booked a courier the shop didn't offer, or bought from a private person. Canada's rules and the other exceptions are in [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).

## Lost parcel refund on Amazon, eBay, Etsy, Vinted and AliExpress

If the seller doesn't help, the marketplace can, if you paid through its checkout:

| Platform | Open a case | Deadline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| eBay | Once the estimated date has passed; the seller has 3 business days to answer | 30 days after that date |
| Etsy | Estimated date passed and the seller contacted over 48 hours ago | 30 days after the estimated date |
| Vinted (its label) | `Parcel seems to be lost`, or 25 business days after `shipped` | 2 days after Vinted's notice |
| AliExpress | From the 11th day after dispatch, or once the delivery time is over if tracking still moves | 15 days after the delivery time ends |

On Amazon, the A-to-z Guarantee covers delivery problems with anything bought in its stores, whether Amazon or another seller sold it: Amazon refunds or replaces it. If another seller shipped it, contact that seller first; if it hasn't replied within 48 hours, Amazon UK says you can claim from Amazon, which decides on the refund.

Vinted refunds once the loss is confirmed, which can take weeks. Etsy won't cover a delay caused by a strike or severe weather, or a parcel you refused to pay customs charges on. More on AliExpress, Temu and Shein in [tracking a parcel from China](guide:tracking-from-china).

## How much compensation do carriers pay for a lost parcel?

Not much. Included cover is capped and pays only the value you can prove, usually plus the postage:

| Carrier | Included cover | Extra cover |
| --- | --- | --- |
| USPS | Up to $100 on Priority Mail Express, Priority Mail, Ground Advantage | Insurance to $5,000; Registered Mail $50,000 |
| Royal Mail | 1st, 2nd Class £20; Tracked 24, 48 £75; Special Delivery by 1pm £750 | More on Special Delivery with extra insurance |
| Evri | £20 | Full Cover |
| Canada Post | CAN$100 on Priority, Xpresspost, Expedited Parcel; none on Regular Parcel | CAN$5,000 in Canada, CAN$1,000 abroad |
| Australia Post | Up to A$100 on most services | Extra Cover to A$5,000 |
| Parcels between national posts (UPU) | 40 SDR plus 4.50 SDR per kg | Insured items |

Between two national posts, a lost 2 kg parcel pays at most 49 SDR plus postage, about €59 at the UPU's 2026 rate of €1.2119 per SDR (Correos), unless the posts agreed a flat 130 SDR.

## How do I file a claim with USPS, Royal Mail or another carrier?

On the carrier's own site, before the deadline in the first table, with:

- **Tracking number and history.** Screenshot it, because tracking pages expire. [Peek](/) shows every scan on the parcel's page, but can't file the claim for you.
- **Proof of posting.** USPS needs the original mailing receipt for insured mail. Royal Mail wants your Post Office receipt or certificate of posting.
- **Proof of value.** Invoice, receipt, bank statement or PayPal record; Royal Mail wants the original. USPS accepts an online transaction printout showing buyer, seller, price, date and item.
- **Contents and both addresses.**

USPS usually decides within 5 to 10 days, Royal Mail within 30 days.

## Bought from a private seller on eBay or Vinted?

Consumer law doesn't cover sales between individuals. In the UK, handing the parcel to the carrier normally counts as delivery to you (Sale of Goods Act 1979, s. 32). What protects you is how you paid:

- **eBay**'s Money Back Guarantee doesn't exclude private sellers, but covers only orders paid through eBay checkout.
- **Vinted** refunds you when a parcel sent with its label is confirmed lost, and compensates the seller. Paying outside Vinted isn't covered.
- **PayPal** Goods and Services is covered; friends and family payments are not.

Paid by bank transfer? Ask the seller to claim from the carrier and pass the money on.

## Last resort: a PayPal or card dispute

When the seller and the platform both fail, the way you paid can still bring the money back. Its clock runs from the payment or your statement, not from the delivery date.

On PayPal, open an Item Not Received dispute within 180 days of paying, and escalate it within 20 days of opening it. In the US, PayPal refuses it if the seller shows proof of shipment or delivery. For a US credit card, send your issuer a written billing-error notice within 60 days after the charge appeared on your statement. Chargebacks, Section 75 and the time limits elsewhere are in [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).
