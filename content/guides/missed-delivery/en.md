After a missed delivery, the card, text or email tells you where your parcel is now and how long it will wait. Book a redelivery on the carrier's own site or app (usually free), or collect it with the notice and ID from the time printed on it, which is rarely the same day. Royal Mail's tracking may say `Due for Redelivery the Next Working Day`; USPS and An Post wait for you to book, and [Australia Post](carrier:australia-post) doesn't redeliver at all.

## What to do if you missed your delivery

:::steps
- Read the notice | Where the parcel is, the number to quote, from when you can collect and the last day.
- Check the tracking | On the carrier's own site or app: the place and time can change after the card.
- Rebook or redirect | A new day, a neighbour or a pickup point, on the carrier's site, app or the card's QR code.
- Collect with ID | Bring the notice and ID, usually with a photo, no earlier than the time printed on it.
:::

Each carrier's notice, and what it does next:

| Carrier | The notice | What happens next |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [USPS](carrier:usps) (US) | PS Form 3849; tracking says `Notice Left` | No automatic second try: book a redelivery or pick it up |
| [FedEx](carrier:fedex) (US) | Door tag | A nearby FedEx location if it can't be left |
| [Royal Mail](carrier:royal-mail) (UK) | A red card with the address and opening hours of where to collect | Collect it there, or book a redelivery to your door |
| [Evri](carrier:evri) (UK) | Calling card with an 8-digit number | Up to three attempts on consecutive working days; no depot collection |
| [DPD UK](carrier:dpd-uk) | "Sorry we missed you" card | A neighbour if possible, otherwise you pick a new day or a Pickup shop |
| [An Post](carrier:an-post) (IE) | "Sorry we missed you" (Docket In Box) | Back to the local Delivery Service Unit; no new attempt unless you book one |
| [Canada Post](carrier:canada-post) | Delivery Notice Card; tracking says `Notice left` | Waits at the post office named on the card |
| Australia Post | App, email or SMS notification (paper cards are being phased out) | A Post Office or Parcel Locker; "We don't offer redelivery" |

## What does "Notice left" mean?

The driver tried, nobody took the parcel, and a notice was left: USPS's PS Form 3849 or Canada Post's Delivery Notice Card. Keep it; you rebook or collect with it. Other carriers' wordings for a missed attempt are in [tracking statuses explained](guide:tracking-statuses).

The number on a FedEx door tag or an Evri card isn't your tracking number, but either works in that carrier's tracker.

Parcels end up at the counter when they need a signature, have something to pay (see [customs](guide:customs) for an import charge) or have no safe place to go. DPD UK, for one, only leaves a parcel with a neighbour or in a safe place if the sender didn't ask for a signature.

## How do I get a parcel redelivered?

On the carrier's own site or app, or with the QR code on the card. Most redeliveries are free, USPS's included.

- **USPS:** scan the QR code, use USPS Redelivery online, or call 1-800-ASK-USPS. Ask by 2 a.m. CST, Monday to Saturday, for redelivery that day. You get one request, to the original address only.
- **FedEx:** redirect it in FedEx Delivery Manager to a FedEx location or a retail partner such as Walgreens.
- **Royal Mail:** book a redelivery to your door on its website, or collect the parcel where the red card says.
- **Evri:** after a missed attempt, divert it in the app or online to a ParcelShop, locker, neighbour or safe place.
- **DPD UK:** the driver can't come back the same day. Change the date, or choose a safe place, a neighbour or a Pickup shop.
- **An Post:** book online, to the same or another address. It can take up to 5 working days.
- **Canada Post:** after `Delivery Attempt Made`, it normally tries again the next business day.
- **Australia Post:** no redelivery. Collect it, send someone, or pay $7.20 to move it to another Post Office.

Want it sent somewhere else next time? See [changing a delivery address or day](guide:change-delivery-address).

## Can I collect a missed parcel the same day?

Usually not: the parcel reaches the counter after the driver's round.

- **USPS:** "on or after the date and time" written on the 3849, at the Post Office printed on it.
- **Royal Mail:** once tracking says `Available for Redelivery or Collection`, at the Customer Service Point on the card, usually in a delivery office. Check its opening hours first.
- **DPD UK:** Pickup shop parcels are ready the next day.
- **FedEx:** a FedEx location may have it "as early as the same day".
- **Canada Post:** after `Delivery Attempt Made`, it "can't be collected from a postal outlet yet".

Check the tracking before going.

## What ID do I need to collect a parcel?

The notice, and ID in the name on the parcel, usually with a photo:

| Carrier | Bring | Watch out |
| --- | --- | --- |
| USPS | Valid photo ID, and the 3849 if you have it | "Digital or electronic forms of identification in any form are not accepted" |
| FedEx location | Tracking number, door tag number and government-issued photo ID | |
| DPD UK Pickup shop | A photo ID and an ID with your address, such as a utility bill | |
| An Post | The notice and ID such as a driving licence | |
| Canada Post | The card if you have one, and government-issued photo ID | |
| Australia Post | One photo ID (a digital driving licence only in some states), or two non-photo IDs | "A photo or image of your ID is not digital ID" |

## Can someone else collect my parcel?

Yes, for most parcels, with your written consent and their own ID.

- **USPS:** sign the back of the 3849, or write a note naming them. For someone who collects regularly, there's PS Form 3801.
- **An Post:** the notice signed by you, plus their ID.
- **Canada Post:** sign the card and print their name on it; they bring their government-issued photo ID.
- **Australia Post:** in the app, "let someone collect this parcel" makes an authorisation image to share; the paper form or the card's authorisation section also works.

Not for Australia Post parcels the sender wants you to collect in person. USPS won't take a note on the 3849 for Registered Mail or Restricted Delivery.

## Missed delivery but you were home

DPD UK admits that "sometimes you'll still miss each other". Australia Post cites an unsecured dog, a locked gate or an apartment block where the driver can only reach the ground floor, and its app shows the driver's photo of the attempt.

Note the time, keep any doorbell camera clip, compare with the tracking and report it to the carrier (Australia Post has a delivery issue form).

Tracking says attempted but there's no card? An Post tries again in the next delivery slot, usually the next day.

If tracking says delivered and there's nothing, see [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).

## How long do I have to collect a missed parcel?

Usually one to three weeks at a post office, 2 to 5 days in a locker. Your notice gives the last day:

- USPS: usually 15 days; Priority Mail Express 5.
- An Post: 5 working days, 16 for items from abroad.
- Canada Post: 15 calendar days.
- Australia Post: 10 business days at a Post Office, 2 in a Parcel Locker.

Then it goes back to the sender. [Parcel not collected](guide:uncollected-parcel) covers extensions and refunds.

## A missed delivery text asking for a fee

> A text saying you missed a delivery and must pay a small fee through a link is the classic scam. USPS redelivery is free, and An Post never sends a payment link by text or email for a parcel coming into Ireland. Don't tap: type your tracking number into the carrier's site or app yourself.

More in [parcel scam texts](guide:delivery-scam-texts). To hear about real missed attempts, [Peek](/) can notify you of important steps, missed attempts included; rebooking stays with the carrier.

## How do I complain about a missed delivery?

Complain to the carrier first and keep its reference. For an online order, tell the shop too: in the UK, Citizens Advice says contacting the seller usually gets a better result. Then:

| Country | After the carrier |
| --- | --- |
| US | USPS District Consumer and Industry Affairs, then the Postal Regulatory Commission |
| UK | Royal Mail: Postal Review Panel, then POSTRS. Others: their own procedure |
| Ireland | An Post's Customer Advocate, then ComReg's Postal Dispute Resolution (€15) |
| Canada | Ombudsman at Canada Post, once you have a closed service ticket |
| Australia | Commonwealth Ombudsman, for Australia Post and StarTrack, if you complained to them within 12 months |
