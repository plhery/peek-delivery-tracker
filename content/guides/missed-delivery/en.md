After a missed delivery, the card, text or email tells you where your parcel is now and how long it will wait. Book a redelivery on the carrier's own site or app (usually free), or collect it with the notice and ID from the time printed on it, which is rarely the same day. Royal Mail's tracking may say `Due for Redelivery the Next Working Day`; USPS waits for you to book, and [Australia Post](carrier:australia-post) doesn't redeliver at all. Most carriers then hold the parcel at a post office for one to three weeks before it goes back to the sender, and someone else can usually collect it with your written consent and their own ID.

## What to do if you missed your delivery

:::steps
- Read the notice | Where the parcel is, the number to quote, from when you can collect and the last day.
- Check the tracking | On the carrier's own site or app: the place and time can change after the card.
- Rebook or redirect | A new day, a neighbour or a pickup point, on the carrier's site, app or the card's QR code.
- Collect with ID | Bring the notice and ID, usually with a photo, no earlier than the time printed on it.
:::

Each carrier's notice, and what it does next:

| Carrier | The notice | What happens next |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [USPS](carrier:usps) (US) | PS Form 3849; tracking says `Notice Left` | No automatic second try: book a redelivery or pick it up |
| [Royal Mail](carrier:royal-mail) (UK) | A red card with the address and opening hours of where to collect | Collect it there, or book a redelivery to your door |
| [Evri](carrier:evri) (UK) | Calling card with an 8-digit number | Up to three attempts on consecutive working days, at no extra cost; no depot collection |
| [DPD UK](carrier:dpd-uk) | "Sorry we missed you" card | A neighbour if possible, otherwise you pick a new day or a Pickup shop |
| [An Post](carrier:an-post) (IE) | Tracking says `We attempted to deliver your item` | Once you can collect it, tracking says `Your item is now available for collection` |
| [Canada Post](carrier:canada-post) | Delivery Notice Card; tracking says `Notice left` | Waits at the post office named on the card |
| Australia Post | App, email or SMS notification (paper cards are being phased out) | A Post Office or Parcel Locker; "We don't offer redelivery" |

## What does "Notice left" mean?

The driver tried, nobody took the parcel, and a notice was left: USPS's PS Form 3849 or Canada Post's Delivery Notice Card. Keep it; you rebook or collect with it. Other carriers' wordings for a missed attempt are in [tracking statuses explained](guide:tracking-statuses).

The 8-digit number on an Evri calling card isn't your 16-character tracking number, but it works in Evri's tracker. On a DPD UK "sorry we missed you" card, the parcel number is beside the barcode.

Parcels end up at the counter when they need a signature, have something to pay (see [customs](guide:customs) for an import charge) or have no safe place to go. DPD UK, for one, only leaves a parcel with a neighbour or in a safe place if the sender didn't ask for a signature.

## How do I get a parcel redelivered?

On the carrier's own site or app, or with the QR code on the card. Most redeliveries are free, USPS's included.

- **USPS:** scan the QR code, use USPS Redelivery online, or call 1-800-ASK-USPS. Ask by 2 a.m. CST, Monday to Saturday, for redelivery that day. You get one request, to the original address only. It comes on the day you pick (any day up to the return date), not at a time you choose. Lost the card? Book with the tracking number.
- **Royal Mail:** book a redelivery to your door on its website, or collect the parcel where the red card says.
- **Evri:** after a missed attempt, divert it in the app or online, with the 16-character tracking code and your postcode, to a ParcelShop, locker, neighbour or safe place. If it can't be diverted, the courier tries again the next working day.
- **DPD UK:** the driver can't come back the same day. With the parcel number and your postcode, change the date, or choose a safe place, a neighbour or a Pickup shop.
- **Canada Post:** after a failed attempt, tracking says `Delivery Attempt Made / Rescheduled for delivery next business day`, and Canada Post normally does try again then.
- **Australia Post:** no redelivery. Collect it, send someone, or pay $7.55 to move it to another Post Office.

Want it sent somewhere else next time? See [changing a delivery address or day](guide:change-delivery-address).

## Can I collect a missed parcel the same day?

Usually not: the parcel reaches the counter after the driver's round.

- **USPS:** "on or after the date and time" written on the 3849, at the Post Office printed on it. USPS Tracking may only show that Post Office's name and hours once the parcel is back there, at the end of the delivery day.
- **Royal Mail:** once tracking says `Available for Redelivery or Collection`, at the Customer Service Point on the card, usually in a delivery office. Check its opening hours first.
- **DPD UK:** Pickup shop parcels are ready the next day.
- **Canada Post:** while tracking says `Delivery Attempt Made / Rescheduled for delivery next business day`, the parcel "can't be collected from a postal outlet yet".

Check the tracking before going.

## What ID do I need to collect a parcel?

The notice, and ID in the name on the parcel, usually with a photo:

| Carrier | Bring | Watch out |
| --- | --- | --- |
| USPS | Valid photo ID, such as a driver's licence, state ID or passport, and the 3849 if you have it | "Digital or electronic forms of identification in any form are not accepted" |
| DPD UK Pickup shop | A photo ID and an ID with your address, such as a utility bill | |
| Canada Post | The card if you have one, and government-issued photo ID | |
| Australia Post | One photo ID (a digital driving licence only in some states), or two non-photo IDs | "A photo or image of your ID is not digital ID" |

## Can someone else collect my parcel?

Yes, for most parcels, with your written consent and their own ID.

- **USPS:** sign the back of the 3849, or write a note naming them. For someone who collects regularly, there's a standing order, PS Form 3801. Sharing your surname and address isn't enough for mail that needs a signature: without that standing order, they need your signed 3849.
- **Canada Post:** sign the card and print their name on it, or give them a letter of authorization; they bring their government-issued photo ID.
- **Australia Post:** in the app, "let someone collect this parcel" makes an authorisation image to share; the paper form or the card's authorisation section also works. Someone who lives at your address only needs proof of that address and their own ID.

Not for Australia Post parcels the sender wants you to collect in person. USPS won't take a note on the 3849 for Registered Mail or Restricted Delivery.

## Missed delivery but you were home

DPD UK admits that "sometimes you'll still miss each other". Australia Post cites an unsecured dog, a locked gate or an apartment block where the driver can only reach the ground floor, and its app shows the driver's photo of the attempt. Canada Post leaves a card even when you're in if the sender asked for one, the parcel needs a signature and nobody answered the door, or it won't fit a community mailbox more than 500 metres from your home.

Note the time, keep any doorbell camera clip, compare with the tracking and report it to the carrier (Australia Post has a delivery issue form).

If tracking says delivered and there's nothing, see [delivered but not received](guide:delivered-not-received).

## How long do I have to collect a missed parcel?

Usually one to three weeks at a post office, 2 to 5 days in a locker. Your notice gives the last day:

- USPS: usually 15 days; Priority Mail Express 5; most parcels from abroad 30. A second notice comes 5 days after the first (3 for Priority Mail Express).
- Canada Post: 15 calendar days.
- Australia Post: 10 business days at a Post Office, 2 in a Parcel Locker before it moves to a nearby Post Office. It can sometimes hold a parcel longer, but doesn't promise to.

Then it goes back to the sender. [Parcel not collected](guide:uncollected-parcel) covers extensions and refunds.

## A missed delivery text asking for a fee

> A text saying you missed a delivery and must pay a small fee through a link is the classic scam. USPS redelivery is free, and so are Evri's three attempts. Don't tap: type your tracking number into the carrier's site or app yourself.

More in [parcel scam texts](guide:delivery-scam-texts). To hear about real missed attempts, [Peek](/) can notify you of important steps, missed attempts included; rebooking stays with the carrier.

## How do I complain about a missed delivery?

Complain to the carrier first and keep its reference. For an online order, tell the shop too: in the UK, Citizens Advice says contacting the seller usually gets a better result. Then:

| Country | After the carrier |
| --- | --- |
| US | USPS District Consumer and Industry Affairs, then the Postal Regulatory Commission, which may pass your inquiry to USPS for an answer within 45 days |
| UK | Royal Mail: Postal Review Panel (answer within 30 days), then POSTRS, or POSTRS directly if Royal Mail takes longer than 90 days. Others: their own procedure |
| Ireland | An Post's Customer Advocate, then ComReg's Postal Dispute Resolution (€15) |
| Canada | Ombudsman at Canada Post, the final appeal authority, once you have a closed service ticket |
| Australia | Commonwealth Ombudsman, for Australia Post and StarTrack, if you complained to them within 12 months |
