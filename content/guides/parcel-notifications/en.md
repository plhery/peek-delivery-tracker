Peek sends a notification when your parcel has news, in your browser on peektracker.com or in the Peek app on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android. You choose how much it tells you, "Every scan", "Important steps" or "Delivery only", and with an account Peek can also email you when a parcel is ready for pickup or delivered.

With an account, it takes three steps:

:::steps
- Open "Notifications" | On the website or in the iPhone, iPad and Android apps, the round account button with your initial opens "Settings": under "Deliveries", choose "Notifications". On a Mac, it's a tab of Peek's Settings window.
- Switch on "Notifications" | That turns them on for this browser or phone. Peek confirms with a first notification, "You’ll hear from Peek".
- Choose under "Which updates?" | "Every scan", "Important steps" or "Delivery only", then "Save preferences". The choice is saved with your account, "On all your devices".
:::

Peek may also offer a small card, "Get delivery updates": your browser or phone asks for permission only once you tap "Enable notifications".

Blocked? The apps show a button to the system's settings, such as "Open iPhone Settings" or "Open Android settings". In a browser, allow notifications in its site settings.

## How do I get a notification when my parcel arrives?

Turn notifications on as above: all three choices include the delivery, which comes as "It’s arrived". "Delivery only" sends just two steps: out for delivery, and delivered. Under the title, a notification names the parcel and, if the carrier gives one, its estimate. Several new scans at once give one notification, for the newest step your choice covers.

How soon you hear depends on when Peek next checks. In its own words: "Parcels are checked hourly, day and night, and up to every 10 minutes on their delivery day or while you look at them." [What time parcels get delivered](guide:delivery-times) and [why tracking stops updating](guide:tracking-not-updating) go into more detail.

On Android, the Google Play build needs Google Play services for notifications. The F-Droid build uses a UnifiedPush app such as ntfy if you have one; otherwise "Peek checks for news about every 15 minutes, so a notification can come a little late." The Android app is at [peektracker.com/android](https://peektracker.com/android).

## Which updates can Peek notify me about?

Up to ten tracking steps, depending on your choice:

| Choice | Peek's description | Steps it notifies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Every scan" | "Every new tracking event, including routine carrier scans." | Label created, received by carrier, in transit, at customs, needs attention, out for delivery, delivery attempted, ready for pickup, delivered, returned |
| "Important steps" | "Delivery, pickup, customs, missed attempts and returns." | At customs, needs attention, out for delivery, delivery attempted, ready for pickup, delivered, returned |
| "Delivery only" | "When your parcel goes out for delivery and when it arrives." | Out for delivery, delivered |

An account that never chose gets every step. Six steps have titles of their own: "Label created", "On its way", "Ready for pickup", "It’s arrived", "Delivery missed" and "On its way back"; the others use the step's name, such as "Out for delivery".

What each step means: [tracking statuses explained](guide:tracking-statuses). After "Delivery missed", see [missed deliveries](guide:missed-delivery); after "Ready for pickup", [parcel lockers](guide:parcel-lockers) or [uncollected parcels](guide:uncollected-parcel). Forwarding shipping emails to Peek adds one more, "Added from your email": see [tracking parcels from your email](guide:add-parcels-by-email).

## Can I get notifications without an account?

Yes, parcel by parcel. On the website, a parcel's page has a "Notify me" button: before the parcel has moved it reads "Notify me when it moves", and while the carrier is still unknown, "Notify me when it’s found". Under "Tell me about", pick "Every scan", "Important steps" or "Delivery only", then "Turn on". It "Works while this page is closed. Stops when the parcel is delivered." Once it's on, "For every parcel in this browser" gives the parcels you track there the same notifications.

The apps without an account have no "Notifications" setting and no choice of steps. The iPhone and Android apps ask for permission the first time a parcel comes to the phone, then sign up each parcel still on its way by themselves: every scan until it moves, the important steps after that. On a Mac, the Settings window has no Notifications tab without an account.

Without an account, notifications never carry the parcel's name or number, Peek can't email you, and "Notify me" isn't offered once the journey is over.

## Why can't my iPhone's browser show notifications?

Because on iPhone and iPad, a browser tab can't get them: Peek has to be on your Home Screen and opened from there. As Peek puts it, "iPhone only sends notifications from sites on your Home Screen." Apple's WebKit team explains how such a Home Screen web app asks for permission: only when you tap to subscribe.

Peek may first offer its app, "Notifications in the Peek app", with "Get"; without an account it adds "No account needed. This parcel comes along." To stay in the browser, select "Show the steps" below it. Peek's steps depend on your browser:

:::steps
- Open the Share menu | Safari 27 or later on iPhone: tap the page menu (⋯) in the address bar, then "Share". Chrome and Firefox on iPhone or iPad: tap "Share" in the address bar. Other browsers, older Safari and Safari on iPad: open your browser's "Share" menu.
- Add Peek | Choose "Add to Home Screen". In Safari 27 or later, tap "View More" first.
- Open Peek from there | Open Peek from your Home Screen, then tap "Notify me" on a parcel, or turn on notifications in Settings.
:::

Inside another app's own browser, such as Instagram's or TikTok's, there's no way to the Home Screen: Peek says "Notifications need Safari." with "Open in Safari", or "Copy link". Elsewhere than iPhone and iPad, a browser that supports notifications gets them in a normal tab.

## Can Peek email me when a parcel is delivered?

Yes, with an account. On the Notifications page, under "By email", switch on "Email me when a parcel arrives": "A short email when a parcel is ready for pickup and when it’s delivered", sent to the address you sign in with. "See an example" shows one; the [example email](https://peektracker.com/email/example?lang=en) is online too.

It's off until you turn it on, and never covers earlier scans or a parcel already delivered when it joined your account. Subjects read "Delivered:", "Ready for pickup:" or "Picked up:" and the parcel's name, and each email has a "See its journey" button. If you've never chosen, a delivered parcel offers it once: "An email when the next one arrives?"

## How do I turn notifications off?

| To stop | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| All of them on one device | Switch "Notifications" off on the Notifications page |
| Most of them | Choose "Delivery only", then "Save preferences" |
| One parcel | Tap its bell ("Turn off parcel notifications"). It's muted on all your devices |
| The delivery email | Its switch on the Notifications page, or "Turn it off here" at the foot of each email |
| A parcel on the website, without an account | "Notifications on" → "Turn off" |
| A parcel in the app, without an account | Forget the parcel: its notifications stop on that device |

With the delivery email on, the bell of a parcel still on its way opens two switches instead, "Notifications" and "Email when it arrives", for that parcel only. The email's own link needs no sign-in and asks first: "Turn off delivery emails?"

Signing out of the website stops the account's notifications in that browser. Signing out of the apps does too, but they remember whether notifications were on and bring them back when you sign in again.
