To share a parcel's tracking in [Peek](/), open the parcel, tap its share button and send the link Peek makes: the person you send it to opens the parcel's page with no account. Turn on "Wrap as a gift" first, and the page keeps what's inside and who sent it a surprise until it's delivered, then shows your note.

Sharing works the same on the website and in the iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android apps.

:::steps
- Open the share sheet | On the parcel's page, tap the share button ("Share this parcel").
- Choose what the link shows | Three switches, all off to start: "Show the full number", "Show its name" (only if the parcel has a name) and "Wrap as a gift". The card above them, "What they’ll see", shows the page as the link will.
- Send the link | "Share…" opens your phone's or computer's own share menu, for Messages, WhatsApp or mail. Or tap "Copy" beside the link.
:::

Peek sends nothing by itself: the link goes only where you send it. Turn "Show the full number" or "Wrap as a gift" on or off later, and a link you already sent shows the change at once. "Show its name" counts only from the next time you tap "Share…" or "Copy".

## How do I share a parcel's tracking with someone?

Open the parcel and tap its share button, or choose "Share" from its menu. Where to find them:

| Where you follow the parcel | Where to share it from |
| --- | --- |
| Website, signed in | The share button on the parcel's card, or "Share" in its ••• menu ("Parcel actions") |
| Website, without an account | The share button in the page's header, or "Share" in the parcel's row of actions |
| iPhone, iPad, Mac | The share button on the parcel's page, or "Share…" after a long press on its card (a secondary click on a Mac) |
| Android | The share button on the parcel's page, or its card's menu |
| A parcel someone shared with you | Its share button, for the plain link only. A gift has none |

You don't need an account to share: without one, the browser or phone that looked the parcel up counts as its owner and gets the same sheet. Someone a parcel was shared with can pass it on, but only as the plain link, with no switches and no name. An archived parcel in your account can't be shared until you restore it. What else an account changes is in [using Peek without an account](guide:account-or-guest).

## What does the other person see, and do they need an account?

They don't need an account: the link opens the parcel's page, marked "Shared with you · no account needed". They see what you see, minus your private details:

| They see | They never see |
| --- | --- |
| Status, estimate, carrier, map and tracking history | A pickup code, a PIN, who signed for the parcel, a door number, a postcode, phone digits, or the recipient's name or address. A delivery just reads "Delivered" |
| The tracking number, if "Show the full number" is on | Otherwise only its last few characters, such as "••• 0899", even in the carrier's scan wording |
| The parcel's name, if "Show its name" is on | Otherwise, any name you gave it |

A name you show travels in the link itself, after the #, so Peek doesn't store it. A chat app's preview of the link shows the status, the carrier and the estimate, never the number, a name or a place. Search engines never index parcel links.

They can also "Add to calendar" when there's an estimate. If they're signed in and the full number is shown, "Add to my deliveries" copies the parcel into their account: "The person who shared it sees nothing of yours." Their copy fetches its own scans, so nothing the carrier told only you passes to them. With the number hidden, they can't keep the parcel.

## How do I share a parcel as a gift without spoiling the surprise?

Turn on "Wrap as a gift" in the share sheet before you send the link. Until the parcel is delivered, the page hides what's inside, who sent it and the town it was sent from; once it's delivered, it shows your note and who it's from.

:::steps
- Turn on "Wrap as a gift" | The preview's caption becomes "What they’ll see until it’s delivered".
- Write a note | Fill in "A note, shown once it’s delivered", up to 280 characters, and "From", up to 60. To reveal what's inside on delivery, also turn on "Show its name".
- Share the link | With "Share…" or "Copy". Your words never go in the link's address: "Stored securely by Peek; shared only after delivery."
:::

Here is what the other person sees:

:::journey
- label | You share it | Wrapped, with your note kept by Peek
- truck | On its way | "Something’s on its way to you", the arrival date if there's an estimate, and a parcel wearing a ribbon
- home | Delivered | "It’s here", your note, who it's from, the parcel's name after "Inside:", and the full journey
:::

Before delivery, the page shows no sender, weight, size, pickup point, tracking number (whatever the number switch says), carrier status line or link to the carrier's website. For a parcel from abroad, the scans in the country it comes from become one row, "Left the sender", with that country and no town; within one country, only the scans of the carrier taking it in do. Import charges still show, since the recipient usually pays at the door: see [parcels held at customs](guide:customs).

The recipient can turn on notifications from the bell on the gift's card; they don't say where it is before it arrives. They can't keep a gift in an account or follow it in the apps until it's delivered. Your own page shows the whole parcel, with a small "Gift" marker.

## Can the person I share with get notifications?

Yes, for that one parcel and without an account: on the website, they tap "Notify me", as [parcel delivery notifications](guide:parcel-notifications) explains. A link takes up to ten such sign-ups.

If they have the [Peek app](https://peektracker.com/app), the link opens in it. Without an account, the app offers "Follow on this iPhone" ("Follow on this iPad", "Follow on this Mac", or "Follow on this device" on Android); signed in, "Add to my deliveries".

Opening the link signs them up to nothing, and their notifications never carry the parcel's name or number.

## How do I stop sharing a tracking link?

Open the share sheet again and tap "Stop sharing" at its foot: the link stops at once. Anyone who opens it then reads "This parcel isn’t shared any more", and their notifications for it end.

For a parcel in your account, sharing again ("Share again" in the apps, "Share…" or "Copy" on the website) makes a new link, and the old one never works again. Deleting the parcel or your account deletes its links at once. On the website without an account, stopping keeps the same link: the sheet says "Sharing is stopped. The link shows nothing to anyone else.", and "Share again" turns that link back on.

To follow the parcel on their own, the other person needs its tracking number: see [how to find your tracking number](guide:find-tracking-number).

## How long does a shared tracking link work?

A link from an account works for as long as you share it. Without an account, the link ends when Peek forgets the parcel: 30 days after it's delivered or returned, or after 90 days without news. On the website, once that parcel is delivered, your sheet says "Works until" and the date, with "Keep it longer", which leads to signing in.

A forgotten link reads "This parcel has been forgotten", just like a link that never existed, so links can't be guessed.
