Temu's order number won't track your parcel: you need the carrier's tracking number. Paste that into [Peek](/) and it works out the carrier among more than 3,500, then follows the local partner if the first carrier names one. Paste the order number instead and Peek tells you it looks like a Temu order number, not a tracking number.

## Is my Temu order number a tracking number?

No. Temu's order number is its own reference, not a tracking number: no carrier can look it up. Peek knows it by its shape, PO followed by 20 digits (`PO-000-00000000000000000`), and holds it back with a note that it looks like a Temu order number. Not sure which one you have? Type it here:

## Which carrier delivers my Temu parcel?

The tracking number usually tells: the checker above names the carrier its shape points to, or the few it could belong to. Who you're likely to see on the last leg, from USPS and Royal Mail to An Post, Canada Post and Australia Post, is in the country table of [tracking a parcel from China](guide:tracking-from-china). Whether you owe anything at the border depends on your country: see [customs](guide:customs).

In Ireland, since 1 July 2026, online orders from outside the EU worth €150 or less pay €3 duty per item, meaning per kind of product, and from 1 November 2026 Revenue says a €2 EU handling fee per item is added. If the checkout showed VAT and duty, that's the final price; if not, the carrier collects them first.

## How long does Temu delivery to Ireland take?

In Ireland you choose standard or Click & Collect. Temu's Irish shipping page gives no number of days: you see the time on the product page and in your order.

## Can I track a Temu parcel in Peek?

Yes, with the carrier's tracking number. Paste it into [Peek](/) and it works out the carrier among more than 3,500. A shape several carriers share may need you to press "Change" and pick the right carrier.

When the first carrier names the local partner taking over, Peek keeps the first history, follows the partner once its own tracking shows fresh progress, and shows the partner's name and number on the card.

Got several tracking numbers? Add each one: Peek follows each parcel on its own. Temu's order number isn't a tracking number, and Peek can't track it.

Peek shows what the carrier publishes. A claim or a refund stays in your Temu account. A return has its own tracking number, separate from your order's: where to find it and when the refund comes, [return tracking](guide:returns-tracking).
