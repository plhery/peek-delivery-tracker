## Description

```text
Where’s my parcel?

Paste a tracking number, a carrier link or a whole shipping email, or scan the barcode on the label. Peek works out which of 3,500+ carriers has the parcel and shows its journey on a map, every scan and the day it should arrive.

No account needed. No ads.

WILL I KNOW WHEN IT MOVES?
Sign in with Apple, Google or a code sent to your email, and Peek notifies you: every scan, the important steps or delivery only. An email can tell you it has arrived.

FOLLOWING MORE THAN ONE?
They share one list, the next to arrive on top, riding on its map. With an account, the same list is on your iPhone and on the web at peektracker.com. A widget keeps your next delivery on the Home Screen.

SOMEONE ELSE WAITING FOR IT?
Share the parcel’s page with a link. They need no app and no account. Wrap it as a gift, and what’s inside stays a surprise until it arrives.

A PASSPORT OF DELIVERIES
With an account, every delivery counts in your passport, and some earn a stamp. Compare passports with friends; your parcels stay private.

3,500+ CARRIERS
Royal Mail, Evri, DPD, DHL, UPS, FedEx, GLS, An Post, USPS, Canada Post, Australia Post, La Poste, Swiss Post, PostNL, Correos, Poste Italiane, InPost, China Post, Cainiao, YunExpress and thousands more: national posts, couriers and marketplace shippers.

ALSO IN THE BOX
• A barcode scanner
• Sharing a number or a tracking link from Mail, Messages or Safari straight into Peek
• Light and dark themes
• English, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, Brazilian Portuguese, Polish, Russian and Simplified Chinese

PRIVATE AND OPEN SOURCE
Peek shows no ads and sells no data. It draws its own maps, so no map service sees where your parcels travel. A parcel followed without an account is forgotten 30 days after it arrives. Download your data or delete your account from the app, whenever you like. Every line of code is on GitHub: github.com/plhery/peek-delivery-tracker

Peek is independent of the carriers it reads. Their names belong to their owners.
```

## What's new

```text
• A parcel out for delivery is coming today, so its card, widget and Live Activity no longer show a day, only the hours the carrier gives for today.
• When the carrier that will deliver a parcel has been told about it but hasn’t scanned it yet, the card now says, for example, “Announced to DHL, not received yet” instead of “Delivery with DHL”.
• A parcel returned to its sender now stays returned while the carrier scans it on its way back, instead of showing as in transit again.
• A place written in another script, such as Chinese, Japanese or Korean, now shows its town and country, like “Shenzhen, China”, in scans and planned steps alike; and one like “SCARBOROUGH - ON - CANADA” now shows Canada and its flag.
• When a carrier publishes its timeline in advance, the parcel’s page now says that Peek shows the real scans as they arrive, and a planned step goes away as soon as its time has passed.
• 72 more carriers now have a truck of their own, in their colours, and when you choose a carrier, those from your country come first among the ones that fit the number.
• A shipping container number is now recognised: the parcel’s page says so, tells you where to find the tracking number and offers to choose the carrier.
• J&T Express Mexico and China show a parcel’s scans with the last 4 digits of the recipient’s or sender’s phone: you can now add them with the parcel, or later from its page.
• After you sign in, if your account has no room for all the parcels on this device, Peek now says how many stayed on it.
• Without an account, a parcel looked up with a postcode no longer asks for it again, a parcel whose saved copy can’t be read comes back to the list, and forgetting a parcel works at once, even offline.
• Refreshing your parcels no longer says “No new updates” beside a connection error, and some C Chez Vous steps now appear in your language instead of French.
```
