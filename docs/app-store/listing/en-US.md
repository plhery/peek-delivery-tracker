## Description

```text
Where’s my package?

Paste a tracking number, a carrier link, or a whole shipping email, or scan the barcode on the label. Peek figures out which of 3,500+ carriers has the package and shows its journey on a map, every scan, and the day it should arrive.

No account needed. No ads.

WILL I KNOW WHEN IT MOVES?
Sign in with Apple, Google, or a code sent to your email, and Peek notifies you: every scan, the important steps, or delivery only. An email can tell you it has arrived.

FOLLOWING MORE THAN ONE?
They share one list, the next to arrive on top, riding on its map. With an account, the same list is on your iPhone and on the web at peektracker.com. A widget keeps your next delivery on the Home Screen.

SOMEONE ELSE WAITING FOR IT?
Share the package’s page with a link. They need no app and no account. Wrap it as a gift, and what’s inside stays a surprise until it arrives.

A PASSPORT OF DELIVERIES
With an account, every delivery counts in your passport, and some earn a stamp. Compare passports with friends; your packages stay private.

3,500+ CARRIERS
USPS, UPS, FedEx, DHL, Canada Post, Royal Mail, Evri, DPD, GLS, An Post, Australia Post, La Poste, Swiss Post, PostNL, Correos, Poste Italiane, InPost, China Post, Cainiao, YunExpress, and thousands more: national postal services, couriers, and marketplace shippers.

ALSO IN THE BOX
• A barcode scanner
• Sharing a number or a tracking link from Mail, Messages, or Safari straight into Peek
• Light and dark themes
• English, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, Brazilian Portuguese, Polish, Russian, and Simplified Chinese

PRIVATE AND OPEN SOURCE
Peek shows no ads and sells no data. It draws its own maps, so no map service sees where your packages travel. A package followed without an account is forgotten 30 days after it arrives. Download your data or delete your account from the app, whenever you like. Every line of code is on GitHub: github.com/plhery/peek-delivery-tracker

Peek is independent of the carriers it reads. Their names belong to their owners.
```
