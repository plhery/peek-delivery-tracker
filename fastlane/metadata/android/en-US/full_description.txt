Where’s my package?

Paste a tracking number, a carrier link, or a whole shipping email. Peek figures out which of 4,000+ carriers has the package and shows its journey on a map, every scan, and the day it should arrive.

No account needed. No ads.

Will I know when it arrives? Peek notifies you when a package moves. This build has no Google libraries: with a UnifiedPush distributor such as ntfy on the phone, notifications come through it; without one, Peek checks for news about every 15 minutes. Settings › Notifications says which one is in use. Sign in with Google or a code sent to your email, and an email can tell you it has arrived too.

Following more than one? They share one list, the next to arrive on top, riding on its map. With an account, the same list is on your phone and on the web at peektracker.com. A widget keeps your deliveries on the home screen.

Someone else waiting for it? Share the package’s page with a link. They need no app and no account. Wrap it as a gift, and what’s inside stays a surprise until it arrives.

With an account, every delivery counts in your passport, and some earn a stamp. Compare passports with friends; your packages stay private.

Peek shows no ads and sells no data. It draws its own maps, so no map service sees where your packages travel. A package followed without an account is forgotten 30 days after it arrives. Download your data or delete your account from the app, whenever you like.

The Google Play build can also scan the barcode on a label; this one has no scanner and asks for no camera.

Peek is independent of the carriers it reads. Their names belong to their owners.
