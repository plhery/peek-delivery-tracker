Noto Sans SC Regular writes the Chinese of the server's pictures and of the Chinese preview picture, which Geist
lacks.

Source: https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk/blob/Sans2.004/Sans/SubsetOTF/SC/NotoSansSC-Regular.otf

Cut to GB2312's characters, CJK punctuation, full-width forms and every character of the Chinese copy by
[scripts/subset-chinese-face.py](../../../scripts/subset-chinese-face.py); run it again when new Chinese copy has a
character the face lacks. Distributed under the SIL Open Font License in OFL.txt.
